About Clairen Haus

Built on the work.
Not the theory.

Clairen Haus LLC is an Atlanta-based AI technology and operations consultancy. We help founders and operators build systems that actually hold — then we build the tools to run them.

The Founder

Nearly two decades
of real work.

Clairen Haus was founded by Michelle Robinson — an operations strategist and AI platform developer with nearly 20 years of hands-on experience managing teams, fixing broken systems, and building processes from the ground up.

This isn't consulting built on frameworks borrowed from other consultants. It's built from time spent inside organizations, in the rooms where the real problems live — the ones that don't show up on any org chart.

That experience now powers two things: a consultancy that helps SMBs build operational clarity, and a suite of AI platforms designed to run the systems we design — so the work doesn't just live in a deck.

Clairen Haus is based in Atlanta, GA and works with clients across the country.

How We Work

We don't do performative consulting.

01

We tell you what we see

If the problem isn't what you think it is, we'll say so. Kindly. Directly. With receipts. You hired us to find the thing — not to agree with your assumptions.

02

We meet you where you are

Not everyone needs a 90-page strategy doc. Some people need a conversation, a clear next step, and someone to hold the thread. We work at the level that actually helps.

03

We build for real life

The best system is the one your team can run without you on speed dial. We design for the reality you have — then help you build toward the one you want.

Tell us what's not working.

We don't do long sales calls or complicated onboarding. You tell us what's going on. We tell you honestly if we're the right fit. Then we get to work.
