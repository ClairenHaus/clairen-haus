i.The problem

Booked in one tool, met in another.

Calls were booked through a generic calendar booking page and held in a separate video app, outside the Clairen Haus brand.

  • ✕A generic booking page
  • ✕A separate video app
  • ✕Booking and calls in two places
  • ✕Not in the Clairen Haus look
  • ✕Links to find before every call

Before: two outside toolsAfter: one branded flow

ii.Process first

Map the guest’s path, from start to room.

I mapped every step a guest takes, from picking a time to sitting in the call, and kept each one inside the Clairen Haus brand.

  1. Pick a timeOnly open times, in the guest’s own time zone
  2. BookedWritten straight to the calendar
  3. ConfirmedA branded email with an invite
  4. The roomA private video room with a lobby
  5. ChangesReschedule or cancel from the email

Illustration of the booking flow; times are examples.

  1. Only open timesThe booking page reads the real calendar and only offers open times, in the guest’s own time zone.
  2. Booked onto the calendarEach booking is written to the calendar.
  3. A branded confirmationThe guest gets a Clairen Haus confirmation email with an invite.
  4. A private room for every bookingEach booking gets its own video room, with a lobby until I join. Nothing to download.
  5. Changes without the back-and-forthGuests can reschedule or cancel from their email.
  6. Room for the whole teamRooms hold up to 7 people, with an audio-only switch for a weak connection.

iv.Where it runs

Live, and it’s the button on this page.

  • It runs at book.clairenhaus.com. Every “Book a call” button on this site goes there.
  • Two calls to choose from today: a 30-minute intro to Commons and a 30-minute call about Layla.
  • Guests see open times in their own time zone and get a private video link, with nothing to download.
  • Rooms were tested with 7 people at once on the live site, and everyone could hear everyone the whole time.
