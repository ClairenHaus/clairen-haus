i.The problem

Everything after the deposit ran through email.

Clairen Haus website clients sign an agreement and pay a deposit, then the build begins. Without a portal, the rest ran through email: sending firm details and photos, finding the signed agreement, and checking where things stood.

Clients needed one place to check, and the business needed a way to move each client forward without writing every update by hand.

  • ✕Firm details sent by email
  • ✕Photos in a separate thread
  • ✕“Where’s the signed agreement?”
  • ✕“Where do things stand?”
  • ✕Every update written by hand

Before: all by emailAfter: one portal

ii.Process first

First, map the journey from yes to live.

Before any code, I mapped the client journey from signed agreement to live site, decided on sign-in by email link so there are no passwords to forget, and defined what clients see and what only the admin sees. Six steps came out of it, and every client can see all six.

  1. SignedThe agreement
  2. Deposit paidThrough Stripe checkout
  3. Content receivedDetails and photos
  4. BuildingThe site takes shape
  5. ReviewClient feedback
  6. LiveLaunched

iii.Then the tool

Then the tool: a portal that opens the moment they pay.

A private portal with its own sign-in, a client view and an admin view. A client who pays their deposit through Stripe checkout lands straight in their portal, signed in. Scroll to watch a build move.

iv.Where it runs

Live, private, and checked.

  • It runs at portal.clairenhaus.com. The sign-in page is public; client pages are private.
  • Planned, built and put live in two days, October 14 and 15, 2026.
  • Every part is checked automatically: sign-in, the client dashboard, the admin view, payments and the checkout hand-off. 65 automated tests, all passing.
  • No real client is shown. Screenshots of the client view use sample data from the test setup.
