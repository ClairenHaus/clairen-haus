i.The problem

You built the community. Someone else owns the house.

If your community still lives in a Facebook group, you don’t own it, you’re renting it.

Your members, your content and your history live on someone else’s platform, under their rules. Members log in to the platform’s app, see the platform’s logo, and get nudged toward other communities.

  • ✕You’re renting
  • ✕Their rules, their pricing
  • ✕Their logo on your community
  • ✕Members nudged elsewhere
  • ✕Your name: one tab among many

Before: a rented platformAfter: a home you own

ii.Process first

It starts with a conversation, not a sign-up form.

Done-for-you Commons starts with a call about your community, your members and what you want them to feel when they log in. Then it’s built in your brand, tested and handed over.

  1. Book a callYour community, your members and what you want them to feel when they log in
  2. Built in your brandYour logo, colors, web address, email sender and first admin account, set up and tested
  3. You open the doorsInvite your members to a home you own. Hosting, updates and support are handled

iii.Then the tool

Then the tool: everything members need, in one private app.

Your logo, your colors and your name from the first screen. No platform branding, and no other communities to wander off to. Try the demo-mode switch.

iv.Where it runs

On any phone, in one tap.

  • Built mobile-first. Members add it to their home screen in one tap, and it opens full screen with your icon.
  • No App Store needed.
  • You own it. It runs under your web address and your accounts. Your members are your members.
  • Moving is simple: invite your members from Skool, Circle or Facebook to your new address.
  • The same members-only platform as Sistahz In Systems, packaged so a coach, brand or organization can launch under its own name.
