i.The problem

The phones ring after everyone goes home.

Calls keep coming at night and on weekends, when nobody is in the office.

Emergencies can’t wait until morning, and routine requests still need booking.

  • ✕A call after hours
  • ✕Nobody in the office
  • ✕An emergency that can’t wait
  • ✕A routine request, still unbooked
  • ✕The office finds out tomorrow

Before: calls waitAfter: a plan for every call

ii.Process first

First, decide how every call should go.

The work started with the call itself, before any AI: which calls can’t wait, who gets them, how routine requests get booked, and what the office needs to know in the morning. Layla follows that map.

  1. A call comes in after hours
  2. Layla answers and sorts itEmergency or routine?
    EmergencyStraight to the on-call technicianRoutineBooked into the next open appointment while the caller is on the line
  3. The customer gets a confirmationBy email
  4. The office is caught upDesigned to send a notice for every booking and a morning briefing of the night’s calls

iii.Then the tool

Then the tool: Layla.

A voice agent that answers the call, sorts emergencies from routine requests, books the next open appointment while the caller is on the line and emails the customer a confirmation. Tap the switch to hear both kinds of call.
