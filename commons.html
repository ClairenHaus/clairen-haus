Why Commons

You built the community. Someone else owns the house.

You’re renting

Your members, your content and your history live on someone else’s platform, under their rules. If they change pricing or policy, you change with them.

Their prices, their cut

The platform sets the price and can change it, and some take a cut of what you sell. From their own pricing pages: Skool is $9/mo plus a 10% transaction fee, or $99/mo. Circle starts at $89/mo and Mighty Networks at $79/mo, billed yearly.*

Your brand gets buried

Members log in to the platform’s app, see the platform’s logo, and get nudged toward other communities. Your name ends up as one tab among many.

What’s inside

Everything your members need, in one private app

Your logo, your colors and your name from the first screen. No platform branding, and no other communities to wander off to.

  • Private, branded community appYour logo, colors and name, on your own web address. Members sign up, get onboarded and build a profile.
  • Feed, posts and commentsMembers post, comment and like, and earn rewards for joining in.
  • Direct messagesPrivate member-to-member messages. You choose when members unlock them.
  • CoursesCourses, modules and lessons with progress tracking, inside the same app.
  • EventsCreate events with your meeting links. Members see them on the calendar and RSVP.
  • Member directoryMembers can find each other and connect.
  • Levels, coins, badges and a leaderboardRewards that give members a reason to come back and take part.
  • Admin toolsManage members, award coins and badges, and keep the space the way you want it.
  • Demo mode for privacyOne switch hides member names, photos and contact details, so you can screen-share your community safely.
  • On their home screenMembers add it to their phone’s home screen in one tap. It opens full screen with your icon, like an app, with no App Store needed.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need to be technical?

Not with done-for-you. We set it up, connect your web address and email, and hand you the admin login. Day to day you post, invite and manage members from the admin tools. DIY is for people comfortable setting up and hosting a web app themselves.

Can I move my members over from Skool, Circle or Facebook?

Yes. You invite your existing members to your new address. With done-for-you we plan the move together on the call so nobody gets lost.

Who owns the community and the data?

You do. It runs under your web address and your accounts. Your members are your members.

What does the $149/month cover?

Hosting, updates and support for your done-for-you community. There’s no cut of what you sell.

Is there a monthly fee with DIY?

Not to Clairen Haus. DIY is a one-time $997 for the code. You pay your own hosting providers directly, and setup and support aren’t included.

Can my members use it on their phones?

Yes. It’s built mobile-first, and members can add it to their home screen so it opens full screen with your icon, like an app. No App Store needed.
