Refund Policy

If your system is not operational within 5 business days of receiving complete access credentials, you are entitled to a full refund. No refunds are issued after the system is operational and in use.

Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep the other's business information, system access, and operational details confidential. Clairen Haus will not share, sell, or use your data for any purpose outside of delivering the agreed services.

Data Access and Security

To deliver the EA service, you authorize Clairen Haus to access your Gmail account (read and draft access) and Google Calendar (read access) via Google OAuth.
