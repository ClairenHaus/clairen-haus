Discuss a Business Problem How we work

Start with the problem that keeps coming back

Most operational problems are not one-off. They repeat because something in the way work moves through the business is unclear, missing or held together by habit. We start there, before recommending any tool.

Problems we help with

  • The same tasks get redone or forgotten every week
  • Client onboarding depends on one person remembering every step
  • Information lives in too many tools and inboxes
  • AI could probably help, but you are not sure where it fits
  • Growth keeps exposing gaps in how work gets done

  1. Understand the problem

    We look at how the work actually happens today: who does what, where it stalls and what it costs you in time.

  2. Fix the operation

    Clear processes, ownership and routines come first. Many problems are solved here, without new software.

  3. Choose the right support

    When a tool would help, we choose the right one: AI inside your existing workflow, software we build, or a better website.

How Clairen Haus helps

Everything we offer grows out of one-on-one operations consulting. The consulting comes first; AI and software are tools we use when the problem calls for them.

Our core service

Operations Consulting

One-on-one support for small businesses with recurring operational problems.

We work directly with you, the owner or operator, to understand where work breaks down and why. Together we put clear processes, responsibilities and routines in place, so the business runs with less firefighting and fewer things depend on you.

Built on the consulting

AI Implementation

Practical AI inside the operations, workflows and systems you already have.

Once the process is clear, AI can take on parts of it. We help you choose where it fits, set it up inside the tools you already use, and make sure your team knows how to work with it. The goal is less repetitive work, not a new system to manage.

  • Identifying the tasks in your workflow that AI can reliably handle
  • Setting it up within your existing tools and processes
  • Clear guidance for your team on when and how to use it
