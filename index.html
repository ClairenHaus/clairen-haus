Who we are

Serious about the work.
Human about the rest.

Clairen Haus is an operations consultancy built on nearly two decades of doing the actual work — not theorizing about it. We've managed teams, fixed broken systems, built processes from scratch, and sat in rooms where the real problems don't show up on any org chart.

We're not a big firm. We're not trying to be. What we are is direct, skilled, and genuinely invested in the people we work with — whether that's a founder figuring out why everything still runs through them, or an operator who knows something's off but can't name it yet.

And because we believe in building tools that fit how people actually work, we also develop AI-powered platforms and communications infrastructure — so the systems we design don't just live in a deck.

What we do

Three ways
we show up.

  1. 01

    Process & Systems Design

    We map how work actually moves through your organization — not how it's supposed to — and design systems that don't fall apart when you're not watching. SOPs, workflows, operating rhythms, team structure. Built to last.

    Core Practice
  2. 02

    Operations Consulting

    Advisory
  3. 03

    When the right tool doesn't exist off the shelf, we build it. AI-powered dispatch, voice agents, SMS infrastructure, custom platforms — designed around your workflow, not the other way around.

Bootcamps & Cohorts

Our bootcamps and cohort programs are for business owners, operators, and entrepreneurs who are ready to stop winging their operations and start building something that works. Whatever stage you're at. Whatever your background.
