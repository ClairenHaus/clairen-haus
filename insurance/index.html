The solution

Six systems. One back office.

We build and install these systems into your business so they run without you touching them. You focus on conversations — the system handles the rest.

01 — Lead tracking

Every lead, captured and organized

A centralized system that captures leads from every source — referrals, social media, events, website — and puts them in one pipeline with full context.

  • Auto-capture from web forms, DMs, and referral links
  • Lead source tagging so you know what's working
  • Pipeline stages from new lead to closed deal
  • Mobile-friendly so you can add leads on the go
02 — Automated follow-up

No lead left behind

Automated text and email sequences that fire based on where a lead is in your pipeline. Follow-up happens whether you remember or not.

  • Timed follow-up sequences (day 1, 3, 7, 14, 30)
  • Personalized templates that sound like you
  • Smart triggers based on lead activity
  • Alerts when a cold lead re-engages
03 — Appointment reminders

Cut no-shows in half

Automated SMS and email reminders before every scheduled appointment — with confirmation requests so you know they're coming.

  • 24-hour and 1-hour reminder sequences
  • One-tap confirm or reschedule links
  • Calendar sync so nothing gets double-booked
  • Automatic reschedule flow for cancellations
04 — Client intake

Intake that runs itself

Digital intake forms that collect everything you need before the first meeting — pre-filled, organized, and ready to go when you sit down with a client.

  • Custom intake forms for different policy types
  • Auto-populate client records from form submissions
  • Document upload for IDs, beneficiary info, etc.
  • Pre-appointment summary sent to your inbox
05 — CRM dashboard

Your whole book of business, one screen

A custom dashboard that shows your full pipeline, follow-up status, upcoming appointments, and revenue — all in real time.

  • Pipeline view with drag-and-drop stages
  • Activity feed showing all touchpoints per lead
  • Revenue tracking and policy status
  • Weekly performance snapshot delivered by email
06 — Client organization

One source of truth for every relationship

A single system where every prospect and client lives — with notes, history, policy details, and next steps in one place.

  • Contact profiles with full interaction history
  • Policy tracking (type, carrier, renewal dates)
  • Birthday and anniversary reminders
  • Tagging system for segmentation and outreach
