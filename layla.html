Why Layla

After hours, a missed call is a missed job.

Things break after five

Homeowners call when the AC quits or the roof starts leaking, and that’s often in the evening or on a weekend, when nobody is in the office.

Voicemail doesn’t book jobs

Plenty of callers won’t leave a message. They hang up and call the next company on the list, and you never hear about it.

You can’t pick up everything

You’re on a roof, under a house, driving or at dinner with your family. Answering every call yourself isn’t realistic.

How it works

Three steps, then she’s on the line.

  1. We set her up for your business

    On a call, we go through your services, service area, hours and the arrival windows you offer. Layla answers in your company’s name.

  2. She answers when you can’t

    After hours, calls to your business line reach Layla. She finds out what’s wrong, takes the caller’s name, number and address, and reads them back to get them right.

  3. The job lands in your inbox

    She books the visit into an open arrival window, emails the customer a confirmation and emails you the details.

Questions

Good to know

Is Layla a real person?

No. Layla is an AI voice receptionist, and she introduces herself to callers as the virtual assistant.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. You keep your business number. After hours, calls to it are sent to Layla. We set that up together.

How does she know when I can come out?

We set her up with the arrival windows you offer, and she books callers into the next open one. How she connects to the schedule you already use is something we work out on the call.

How do I hear about new bookings?

By email. After each booking, you get the caller’s name, number, address, the problem and the booked time, and the customer gets a confirmation email.

What about emergencies?

Layla asks questions that help tell an emergency from a routine visit, like whether anyone in the home is elderly, very young or has a medical condition. On the call, we decide together how urgent calls should be handled for your business.

Are the calls on this page real customers?

No. They’re samples with AI voices. The videos are dramatized, and the longer call is a test call with Layla’s own replies and a scripted caller.
