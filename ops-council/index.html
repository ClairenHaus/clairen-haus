MEET YOUR COUNCIL

Four perspectives.
One shared context.

01

JORDAN

Strategy & Alignment

“I see your priorities. I call the right things.”

CORE QUESTION Are we working on the right things?
  • Clarifies priorities and competing commitments
  • Connects weekly decisions to 90-day outcomes
  • Creates focus, sequencing, and decision clarity

02

CAMERON

Systems & Operations

“I design the way the work moves. I make it stick.”

CORE QUESTION Does the way the work moves make sense?
  • Finds bottlenecks, friction, and repeat work
  • Builds workflows, processes, SOPs, and handoffs
  • Understands the process before automating it

03

RILEY

People & Culture

“I tune the dynamics so your team brings their best.”

CORE QUESTION Do the right people know what they own, and can they execute it?
  • Clarifies roles, ownership, and accountability
  • Strengthens communication and follow-through
  • Reduces founder dependency across the team

04

BLAKE

Performance & Foresight

“I quantify the patterns. I de-risk the next, and what’s next.”

CORE QUESTION What is the data telling us, and what is likely to happen next?
  • Tracks KPIs and operating performance
  • Spots trends, risks, and early warning signals
  • Turns evidence into forecasts and better decisions
HOW IT WORKS
