This Agreement is made on the date signed below between Clairen Haus (Clairen Haus LLC, …) (“Clairen Haus”) and … (…) (“Client”).

1. Project

Clairen Haus will rebuild Client’s website at …, based on the preview at … (the “Site”). The Site will include up to … pages built from Client’s existing content, and:

  • a mobile-friendly, responsive design;
  • HTTPS (an SSL security certificate) on Client’s domain;
  • structured data (schema) and FAQ sections to help search engines and AI assistants understand the firm;
  • a contact form that delivers messages to …;
  • launch on Client’s domain and a post-launch check of every page.

2. Build fee

The build fee is $3,000, paid in two parts:

  • $1,500 deposit due on signing, paid through the secure payment link on this page. Work begins when the deposit is received.
  • $1,500 balance due when Clairen Haus confirms the Site is ready to launch. The Site goes live once the balance is paid.

The deposit covers work started and is non-refundable once work has begun, except as stated in Section 11.

3. Care plan

After launch, Client’s care plan costs $199 per month. The first month after launch is free: billing starts 30 days after the launch date and is then billed monthly in advance via …. It includes:

  • hosting of the Site;
  • SSL certificate renewal;
  • regular backups;
  • security and uptime monitoring;
  • small content edits, up to 30 minutes per month (unused time does not roll over).

The care plan is month-to-month. Either party may cancel it with 30 days’ written notice (email is fine).

4. Not included

The following are not included and will be quoted in writing before any work starts: new copywriting beyond Client’s existing content; pages beyond the number in Section 1; logo or brand design; photography; third-party integrations (for example scheduling, payments or practice-management tools); domain registration and email hosting fees; answer-engine optimization (AEO) services; and content edits beyond the monthly care-plan time. Extra work is billed at … or a fixed quote Client approves first.

5. Revisions and timeline

The build fee includes two rounds of revisions before launch. Clairen Haus aims to launch within 7–14 days after receiving the deposit, Client’s approval of the preview and changes, and the access listed in Section 6. Delays in Client’s feedback, content or access extend the timeline. If Client does not respond for … days, Clairen Haus may pause the project until Client is ready.

6. Client responsibilities

Client is responsible for:

  • the accuracy of all content on the Site, including descriptions of services, fees and results;
  • making sure the Site complies with the attorney advertising and professional conduct rules that apply to Client. Clairen Haus does not provide legal, ethics or compliance review;
  • providing timely feedback and access to the domain and DNS settings needed to launch;
  • having the right to use any text, logos, photos or other materials Client provides.

7. Ownership

Once the build fee is paid in full, Client owns the Site’s content and the design files created specifically for Client. Clairen Haus keeps ownership of its general tools, code, templates and know-how, and grants Client a permanent license to use them as part of the Site. Third-party software and fonts remain under their own licenses. Clairen Haus may show the Site in its portfolio unless Client says no in writing.

8. Hosting and domain

Client’s domain stays registered in Client’s name. Clairen Haus hosts the Site while the care plan is active. If the care plan ends, Clairen Haus will provide a copy of the Site’s files within … days, as long as all fees are paid, and Client is responsible for hosting from then on.

9. Confidentiality

Each party will keep confidential any non-public information it receives from the other. Clairen Haus does not need, and will not request, access to client files or case information.

10. Limitation of liability

Clairen Haus will perform its services with reasonable care. It does not guarantee search rankings, mentions by AI assistants, new clients, or uninterrupted uptime. Clairen Haus is not liable for indirect or consequential damages, such as lost profits or lost business. Its total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees Client paid in the … months before the claim.

11. Termination

Either party may end this Agreement with written notice. If Client ends the project before launch, Clairen Haus keeps the deposit for work done. If Clairen Haus ends the project before launch for any reason other than Client’s non-payment or non-response, it will refund the deposit. Fees for work completed and care-plan months already started are not refundable.

12. General

This is the entire agreement between the parties about the Site, and changes must be in writing. It is governed by the laws of …. Electronic signatures count as originals.
