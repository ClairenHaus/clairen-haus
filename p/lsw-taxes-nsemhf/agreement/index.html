This Agreement is made on the date signed below between Clairen Haus (Clairen Haus LLC, …) (“Clairen Haus”) and … (…) (“Client”).

1. Project

Clairen Haus will rebuild Client’s website at …, based on the preview at … (the “Site”). The Site will include up to … pages built from Client’s existing content, and:

  • a mobile-friendly, responsive design;
  • HTTPS (an SSL security certificate) on Client’s domain;
  • structured data (schema) and FAQ sections to help search engines and AI assistants understand the business;
  • a contact form that delivers messages to …;
  • launch on Client’s domain and a post-launch check of every page.

2. The build is a gift

Clairen Haus is building the Site as a gift. There is no build fee ($0), and nothing is charged for the build now or at launch. The build includes two rounds of revisions.

3. What Client gives in return

In return for the free build, Client agrees to:

  • leave an honest review of Clairen Haus (for example on Google or Facebook) once the Site is live; and
  • give Clairen Haus permission to feature Client’s name, business name and logo, the Site (including screenshots and before-and-after images) and Client’s testimonial in Clairen Haus marketing and advertising, such as its website, social media, emails and ads.

The review and testimonial should be Client’s own honest opinion. Clairen Haus will never ask Client to say anything that isn’t true.

4. Hosting

Client’s hosting plan (hosting only) costs $29 per month. It starts on the day Client signs and is billed monthly in advance by card through Stripe, using the secure payment link on this page. It includes:

  • hosting of the Site;
  • SSL certificate renewal;
  • regular backups;
  • security and uptime monitoring.

The hosting plan is month-to-month. Either party may cancel it with 30 days’ written notice (email is fine).

5. Not included

The following are not included and will be quoted in writing before any work starts: new copywriting beyond Client’s existing content; pages beyond the number in Section 1; logo or brand design; photography; third-party integrations (for example online scheduling, payments or field-service software); domain registration and email hosting fees; answer-engine optimization (AEO) services; and content edits. Extra work is billed at … or a fixed quote Client approves first.

6. Revisions and timeline

The build includes two rounds of revisions before launch. Clairen Haus aims to launch within 0–3 business days after Client signs, approves the preview and changes, and provides the access listed in Section 7. Delays in Client’s feedback, content or access extend the timeline. If Client does not respond for … days, Clairen Haus may pause the project until Client is ready.

7. Client responsibilities

Client is responsible for:

  • the accuracy of all content on the Site, including descriptions of services, prices, brands, licenses and customer reviews;
  • making sure the Site complies with the advertising, licensing and consumer-protection rules that apply to Client’s business (including any tax-preparer credentials or registration details Client chooses to display). Clairen Haus does not provide legal or compliance review;
  • providing timely feedback and access to the domain and DNS settings needed to launch;
  • having the right to use any text, logos, photos or other materials Client provides.

8. Ownership

Once this Agreement is signed, Client owns the Site’s content and the design files created specifically for Client. Clairen Haus keeps ownership of its general tools, code, templates and know-how, and grants Client a permanent license to use them as part of the Site. Third-party software and fonts remain under their own licenses. Clairen Haus may show the Site in its portfolio and marketing as described in Section 3.

9. Hosting and domain

Client’s domain stays registered in Client’s name. Clairen Haus hosts the Site while the hosting plan is active. If the hosting plan ends, Clairen Haus will provide a copy of the Site’s files within … days, as long as all hosting fees are paid, and Client is responsible for hosting from then on.

10. Confidentiality

Each party will keep confidential any non-public information it receives from the other. Clairen Haus does not need, and will not request, access to Client’s customer records.

11. Limitation of liability

Clairen Haus will perform its services with reasonable care. It does not guarantee search rankings, mentions by AI assistants, new clients or calls, or uninterrupted uptime. Clairen Haus is not liable for indirect or consequential damages, such as lost profits or lost business. Its total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees Client paid in the … months before the claim.

12. Termination

Either party may end this Agreement with written notice. Because the build is a gift, no build fee is owed or refunded. If Clairen Haus ends the project before launch for any reason other than Client’s non-payment or non-response, it will refund the hosting already paid. Hosting months already started are otherwise not refundable.

13. General

This is the entire agreement between the parties about the Site, and changes must be in writing. It is governed by the laws of …. Electronic signatures count as originals.
