    Who owns the site?

    You do. Once you sign, you own your site’s content and the design files made for you, and your domain stays registered in your name.

    How long does it take?

    0–3 business days from your signed agreement, your approval of the preview and any changes, and access to your domain settings. We walk you through the domain step.

    No. Your new site is built from the content already on your current site. If you’d like new pages or rewritten copy, we quote it first.

    Answer-engine optimization: helping your business show up when people ask Google or AI assistants like ChatGPT for a tax preparer near them. Your new site already has the groundwork (structured data and FAQs). Ongoing AEO is optional.

    Can I add photos or change the wording?

    Yes. Send a photo of yourself or your office any time, and tell us anything you’d like reworded. You check the content for accuracy, and we make the changes you ask for.

    Can I cancel hosting?

    Yes. It’s month-to-month with 30 days’ written notice, and email is fine. If you leave, we hand over a copy of your site files.

    What happens after I sign?

    Within 1 business day you’ll get an email with your build-portal login, where you can follow progress, update your information and add photos.
