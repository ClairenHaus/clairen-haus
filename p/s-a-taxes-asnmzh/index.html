    Questions

    Good questions to ask

    Who owns the site?

    You do. Once the build fee is paid in full, you own your site’s content and the design files made for you, and your domain stays registered in your name.

    How long does it take?

    Usually 0–3 business days from your payment, your approval of the preview and any changes, and access to your domain settings. We walk you through the domain step.

    Which package should we pick?

    If you mainly want a modern, secure site, The Fast Track. If busy season means phone tag and documents by email, The Automator adds online scheduling and a link to your secure client portal. The Client Machine adds a tax intake form and local SEO, so new clients arrive pre-screened and more local taxpayers find you.

    Do I need to write anything?

    No. Your new site is built from the content already on your current site. If you’d like new pages or rewritten copy, we quote it first.

    What is AEO?

    Answer-engine optimization: helping your business show up when people ask Google or AI assistants like ChatGPT for an HVAC company near them. Your new site already has the groundwork (structured data and FAQs). Ongoing AEO is optional.

    Can I add photos of our work or change the wording?

    Yes. Send photos of your office or team any time, and tell us anything you’d like reworded. You check the content for accuracy, and we make the changes you ask for.

    When does the care plan start?

    At launch, and the first month is free. The $99/month billing starts 30 days after launch.

    Can I cancel the care plan?

    Yes. It’s month-to-month with 30 days’ written notice, and email is fine. If you leave, we hand over a copy of your site files.

    What happens after I pay?

    Within 1 business day you’ll get an email with your build-portal login, where you can follow progress, update your information and add photos.
