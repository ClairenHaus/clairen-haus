    Questions

    Good questions to ask

    Who owns the site?

    You do. Once the build fee is paid in full, you own your site’s content and the design files made for you, and your domain stays registered in your name.

    How long does it take?

    Usually 7–14 days from your deposit, your approval of the preview and any changes, and access to your domain settings. We walk you through the domain step.

    Do I need to write anything?

    No. Your new site is built from the content already on your current site. If you’d like new pages or rewritten copy, we quote it first.

    What is AEO?

    Answer-engine optimization: helping your firm show up when people ask Google or AI assistants like ChatGPT for a lawyer. Your new site already has the groundwork (structured data and FAQs). Ongoing AEO is optional.

    Who checks the content against bar advertising rules?

    You do. Content accuracy and attorney advertising compliance stay with the firm, and we make any changes you ask for.

    Can I cancel the care plan?

    Yes. It’s month-to-month with 30 days’ written notice, and email is fine. If you leave, we hand over a copy of your site files.

    What happens after I pay the deposit?

    Within 1 business day you’ll get an email with your build-portal login, where you can follow progress, update your information and add photos.
