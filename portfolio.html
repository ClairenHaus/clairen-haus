Community Platforms

Private, branded spaces where members learn, post and connect, with the admin tools to run them.

Live

Sistahz In Systems Community Platform

A private members’ community for Sistahz In Systems, a community for women in tech. Members get a feed with posts, questions and polls sorted by topic, discussion threads, private messages, courses, an events calendar and a member directory, with coins, levels and badges that reward taking part.

  • Admin dashboard for members, roles, badges and post categories
  • Animated, on-brand sign-in screen
  • Every permission rule enforced in the database, and 35 member workflows tested on the live site before launch

AI Agents & Demos

Working demos of AI that takes recurring front-office work off a team’s plate. Demo builds are labeled as demos.

Demo build

After-Hours AI Phone Agent (“Daisy”)

A demo voice agent built for a confidential client, a heating and cooling company that needs its phones covered at night and on weekends. Daisy answers the call, sorts emergencies from routine requests, books the next available appointment while the caller is on the line and emails the customer a confirmation.

  • Emergencies go straight to the on-call technician
  • Designed to send the office a booking notice and a morning briefing of the night’s calls
  • In the demo, bookings come from a sample schedule; a live install connects to the company’s own scheduling system
