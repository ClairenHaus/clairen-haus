How I work

Process first. Then the tool.

Most operational problems aren’t one-off. They repeat because something in how work moves is unclear, missing or held together by habit. I start there, before recommending any tool. Many problems are solved without new software at all.

a.

A better process

Clear processes, ownership and routines, documented so others can do them.

b.

Practical AI

AI inside the tools you already use, on the tasks it can reliably handle.

c.

Software built for the job

When the right tool doesn’t exist, I build it, like every system on this page.

Working together

A simple start. No long sales calls.

  1. i.Tell me the problem

    In your own words. It doesn’t need to be figured out.

  2. ii.Talk it through

    How the problem shows up in your business, day to day.

  3. iii.Get a clear recommendation

    What I would do and which kind of support fits, or if I’m not the right fit.

  4. iv.Do the work together

    We put the fix in place and make sure it holds up in real life.

The founder

Nearly two decades of real work.

I’m Michelle Robinson, founder and CEO of Clairen Haus. Before I built software, I spent nearly 20 years in operations: managing teams, fixing broken systems and building processes from the ground up.

That’s still how I work. I start with the operation itself, stay close to the people running it, and only bring in AI or software when it genuinely makes the work easier.

“I’ve been in the room. I know what broken looks like, and I know what fixed feels like.”
