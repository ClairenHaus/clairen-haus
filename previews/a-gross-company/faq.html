FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the firm and its services.

Do you work with individuals or businesses?

Both. The firm serves individual and business clients, with tax, accounting, consulting, forensic and specialized services.

What kinds of tax returns do you prepare?

Tax planning and return preparation for individuals, corporations, partnerships, LLCs and LLPs, estates, trusts and gifts, and not-for-profit organizations.

Is tax planning only at filing time?

No. The firm takes a proactive rather than reactive approach, keeping current on new tax laws and legislation to find planning opportunities that reduce current and future taxes throughout the year.

Can you help if I get a letter from the IRS or the state?

Yes. Taxing authority representation is one of the firm’s tax services.

Do you handle bookkeeping and payroll?

Yes. Bookkeeping (monthly, quarterly or annual), general ledger and financial statements, and computerized payroll services.

I’m starting a business. Can you help set it up?

Yes. Services include accounting system setup for new businesses and help choosing the type of entity.

Do you offer forensic accounting?

Yes. The firm analyzes financial records in connection with dispute resolution and fraud or theft investigations, and measures the extent of financial losses. Litigation support and expert witness services are also offered.

What if the service I need isn’t listed?

Please ask. The list isn’t all-inclusive, and if it isn’t a service the firm provides, you’ll be referred to a qualified professional.

I’m a new client. How do I get my information together?

Start with the client tax organizer. Returning clients can ask for a pro forma organizer that includes last year’s information and carryover data.

Where is the office and how do I reach you?

The office is at 700 Kinderkamack Rd., Oradell, NJ 07649. Call (201) 265-1722, fax (201) 265-0207, or email agross@agrosscpa.com.
