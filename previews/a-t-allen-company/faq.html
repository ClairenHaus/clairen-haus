FAQ

Questions clients ask

Straight answers about the firm, our services and how to reach us.

When was A.T. Allen & Company founded?

The firm was founded in 1926 by A.T. Allen in Raleigh, North Carolina, and has operated as a partnership since its inception.

Where is your office?

Our practice is conducted from one office: 2245 Gateway Access Point, Suite 201, Raleigh, NC 27607. We moved there on July 11, 2008. Get directions.

Do you work with individuals as well as businesses?

Yes. We prepare income tax returns for all types of businesses as well as individuals, and offer a wide range of accounting, tax and consulting services to both.

What if I need a service that isn’t listed?

Ask. Our list is by no means all-inclusive. If it is not a service we provide, we will be happy to refer you to a qualified professional.

Can you represent me in an IRS or state audit?

Yes. IRS and state audit representation is one of our services, and we represent clients before the various federal and state tax agencies.

Can I send you documents securely?

Yes. Clients can access their files through our secure, online document-sharing application. Call the office to get set up.

Can I pay my bill online?

Yes, we accept payments online. Call the office if you need help with a payment.

Do you help with QuickBooks?

Yes. We provide QuickBooks setup, support and training, and monthly bookkeeping either in-house or by remote access.

Who are the partners?

Ernest W. Warner, Jr., CPA is the Managing Partner. The other partners are Robert Alexander, CPA, James Carter, CPA and Tina Goertz, CPA.

How do I reach you?

Call (919) 781-7550, fax (919) 781-7525, or email administrator@atallencpa.com.
