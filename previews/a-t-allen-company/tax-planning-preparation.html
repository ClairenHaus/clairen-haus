Tax Planning & Preparation

Tax planning and preparation form a winning combination for our successful individual and business clients.

For individuals and businesses

We prepare income tax returns for all types of businesses, as well as individuals. Whether you are an individual or a multi-tiered partnership, our experienced staff can develop tax strategies that take advantage of new tax laws and legislation.

Getting your information together

A tax organizer can make it easier to pull your tax information together. Current clients can ask the office for a “proforma” organizer that includes prior-year information and carryover data. See Client Resources.
