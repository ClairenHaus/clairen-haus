About the firm

Rooted in Boone County since 1945

A Zionsville law office with a long history of serving the residents, landowners and businesses of Boone County.

Experienced, focused & accessible

The firm was founded in 1945 under the name Donaldson, Andreoli & Truitt in Lebanon, Indiana, primarily serving clients in and around Boone County. It was later named Andreoli & Jacob, and was renamed Andreoli Law Office in 2011.

As Boone County has grown, Michael Andreoli has worked to become the attorney of choice for people interested in commercial, residential or business development in the county. The firm has also represented a great number of Boone County residents in family, criminal, personal injury and general civil litigation matters.

You’ll find Michael Andreoli knowledgeable and accessible to clients, and dedicated to providing sound legal solutions to your needs.

Recognition

On January 4, 2010, Mike Andreoli was presented with a Distinguished Hoosier Award signed by Indiana Governor Mitch Daniels, recognizing his work on a Government Reorganization Committee for Eagle Township, Union Township and the Town of Zionsville.
