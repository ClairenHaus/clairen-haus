Practice area

Criminal Law

Felony and misdemeanor defense, primarily in Boone County and its surrounding counties.

What the office does

Andreoli Law Office has acted as lead counsel in a number of high-profile cases involving arson, burglary and other felony charges. The office has also helped clients deal quickly and effectively with matters such as license suspensions after an OWI arrest.

The firm’s criminal practice is mainly in Boone County and its surrounding counties. Areas it handles include:

  • Felonies, misdemeanors and juvenile cases
  • Drug offenses
  • Operating While Intoxicated (OWI / DUI / DWI)
  • Traffic infractions and offenses
  • Battery
  • Protective orders
  • Probation violations
  • Appeals

How a criminal case generally moves in Indiana

A case usually begins with an arrest or a summons, followed by an initial hearing where the charges are read and bail or release conditions are addressed. From there come discovery, motions, plea discussions and, if the case is not resolved, a trial.

OWI. Indiana uses the term Operating While Intoxicated. An OWI arrest can lead to a driver’s license suspension that runs separately from the criminal charge itself, which is why acting early matters.

Protective orders are civil orders, but violating one can be a crime. People seeking an order and people served with one both benefit from understanding the process before the hearing.

If you have been charged or expect to be, talk to a lawyer before you talk about the case with anyone else.
