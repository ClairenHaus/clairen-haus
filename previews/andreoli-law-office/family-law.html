Family Law

From a complex dissolution of marriage to a simple recalculation of child support.

What the office does

Whether it is a complex dissolution of marriage involving the various dimensions of wealth management and an exit strategy, or a simple recalculation of child support, Andreoli Law Office provides legal experience and ongoing assistance.

The firm represents clients in family law disputes involving:

  • Divorce (dissolution of marriage)
  • Child custody
  • Visitation (parenting time)
  • Adoption
  • Child support
  • Non-payment or recalculation of child support

How these matters generally work in Indiana

Dissolution of marriage. Indiana calls divorce “dissolution of marriage.” A final decree generally cannot be entered until at least 60 days after the petition is filed. Property is divided under Indiana law, which starts from a presumption that an equal division is just and reasonable.

Custody and parenting time. Courts decide custody based on the best interests of the child. Indiana’s Parenting Time Guidelines are commonly used as the starting point for a schedule.

Child support. Support is calculated under the Indiana Child Support Guidelines, based mainly on both parents’ incomes and the parenting time arrangement. When circumstances change, either parent can ask the court to modify the amount.

Every family is different, and the details of your situation will shape what applies.
