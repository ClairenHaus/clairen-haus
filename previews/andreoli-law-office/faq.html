FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the office and how it can help.

Where is Andreoli Law Office located?

The office is at 1393 West Oak Street, Zionsville, Indiana 46077, at the southeast corner of Ford Road and West Oak Street (SR 334). It sits on the south side of the PNC Bank building, with a private entrance and parking outside the door.

How do I schedule a meeting?

Call the office at (317) 873-6266 or email Michael Andreoli at mandreolilaw@gmail.com.

What areas of law does the firm handle?

Zoning and development, real estate and land use, family law, criminal law, general civil litigation, personal injury, appellate law and agricultural law.

Which areas does the firm serve?

The firm primarily serves clients in and around Boone County, Indiana. Its zoning and development work extends throughout the state, and its criminal practice is mainly in Boone County and the surrounding counties.

How long has the firm been practicing?

The firm was founded in Lebanon, Indiana in 1945 as Donaldson, Andreoli & Truitt. It was later named Andreoli & Jacob and was renamed Andreoli Law Office in 2011. Michael Andreoli has practiced with the firm since 1978.

Who will handle my matter?

Michael J. Andreoli is the firm's attorney. He is assisted by his paralegal, Barri Andreoli.

Does the firm help with rezoning, variances and plats?

Yes. The firm has handled rezoning and platting from the largest residential subdivision in Boone County to small personal minor plats, as well as variances, permits and reviews of municipal ordinances for project compliance.

Can the firm help me buy or sell a home or land?

Yes. The office can assist with all phases of the transaction, including negotiation, the purchase agreement and closing, and it has resolved property line disputes through negotiation and litigation.

What family law matters does the firm handle?

Divorce, custody and visitation, adoption, and setting, enforcing or recalculating child support, from complex dissolutions to simple support recalculations.

Can the firm help after an OWI arrest?

Yes. The office has helped clients deal quickly and effectively with matters such as license suspensions after OWI arrests, and handles OWI, drug offenses, traffic, battery, protective orders, probation violations, felonies, misdemeanors and juvenile cases.

Does the firm handle appeals?

Yes. The firm has represented individuals, corporations and municipalities in the appeal process, as both appellant and appellee.

Answers are general information drawn from the firm’s own materials, not legal advice about your situation.
