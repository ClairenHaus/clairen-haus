General Civil Litigation

Representing plaintiffs and defendants through the stages of litigation.

What the office does

The firm handles a wide variety of litigation, ranging from construction-based litigation to contract disputes, and from debt collection to business disputes. It has represented clients through the stages of litigation, on behalf of both plaintiffs and defendants.

  • Construction disputes
  • Contract disputes
  • Debt collection
  • Business disputes
  • Property line disputes

The stages of a civil case

  • Before filing. Many disputes are resolved by a demand letter or negotiation before a lawsuit is ever filed.
  • Pleadings. The plaintiff files a complaint; the defendant answers and may raise defenses or counterclaims.
  • Discovery. Each side exchanges documents and information and may take depositions.
  • Motions and settlement. Many cases narrow or settle at this stage, often through mediation.
  • Trial and judgment. Cases that do not settle are decided by a judge or jury, and a judgment may then need to be collected or appealed.

Deadlines to bring a claim depend on the type of claim, so it is worth getting advice early.
