About the practice

Experienced, focused and accessible.

  • B.S. Business, Indiana University, 1970
  • J.D., Indiana University School of Law, 1973; admitted to the Indiana Bar, 1974
  • County Attorney, Boone County, 1979–91
  • Attorney for the Boone County Board of Zoning Appeals (1979–89), Boone County Drainage Board and Zionsville Parks Board (1979–86)
  • Town Attorney, Zionsville, 1979–86
  • Member: Indianapolis, Boone County (President 1982–83), Indiana State (Delegate 1978–91) and American Bar Associations

The firm was founded in 1945 as Donaldson, Andreoli & Truitt in Lebanon, Indiana, primarily serving clients in and around Boone County. It later became Andreoli & Jacob and was renamed Andreoli Law Office in 2011.

With Boone County's growth, Michael Andreoli has worked to become the attorney of choice for commercial, residential and business development in the county, while continuing to represent residents in family, criminal, personal injury and civil matters.

Business clients named on the firm's site include Beazer Homes, Centex Homes, Pulte Homes, Kite Companies, Prologis and Gene B. Glick Company. In January 2010 Mike received a Distinguished Hoosier Award for his work on a government reorganization committee for Eagle Township, Union Township and the Town of Zionsville.

Common questions

Answers from the firm.

Short answers drawn from the firm's own published information.

What zoning and development matters does the firm handle?

Commercial, industrial and residential matters, from rezoning and platting a large residential subdivision to a small minor plat, obtaining variances or permits, and reviewing municipal ordinances for project compliance.

Can the firm help me buy or sell a home or land?

Yes. The firm assists with all phases of a real estate transaction, including negotiation, the purchase agreement and closing, and has resolved property line disputes through negotiation and litigation.

What criminal cases does the firm take?

Cases primarily based in Boone County, commonly including Operating While Intoxicated (OWI/DUI/DWI), battery, traffic offenses and drug-related offenses.

Where is the office?

At 1393 West Oak Street in Zionsville, at the southeast corner of Ford Road and West Oak Street (SR 334), on the south side of the PNC Bank building, with a private entrance and parking outside the door.
