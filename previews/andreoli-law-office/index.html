Zoning & development

From the county’s largest subdivision to a small minor plat

The firm has handled rezoning and platting for the largest residential subdivision in Boone County as well as small personal minor plats, and has represented commercial clients through every stage of development in industrial, commercial and general business complexes.

Michael Andreoli served as Boone County Attorney (1979–91), attorney for the Boone County Board of Zoning Appeals (1979–89) and Town Attorney for Zionsville (1979–86).

“Our local knowledge of the Boone County area provides our clients with exceptional representation in all zoning and development hearings and negotiations.”Michael Andreoli
