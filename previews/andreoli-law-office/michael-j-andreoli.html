Michael J. Andreoli

Attorney · Andreoli Law Office

(317) 873-6266

A long-time Boone County resident, Michael Andreoli has practiced law at the former Donaldson, Andreoli & Truitt, then Andreoli & Jacob, and now Andreoli Law Office since 1978.

Education and admission

  • B.S. Business, Indiana University, 1970
  • J.D., Indiana University School of Law, 1973
  • Admitted to the Indiana Bar, 1974

Public service

  • County Attorney, Boone County, Indiana, 1979–91
  • Attorney for the Boone County Board of Zoning Appeals, 1979–89
  • Attorney for the Boone County Drainage Board, 1979–86
  • Attorney for the Zionsville Parks Board, 1979–86
  • Town Attorney, Zionsville, Indiana, 1979–86

Professional memberships

  • Indianapolis Bar Association
  • Boone County Bar Association (President, 1982–83)
  • Indiana State Bar Association (Delegate, 1978–91)
  • American Bar Association

Recognition

On January 4, 2010, Mike Andreoli was presented with a Distinguished Hoosier Award signed by Indiana Governor Mitch Daniels. He was recognized for his work serving on a Government Reorganization Committee for Eagle Township, Union Township and the Town of Zionsville.

“Our local knowledge of the Boone County area provides our clients with exceptional representation in all zoning and development hearings and negotiations.”Michael Andreoli
