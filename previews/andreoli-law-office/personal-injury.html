Personal Injury

Andreoli Law Office has handled a gamut of accident-related cases for all manner of clients.

What the office does

Andreoli Law Office has the experience and background to represent clients in personal injury matters, and has handled a gamut of accident-related cases for all manner of clients.

A personal injury is any harm caused to a person, such as a broken bone, a cut or a bruise. Bodily injury resulting from an attack, negligence or an accident falls in the same category, and you can file a lawsuit to recover damages from whoever is responsible.

Common causes of personal injury

  • Automobile accidents
  • Trucking accidents
  • Motorcycle accidents
  • Bus accidents
  • Boat accidents
  • Aviation accidents
  • Slip and fall
  • Workplace injuries
  • Defective products
  • Medical malpractice
  • Nursing home abuse
  • Animal bites
  • Head and brain trauma
  • Catastrophic injuries
  • Wrongful death

Deadlines and rules worth knowing in Indiana

Time limits. In Indiana, most personal injury claims must generally be filed within two years. Claims against a government body usually require a written tort claim notice much sooner, often within 180 days.

Shared fault. Indiana uses modified comparative fault: your recovery can be reduced by your share of fault, and it is barred if you are found more than 50% at fault.

Medical malpractice claims in Indiana generally go through a medical review panel process before a lawsuit can proceed in court.

These are general rules with exceptions, so the sooner you ask about your own situation, the more options you keep.
