About Asheville Law Offices

A boutique law practice on Pack Square in downtown Asheville, North Carolina.

Our focus

Understand the situation. Choose the right course. Reach the right resolution.

Asheville Law Offices is a boutique law practice in Asheville, North Carolina which represents clients in a wide variety of legal matters. Our focus is to help our clients understand their situation, advise on the best course of action, and help them seek the appropriate resolution.

We offer skilled representation on matters ranging from real estate transactions, residential and commercial, to corporate and business law, estate planning and wills.

Service, expertise and commitment are the foundation of Asheville Law Offices, and we look forward to assisting you with any legal matters you may have.

Jennifer Belter Formichella

Attorney

Born and raised on a dairy farm in South Burlington, Vermont, Jennifer has served the Western North Carolina area since 2002, with a reputation for resolving legal issues, simple and complex, efficiently and correctly.
