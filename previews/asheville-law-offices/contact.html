Getting here

Directions

The office is on the 4th floor, Suite 414, of the Biltmore Building in downtown Asheville’s Pack Square, close to the Buncombe County Courthouse and Asheville City Hall.

Take Exit 5-A (Merrimon Avenue) from I-240 and follow the signs to Pack Place. At the end of the exit ramp, turn left onto Broadway/Rt. 25 South (coming from the east, this takes you back under the interstate). Travel about 3 blocks to the crest of the hill, then turn left into Pack Square. The Biltmore Building runs along the entire left side of the Square; the front entrance is to the left of the flag poles in front of the building.

Parking

Street and meter parking is available in the Square. These garages are all nearby:

  • Pack Square Garage: Across from Mast General Store
  • Biltmore Avenue Garage: Under the Aloft Hotel
  • Civic Center Garage: Behind the Buncombe County Public Library, off Haywood Street
  • Rankin Avenue Garage: Between Haywood Street and Rankin Avenue
  • Wall Street Garage: Off Otis Street, between Wall Street and Battery Park Avenue
