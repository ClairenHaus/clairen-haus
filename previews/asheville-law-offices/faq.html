Frequently asked questions

About the firm, the matters it handles, and getting to the office.

What kinds of legal matters does Asheville Law Offices handle?

The firm handles residential and commercial real estate (home purchases and refinances, home equity loan closings, commercial property title examinations, and offers to purchase and contracts), title searches and title problems, foreclosures, the formation and modification of commercial and residential leases, creditor bankruptcy, contract negotiations, the creation of corporate entities, general business and commercial law, corporate law, construction law, general business litigation, and wills and estates.

Who will I work with?

Asheville Law Offices is operated by attorney Jennifer Belter Formichella, who has served the Western North Carolina area since 2002 in a variety of legal areas. She is a graduate of the University of Vermont and the Benjamin N. Cardozo School of Law in New York City.

Where is the office?

The office is on the 4th floor, Suite 414, of the Biltmore Building at 1 North Pack Square in downtown Asheville, NC 28801. It is easily accessible from I-240 (exit 5-A, Merrimon Avenue) and close to the Buncombe County Courthouse and Asheville City Hall.

Where can I park?

Street and meter parking is available in the Square. Nearby garages include the Pack Square Garage (across from Mast General Store), the Biltmore Avenue Garage (under the Aloft Hotel), the Civic Center Garage (behind the Buncombe County Public Library off Haywood Street), the Rankin Avenue Garage (between Haywood Street and Rankin Avenue) and the Wall Street Garage (off Otis Street between Wall Street and Battery Park Avenue).

How do I get to the office from I-240?

Take Exit 5-A (Merrimon Avenue) from I-240 and follow the signs to Pack Place. At the end of the exit ramp, turn left onto Broadway/Rt. 25 South (coming from the east, this takes you back under the interstate). Travel about 3 blocks to the crest of the hill, then turn left into Pack Square. The Biltmore Building runs along the entire left side of the Square; the front entrance is to the left of the flag poles in front of the building.

Does the firm handle both residential and commercial real estate?

Yes. The firm represents clients in residential and commercial real estate matters, including home purchases and refinances, home equity loan closings, commercial property title examinations, and offers to purchase and contracts.

Can the firm help me set up a business?

Yes. The firm handles the creation of corporate entities, contract negotiations, and general business, commercial and corporate law matters.

Does the firm prepare leases?

Yes. The firm handles the formation and modification of leases, both commercial and residential.
