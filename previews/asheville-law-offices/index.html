What changes

Specific fixes, drawn from the current site.

  • A site that works on a phone

    The current pages have no mobile viewport on an HTML 4 layout, so phones show a shrunken desktop page, and the homepage’s Flash slideshow (img-rotate4.swf) no longer plays in any current browser. The concept is built mobile-first.

  • Put the attorney on the homepage

    The homepage never names the lawyer; Jennifer Belter Formichella appears only on the About page. The concept introduces her up front, with her background.

  • A practice list people can scan

    Today the practice areas run together as one long line in the page header. The concept groups them into a clear list.

  • Schema that says “law firm”

    The site has no structured data. The concept adds LegalService, Person and FAQPage markup with the full address and phone, so search engines and AI assistants can read it.

About the practice

A boutique practice on Pack Square.

University of Vermont · Benjamin N. Cardozo School of Law · Member, 28th Judicial District Bar Association

Asheville Law Offices is a boutique law practice in Asheville, North Carolina, operated by Jennifer Belter Formichella. The firm offers skilled representation on matters ranging from real estate transactions, residential and commercial, to corporate and business law, estate planning and wills.

The office is on the 4th floor, Suite 414, of the Biltmore Building in downtown Asheville’s Pack Square, easily reached from I-240 exit 5-A (Merrimon Avenue). Street and meter parking is on the square, with the Pack Square, Biltmore Avenue, Civic Center, Rankin Avenue and Wall Street garages nearby.

Common questions

Answers from the firm’s own site.

Each answer below is drawn from facts the firm already publishes, marked up so search engines and AI assistants can cite it. General information only, not legal advice.

Who runs Asheville Law Offices?

Asheville Law Offices is operated by attorney Jennifer Belter Formichella, who has served the Western North Carolina area since 2002. She is a graduate of the University of Vermont and the Benjamin N. Cardozo School of Law.

What kinds of matters does the firm handle?

Residential and commercial real estate, including purchases, refinances, home equity loan closings, title examinations and title problems, leases and foreclosures, plus business and corporate law, construction law, business litigation, creditor bankruptcy, and wills and estates.

Where is the office?

1 North Pack Square, Suite 414, Asheville, NC 28801, on the 4th floor of the Biltmore Building in downtown Asheville’s Pack Square.

Where can I park?

Street and meter parking is available on the square. The Pack Square, Biltmore Avenue, Civic Center, Rankin Avenue and Wall Street garages are all nearby.
