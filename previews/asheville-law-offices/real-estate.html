What a real estate matter involves

Buying, selling or refinancing property brings together several documents and parties at once: the contract between buyer and seller, the lender’s loan documents, the title to the property, and the closing itself, where money changes hands and the new deed and deed of trust are signed and recorded.

In North Carolina, attorneys play a central role in real estate closings. Reviewing title, preparing and explaining the closing documents, and seeing that everything is properly recorded are all part of the work.

Residential and commercial

On the residential side, that can mean a first home purchase, a refinance, or a home equity loan closing. On the commercial side, a title examination of the property is an important early step, so that easements, liens, restrictions or other issues are known before you commit.

Asheville Law Offices represents clients on both residential and commercial transactions, and can review an offer to purchase or contract before you sign it.
