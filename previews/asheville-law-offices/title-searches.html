Title Searches & Title Problems

A title search shows what the public record says about a property. When that record has a gap or a defect, it needs to be resolved before the property can change hands cleanly.

What the firm handles

  • Title searches
  • Title problems
  • Commercial property title examinations

What a title search covers

A title search traces a property’s ownership through the public records, typically at the county Register of Deeds and the courts, and looks for anything that affects it: recorded deeds of trust and liens, judgments, easements, restrictions and other recorded interests.

When there is a title problem

Title problems come in many forms, such as a gap in the chain of ownership, a loan that was paid off but never released of record, an error in a deed or property description, or an unresolved estate. Each one calls for a different fix, and some can be handled with a corrected or supplemental document while others take more work.

The firm handles both the search and the problems it turns up, for residential and commercial property.
