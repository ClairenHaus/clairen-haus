Practice

Mr. Green has extensive trial experience in both civil and criminal defense litigation. He has represented individuals, corporations and business owners in premises liability matters, personal injury cases and contractual issues. While much of his experience is in business litigation, he also has extensive experience in white collar criminal defense, probate, and complex family law custody and divorce matters. He has also been hired as a litigation consultant for his expertise in trial strategy and trial preparation.

Mr. Green believes that careful, well-thought-out preparation is paramount to representing clients properly, and relies on a strong work ethic to give each client the best representation possible.

At St. Mary’s Law School, Mr. Green was recognized for both academics and advocacy. He was appointed to the Office of Academic Excellence, received the Judge Jack B. Miller Academic and Advocacy Scholarship and the Educating for Justice Award, and was selected for the National Order of Barristers and the Inns of Court. He was the first second-year law student, and the first St. Mary’s student, to win the American Trial Lawyers Association Trial Competition.

Personal
