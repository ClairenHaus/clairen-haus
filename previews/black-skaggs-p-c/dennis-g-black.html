Practice

Since 1979, Dennis Black has been dedicated to building his practice as a trial lawyer, following a two-year appointment as briefing attorney to the Honorable William M. Steger, U.S. District Judge for the Eastern District of Texas. He maintains board certification with the Texas Board of Legal Specialization in Personal Injury Trial Law.

Mr. Black has extensive experience handling a wide variety of complex litigation, in the courtroom and in managing complex multi-party litigation. He serves the firm’s clients as both trial and coordinating local counsel, and has taken over 100 cases to verdict in jury trials in state and federal court. He has represented individuals, corporations, business owners and premises owners in a wide variety of personal injury and wrongful death matters.

Mr. Black offers a hands-on approach tailored to each client’s needs, and enjoys many long-term client relationships. He has earned a reputation among his peers as a thorough, well-prepared competitor.

Past cases typical of Mr. Black’s practice

  • Defense of an oil exploration company in a wrongful death suit arising from head trauma on drilling premises
  • Defense in a trucking company accident involving wrongful death and serious personal injuries
  • Defense of an apartment complex in a wrongful death case arising from a falling tree on the premises
  • Defense of the owner of a plumbing company sued for allegedly causing brain and neuropsychological injuries in a motor vehicle collision
  • Defense of a feed manufacturer in a case arising from the exposure of cattle to a toxin
  • Defense of business owners for injury-causing falls on premises
  • Defense of an East Texas company regarding alleged construction defects involving breach of contract and the Texas Deceptive Trade Practices Act

Past matters are listed to describe the practice; they do not predict the outcome of any other case.

Personal

Dennis Black grew up in Tyler and met his wife, Pam, at John Tyler High School. He attended Tyler Junior College and Baylor University, then Baylor University School of Law, graduating second in his class with cum laude honors in 1979. He served as briefing attorney for Judge William M. Steger, Eastern District of Texas, Tyler Division, from 1979 to 1981. He joined Ramey & Flock, P.C. in 1981 and became a shareholder in 1985. In 2002, after more than 20 years there, he started his own firm, and in 2007 he formed Black & Skaggs, P.C. with Jeramy “Jamy” Skaggs. Dennis and Pam make their home in Whitehouse, Texas, and have raised three children, Camille, Travis and Maci; they have two grandchildren. He enjoys serving in his church, lifting weights, working on cars and spending time with his family.
