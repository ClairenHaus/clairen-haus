FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers drawn from the firm’s own description of its practice. For anything about your own matter, call the firm.

Where are Black & Skaggs’ offices?

The firm has two offices: 103 Baylor Drive, Tyler, Texas 75703, and 11107 Wurzbach Rd, Suite 101, San Antonio, Texas 78230. Both can be reached at (903) 593-9400; the fax number is (903) 593-9401.

What kinds of cases does the firm handle?

Black & Skaggs handles personal injury defense, toxic torts, commercial litigation, labor and employment, will contest and trust litigation, and wills and estate planning, in both state and federal courts.

Whom does the firm represent in personal injury cases?

The firm defends self-insured corporations, insured clients and other entities in personal injury cases involving claims such as negligence, product liability, premises liability, toxic torts and breach of warranty, from two-party cases to complex multi-party and mass tort litigation.

Does the firm take cases to trial?

Yes. The firm’s primary objective in litigation is to evaluate and prepare cases to be presented to a jury, if needed. Dennis Black and Marcus Carroll have each taken over 100 cases to verdict in jury trials in state and federal court.

Does the firm prepare wills and trusts?

Yes. The firm drafts wills, trusts, powers of attorney, advance directives and codicils, and helps parents name a guardian for minor children in a valid will.

Can the firm help with a dispute over a will or trust?

Yes. The firm handles will contests, including disputes over undue influence, incapacity and administration expenses, and trust litigation, including challenges to a trust’s validity, requests to modify a trust and claims of trustee self-dealing.

Is a consultation free?

For estate disputes, the firm invites people to contact it for a free initial consultation. For other matters, call (903) 593-9400 to discuss your situation.

How does the firm approach fees?

The firm says it prides itself on providing excellent legal representation without charging excessive fees and costs, and constantly reevaluates how to avoid unnecessary overhead and over-lawyering.

Does the firm represent employers?

Yes. The firm represents employers in discrimination, wrongful discharge, retaliation, wage and hour and non-compete matters, and counsels them on hiring, discipline, policies and terminations to reduce the risk of litigation.

Is any attorney board certified?

Dennis G. Black is Board Certified in Personal Injury Trial Law by the Texas Board of Legal Specialization.

How can I reach a specific attorney?
