Common questions
Who does Black & Skaggs represent in personal injury cases?

Its website says the firm represents self-insured corporations, insured clients and other entities in the defense of personal injury claims, including negligence, product liability, premises liability, toxic torts and breach of warranty, in both complex multi-party cases and smaller two-party cases.

Does the firm handle will contests and trust disputes?

Yes. The firm describes handling will contests arising from errors in administration, undue influence or incapacity, and trust litigation over the validity of a trust, requests to alter a trust, or self-dealing by a trustee.

Can the firm prepare my will or estate plan?

Yes. The firm drafts wills, trusts, powers of attorney, advance directives and codicils, and says estate plans should be updated as financial, marital, parenting and philanthropic circumstances change.

About the practice

The information clients need before they call.

  • Dennis G. Black: Board Certified in Personal Injury Trial Law, Texas Board of Legal Specialization
  • Baylor University School of Law · The University of Texas School of Law · St. Mary’s University School of Law
  • Admissions include the State Bar of Texas, U.S. Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit and the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Texas

Dennis Black began his trial practice in 1979 after a two-year appointment as briefing attorney to U.S. District Judge William M. Steger in the Eastern District of Texas. After more than 20 years at Ramey & Flock, P.C., he started his own firm in 2002 and formed Black & Skaggs, P.C. with Jamy Skaggs in 2007.

The firm’s toxic tort work includes asbestos, silica and chemical-exposure claims for manufacturers, distributors and premises owners, in cases filed throughout Texas and in several other states. Its commercial work covers fraud, breach of contract, business torts, tortious interference and injunctive relief, and it counsels employers on hiring, discipline, policies and terminations.
