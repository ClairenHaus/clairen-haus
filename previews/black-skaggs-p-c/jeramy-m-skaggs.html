Practice

Jamy Skaggs is a trial lawyer who handles a wide variety of complex litigation defending small and large corporations, small business owners and individuals. He has handled cases involving personal injury and wrongful death, construction defects, premises liability, product liability and trucking and transportation, and has tried cases to verdict in state and federal courts.

He has extensive experience in toxic tort litigation, including asbestos and silica cases involving products liability and premises liability claims, representing both manufacturer and premises-owner clients. He has expertise in the state-of-the-art defense, medical causation and epidemiology, and has worked extensively with experts whose opinions on medical information, industrial hygiene data and scientific literature are vital to a defense. He also has first-hand experience with the legislative changes affecting asbestos and silica litigation in Texas, including the role of the Multi-district Litigation courts.

In insurance defense, Mr. Skaggs represents insured individuals and companies sued over personal injuries or property damage, working with experts across the country on medical, engineering, construction, financial and other technical questions. He has built a practice that provides cost-effective service while vigorously representing insureds and keeping insurance companies well informed.

He has also represented companies in commercial disputes involving breach of contract, business torts, deceptive trade practices, tortious interference, negligent misrepresentation and injunctive relief, and advises businesses on the employment-law implications of day-to-day decisions such as hiring, discipline, employment policies and terminations.

Past cases typical of Mr. Skaggs’ practice

  • Defense of a large industrial product manufacturer in cases filed throughout Texas claiming serious personal injuries and death allegedly caused by the design or manufacture of an asbestos-containing product
  • Defense of a premises owner in cases involving personal injury and wrongful death
  • Defense of a large telecommunications corporation in a case brought by an employee over traumatic on-the-job injuries
  • Defense of a home health company against allegations of breach of contract, breach of a non-compete and interference with business relations
  • Representation of an East Texas company and business owner in a case involving claims of fraud and deceptive trade practices
  • Defense of an individual sued for allegedly causing a traumatic spinal injury

Past matters are listed to describe the practice; they do not predict the outcome of any other case.

Personal

Jamy Skaggs was raised in Midland, Texas, and graduated from Midland Lee High School in 1992. He attended Texas A&M University from 1992 to 1996, graduating summa cum laude with a B.S. in Political Science, and the University of Texas School of Law from 1996 to 1999, then moved to Tyler to join Ramey & Flock, P.C. In 2007 he formed Black & Skaggs, P.C. with Dennis Black. He married Kristin Gentry, an East Texas native, in 2004; they have five children, Micah, Gavin, Nathan, Kallie and Abbie, and serve together in their church. He enjoys weight training, running, golf and travel, and follows Texas A&M Aggies sports.
