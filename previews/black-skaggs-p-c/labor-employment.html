Practice area

Labor & Employment

Black & Skaggs represents employers in litigation and counseling on all types of employment discrimination issues and claims.

Employment claims

  • Employment discrimination
  • Wrongful discharge
  • Defamation
  • Retaliation
  • Personal injury
  • Intentional infliction of emotional distress
  • Invasion of privacy

The firm also has experience with wage and hour issues, employee health and safety, and covenants not to compete.

Counseling to prevent litigation

The firm counsels employers on the employment-law implications of day-to-day business decisions, such as hiring, disciplining employees, adopting employment policies and terminations, with the goal of minimizing the likelihood of litigation from those decisions.

This page describes the area in general terms and is not legal advice. Every matter is different; call the firm to discuss yours.
