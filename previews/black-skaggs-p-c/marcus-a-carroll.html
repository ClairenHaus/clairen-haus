Practice

Mr. Carroll has extensive experience handling a wide variety of complex litigation, in the courtroom and in managing complex multi-party litigation. He serves the firm’s clients as both trial and coordinating local counsel, and has taken over 100 cases to verdict in jury trials in state and federal court. He has represented individuals, corporations, business owners and premises owners in a wide variety of commercial, personal injury and wrongful death matters.

Mr. Carroll has represented a wide variety of clients across many areas and enjoys many long-term client relationships. He has earned a reputation among his peers as a thorough, well-prepared attorney, with an ability to relate to clients and juries from diverse backgrounds and experiences.

Past cases typical of Mr. Carroll’s practice

  • Defense of a large supplier in cases filed throughout Texas claiming serious personal injuries and death allegedly caused by the sale of asbestos-containing products
  • Defense of an oil exploration company in a wrongful death suit arising from a frack tank explosion
  • Representation of the defendants in an oil and gas case, including cross-claims tried to a jury
  • Defense of a trucking company in an accident involving serious personal injuries
  • Defense of an apartment complex in a wrongful death case
  • Representation of plaintiffs severely injured or killed in serious collisions
  • Representation of oil and gas clients

Past matters are listed to describe the practice; they do not predict the outcome of any other case.

Personal

Marcus Carroll grew up in Tyler, as did his wife, Jane. He graduated from Robert E. Lee High School in 1977, earned a B.B.A. at Baylor University in 1981 and graduated from Baylor University School of Law in 1983. He joined Potter, Guinn, Minton, Roberts and Ireland in 1983, moved to Ireland, Carroll & Kelley in 1984 and became a shareholder in 1987. In 2000 he started his own law firm, and in 2016 he became of counsel to Black & Skaggs, P.C.
