FAQ

Questions, answered.

Short answers about the firm and how we work. Anything else, call (208) 322-1852.

How long has the firm been in business?

Since 1984. Our philosophy of treating every client relationship like a partnership dates to when we started this business.

Are you the same firm as Carolyn Boyce CPA and Associates?

Yes. Boyce & McKenney CPA, PLLC was formerly Carolyn Boyce CPA and Associates PC, at the same office on S Americana Blvd in Boise with the same phone number.

What kinds of tax returns do you prepare?

Returns and planning for individuals, corporations, partnerships, LLCs and LLPs, estates, trusts and gift, and not-for-profit organizations.

Can you help if the IRS or the state contacts me?

Yes. We represent clients before federal and state tax agencies. Call the office and send us a copy of the notice.

Do you only work at tax time?

No. We provide tax expertise throughout the year, plus monthly, quarterly or annual bookkeeping and payroll.

What if I need a service you don’t list?

Please ask. If it isn’t a service we provide, we’ll gladly refer you to a qualified professional.

How do I send documents securely?

Use our secure FileShare portal; the office can set up your login.

Who do I contact?

Call (208) 322-1852, or email Kimberly McKenney, CPA at Kim@cpaboyce.com, Nancy Davis (bookkeeper and office manager) at nancy@cpaboyce.com, or the front desk at FD@cpaboyce.com.
