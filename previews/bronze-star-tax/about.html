About the firm

A family tax office in Coppell since 2006

Bronze Star Tax Service is a family owned tax office licensed to practice tax reporting in Texas, run by Neil and Lisa Thomas since January 2006.

Our story

Neil and Lisa Thomas opened Bronze Star Tax Service in Coppell in January 2006. Neil brought an accounting degree, early years with the IRS, an Enrolled Agent credential earned in 2001 and experience running a 48-member team for a nationwide tax franchise. Lisa brought her own years of tax preparation, starting in 2003.

For the 2006–2007 and 2009–2010 tax seasons, while Neil was deployed overseas with the US Army Reserves, Lisa ran all office operations and tax consultations. Neil has since retired as a Lieutenant Colonel after 36 years.

What that means for you

  • You work directly with the owners, not a rotating seasonal staff
  • An Enrolled Agent who can represent you before the IRS if a problem comes up
  • Tax preparation, planning, bookkeeping and payroll in one office
  • Authorized IRS e-file provider

Professional memberships

National Association of Enrolled Agents (NAEA) and the Texas Society of Enrolled Agents (TxSEA). Neil has served on the executive council of the DFW Area Chapter of TxSEA, including as president.
