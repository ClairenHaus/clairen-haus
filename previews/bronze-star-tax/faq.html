Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about the firm, IRS problems and getting ready for your appointment.

Who will prepare my return?

Bronze Star is a two-person, family owned office. Lisa Thomas, in tax preparation since 2003, and Neil Thomas, an Enrolled Agent since 2001, prepare returns and meet with clients themselves.

What is an Enrolled Agent?

Enrolled Agents are federally authorized tax practitioners, empowered by the U.S. Department of the Treasury to represent taxpayers before all administrative levels of the IRS for audits, collections and appeals. Neil Thomas has been an EA since 2001.

Can you help if I owe the IRS or haven’t filed?

Yes. Bronze Star helps with back taxes, non-filed returns, payroll tax problems, liens, levies, wage garnishment, seizures, offers in compromise, payment plans and innocent spouse relief. The first consultation on an IRS problem is free.

Do you e-file?

Yes. Bronze Star Tax Service is an authorized IRS e-file provider.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes: business tax returns, small business bookkeeping and accounting, P&L statements, online payroll and new business formation.

How do I get ready for my appointment?

Ask for the free tax organizer. Filling it out before you come in helps uncover deductions and makes sure nothing is missed.

Where is the office?

320 S. State Highway 121 N, Suite 305, Coppell, TX 75019. The office is co-located on the SecurLock Storage property. Business hours are 9am to 6pm.

Can I share documents online?

Yes. Clients can use the firm’s secure client portal, and payroll clients have their own online payroll login.
