Meet us

Neil Thomas, EA

Enrolled Agent and co-owner, NTPI Fellow

Neil is an Enrolled Agent certified by the IRS since 2001 to work with clients on their IRS collections issues. Enrolled Agents are federally authorized tax practitioners, empowered by the U.S. Department of the Treasury to represent taxpayers before all administrative levels of the IRS for audits, collections and appeals.

In 2015 Neil added another level to his credentials after completing a three-year course on representation from the National Tax Practitioner Institute (NTPI) of the NAEA. He is an NTPI Fellow.

Background

Neil earned his BA in accounting from Oklahoma State University in Stillwater and has been in the public accounting field since 1985. After a career with the IRS, he purchased and operated a 48-member team for a nationwide tax franchise. With his wife Lisa, he started Bronze Star Tax Service in Coppell in January 2006.

Neil is a longtime member of the DFW Area Chapter of the Texas Society of Enrolled Agents and has served on its executive council, including as president.

Service

Neil retired from a 36-year career as a Lieutenant Colonel (LTC) in the US Army Reserves, including deployments in 2006–2007 and 2009–2010. He and Lisa like to travel and see the country.
