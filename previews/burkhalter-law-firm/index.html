About the practice

The information clients need before they call.

People onlyThe firm represents employees, injured individuals and their families, and whistleblowers. It does not represent employers.

You pay nothing unless you winThe firm pays the fees and expenses incurred during the litigation, including court reporters and experts; the contingency percentage may vary with the facts of the case.

The Burkhalter Law Firm focuses on employment law for a reason the firm states plainly: its clients’ financial lives, and often their integrity, are at stake. The firm represents clients with challenging and complex legal issues from all walks of life, whether a business executive, white-collar professional or blue-collar worker.

Its stated strategy is that success is defined by the client, not the firm: for every case, a plan is built around the client’s priorities. The firm exists, in its own words, for three reasons: to offer counsel, to properly do it, and to do it more effectively. Its promise to clients includes staying in their corner, keeping them updated, always being prepared, responding promptly to calls and emails, and being honest throughout the process.

Common questions

Answers drawn from the firm’s own site.

Question-and-answer content, marked up as FAQ schema, is the format search engines and AI assistants quote directly.

Do you represent employers?

No. The firm states that it represents only people: current and former employees, injured individuals and their family members, and whistleblowers.

Is the case evaluation free?

Yes. Case evaluations are free, and if the attorneys agree to represent you, you owe no attorney’s fees if there is no recovery.

Who pays the costs of the case?

The firm says it pays the fees and expenses incurred by the firm during the litigation, including court reporters and experts. The contingency-fee percentage may vary depending on the facts of the case.

What kinds of whistleblower cases do you handle?

False Claims Act, qui tam and whistleblower cases such as contractor fraud, healthcare fraud, Medicaid and Medicare fraud, kickbacks and Stark Act violations, as well as IRS and SEC whistleblower cases.

Where is the office and when is it open?

111 S. Central St., Knoxville, TN 37902. Office hours are Monday through Friday, 8:45 am to 5:30 pm, and the firm says it is available 24/7 by phone.
