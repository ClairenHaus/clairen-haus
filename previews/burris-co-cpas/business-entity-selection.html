Business entity selection

Choosing your form of entity is a crucial decision with long-standing tax implications, positive or negative.

S corporation, sole proprietor, limited liability company, partnership: we help you evaluate the pros and cons of each entity type and determine the most advantageous form for your business.

What we weigh

Taxes now

How income is taxed to the business and to its owners.

Taxes later

What happens when you add owners, sell, or pass the business on.

Allocations

How profits and losses can be shared among partners.

Your facts

The right answer depends on your situation, not a template.
