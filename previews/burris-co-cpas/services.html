What we do

Tax, accounting and consulting services

A wide range of services for individual and business clients, with personalized, quality service. If you don’t see a service listed, please ask.

  • 01

    Tax Minimization Planning

    Proactive planning for closely held businesses, owners and families: S-Corps, partnerships and 704(b) special allocations.

  • 02

    Tax Preparation

    Federal and multi-state returns for individuals, corporations, partnerships, trusts, estates and not-for-profits.

  • 03

    Estate & Trust Tax

    Estate, gift and trust returns, and planning for an orderly transfer of your business and other assets.

  • 04

    Financial Statements & Compilations

    Timely, accurate financial records and compiled statements for internal use and decision-making.

  • 05

    Business Entity Selection

    S corporation, LLC, partnership or sole proprietor: the pros and cons of each, weighed for your facts.

  • 06

    QuickBooks & Software

    QuickBooks setup, support and training, software selection, and fixing bottlenecks in your processes.

  • 07

    IRS & State Audit Representation

    Representation before the IRS and state tax authorities when a notice or audit arrives.

  • 08

    CFO, CIO & Business Consulting

    On-demand consulting, CFO and CIO assistance, process improvement, and mergers and acquisitions.
