About the practice

Law, ministry and international service.

  • J.D., Emory University School of Law, 1994
  • M.Div., Candler School of Theology, 1994
  • B.A., University of Kansas, 1986
  • Admitted 1994: Georgia; Georgia Court of Appeals; Georgia Supreme Court; U.S. District Court, Southern District of Georgia
  • Member: State Bar of Georgia; Glynn County Bar Association; American Immigration Lawyers Association; Christian Legal Society
  • Languages: Spanish

John M. Butin earned his law degree from Emory University School of Law and his Master of Divinity from Candler School of Theology, both in 1994, the year he was admitted to practice in Georgia.

His biography lists service as General Counsel of MAP International since 1994 and as Priest Associate at St. Marks Episcopal Church since 2003.

The firm is located on St. Simons Island and is a member of the Glynn County Bar Association.
