Employee Benefits

Benefit plans and the rules that govern them, one of the areas Thomas Callow concentrates in.

How the firm handles it

Thomas Callow concentrates his practice in the areas of employee benefits, federal and state taxation, estate planning and general business services for closely held businesses.

What employee benefits law covers

Employee benefits law deals with the plans an employer offers its people, such as retirement plans (for example 401(k), profit sharing and pension plans) and welfare plans like health coverage. These plans are governed by federal law, including ERISA and the Internal Revenue Code, which set rules for plan documents, eligibility, funding, reporting and the responsibilities of the people who run the plan.

Benefits for closely held businesses

For an owner-run company, a benefit plan is both a business decision and a personal one: it affects the owners’ own retirement savings and taxes as well as their employees. Mr. Callow’s practice combines employee benefits with taxation, estate planning and general business services for closely held businesses.

This page explains the area in general terms. It is not legal advice; every situation is different, and the attorneys can tell you how it applies to yours.
