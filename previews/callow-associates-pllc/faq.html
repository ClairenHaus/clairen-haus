FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about what the firm handles, who to ask for, and how to reach the office.

What kinds of matters does Callow & Associates handle?

Estate planning, probate and trust administration, probate and trust litigation, federal and state taxation (including income taxation), employee benefits, real estate law, and general business services for closely held businesses.

Who are the attorneys at Callow & Associates?

Thomas Callow and Charles Lillie.

Which attorney should I ask for?

Thomas Callow concentrates in employee benefits, federal and state taxation, estate planning and general business services for closely held businesses. Charles Lillie focuses on estate planning, probate and trust administration and litigation, and also practices income taxation, real estate law and general business services for closely held businesses.

Does the firm do estate planning for families of second marriages?

Yes. Charles Lillie has done an extensive amount of estate planning for families of second marriages.

Does the firm work with both large and small estates?

Yes. Mr. Lillie has done estate planning for both large and small estates, and has administered both large and small probate estates and trusts.

Can the firm help administer a probate estate or a trust?

Yes. Charles Lillie has administered both large and small probate estates and trusts.

Does the firm handle probate and trust litigation?

Yes. Charles Lillie has handled probate and trust litigation.

Does Callow & Associates work with businesses?

Yes. Both attorneys provide general business services for closely held businesses, and Thomas Callow also concentrates in employee benefits and federal and state taxation.
