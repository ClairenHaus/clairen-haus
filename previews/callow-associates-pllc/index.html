What changes

The same firm, easier to find and easier to read.

Two specific fixes drawn from the current site, plus the details visitors look for first.

On a phone
A page that fits the screen

The current site has no mobile viewport and a table layout, so on a phone it renders about 1,028px wide and visitors pinch and zoom. This version reflows to any screen size.

Secure & structured
HTTPS and readable details

The current site serves over plain HTTP (its HTTPS certificate fails) and carries no structured data. This version is built for HTTPS and names the firm, attorneys, address and practice areas in schema that search engines and AI assistants can read.

One page, organized
Practice areas you can scan

Today the practice areas live inside two short bios. Here they are listed once, with each attorney’s focus, so a visitor knows who to ask for.
