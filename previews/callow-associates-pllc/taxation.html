Practice area

Federal & State Taxation

Federal and state tax matters, including income taxation, connected to the firm’s estate planning and business work.

How the firm handles it

Thomas Callow concentrates his practice in federal and state taxation, among other areas. Charles Lillie also practices in the area of income taxation.

Where tax questions come up

Tax rules touch most of the decisions people bring to a lawyer: how a business is organized and how its owners are paid, how a retirement or benefit plan is set up, how property passes at death, and how an estate or trust reports its income. Federal law is only part of the picture; state rules can apply to the same transaction.

Tax alongside planning

Because the same office handles estate planning, employee benefits and closely held businesses, tax questions can be considered as part of the larger plan rather than after the fact. Mr. Callow’s practice spans federal and state taxation, employee benefits, estate planning and business services; Mr. Lillie’s includes income taxation as well as estate planning and business matters.

This page explains the area in general terms. It is not legal advice; every situation is different, and the attorneys can tell you how it applies to yours.
