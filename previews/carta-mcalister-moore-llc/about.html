About the firm

Counselors at law, focused by design.

At CMM, you work directly with a partner who has focused on a discrete area of law.

Rather than trying to be all things to all clients, we are committed to providing cost-effective services to those who need an experienced professional in our specific areas. We share a common commitment to working collaboratively with you on a customized and coordinated approach to your legal challenge. As we learn about your circumstances and needs, we become stronger advocates and counselors, and as we help you get your bearings, you are better able to act as a co-manager of your legal needs.

We help you navigate unfamiliar terrain

As unique and complex as your legal issues may seem, take comfort in knowing we can help lead the way. We each focus on one specific area of practice, and are confident in our ability to evaluate your legal needs, foresee unanticipated changes, and use our skills to help you reach your destination.

It all starts with a consultation

Your initial consultation is pivotal, and chemistry is key when beginning a legal relationship. From the outset, we gain insight from your experience and perspective. Together we identify the issues, define your goals and develop a strategic plan to reach a fair and reasonable resolution. That process is the foundation for a relationship built on mutual respect and shared judgment.

Your community is our community

We were all born and raised in this area and maintain deep roots in our communities. We understand the sophisticated legal needs and stressful lifestyles of lower Fairfield County residents. See our community involvement.

A LEGUS member firm

Carta, McAlister & Moore is affiliated with LEGUS, an international network of law firms with more than 2,500 lawyers, with affiliated firms in nearly every U.S. state and in 35 countries on five continents. Through LEGUS we can place and receive referrals around the world, so you are in good hands wherever your legal needs arise.
