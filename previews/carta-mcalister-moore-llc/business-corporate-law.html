Business & corporate law

Counsel for privately held companies, from formation to sale.

We advise small to mid-sized privately held companies, closely held start-ups, family offices and entrepreneurs across a broad range of business sectors.

New business formation

When you embark on a new venture, the threshold question is which business form best suits your current needs and future plans. We help you understand the operational, tax and liability issues of each structure and guide you to the best choice. We assist with entity structuring and formation for:

  • Corporations
  • Limited liability companies
  • Partnerships
  • Limited liability partnerships

Operations and governance

As your business moves through its life cycle, we counsel you on compliance, regulatory, staffing, operational and governance issues, and prepare and negotiate the documents that govern it, including succession planning:

  • Shareholder agreements for corporations
  • Operating agreements for limited liability companies
  • Buy-sell agreements between business owners
  • Partnership agreements
  • Investment and buy-in agreements
  • Distributorship agreements
  • Leases and financial transactions
  • Contracts for the sale of goods and services

Buying or selling a business

Whether to structure a sale as an asset sale or an equity sale can have significant consequences. We help you weigh the risks, benefits and tax implications, then guide and manage the transaction from the letter of intent through the closing, including:

  • Asset purchase agreements
  • Stock purchase agreements
  • Membership interest purchase agreements
  • Intellectual property assignments

Business divorce

Closely held businesses sometimes face insurmountable obstacles. When owners need to wind up and dissolve an entity, our corporate and litigation attorneys work together to negotiate and, when necessary, litigate the internal controversy, so your rights and equity interests are protected.

Business and employment law together

Our corporate and employment lawyers often work collaboratively so that all of your business issues are addressed, sometimes before they arise, including executive compensation agreements and employee policies as your business grows.
