Partner

Darcy McAlister, Esq.

Divorce and family law, mediation and collaborative divorce. Darcy believes attorneys should be “counselors at law,” working with you toward a personalized and comprehensive resolution.

Judgment is key

All family law matters are infused with complicated issues that are best addressed with personal care and attention. The choice of counsel shapes how a matter will proceed, and in matters as complex and personal as those involving your family, it is important that you trust the judgment of the lawyer representing you.

A longstanding commitment to client advocacy

From her first job finding employment for developmentally delayed and physically challenged adults, to her MSW clinical externship counseling terminally ill cancer patients, Darcy’s natural inclination is to empower her clients through difficult transitions. She later spent years in the entertainment industry managing internationally known recording artists, including Kool & the Gang, KC and the Sunshine Band, and George Clinton and Parliament Funkadelic, overseeing their professional commitments, negotiating live appearances and advising them on public persona matters. Her family law practice is a natural extension of that commitment.

Dedication, discipline and presentation

As a young, serious student of dance, taught daily by professional artists from New York City dance companies, Darcy learned the dedication and discipline needed to excel, and how subtle nuances can transform a role. That insight shapes how she works with clients to decide whether a case should be mediated, negotiated or litigated.

Personal involvement in professional endeavors

Darcy volunteers as a Special Master for Superior Court family matters and has served as co-chair of the Collaborative Attorneys of Fairfield County. Recognizing that it is not always cost-effective to hire counsel for the entire scope of a matter, she also offers an array of limited representation.

Education

  • J.D., Quinnipiac University School of Law, 2002; Quinnipiac Law Review; federal judicial externship
  • Year of study, Boston College Master of Social Work program
  • B.A., Psychology / Sociology, cum laude, Skidmore College

On a personal note

A Westport native, Darcy lives in lower Fairfield County. She enjoys her dog, traveling, the coastal communities’ activities, and volunteering, including for the Candlewood Watershed Initiative. She serves on the Board of Directors of STAR, Inc., Lighting the Way, which serves individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities and their families.
