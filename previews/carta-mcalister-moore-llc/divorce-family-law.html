Divorce & family law

Family law handled with personal care, and a strategy built around your family.

Our clients deserve more than a simple and formulaic analysis of their legal rights and responsibilities. As counselors at law, our practice is both client-centered and family-focused.

You and your attorney as co-managers

We see our role as co-managers, working cooperatively with you to reach a resolution that reflects the many factors that matter to you. It is our duty to provide a well-reasoned perspective and a strategic approach to the issues in contention, and that depends on the input and perspective you share with us. As you learn through consultation, counseling and education, you become more confident managing your situation, and together we build a personalized strategy intended to reach the best resolution for you and for the family as a whole.

Court, mediation or collaborative divorce?

One of the first and most important decisions is whether your matter will follow the traditional, adversarial model or whether mediation or collaborative divorce would be better. While we are prepared to litigate when circumstances demand, the vast majority of family cases can, and should, be resolved outside the courtroom. We encourage every client to consider the alternatives.

Review counsel and limited representation

We also act as review counsel for parties in mediation, or for anyone who wants an independent assessment of proposed settlement terms, and we offer unbundled services and document preparation for parties who have settled matters themselves. See limited representation.
