Employment law · Darien, CT

Employment counsel for executives, employees and employers.

Carta, McAlister & Moore provides representation to management and employees, with a practice centered on C-suite executives and employees in the financial sector.

Both sides of the table

While practicing with a New York law firm in the first 15 years of his career, Mark Carta primarily represented the interests of corporate management. Although he continues to serve as employment counsel for several corporations and non-profit organizations, Mark now primarily represents individual employees, predominantly C-suite executives and employees in the financial sector.

That prior experience as a management lawyer gives him insight and a balanced perspective that he uses for the benefit of individual clients. Having litigated many key employment contract terms, such as covenants not to compete, confidentiality provisions and change-in-control clauses, he can deliver added value for employment clients.

Even when an employer has no written severance policy, there are many reasons an employee may negotiate a favorable severance in exchange for providing a general release.

Matters we handle

  • Employment agreements
  • Severance agreements
  • Wrongful discharge claims
  • Age and sex discrimination claims
  • Americans with Disabilities Act claims
  • CHRO / EEOC disputes
  • Controversies involving covenants not to compete
  • Trade secret and confidential information disputes
  • Change-in-control provisions
  • Wage and hour violations
  • Mediation of employment law matters
