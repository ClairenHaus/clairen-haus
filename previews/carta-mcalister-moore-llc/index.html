About the practice

The information clients need before they call.

How the firm worksYou work directly with a partner who has focused on a discrete area of law; the firm says it is committed to cost-effective service rather than being all things to all clients.

AffiliationThe firm states it is the only Connecticut firm affiliated with LEGUS, an international network of law firms with members in nearly every U.S. state and 35 countries.

The current site describes a refreshingly simple approach: working closely with each client to design a comprehensive plan for their personal and professional objectives, and forging relationships built on trust, confidence and collaboration. As “counselors at law,” the attorneys commit to understanding a client's goals and working together toward them.

The initial consultation is described as pivotal. From the outset the firm works with the client to identify issues, define goals and develop a strategic plan toward a fair and reasonable resolution.

The attorneys were all born and raised in the area and maintain deep roots in their communities; the firm says it understands the sophisticated legal needs of lower Fairfield County residents.

Common questions

Answers people search for.

Short answers drawn from the firm's own published information, marked up so search engines and AI assistants can quote them.

Where is Carta, McAlister & Moore located?

777 Post Road, 2nd Floor, Darien, CT 06820. The main number is (203) 202-3100 and the general email is info@cmm-law.com.

Who handles employment matters?

Mark R. Carta, the managing partner, focuses on employment law and litigation. The firm represents both management and employees, and Mr. Carta now primarily represents individual employees, predominantly senior executives, at hiring and in severance negotiations.

Does the firm handle divorce without going to court?

Yes. Alongside family law litigation, the firm offers divorce mediation and collaborative alternatives, and Darcy McAlister works with clients to decide whether a matter should be mediated, negotiated or litigated.

What does the wills, trusts and estates practice cover?

Four areas, per the firm's site: drafting wills, estate planning, representing the fiduciaries of decedents' estates, and representing trustees and other fiduciaries such as conservators and guardians.

What is LEGUS?

An international network of law firms. Carta, McAlister & Moore states it is the only Connecticut firm affiliated with LEGUS, which lets it place and receive referrals around the world.
