Litigation

Business litigation in federal and state courts.

Litigation is a strength of our practice. We have litigated business issues before federal and state courts, at both the trial and appellate levels.

When a dispute has to be tried

Deciding to embark on a lawsuit has significant financial and emotional consequences. Our view is that litigation should be a last resort, used in the appropriate instances, and that the lawyer you trust with it should be an excellent listener, a gifted writer, a strategic thinker, and comfortable and talented in a courtroom.

When appropriate, we also take full advantage of arbitration, mediation and other alternative dispute resolution options.

Matters we litigate

  • Commercial “divorce” actions among partners and shareholders of closely held corporations
  • Employment discrimination and wrongful discharge claims
  • Probate litigation
  • Covenants not to compete or solicit
  • Disputes involving trade secrets and confidential information
  • Fiduciary disputes arising out of trust and estate administration
  • Business fraud, unfair trade practice actions and tortious interference claims
  • Complex business and commercial litigation involving licensing and proprietary matters
  • Construction contract disputes and general commercial contract disputes
  • Real estate litigation, including breach of sales contracts, development agreements, commercial leases, property line disputes and title insurance matters
