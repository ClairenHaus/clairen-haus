Residential real estate · Darien

A real estate lawyer with deep roots in Darien.

Given the value of real estate in Fairfield County, it is critical to retain a seasoned real estate attorney who focuses on real estate, not a general practitioner who dabbles in it.

Dedicated to residential real estate

Glen Moore and his staff are dedicated to residential real estate. A life-long Darien resident, Glen understands the Fairfield County real estate market and has in-depth knowledge of Darien’s neighborhoods, schools and market trends. His relationships with key players in town often prove invaluable: whether you need a broker, banker, builder, home service provider or the right contact at Town Hall, Glen will connect you with the right person.

From simple to complex purchases and sales

Under Glen’s leadership, the firm’s attorneys handle residential closings of every kind:

  • Residential conveyancing
  • Mortgage loans for purchases, refinances and home equity lines
  • Title insurance
  • Property ownership and estate planning
  • Property disputes

The firm’s residential real estate paralegals, Lisa Graveline and Donna Simmons, each bring decades of experience to making the closing process as seamless as possible for sellers and buyers.
