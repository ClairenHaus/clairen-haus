Timothy R. Fray, Esq.

Probate, trusts and estates. Tim prepares wills, trusts and advance directives and represents executors and administrators through estate settlement in the local probate courts.

Local talent

Born and raised in Fairfield, Tim attended Fairfield Prep before graduating from Saint Joseph’s University in Philadelphia with a B.S. in 2016. Law school brought him back to Connecticut: he earned his J.D., cum laude, from Quinnipiac University School of Law in 2020. In law school he served as executive managing editor of the Quinnipiac Probate Law Journal, worked as a judicial extern at the U.S. District Court for the District of Connecticut in New Haven, and appeared in the Regional Children’s Probate Court through the law school’s Civil Justice Clinic on an application for special immigrant juvenile status.

Practice

Tim has focused on probate, trusts and estates since law school. Believing in a practical approach, he gets to know each client personally to understand their goals and develop an individualized estate plan. In addition to preparing wills, trusts and other advance directive documents, he represents executors and administrators throughout the estate settlement process in the local probate courts.

Tim also gives back, frequently serving as court-appointed counsel for indigent individuals in sensitive probate proceedings such as conservatorships, guardianships and involuntary commitments.

Home and heart

When not in the office, Tim can often be found hiking in Connecticut’s parks and open spaces, reading, or traveling. He is especially proud of having walked the Camino de Santiago in 2022.
