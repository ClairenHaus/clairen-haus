Who we are

About the firm

A full-service law firm in San Angelo, serving the legal needs of West Texas and beyond.

Carter, Boyd, Lisson & Hohensee, P.C. is a full-service law firm in San Angelo, Texas. Its six attorneys focus on estate planning and probate, employment law, business formation and representation, taxation, civil litigation and real estate.

What the firm does most

The firm frequently represents clients regarding trusts, wills, estate planning, estate litigation, land disputes, business and corporate issues, employer-side employment law and general civil matters. For other legal matters, it has a referral network of attorneys it knows and trusts.

Law and accounting under one roof

James A. Carter and James A. Boyd, Jr. are both certified in Tax Law by the Texas Board of Legal Specialization, and Mr. Carter is also certified in Estate Planning & Probate Law. Both are CPAs, as is Elizabeth A. New. That combination matters when an estate plan, a business decision or a real estate transfer carries tax consequences.

A firm with deep roots and new attorneys

Mr. Carter has been licensed in Texas since 1967 and remains with the firm as of counsel. In 2025, Kolton C. Looka and Elizabeth A. New joined the State Bar of Texas, after graduating from Texas Tech University School of Law and Southern Methodist University.

Involved in the Concho Valley

The firm’s attorneys have served on local boards, from the Concho Valley Community Action Agency and Water Valley ISD to the Angelo State Alumni Association, and in the Tom Green County Bar Association. See each attorney’s page for details.

The office

The office is just outside downtown San Angelo, adjacent to the Santa Fe Golf Course and overlooking the Concho River, one block west of the Bryant freeway, at 515 W. Harris Ave., Suite 100.
