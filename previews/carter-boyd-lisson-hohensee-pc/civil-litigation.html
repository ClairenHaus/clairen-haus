Practice area

Civil Litigation

Representation in civil disputes, in state and federal court.

At this firm: Jeffrey S. Lisson’s practice covers civil litigation, probate litigation, real property litigation and employment law. He is admitted in Texas, Nevada and North Carolina (inactive), in U.S. District Courts in Texas, and in the U.S. Courts of Appeals for the Fifth and Fourth Circuits.

What civil litigation is

Civil litigation is the process of resolving a dispute between people or businesses in court, as opposed to a criminal case. It runs from investigating the claim and filing or answering a lawsuit, through exchanging information, motions and settlement talks, to trial and appeal when needed.

Kinds of cases

  • Disputes over wills, trusts and estates (probate litigation)
  • Land, title and boundary disputes (real property litigation)
  • Business and contract disputes
  • Employment disputes, on the employer side
  • General civil matters

State and federal court

Some cases belong in state court and some in federal court. Being admitted in both, and in the Fifth Circuit Court of Appeals, lets the firm follow a case where it goes.
