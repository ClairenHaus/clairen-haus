FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the firm, its practice areas and how to reach it.

What kinds of matters does the firm handle?

The primary practice areas are estate planning and probate, employment law, business formation and representation, taxation, civil litigation and real estate. The firm frequently represents clients regarding trusts, wills, estate planning, estate litigation, land disputes, business and corporate issues, employer-side employment law and general civil matters.

What if my matter is outside those areas?

For other legal matters, the firm has a referral network of attorneys it knows and trusts. Call (325) 655-4889 and the office can point you in the right direction.

Does the firm represent employees in employment matters?

The firm’s employment law practice is on the employer side.

Are any of the attorneys board certified?

Yes. James A. Carter is certified by the Texas Board of Legal Specialization in Estate Planning and Probate Law (1977) and in Tax Law (1983). James A. Boyd, Jr. is certified in Tax Law (2000).

Do any of the attorneys have an accounting background?

Yes. James A. Carter, James A. Boyd, Jr. and Elizabeth A. New are Certified Public Accountants.

Does the firm handle oil and gas matters?

Mindy L. Hohensee and Kolton C. Looka list oil and gas among their primary practice areas, along with real estate.

Where is the office?

515 W. Harris Ave., Suite 100, San Angelo, TX 76903. It is just outside downtown San Angelo, adjacent to the Santa Fe Golf Course and overlooking the Concho River, one block west of the Bryant freeway.

What area does the firm serve?

The firm serves the legal needs of West Texas and beyond, from its office in San Angelo.

How do I reach the firm?

Call (325) 655-4889, send a fax to (325) 657-2070, or email firm@carterboyd.com. Several attorneys also list a direct email address on their profile page.

These answers are general information, not legal advice.
