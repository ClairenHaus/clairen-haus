Real Estate, Oil & Gas

Land, minerals and the documents and disputes that come with them.

At this firm: Mindy L. Hohensee and Kolton C. Looka list real estate and oil and gas among their primary practice areas, and Jeffrey S. Lisson handles real property litigation. The firm frequently represents clients in land disputes.

Real estate

Real estate work covers buying, selling and transferring land and buildings, and the deeds, contracts, easements and leases involved. In estate planning, how land is titled often decides how it passes to the next generation.

Oil and gas

In West Texas, owning land often means owning, or sharing, what lies beneath it. Oil and gas work can involve mineral and royalty interests, leases, and questions of title when minerals pass through an estate.

Land disputes

Boundary lines, access, title and use can all lead to disagreements between neighbors, co-owners or heirs. When they can’t be worked out, they may end up in court as civil litigation.
