About the firm

A Birmingham law firm

Charles H. Boohaker, P.C. is a law firm in Birmingham, Alabama, led by attorney Charles H. Boohaker.

The firm

Charles H. Boohaker, P.C. handles legal matters across eleven practice areas: general practice, personal injury law, dram shop liability, construction litigation, consumer fraud, contract litigation, fraud, civil litigation, automobile accidents and injuries, wrongful death, and workers’ compensation.

The firm practices from one office, at 1905 14th Avenue South in Birmingham, and can be reached by telephone at 205-930-5900, by fax at 205-930-5909, or by email at charles@boohaker-law.com.

The attorney

The firm is led by Charles H. Boohaker, a Birmingham native and University of Alabama graduate (B.S., cum laude, 1986; J.D., 1989). He was admitted to practice in Alabama in 1989 and in Florida in 1990.

Courts
