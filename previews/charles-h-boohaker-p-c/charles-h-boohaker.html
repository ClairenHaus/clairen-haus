Attorney

Charles H. Boohaker

Attorney and Member of Charles H. Boohaker, P.C., Birmingham, Alabama.

Charles H. Boohaker was born in Birmingham, Alabama. He studied at the University of Alabama, earning his B.S., cum laude, in 1986, and his J.D. from the University of Alabama in 1989. That year he was admitted to practice in Alabama and before the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Alabama. In 1990 he was admitted in Florida and before the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit.

He practices through Charles H. Boohaker, P.C., where he is the firm’s Member, from the office at 1905 14th Avenue South in Birmingham.
