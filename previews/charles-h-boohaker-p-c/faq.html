Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the firm, its attorney and how to get in touch, drawn from the firm’s own information.

Where is the firm’s office?

What kinds of cases does the firm handle?

The firm lists eleven practice areas: general practice, personal injury law, dram shop liability, construction litigation, consumer fraud, contract litigation, fraud, civil litigation, automobile accidents and injuries, wrongful death, and workers’ compensation.

Does the firm handle car accident cases?

Yes. Automobile accidents and injuries are one of the firm’s listed practice areas, along with personal injury law and wrongful death.

Does the firm handle workers’ compensation claims?

Yes. Workers’ compensation is one of the firm’s listed practice areas.

What is dram shop liability, and does the firm handle it?

Dram shop liability concerns claims against a person or business that unlawfully sold or served alcohol to someone who then caused an injury. It is one of the firm’s listed practice areas.

Who is the attorney at the firm?

Charles H. Boohaker, the firm’s Member. He was born in Birmingham and earned his B.S., cum laude (1986), and J.D. (1989) from the University of Alabama.

Which courts is Charles H. Boohaker admitted to?

He was admitted in 1989 to practice in Alabama and before the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Alabama, and in 1990 in Florida and before the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit.

Which bar associations is he a member of?

The Alabama State Bar, The Florida Bar, the Birmingham Bar Association and the American Bar Association.

Can I email the attorney directly?

Yes. Charles H. Boohaker’s published email address is charles@boohaker-law.com.
