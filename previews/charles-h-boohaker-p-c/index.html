Your attorney

A Birmingham native, trained at the University of Alabama

Charles H. Boohaker was born in Birmingham. He earned his B.S., cum laude, from the University of Alabama in 1986 and his J.D. from the University of Alabama in 1989, the year he was admitted to practice in Alabama and before the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Alabama. He was admitted in Florida and before the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit in 1990.

He is a member of the Alabama State Bar, The Florida Bar, and the Birmingham and American Bar Associations.
