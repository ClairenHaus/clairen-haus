Practice area

Workers’ Compensation

If you were hurt at work, Alabama’s workers’ compensation system is meant to cover medical treatment and part of your lost wages. Workers’ compensation is one of the practice areas Charles H. Boohaker, P.C. handles.

What this can involve

  • Injuries from accidents at work
  • Disputes over medical treatment or the choice of doctor
  • Denied, delayed or stopped benefit payments
  • Questions about permanent injury and settlement

Points to know in Alabama

Report the injury in writing

Alabama law generally expects an injured worker to give written notice of the accident to the employer within five days, and benefits can be lost if notice is not given within 90 days unless the employer already knew.

There is a filing deadline

A workers’ compensation claim in Alabama must generally be filed within two years of the injury or of the last payment of compensation.

Doctors and benefits

The employer usually selects the treating physician at first; Alabama law provides a way to ask for a panel of other doctors if you are dissatisfied. Most employers with five or more employees are covered by the Act.
