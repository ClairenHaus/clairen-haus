About Clark & Stevens, P.A.

Hilton Head Island family law attorneys combining depth of experience and integrity.

Our Mission

Achieving optimal legal resolutions

Our goal is to use our experience and integrity to reach the best resolution of your legal problems, including divorce, child custody, child support, alimony, family law and estate planning. Testimony to the firm’s experience is evident not only in a history of satisfied clients but in the recognition of our partners: Clark & Stevens has earned the AV rating from Martindale-Hubbell, the highest possible ranking for legal ability and ethics as judged by peers in the legal profession.

We understand the importance of privacy and discretion within a small community, and we have a long-standing presence on Hilton Head Island and in the Beaufort County Family Court.

Community Commitment

Supporting Hilton Head initiatives

The firm is dedicated to community efforts and services, including its work with the Lowcountry Community Church and The Island Recreation Center, among others.
