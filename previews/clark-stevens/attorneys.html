Attorney & Partner

Robert E. Stevens

Bob Stevens has practiced in the family courts for more than 40 years. His practice is exclusively matrimonial and family law: uncontested separation and divorce; the division and distribution of assets involving complex financial issues, including interests in family or closely held businesses and professional practices such as medical, dental or legal practices; custodial and visitation disputes; establishing or modifying alimony or support; and mediation and arbitration of these matters.

Since receiving his J.D. from Syracuse University, Bob has been admitted to both the South Carolina and New York State Bars. He has been a Fellow of the American Academy of Matrimonial Lawyers since 1993, served as President of its South Carolina Chapter from 2016 to 2018, and currently serves as the South Carolina Delegate to the AAML National Board of Governors.

Bob’s family has lived in the Lowcountry since the early 1970s, and he and his immediate family moved here permanently in the mid-1990s. He serves on the Board of Directors of the Hilton Head Island Recreation Association and is a past president of its board.

Credentials

  • Admitted to practice in South Carolina and New York State
  • Certified AAML Matrimonial Law Arbitrator & Mediator
  • Fellow, American Academy of Matrimonial Lawyers; President of the South Carolina Chapter, 2016–2018
  • Board of Governors, American Academy of Matrimonial Lawyers
  • J.D., Syracuse University

Attorney & Partner (Retired)

William C. Clark

Bill Clark practiced in the fields of family law, personal injury, medical malpractice, construction litigation and civil litigation. He earned his J.D. from the University of South Carolina and was admitted to the South Carolina Bar in 1974. He is admitted to practice before the U.S. District Court for the Fourth Circuit and the U.S. Supreme Court.

Bill is a member of the National Trial Lawyers Association, a graduate and Fellow of the National Litigation Academy, and a member of the Multi-Million Dollar Advocates Forum, the Christian Legal Society, the SC Association for Justice and the Beaufort County Bar Association. He has represented numerous parties in child adoption proceedings, including international adoptions.

A resident of Hilton Head Island since 1988, Bill is a founder of the Lowcountry Community Church.

Credentials

  • Certified Circuit Court Mediator
  • Certified Family Court Mediator
  • Certified Circuit & Federal Court Arbitrator
  • Fellow of the National Litigation Academy
  • J.D., University of South Carolina; admitted to the South Carolina Bar, 1974
