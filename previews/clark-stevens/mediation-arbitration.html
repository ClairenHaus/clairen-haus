Mediation & Arbitration in Hilton Head Island

Alternative dispute resolution for individuals and businesses in Beaufort County.

Contested litigation is the traditional way to resolve a legal dispute, but it can be time-consuming and expensive. Mediation and arbitration are alternatives, or companions, to that process, and our attorneys have extensive experience helping clients use them.

Mediation

A mediator helps the parties find a settlement without deciding the case for them. The process is confidential, so nothing from it can be used against either party if the matter later goes to trial. We use mediation to address the needs of large and small businesses as well as individuals in conflict.

Arbitration

In arbitration the parties agree to submit their dispute to a neutral arbitrator, deciding in advance which issues are submitted, how the proceeding will run and whether the decision is binding. It is normally less formal and faster than a hearing or trial.

Certified neutrals

Both partners hold mediation and arbitration certifications and can serve as the neutral in your matter, or represent you in a mediation or arbitration.

Frequently asked questions

What is mediation?

Mediation is a formal effort to help the parties reach a settlement of their dispute. A neutral third party, the mediator, does not decide anything for the parties but leads them toward viable settlement solutions. The process is confidential, and no part of it can be used against either party if settlement fails and the case goes to trial.

What is arbitration?

Arbitration is a voluntary process in which the parties agree to submit their dispute to a neutral third party, the arbitrator. The parties and arbitrator agree in advance which issues will be submitted, how the arbitration will be conducted, and whether the decision will be binding or non-binding. Arbitration is normally less formal and less time-consuming than a hearing or trial.
