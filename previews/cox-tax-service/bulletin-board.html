What the IRS asks for

The IRS requires these for everyone on the tax return:

  • A Social Security card
  • A long-form birth certificate, or a hospital-issued birth certificate showing the child’s name, date of birth and parents’ names (for taxpayer and spouse too)
  • A school demographic form
  • A medical information sheet showing the medical center’s name and address
  • A current state-issued ID card or driver’s license

All addresses must match, and documents must be for the tax year you are filing.

Filing as head of household? Also bring:

  • Your rental lease or mortgage interest statement, with your name on it
  • A utility bill with your name on it
  • A property tax statement with your name on it

IRS scam alert

An email scam is going around that looks like it comes from the IRS. It starts with the IRS logo and says something like:

“After we received your financial activities, we decided to provide a tax refund of $… To process this payment, we need to verify your complete data. Please click the button below.” [Submit Now]

Please do not click Submit Now. This is a scam. The IRS will not start communications with you through email or social media. Don’t click the link or reply to the email.
