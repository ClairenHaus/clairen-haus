Our story

A Ferrellyn Alsbrook Cox production, since 1971.

How a mother’s tax office in Manning became a family tradition.

Funny

Ferrellyn “Funny” Alsbrook Cox is the founder of Cox Tax. She is the mother of Kasey, Kris and Charity, the grandmother of Brenden and Wyatt, and the adopted mother and grandmother of more than we could count or list.

In 1971 Funny started preparing taxes, and by 1974 she was opening her own tax prep office. In 2001 she decided to pass the office to her daughter, Charity.

In 2015, Funny was called home to become the true guardian angel of her family and friends.

The timeline

Fifty-plus years of tax seasons.

Today

Still the Funny way.

From 2001 to the present, Cox Tax is owned and operated by Charity Cox. Just like Funny, Charity keeps it a family production, which is why we say it’s still the Funny way.

As of 2022 there are two preparers, a tax analyst, an accountant, and a receptionist (or two). There are always changes and new ideas floating around to make the office a better space for our clients.

We love our clients and thank them for the years past and the years to come.
