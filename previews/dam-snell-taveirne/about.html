02About the firm

A legacy that began in 1971.

Professional financial and business guidance, with personalized advice. We’re concerned not only with the past, but with how we can help you plan for the future.

Relationships first

At Dam, Snell & Taveirne, Ltd., we believe in the value of relationships. We view every client relationship like a partnership and believe our success is a result of yours. We are committed to close, personal attention, backed by years of advanced training, technical experience and financial acumen.

We are small enough to keep the personal relationship that began with our first meeting, and large enough to serve you with a wide range of expertise. Our team includes numerous CPAs, former Internal Revenue Service agents, Certified Valuation Analysts, QuickBooks ProAdvisors and others with tax and financial certificates.

Our mission

To be a trusted advisor and respected accounting firm. To achieve it:

  • We will deliver the highest quality audit, tax and related professional services.
  • We will provide our clients with expert advice, clear and timely communications and the highest levels of integrity and professionalism.
  • We will strive to help our clients save time and money to help them realize their financial goals.

Our vision

To uphold and continue the Dam, Snell & Taveirne legacy that began in 1971 by nurturing client relationships through multiple generations, while adapting the firm to the changing landscape of our profession.

We are dedicated to the well-being of our staff and their families, and to a work environment focused on development and growth. We give back to our local community by taking part in several annual charitable events.

Quality review

Membership in the AICPA requires an independent quality review of our practices and procedures. We received the highest possible opinion on that review.

Leadership

Matt Dabrowski, CPA, managing partner since 2017.
