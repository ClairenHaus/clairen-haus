Currently hiring: Tax Accountant

Responsibilities for this full-time position include preparing tax returns and financial statements, tax planning, and general accounting services for privately held companies.

Qualifications

  • 2 or more years of public accounting experience with a focus in taxation
  • Bachelor’s degree or higher in accounting
  • Experience preparing individual and business tax returns
  • Knowledge of QuickBooks
  • A CPA designation, or working toward it
  • Working knowledge of GAAP
  • Motivated, able to work independently, with strong attention to detail and good communication skills

How to apply

Email your resume to Scott@dstcpa.com, or mail it to Dam, Snell & Taveirne, 21 Rollins Road, Fox Lake, IL 60020.
