Estate & Trust Tax

Pass what you have to whom you want, when you want, in the most cost-efficient way possible.

Estate and gift planning

Effective estate and gift planning helps transfer assets to your beneficiaries in an orderly way, provides security for a surviving spouse, and can reduce or eliminate the tax due on the transfer of your business and other assets. For business owners, continuity and succession of ownership are essential.

What our Estate/Trust Services group does

  • Prepares estate and trust tax returns
  • “Runs the numbers” to show the tax impact of a plan you are developing
  • Provides trust and estate support services to the legal profession
  • Takes a team approach focused on getting the work done promptly and proficiently
