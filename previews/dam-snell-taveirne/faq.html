07FAQ

Questions, answered.

Short answers about the firm and how we work. For anything else, call (847) 587-3022.

How long has the firm been around?

The Dam, Snell & Taveirne legacy began in 1971. Matt Dabrowski, CPA, has been managing partner since 2017.

Where are your offices?

Fox Lake: 21 Rollins Road, Fox Lake, IL 60020 (enter on Dena Ave), (847) 587-3022. Libertyville: 1512 Artaius Parkway, Suite 100, Libertyville, IL 60048, (847) 367-4448.

Who do you work with?

Individuals and families, and small and mid-size companies in northern Illinois and southern Wisconsin.

Can you help if I am audited by the IRS or the state?

Yes. We provide IRS and state audit representation, and our team includes former Internal Revenue Service agents.

Do you prepare returns for trusts, estates and not-for-profits?

Yes. We prepare returns for individuals, corporations, partnerships, LLCs and LLPs, estates, trusts and gifts, and not-for-profit organizations.

Does an outside firm review your quality?

Yes. Membership in the AICPA requires an independent quality review of our practices and procedures, and we received the highest possible opinion on that review.

How do I log in to the client portal?

Click “Sign in with Thomson Reuters Account”. Since November 2023, the first login asks you to update your password and choose a multi-factor option; email or text message is preferred.

Can I pay my invoice online?

Yes, through the secure payment page linked from our Client Center.

What if I need a service you don’t list?

Please ask. Our list of services is not all-inclusive.
