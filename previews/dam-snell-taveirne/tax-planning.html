Tax Planning

Tax planning is an essential part of the tax preparation process. Made part of your overall strategy, it helps reduce both current and future tax liabilities.

Planning, not just filing

We use our experience and access to the latest developments in tax law to help you minimize what you owe now and later. We help develop up-to-date strategies for a family or business plan based on current and future objectives.

Areas we help with

  • Individual, business, corporate, estate and trust returns and related forms
  • Tax-free exchanges
  • Family limited partnerships
  • Business mergers, acquisitions and liquidations, where the rules are especially complex
  • Keeping your estate plan current as your situation changes

In general

Planning works best before year end, while there is still time to act on decisions about income, deductions, retirement contributions and the timing of large transactions. A short conversation during the year is often worth more than a long one in April.
