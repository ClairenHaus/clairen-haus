About the firm

A Lake Forest firm built on commitment, honesty and trust

Daniel E. Levy, Ltd. is a progressive, results-oriented law firm in Lake Forest, Illinois, near the border of Cook County and Lake County.

The firm

The principal of the firm, attorney Daniel E. Levy, has steadily built his practice since 1992 with a constant determination to provide quality service at reasonable rates. With that goal at the forefront, Mr. Levy stays current in his knowledge of the law while keeping a finger on the pulse of his clientele, so the firm addresses each client’s particular legal and financial needs.

The firm strives to earn and keep each client’s trust and prides itself on constant communication with its clients. It aims to be the law firm of choice for individuals and businesses that expect nothing less than a 100% effort from their attorney, and it brings its experience with a wide range of business and personal legal matters to every client.

“Commitment, honesty and trust are the principles upon which the firm was founded and which shall always guide it.”Daniel E. Levy, Ltd.

What the firm handles

Real Estate LawResidential and commercial closings, leases, refinancing and title insurance.Estate Planning & ProbateWills, revocable living trusts, and probate of a loved one’s estate.Business LawCorporations, LLCs, business contracts and choosing the right form of ownership.

Where

The office is at 100 S. Saunders Rd., Suite 150, Lake Forest, Illinois 60045. Real estate closings are coordinated with title companies that have locations throughout Cook, Lake, DuPage, Kane and McHenry counties, and the firm has probated estates in Cook, Lake and DuPage counties.
