Background

Daniel E. Levy was born in Chicago, Illinois. He attended the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, where he received a Bachelor of Science degree in Accountancy. At the time of his graduation, and still today, the University of Illinois has one of the top accounting programs and business schools in the United States.

He then attended the Northern Illinois University College of Law, where he received his Juris Doctor in 1986. At NIU he was a member of the Dean’s List and won the American Jurisprudence Award in Business Planning and the U.S. Law Week Award for significant academic achievement.

Mr. Levy began his career with two small law firms in Chicago from 1986 to 1992. In September 1992, he formed his own practice, which he has steadily built with the constant determination to provide quality service at reasonable rates.

Practice

Mr. Levy’s practice covers real estate law, estate planning and probate, and business law. He is an approved title policy issuing agent of most major title insurance companies in Illinois. He is an Illinois attorney, licensed to practice only within the State of Illinois.
