Real estate

Do you represent buyers or sellers in a home sale?

Both. The firm represents buyers and sellers in approximately 300 residential transactions each year, most of them single-family homes, condominiums, townhomes, quads, coach homes, cooperatives and vacant land.

Do you handle commercial real estate and leases?

Yes. The firm represents buyers and sellers of commercial buildings for manufacturing, industrial and office use, and represents both landlords and tenants in residential and commercial lease negotiations and leasehold documents.

Can you help with refinancing a mortgage?

Yes. The firm represents many borrowers at the closing on the refinancing of their residential mortgage, and has represented commercial clients on loan closings, negotiating mortgage documents, personal guarantees and security agreements.

Can the firm issue title insurance?

Mr. Levy is an approved title policy issuing agent of most major title insurance companies in Illinois. Those title companies have locations throughout Cook, Lake, DuPage, Kane and McHenry counties.

Estate planning and probate

What is the difference between a simple will and a pour-over will?

A simple will names an executor of the estate and a guardian for minor children, and states how the testator wants assets distributed. When a living trust is recommended, the firm prepares a pour-over will that names the client’s living trust as the beneficiary of the estate.

Do you prepare revocable living trusts?

Yes. A revocable living trust is often recommended to avoid both estate tax and the probate of assets. The firm prepares the trust and counsels clients on how to fund it once it is created.

A family member has passed away. Can you help with probate?

Yes. The firm counsels the family on who owns the decedent’s assets, how to transfer them to the beneficiaries, and how and when to pay final bills. Depending on many factors, a probate estate may need to be opened; the firm has probated estates in Cook, Lake and DuPage counties.

Business law

Should my business be a corporation or an LLC?

Before forming anything, the firm discusses the forms of ownership (partnership, corporation, limited liability company, sole proprietorship) and recommends a meeting with your accountant on the tax side. Once you understand the pros and cons, the firm prepares the documents that fit your goals.

What business contracts does the firm prepare?

Buy/sell agreements, employment contracts, restrictive covenants and confidentiality agreements, prepared to protect the business’s interests.
