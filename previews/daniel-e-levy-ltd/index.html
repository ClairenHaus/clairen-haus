Questions

Common questions, answered.

Straight answers from what the firm does every day. More on the FAQ page.

Do you represent buyers or sellers in a home sale?

Both. The firm represents buyers and sellers in approximately 300 residential transactions each year, most of them single-family homes, condominiums, townhomes, quads, coach homes, cooperatives and vacant land.

Can the firm issue title insurance?

Mr. Levy is an approved title policy issuing agent of most major title insurance companies in Illinois. Those title companies have locations throughout Cook, Lake, DuPage, Kane and McHenry counties.

Do you prepare revocable living trusts?

Yes. A revocable living trust is often recommended to avoid both estate tax and the probate of assets. The firm prepares the trust and counsels clients on how to fund it once it is created.

Should my business be a corporation or an LLC?

Before forming anything, the firm discusses the forms of ownership (partnership, corporation, limited liability company, sole proprietorship) and recommends a meeting with your accountant on the tax side. Once you understand the pros and cons, the firm prepares the documents that fit your goals.
