What changes

What this redesign fixes.

Two honest observations from the current site, and what this concept does about them.

  • Nothing machine-readable about the firmThe current site has no structured data, no meta description, no sitemap and no robots.txt. The concept adds LegalService, Person and FAQ markup with the full name, address and phone so search engines and AI assistants can read who the firm is.
  • The practice detail is already there, just buriedThe real estate, estate planning and business pages hold specific, useful detail. The concept surfaces it on one page with a question-and-answer section built from those pages.
  • Images without descriptionsAll four images on the current home page lack alt text, and they are stock photos. The concept uses described, firm-specific elements instead of stock imagery.
  • A contact form that explains itselfThe current form offers no context. The concept pairs it with a tap-to-call number and email, and makes the office and fax easy to find.

Common questions

Answers people search for.

Short answers drawn from the firm's own published information, marked up so search engines and AI assistants can quote them.

Where is Daniel E. Levy, Ltd. located?

100 S. Saunders Rd., Suite 150, Lake Forest, IL 60045, near the border of Cook and Lake Counties. Phone (847) 808-0222, fax (847) 808-1221, email dan@danielelevy.com.

Does the firm handle residential closings?

Yes. The firm represents buyers and sellers in approximately 300 residential transactions a year, including single-family homes, condominiums, townhomes, quads, coach homes, cooperatives and vacant land, and also handles refinance closings.

Which counties does the probate practice cover?

The firm reports having probated estates in Cook, Lake and DuPage Counties, and counsels families on how a decedent's assets are owned and transferred.

Can the firm set up a corporation or LLC?

Yes. It routinely prepares articles of incorporation, bylaws, resolutions, S-corporation elections, and LLC articles of organization and operating agreements, after discussing the forms of ownership and recommending a meeting with the client's accountant on tax implications.

Is Mr. Levy a title agent?

The firm states he is an approved title policy issuing agent of most major title insurance companies in Illinois.
