In plain terms

Some general background

General information about how these matters usually work in Illinois, not legal advice about your situation.

What a real estate attorney does

In a typical Illinois home sale, each side has its own attorney. The attorney reviews the signed contract, negotiates changes, follows the title work and the lender’s requirements, and prepares or reviews the closing documents so the deal closes the way the contract says.

Attorney review

Many Chicago-area residential contracts allow a short attorney-review period after both sides sign. That window is when terms can still be modified or the contract cancelled, which is why it pays to have your attorney lined up before you make or accept an offer.

Why title insurance matters

A title policy protects the buyer and the lender against covered defects in the title that were not found before closing. The title commitment issued before closing shows what needs to be cleared for the sale to go through.

Questions about real estate law

Do you represent buyers or sellers in a home sale?
