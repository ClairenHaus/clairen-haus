About

A partnership with every client

Delores I. Nance, CPA, P.C. is a relatively small Gilbert, Arizona firm, built on close, personal attention.

Delores I. Nance, CPA, CFP®

At Delores I. Nance, CPA, P.C., we believe in the value of relationships. We view every client relationship like a partnership, and truly believe that our success is a result of your success.

Our goal is to provide each individual and business that we serve with appropriate tax and financial planning solutions. We recognize our clients are faced with continual change and we strive to help them face the challenges encountered.

We are committed to providing close, personal attention to our clients. We take pride in giving you the assurance that the personal assistance you receive comes from years of advanced training, technical experience and financial acumen. Our continual investment of time and resources in professional continuing education, state-of-the-art computer technology and extensive business relationships is indicative of our commitment to excellence.

Credentials

  • Certified Public Accountant (CPA)
  • CERTIFIED FINANCIAL PLANNER™ professional (CFP®)
  • The firm is a Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor®

Small firm, personal service

Because the firm is relatively small, clients get personalized, quality service. Delores works with individuals, businesses and non-profit organizations from the Gilbert office, which is accepting new clients.
