FAQ

Questions, answered

The things new clients ask most.

Is the firm accepting new clients?

Yes, at the Gilbert office: 2680 South Val Vista Drive, Building 16, Suite 191, Gilbert, AZ 85295. The Tempe office is not accepting new clients.

How do I schedule a meeting?

Book a 30-minute or 60-minute in-person meeting at the Gilbert office online, or call (480) 821-4005.

How do I find the Gilbert office?

It’s in the Spectrum Falls complex on Val Vista Drive between Pecos and Williams Field, about a mile north of Mercy Gilbert Hospital and the 202 Santan Freeway. Suite 191 is directly behind the water fountain; see the Offices page for turn-by-turn directions.

What kinds of tax returns do you prepare?

Individuals, corporations, partnerships (general, limited, LLPs), LLCs, estates (Form 706 and states), gift tax (Form 709), trusts (Form 1041 and states), charitable remainder trusts, private foundations (Form 990PF), public charities (Form 990) and multi-state returns.

Do you file electronically?

Yes. The firm offers federal and state (all) electronic filing.

Can you represent me in an audit?

Yes. The firm provides IRS and Arizona Department of Revenue audit representation.

Do you help with QuickBooks?

Yes. The firm is a Certified QuickBooks ProAdvisor® and can assist with QuickBooks setup, training and reporting.

Do you work with non-profits?

Yes: non-profit accounting and tax preparation, compilation and review of financial statements, and help gaining and keeping IRS tax-exempt status, including the exemption application.

What should new clients bring?

A basic tax organizer is available for new clients to pull their tax information together. Current clients can ask the office for a “proforma” organizer with prior-year information.

What if I need a service that isn’t listed?

Ask. The list is by no means all-inclusive, and if it isn’t a service the firm provides, Delores will be happy to refer you to a qualified professional.
