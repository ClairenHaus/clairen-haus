Directions, in Delores’s words

We are located in the Spectrum Falls complex on Val Vista Drive between Pecos and Williams Field. We are about a mile north of the Mercy Gilbert Hospital and the 202 Santan Freeway.

There are several entrances into the complex. I would recommend entering the one with Breeze Dental and State Farm. If you are going north on Val Vista, there is a “new” left turn sign at the driveway. (The driveway with the “2680” sign is just north of the driveway you need.)

Once you turn in to the driveway, if you look directly back you will see a circular driveway and a building with a water fountain/structure. My office is directly behind the water fountain. You will probably see my neighbor’s sign “Watson CPAs”.

The water fountain is actually at the back of the building. So you need to go around the building by making a right turn toward the Kohl’s shopping center which is north of us. At the first opportunity turn left and then left again into the parking lot. I am suite 191 and my name is on the doors.

Call if you need further directions: (480) 821-4005.
