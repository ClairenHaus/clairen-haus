Gary W. Crews, PLLC is a boutique law firm based in Tulsa. The firm's business knowledge and industry experience let it navigate the complex legal issues its clients face, and it describes its values plainly: ethics and experience, dedication, responsiveness, communication and loyalty.

Clients are treated as a relationship rather than a case. The firm gets to know who they are and what their priorities are, responds quickly, communicates regularly about the status of a matter, and avoids legal jargon. Much of its client base grows through referrals from existing clients.

Common questions

Questions people ask before they call.

Taken from the firm's own FAQ and probate pages and shortened. General information about Oklahoma probate and estate planning, not legal advice.

What does it mean to die “intestate”?

If a person dies without a Last Will and Testament, they die intestate, and their property passes according to Oklahoma's intestate succession laws rather than their own wishes.

What is a revocable living trust?

A trust designed primarily to avoid probate. It provides for management of the trust assets during your lifetime and distribution to the named beneficiaries at death, usually without court involvement. It can be amended or revoked at any time while you are mentally competent.

What is a Last Will and Testament?

A legal document that communicates a person's final wishes for their possessions and the custody of dependents. It names a personal representative to manage the estate under the supervision of the probate court.

What is adult guardianship?

When a person is unable to make important financial or personal decisions, a court appoints a guardian to make those decisions for them, with the legal backing of the court.

Can heirs handle probate themselves?

Heirs are legally allowed to manage probate themselves, but the site notes it can be complicated, involving tax, real estate, probate and inheritance rules, and a person can be held individually liable for errors.
