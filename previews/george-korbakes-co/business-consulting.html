Business Consulting & Advisory

We gauge our worth by the personal and business successes of our clients.

We gauge our worth by the personal and business successes of our clients. We can help your business find what is holding back profitability and growth, and develop solutions that are practical and technically sound. Beyond acting as a sounding board for management, we provide flexible strategies for the issues affecting your business.

Management advisory services

Even the most qualified managers benefit from competent, objective feedback. We can help your management team set long-term objectives and specific strategies for future growth.

Business entity selection

Choosing the form of your business, such as an S corporation, sole proprietorship or limited liability company, is a crucial decision with long-lasting tax effects. We help you weigh the pros and cons of each to find the best fit.

Accounting software selection and implementation

With so many accounting packages available, choosing the right one is harder than ever. We see most programs through trade journals and actual use, and we can help you choose and implement the system that fits your needs.
