Frequently asked questions

About the firm, our clients and how we work.

When was George Korbakes & Co. founded?

The firm was organized in 1993 by George P. Korbakes, CPA and Barry L. Gershinzon, CPA, who had worked together at a large Chicago CPA firm.

What kinds of clients does the firm serve?

Mostly closely held businesses, often with multiple entities, ranging from $50,000 to $100,000,000 in sales, plus individuals. Industries include food and beverage concessions, rental equipment, commercial and residential real estate, entertainment, custom job shops and professional firms.

Who do you prepare tax returns for?

Individuals, corporations, partnerships, LLCs and LLPs, estates, trusts and gifts, and not-for-profit organizations.

Is the firm peer reviewed?

Yes. The firm has been peer reviewed as required by the AICPA every three years since 1993.

Can you represent me before the IRS or the state?

Yes. We represent clients before the various federal and state tax agencies.

Will I talk to a real person when I call?

Yes. Each phone call is personally answered by our staff.

What if I need a service you don't list?

Ask us. If it is not a service we provide, we will be happy to refer you to a qualified professional.

How do I send documents securely?

Clients can use the Client Axcess portal for secure online document sharing. Links are on our client resources page.
