Our firm and its history

Organized in 1993 to bring large-firm service to clients in a more personal, customized format.

George Korbakes & Company, LLP was organized in 1993 by George P. Korbakes, CPA and Barry L. Gershinzon, CPA. They had worked together at a large Chicago CPA firm and decided to offer the services found in a large accounting firm to clients in a more personal and customized format. That experience lets the firm relate to large and small clients alike, and to their specific problems.

A boutique firm

We offer services that extend beyond filling in forms or recording checks. Our clients are usually closely held, often with multiple entities, and range from $50,000 to $100,000,000 in sales. We help them with financial reporting, contract and government requirements, and tax and estate planning.

Industries we serve

  • Food and beverage concessions
  • Rental equipment
  • Commercial and residential real estate
  • Entertainment
  • Custom job shops
  • Professional firms

Personal attention

In addition to the partners, the firm has a complement of full- and part-time professional and administrative staff. We believe in personal attention to our clients, and each phone call is personally answered by our staff.

Quality and tools

The firm has been peer reviewed, as required by the AICPA, every three years since 1993. We use current financial reporting and tax software and online services from Thomson Reuters (NetClient CS and PPC) and Wolters Kluwer (CCH).
