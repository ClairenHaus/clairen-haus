A boutique firm

Beyond filling in forms or recording checks

“We believe in personal attention to our clients. Each phone call is personally answered by our capable staff.”

Our clients are usually closely held, often with multiple entities. We help them with financial reporting, contract and government requirements, and tax and estate planning.

Because our firm is relatively small, clients get personalized, quality service, with full- and part-time professional and administrative staff behind the partners. And if you need something we don't provide, we will gladly refer you to a qualified professional.
