Tax Planning & Preparation

A proactive, not reactive, approach to tax, all year long.

We prefer a proactive rather than reactive approach to taxes. By keeping current on new tax laws and legislation, we can identify planning opportunities that minimize both your current and future tax liabilities, and we give our individual and business clients that expertise throughout the year.

Returns and planning for

  • Individuals
  • Corporations
  • Partnerships
  • LLCs and LLPs
  • Estates, trusts and gifts
  • Not-for-profit organizations

Also

  • Divorce and support tax issues
  • Tax effects of buying or selling a business

Whether you are an individual or a multi-tiered partnership, our staff can develop tax strategies that take advantage of new tax laws and legislation.
