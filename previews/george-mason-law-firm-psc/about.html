About the firm

George Mason Law Firm, PSC, formerly known as A. George Mason, Jr., PSC, was established in 2002 to advise companies and individuals on oil, gas, coal and coalbed methane.

An energy practice since 2002

George Mason established the firm in 2002 to advise companies and individuals on contracts and strategies for the acquisition, development and sale of oil, gas, coal and coalbed methane. Before that, he spent his career in the industry itself, as Assistant General Counsel for Massey Energy Company and, for fifteen years, as Vice President and General Counsel for Equitable Production Co., now EQT Production Co.

Today the firm’s practice areas focus on the energy industry: oil, gas and coal, coalbed methane, and the Utica, Marcellus and Devonian shales.

How the firm works

  • Project by project. Mr. Mason is not on a retainer with any client; each matter is taken on as its own project.
  • A dedicated title team. The firm’s Title Opinion department is made up of experienced and trained paralegals and attorneys.
  • Independent contractors. The firm employs attorneys and paralegals as independent contractors. Interested in joining? Email info@georgemasonlf.com.

Staying current

In addition to years of mineral law practice, the firm works to stay up to date on industry standards and developments by participating in conferences and workshops and serving on committees that address issues relevant to the energy industry. Mr. Mason has worked on mineral rights legislation in Kentucky, Virginia, West Virginia and Louisiana, as well as federal legislation, has served as a Trustee of the Energy and Mineral Law Foundation, and has presented and moderated at its institutes and mineral law conferences.
