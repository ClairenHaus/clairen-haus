Devonian Shale

Natural gas in the Devonian shales of the Appalachian Basin, where most of the Devonian shale gas in the United States is found.

The firm’s work in the Devonian shales

The firm’s practice covers the Utica, Marcellus and Devonian shales. For Devonian shale matters, as with its other energy work, the firm handles contract review and drafting, title and drilling title opinions, negotiations for sale or purchase, well permitting, state board representation and counsel in litigation.

About Devonian shale

Devonian shales formed from the mud of shallow seas about 350 million years ago, during the Devonian period. Shale is a fine-grained sedimentary rock that splits easily into thin layers. These shales can hold natural gas, often where thick black shale deposits sandwich a thinner layer. Producing gas from shale is more difficult than producing conventional natural gas, and most of the Devonian shale holding natural gas in the United States lies in the Appalachian Basin.
