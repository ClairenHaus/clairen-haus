FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers drawn from the firm’s own information. For anything specific to your matter, call the office.

Which states is George Mason licensed in?

Kentucky (1978), Tennessee (1986), Virginia (2003), West Virginia (2004) and Pennsylvania (2011). He is also admitted before the U.S. Supreme Court, the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Fourth and Sixth Circuits, and the U.S. District Courts for the Eastern and Western Districts of Kentucky, the Eastern and Western Districts of Virginia, and the Southern District of West Virginia.

What kinds of title opinions does the firm handle?

All types of requests: general title inquiries, preliminary drilling title opinions for companies, and basic title opinions for individuals. The firm's Title Opinion department is made up of experienced and trained paralegals and attorneys. Call (859) 224-8277 to schedule a consultation or email info@georgemasonlf.com.

Does the firm work on retainer?

No. Mr. Mason is not on a retainer with any client and represents clients on a project-by-project basis.

Who does the firm represent?

Companies and individuals, on contracts and strategies for the acquisition, development and sale of oil, gas, coal and coalbed methane. On Marcellus Shale matters, the firm advises landowners, mineral owners and operators. It is also a resource for in-house corporate counsel.

What services does the firm provide?

Contract review and drafting, legal opinions and drilling title opinions, negotiations for sale or purchase, vertical and horizontal well permitting, representation before state boards, and legal counsel in litigation.

How long has George Mason worked in oil, gas and coalbed methane?

He has been involved in oil and gas operations since 1985 and in coalbed methane operations since 1988, when EQT Production Co. drilled the first commercial CBM well in Dickenson County, Virginia.

What was his role before founding the firm?

He was Assistant General Counsel for Massey Energy Company in Richmond, Virginia, and, for fifteen years, Vice President and General Counsel for Equitable Production Co., now known as EQT Production Co.

Where is the office, and when is it open?

The office is at 103 Wind Haven Drive, Suite 201, Nicholasville, Kentucky 40356, just across the Jessamine County line off Harrodsburg Road. Office hours are 9:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday through Friday. The mailing address is 3070 Lakecrest Circle, Suite 400, PMB 278, Lexington, Kentucky 40513.

How do I contact the firm?

Call the office at (859) 224-8277 (mobile (859) 327-4740, fax (859) 296-2998) or email info@georgemasonlf.com.

Does the firm work with other attorneys and paralegals?

Yes. The firm employs attorneys and paralegals as independent contractors. If you are interested in joining the team, email info@georgemasonlf.com.
