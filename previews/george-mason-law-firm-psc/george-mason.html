Attorney

A. George Mason, Jr.

Attorney at law. Founder of George Mason Law Firm, PSC, former energy company general counsel, and retired U.S. Army Colonel.

Background

In 2002, Mr. Mason established George Mason Law Firm, PSC, formerly known as A. George Mason, Jr., PSC, to advise companies and individuals on contracts and strategies for the acquisition, development and sale of oil, gas, coal and coalbed methane (“CBM”).

He has been involved in oil and gas operations since 1985 and in CBM operations since 1988. He is not on a retainer with any client and represents clients on a project-by-project basis.

Call (859) 224-8277

Energy industry career

Before establishing the firm, Mr. Mason was the Assistant General Counsel for Massey Energy Company in Richmond, Virginia, one of the largest energy companies in the Southeast. In that role he managed general corporate legal activities, including negotiating, drafting and closing coal and real estate related transactions, acquisitions and divestitures, while handling litigation and administrative hearings.

For fifteen years, until the corporate office in Kingsport, Tennessee was closed, he was the Vice President and General Counsel for Equitable Production Co., now known as EQT Production Co.

Legislation and the profession

Mr. Mason has worked on mineral rights legislation in Kentucky, Virginia, West Virginia and Louisiana, as well as federal legislation. His knowledge of oil, gas and coalbed methane legislation, the model for which he helped author, makes him a valuable resource in his field. He has served as a Trustee of the Energy and Mineral Law Foundation (EMLF) and as a presenter and moderator at EMLF annual institutes and mineral law conferences.

Military service

Mr. Mason is a veteran of the United States Army, having served active duty tours with Special Forces and the 101st Airborne Division, and retired with the rank of Colonel.
