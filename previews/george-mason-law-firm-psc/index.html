About George Mason

Decades on the company side of oil, gas and coal.

Before founding the firm in 2002, Mr. Mason was Assistant General Counsel for Massey Energy Company in Richmond, Virginia, negotiating, drafting and closing coal and real estate transactions, acquisitions and divestitures, and handling litigation and administrative hearings.

For fifteen years before that he was Vice President and General Counsel for Equitable Production Co., now EQT Production Co. He was involved in coalbed methane operations from 1988, when EQT drilled the first commercial CBM well in Dickenson County, Virginia.

  • Worked on mineral rights legislation in Kentucky, Virginia, West Virginia and Louisiana, and on federal legislation
  • Helped author the model for oil, gas and coalbed methane legislation
  • Presenter and moderator at Energy & Mineral Law Foundation institutes and mineral law conferences
