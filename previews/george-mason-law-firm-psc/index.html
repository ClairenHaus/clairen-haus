About the practice

The information clients need before they call.

Formerly known as A. George Mason, Jr., PSC

Not on retainer with any client; engaged project by project

George Mason has been involved in oil and gas operations since 1985 and in coalbed methane operations since 1988, when EQT Production Co. drilled the first commercial CBM well in Dickenson County, Virginia.

The firm keeps current with industry standards by taking part in conferences and workshops and serving on committees that address energy issues. Mr. Mason has served as a presenter and moderator at Energy and Mineral Law Foundation institutes and mineral law conferences.

Memberships listed by the firm include the AAPL, the Energy & Mineral Law Foundation, the Fayette County, Tennessee, Virginia and West Virginia bar associations, the Tennessee Oil & Gas Association, and the West Virginia Oil & Natural Gas Association.

Common questions

Answers from the firm.

Drawn from the firm's current site.

Which states is George Mason licensed in?

Kentucky (1978), Tennessee (1986), Virginia (2003), West Virginia (2004) and Pennsylvania (2011). He is also admitted before the U.S. Supreme Court, the Fourth and Sixth Circuits, and federal district courts in Kentucky, Virginia and the Southern District of West Virginia.

What kinds of title opinions does the firm handle?

All types: general title inquiries, preliminary drilling title opinions for companies, and basic title opinions for individuals. The work is handled by experienced paralegals and attorneys.

Can the firm help a landowner or mineral owner with a Marcellus Shale matter?

Yes. The firm advises landowners, mineral owners and operators on Marcellus Shale matters and invites them to get in touch.

Does the firm work on retainer?

No. Mr. Mason is not on a retainer with any client and represents clients on a project-by-project basis.

Where is the office and when is it open?

103 Wind Haven Drive, Suite 201, Nicholasville, KY 40356, just across the Jessamine County line off Harrodsburg Road. Office hours are 9:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday through Friday.
