Acquisition, development and sale

George Mason established the firm in 2002 to advise companies and individuals on contracts and strategies for the acquisition, development and sale of oil, gas, coal and coalbed methane. The work covers the full life of a mineral interest: buying it, leasing or developing it, and selling it.

  • Contract review and drafting
  • Negotiations for sale or purchase
  • Legal opinions and drilling title opinions
  • Well permitting, both vertical and horizontal
  • Representation before state boards
  • Legal counsel in litigation

Experience on the company side

As Assistant General Counsel for Massey Energy Company in Richmond, Virginia, Mr. Mason managed general corporate legal activities, including negotiating, drafting and closing coal and real estate related transactions, acquisitions and divestitures, while handling litigation and administrative hearings. For fifteen years before that, he was Vice President and General Counsel for Equitable Production Co., now known as EQT Production Co.

About oil, gas and coal

Crude oil, natural gas and coal are fossil fuels, formed over millions of years from buried plant and animal remains. Oil and gas collect in porous rock reservoirs; coal formed mainly from ancient plant material buried and compacted beneath sediment. Because these resources are non-renewable and valuable, the rights to them are bought, sold, leased and divided, and the agreements behind those transactions carry real consequences for owners and operators alike.
