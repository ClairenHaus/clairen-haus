Who the firm represents

Companies and individuals.

The firm advises companies and individuals on contracts and strategies for the acquisition, development and sale of oil, gas, coal and coalbed methane. On Marcellus Shale matters it advises landowners, mineral owners and operators.

It also serves as a resource for in-house corporate counsel, and represents clients on a project-by-project basis rather than on retainer.

  • Contract review and drafting
  • Legal opinions and drilling title opinions
  • Negotiations for sale or purchase
  • Well permitting, vertical and horizontal
  • Representation before state boards
  • Legal counsel in litigation
