About the Firm

Client-centered legal services

Our attorneys serve as legal counsel and trusted advisors to individuals, businesses and community groups, and have helped clients in Connecticut for more than 30 years.

What we do

At Green and Gross, P.C. we take pride in delivering top-quality, cost-effective legal services that meet the needs of our clients.

Our business attorneys are experienced in commercial and residential real estate transactions, business formation, business acquisitions, sales and succession planning, and in commercial litigation.

Our land use attorneys have deep experience with municipal land use agencies, including planning and zoning commissions, zoning boards of appeal, inland wetlands commissions, historic district commissions and harbor management commissions. We frequently represent individuals and neighborhood groups in opposing development applications.

Our personal injury attorneys represent people injured by the negligence of others, for example in automobile accidents and premises liability cases.

Our experienced elder law attorneys offer will drafting and estate planning services to protect family financial resources and address health care needs, and help clients through the process of estate settlement and probate administration.

The firm

Eric M. Gross is a founding member of the firm and was elected its president in 2003. Barbara F. Green has been a member of the firm since 1990. Today the firm’s four principals, Eric M. Gross, Samuel T. Rost, Joel Z. Green and Barbara F. Green, practice with associate Linda Pesce Laske from one office on Broad Street in downtown Bridgeport.

Rooted in the community

The firm’s attorneys have long served the communities they practice in: on town bodies in Fairfield and Trumbull, on the boards of organizations such as The Kennedy Center in Trumbull, Operation Hope of Fairfield, Sullivan McKinney Elder Housing, the Jewish Center for Community Services and the International Institute of Connecticut, and through years of service to the Bridgeport and Connecticut Bar Associations and the state's Bar Examining Committee.

Where we practice

The firm serves clients in Bridgeport and throughout Fairfield and New Haven Counties, before local land use agencies, the Connecticut Probate Courts, the Superior Court, the Connecticut Appellate and Supreme Courts, and the federal courts in Connecticut.
