Barbara F. Green

A member of the firm since 1990, Barbara Green concentrates her practice in family law, probate administration, estate planning and elder law, with much of her work devoted to special needs trusts.

Phone (203) 335-5141Email bgreen@gglaw.netFax (203) 367-9964

Practice

Ms. Green concentrates her practice in family law, probate administration (including trusts, estates, conservatorships and guardianships), estate planning and elder law. Much of her practice is devoted to helping persons with disabilities, and their family members, establish special needs trusts.

She is admitted to practice law in Connecticut and Ohio. She is a member of the Bridgeport Bar Association, where she previously chaired its estates and probate section, and of the Connecticut Bar Association, where she is a member of the trusts and estates and elder law sections. Before law school she earned a master's degree in social work.

Community

Like her partners, Ms. Green is involved in many community organizations. She has been an active volunteer with The Kennedy Center, Inc. of Trumbull, Connecticut, serving as a member and officer of its Board of Directors since 1994 and as Chairman of the Board from 2002 through 2004. Since 2000 she has been a member of the Legal Advisory Board for Planned Lifetime Assistance Network, Inc., and she has served on the Board of Directors of the International Institute of Connecticut since 2009, currently as Secretary.

She previously chaired the Annual Campaign of the Jewish Federation of Greater New Haven and has long served on its Board of Directors. She has also served as President of the Federation's Women's Division, Chairperson of its Planning and Allocations Committee, and Co-chairperson of its 2002 Israeli Emergency Campaign, and on the Board of Directors of Congregation Or Shalom in Orange.

Background

Ms. Green was raised in the Bridgeport area, lived in New Haven for many years and now lives in Milford. She graduated with honors from Connecticut College in 1976, earned a master's degree from the University of Connecticut School of Social Work in 1978, and received her J.D. from the University of Connecticut School of Law in 1985.
