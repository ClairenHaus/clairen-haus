FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers drawn from the firm’s own information. For advice about your situation, call the office.

What areas of law does Green and Gross handle?

The firm handles commercial litigation, business transactions and business formation, commercial and residential real estate transactions, zoning and land use law, civil and administrative appeals, elder law, estate planning, probate and estate settlement, and personal injury litigation. Barbara F. Green also practices family law.

Where is the office?

The office is at 1087 Broad Street, Suite 401, Bridgeport, Connecticut 06604. The phone number is (203) 335-5141 and the fax number is (203) 367-9964.

How do I email an attorney?

Each attorney has a direct email address: Eric M. Gross, egross@gglaw.net; Samuel T. Rost, srost@gglaw.net; Joel Z. Green, jgreen@gglaw.net; Barbara F. Green, bgreen@gglaw.net; and Linda Pesce Laske, llaske@gglaw.net. You can also call the office to arrange a meeting or a phone call.

Does the firm represent neighbors who oppose a development?

Yes. The firm frequently represents individuals and neighborhood groups opposing development applications before planning and zoning commissions, zoning boards of appeals, inland wetlands commissions, historic district commissions and harbor management commissions, and in appeals that follow.

Can the firm help me get a land use approval?

Yes. Linda Pesce Laske and Joel Z. Green help property owners with applications such as special permits, site plan approvals, zone changes, subdivisions, zoning text amendments, affordable housing and variances.

I am a lawyer. Can the firm help with an appeal or a brief?

Yes. Linda Pesce Laske has argued before the Connecticut Supreme and Appellate Courts and welcomes inquiries from attorneys who want help writing an appellate brief or navigating appellate procedure or motion practice.

Does the firm set up special needs trusts?

Yes. Much of Barbara F. Green's practice is devoted to helping persons with disabilities and their family members establish special needs trusts.

Can the firm help settle an estate in Probate Court?

Yes. Samuel T. Rost and Barbara F. Green guide executors and families through probate and estate settlement in the Connecticut Probate Courts, and Eric M. Gross handles probate litigation.

Does the firm handle real estate closings and commercial loans?

Yes. Samuel T. Rost helps clients buy, sell, finance, refinance and lease homes and commercial properties, has worked on many condominium projects, and represents Newtown Savings Bank in commercial real estate loan transactions in Fairfield and New Haven Counties.

Does the firm handle personal injury cases?

Yes. The firm represents people injured by the negligence of others, for example in automobile accidents and premises liability cases.

Which courts are the attorneys admitted to?

The attorneys are admitted in Connecticut, and several are admitted to the U.S. District Court for the District of Connecticut. Eric M. Gross and Samuel T. Rost are also admitted to the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Second Circuit and the U.S. Supreme Court. Barbara F. Green is also admitted in Ohio.

Does an attorney serve as an arbitrator or fact finder?

Yes. Eric M. Gross has served as an arbitrator in commercial and real estate disputes, privately and by court appointment, and is an attorney state trial referee and fact finder/arbitrator for the Judicial District of Fairfield.

How long has the firm been in practice?

Green and Gross has helped individuals and businesses in Connecticut for more than 30 years. Eric M. Gross is a founding member and has been president since 2003, and Barbara F. Green has been a member of the firm since 1990.
