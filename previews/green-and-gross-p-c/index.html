Land use & zoning

At home before Connecticut’s land use agencies

Our land use attorneys have deep experience with municipal land use agencies across Fairfield and New Haven Counties. We frequently represent individuals and neighborhood groups opposing development applications, help owners seek approvals, and take land use appeals as far as the Connecticut Supreme Court.

  • Planning & zoning commissions
  • Zoning boards of appeals
  • Inland wetlands commissions
  • Historic district commissions
  • Harbor management commissions

Zoning & land use

Recent appellate work

E & F Associates, LLC v. Zoning Board of Appeals, 320 Conn. 9 (2016): a successful variance appeal that clarified Connecticut law on the hardship that can justify a waiver of zoning regulations.

St. Joseph’s High School v. Planning & Zoning Commission, 176 Conn. App. 570 (2017): successfully defended a neighborhood association opposing stadium lights on a school athletic field.
