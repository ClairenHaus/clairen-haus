About the practice

The information clients need before they call.

Joel Z. Green · Case Western Reserve University School of Law, J.D., 1982

The current site describes the firm’s attorneys as legal counsel and trusted advisors to individuals, businesses and community groups. Its business attorneys handle commercial and residential real estate transactions, business formation, acquisitions, sales, succession planning and commercial litigation.

Its land use attorneys have deep experience with municipal land use agencies, including planning and zoning commissions, zoning boards of appeal, inland wetlands commissions, historic district commissions and harbor management commissions, and frequently represent individuals and neighborhood groups in opposing development applications.

The firm also lists personal injury representation in matters such as automobile accidents and premises liability, and elder law services covering will drafting, estate planning, estate settlement and probate administration.
