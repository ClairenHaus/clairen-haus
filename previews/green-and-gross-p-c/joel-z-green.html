Joel Z. Green

Joel Green concentrates his practice in general civil and commercial litigation and land use, and has brought and defended hundreds of cases for individual and corporate clients at trial and on appeal.

Litigation

Mr. Green concentrates his practice in general civil and commercial litigation and land use matters. Admitted in Connecticut in 1982, he has successfully brought and defended hundreds of cases on behalf of individual and corporate clients at both the trial and appellate level.

Land use

In his land use practice, Mr. Green has appeared before planning and zoning commissions, inland wetlands commissions, zoning boards of appeals, historic district commissions and other land use and development-related agencies throughout Fairfield and New Haven Counties. He has also tried land use appeals in the Connecticut courts.

He has represented a number of individuals, neighborhood groups and community groups in opposing massive and inappropriate development projects in neighborhoods throughout northern Fairfield County.

Civic life

Mr. Green is active in civic affairs in the greater Bridgeport area. He is a member of the Board of Directors of Sullivan McKinney Elder Housing, and served as Chairman of the Board of Directors of Operation Hope of Fairfield, an organization dedicated to innovative solutions to homelessness, where he is now a member of the Advisory Board. In 2008 he was elected to the Board of Commissioners of the Bridgeport Downtown Special Services District.

He held elected office as a five-term member of the Representative Town Meeting of the Town of Fairfield from 1997 through 2007, serving as Moderator from 2001 through 2007 and on its Legislation and Administration Committee and Public Works and Planning Committee. First elected to the Fairfield Democratic Town Committee in 1995, he was a District Leader from 2000 through 2005, and in 2004 received the committee's Eunice Postol Community Service Award for his years of service to the Fairfield community.

Mr. Green is a sustaining member of the Bridgeport Bar Association, a member of the Connecticut Bar Association's Land Use and Planning Section and Litigation Section, and a member of the Connecticut Trial Lawyers Association. Away from the office he is a former Commodore and Board of Governors member of the Windjammers Sailing Club.

Background

Mr. Green was born and raised in the Bridgeport area and lives in Fairfield with his wife and two daughters. He received a B.A. in geography from Clark University in 1979 and his J.D. from Case Western Reserve University School of Law in 1982.
