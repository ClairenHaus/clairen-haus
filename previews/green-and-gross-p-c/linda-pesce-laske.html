Linda Pesce Laske

Linda Laske represents clients in zoning and land use, commercial litigation and administrative agency matters, and frequently handles appeals in those areas, including before the Connecticut Supreme and Appellate Courts.

Phone (203) 335-5141Email llaske@gglaw.netFax (203) 367-9964

Zoning and land use law

Attorney Laske works in all areas of zoning and land use law. She has deep experience handling appeals of decisions of municipal land use agencies such as planning and zoning commissions, zoning boards of appeals, inland wetlands commissions and historic district commissions.

If you need help challenging or winning approval of a land use application, Attorney Laske welcomes your inquiry. Examples include applications for special permits, site plan approvals, zone changes, subdivisions, zoning text amendments, affordable housing and variances.

Other areas of real property law

She is also experienced in easement rights, boundary disputes, actions to quiet title, nuisance actions, eminent domain, public and private road issues, outdoor advertising regulation, the rights of waterfront property owners, and property tax appeals.

Commercial litigation, employment, election law and voting rights

Attorney Laske has substantial experience in complex commercial litigation, employment law, and matters involving elections, voting rights and the duties of public officials.

Administrative law

She advises clients on regulatory compliance, permitting and administrative appeals in matters involving state agencies.

Appeals

Attorney Laske has argued before the Connecticut Supreme and Appellate Courts and has written dozens of briefs in those courts and in appeals to the Superior Court from decisions of zoning commissions, zoning boards of appeals and inland wetlands agencies. If you are seeking counsel to take an appeal, or you are an attorney looking for help writing an appellate brief or navigating appellate procedure or motion practice, she welcomes your inquiry.

Representative appellate matters

  • E & F Associates, LLC v. Zoning Board of Appeals, 320 Conn. 9 (2016): successful variance appeal that clarified Connecticut law on the type of hardship that can lawfully justify a waiver of zoning regulations.
  • St. Joseph’s High School v. Planning & Zoning Commission, 176 Conn. App. 570 (2017): successfully defended a neighborhood association opposing a special permit for stadium lights on a school athletic field.

Case results depend on the facts and law of each matter. Past results do not guarantee a similar outcome.

Background

Attorney Laske is a graduate of the University of Connecticut School of Law (J.D., 1993) and Wesleyan University (B.A., 1989). She holds a Certificate in Mediation from the UConn Labor Education Center / Quinnipiac Law School, is a member of the Connecticut Bar Association (Zoning Section) and the Greater Bridgeport Bar Association, and volunteers in her community, including with the National Charity League, where she served as her chapter's Parliamentarian.
