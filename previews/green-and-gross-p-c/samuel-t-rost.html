Samuel T. Rost

For over thirty years, Samuel Rost has helped clients buy, sell, finance and lease homes and commercial property, build and restructure businesses, and settle estates in the Connecticut Probate Courts.

Phone (203) 335-5141Email srost@gglaw.netFax (203) 367-9964

Practice

Mr. Rost represents clients in commercial and residential real estate transactions, business law transactions and probate matters. He helps clients purchase, sell, finance, refinance and lease homes and commercial properties; form, purchase, sell and restructure business entities; and probate and settle estates in the Connecticut Probate Courts.

He has represented many clients in the formation and completion of condominium projects, so that units can be sold in the residential market. His experience in commercial matters has led to his current representation of Newtown Savings Bank in loan transactions with commercial owners of real estate in Fairfield and New Haven Counties.

Teaching and service

Mr. Rost is a member of the Connecticut Bar Association and the Bridgeport Bar Association. For five years he taught the basic principles of business law to undergraduate and graduate students as an instructor in Business Law at Fairfield University.

He has served on the Board of Directors of the Jewish Center for Community Services, holding the offices of President, Vice President and Treasurer, and is a past President of the Probus Club of Greater Bridgeport. Over the years he has volunteered his time and provided legal services to many local charitable organizations.

Background

Born in New York, New York, Mr. Rost has lived in Connecticut for more than sixty years. He graduated from Bowdoin College in 1965 and received his J.D. from Boston University School of Law in 1968. He also taught mathematics for a year at a middle school in Monroe, Connecticut, and, having played college basketball and soccer, coached junior high school students part-time.
