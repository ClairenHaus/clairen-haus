About the Firm

Our story

Serving Mid-Missouri for generations

The law firm of Grimes, Fay & Kopp has served clients in Mid-Missouri for decades. The firm’s practice began in Centralia, Missouri in 1946 and later expanded into Columbia. Elton W. Fay joined the firm in 1973, and Andrew C. Kopp in 2005.

Our years of experience and small-firm atmosphere allow us to give you high-quality representation while still providing the personal attention and dedication that each case needs and deserves.

It remains the goal of our firm to provide competent and personal service to individuals in Mid-Missouri.
