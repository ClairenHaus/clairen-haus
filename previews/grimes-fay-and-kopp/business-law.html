Business Law

Starting a business, enforcing a contract, defending your company or collecting what it is owed: our attorneys provide trusted guidance for small and family-owned businesses.

Whether you are starting a business, enforcing contract agreements, defending your business or trying to collect what your business is entitled to, the attorneys at Grimes, Fay & Kopp provide accurate and fair representation. Our firm has decades of experience representing small and family-owned businesses.

How we help businesses

Business organization

Choosing and forming the right structure when you start a business.

Contracts

Drafting, reviewing and enforcing the agreements your business depends on.

Defending your business

Representation when your business faces a claim or dispute.

Collections

Pursuing what your business is owed.

Built for small and family-owned businesses

We understand the time pressures and difficulties that come with creating and running a small business. Let our attorneys ease the burden with trusted guidance and representation for your interests, so you can focus on the work of the business itself.

Planning ahead

In general, many business problems are easier and less expensive to prevent than to fix: a clear operating agreement, written contracts with customers and vendors, and a plan for what happens if an owner leaves or passes away. Because our firm also handles estate planning and real estate, we can look at how your business fits with the rest of your plans.
