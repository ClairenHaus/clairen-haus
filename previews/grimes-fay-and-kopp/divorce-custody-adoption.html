Divorce in Missouri

Missouri calls divorce a “dissolution of marriage.” Ending a marriage can be one of the most emotional and stressful times in a person’s life. Beyond the decision itself, the court has to address how property is divided, whether maintenance is paid, and how debts are allocated. Our team will be with you step by step through the court process to help reduce the stress of a difficult time.

How Missouri courts decide custody

Missouri courts are required to decide custody based on the best interests of the child. That is not always exactly what a parent wants, or what a child wants; the court weighs all relevant factors, including:

  • The wishes of the child’s parents
  • The child’s need for a frequent, continuing and meaningful relationship with both parents, and each parent’s willingness to function as a parent
  • The child’s interaction with parents, siblings and others who may affect the child’s best interests
  • Which parent is more likely to allow a frequent, continuing and meaningful relationship with the other parent
  • The wishes of the child

Our attorneys will analyze the facts of your case, explain how the law applies to them, give you a realistic evaluation of the custody arrangement a court may order, and work on your side.

Paternity

If you are the parent of a child born to parents who were not married, you may need to establish legal paternity, support obligations and custodial arrangements. Whether or not the father’s name is on the birth certificate, it may be necessary to ask the court to establish each parent’s rights. A court order of paternity sets significant rights and obligations, including child support, a custody schedule and other financial responsibilities for the child.

Child support and Form 14

Missouri sets child support using guideline calculations on a form known as Form 14. The guidelines provide a starting point, but they allow for many adjustments that can significantly affect the result. Our attorneys help you build the right strategy for those factors so you can anticipate and negotiate a fair payment for the benefit of your child.

Adoption

Adoption is an emotional time for families, whether it is a private, agency or step-parent adoption. Attorneys in our firm have both legal and personal experience with adoption and are happy to represent parents in creating or expanding their families.
