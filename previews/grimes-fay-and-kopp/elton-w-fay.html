Elton W. Fay joined Grimes, Fay & Kopp in 1973. The firm serves clients from its offices in Columbia and Centralia, primarily throughout Boone, Cooper, Cole, Callaway, Audrain, Randolph and surrounding counties.

Working with Elton

Being a smaller firm, Grimes, Fay & Kopp has always focused on personalized service. Your personal attorney deals directly with you, rather than shuffling you from one attorney to another throughout your case, and every matter starts with a one-on-one consultation to discuss your case and the best route forward.
