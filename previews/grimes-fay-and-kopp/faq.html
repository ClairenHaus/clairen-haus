Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about consultations, our offices and how we can help.

Do you offer a free consultation?

Yes. You can schedule a complimentary 30-minute consultation with our attorneys in Columbia or Centralia. Call the Columbia office at (573) 449-2969 or the Centralia office at (573) 682-3933.

Where are your offices?

Our Columbia office is at 11 N 5th Street, Columbia, MO 65201, and our Centralia office is at 203 S Allen Street, Centralia, MO 65240.

What areas do you serve?

We provide legal services primarily throughout Boone, Cooper, Cole, Callaway, Audrain, Randolph and surrounding counties in Mid-Missouri.

What are your office hours?

Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. and 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m.

Will I work with the same attorney throughout my case?

Yes. Being a smaller firm, we focus on personalized service: your personal attorney deals directly with you rather than shuffling you from one attorney to another throughout your case.

What kinds of cases do you handle?

Our practice includes divorce, custody, paternity, child support and adoption; personal injury and wrongful death; business law; wills, trusts and estate planning; real estate; estate and trust administration, including guardianships and conservatorships; and traffic defense.

How does the estate planning process work?

Step 1: contact our office to set up a consultation about your goals and expectations. Step 2: collect the documents needed to prepare your plan, and review drafts so you understand every part of it. Step 3: put the plan in place through formal signing, with help on any asset transfers it needs.

Do you help with smaller estates as well as larger ones?

Yes. Our attorneys help clients with both modest estates and those with substantial net worth, for estate planning and for estate administration.

How do Missouri courts decide child custody?

Missouri courts must decide custody based on the best interests of the child, weighing all relevant factors, including the wishes of the parents and the child, the child’s need for a meaningful relationship with both parents, the child’s relationships with family members, and which parent is more likely to support the child’s relationship with the other parent.

How is child support calculated in Missouri?

Missouri uses guideline calculations on a form known as Form 14. The guidelines provide a basis for calculating support, but they allow adjustments that can significantly affect the result, and our attorneys can help you plan for them.

What does estate administration include?

It can include probate of a deceased person’s estate, administration of a revocable or irrevocable trust, guardianship for an incapacitated or disabled adult or a minor child, and conservatorship to manage a person’s financial affairs.

Can I rent your conference room if I’m not a client?

Yes. Our downtown Columbia conference room is available to clients and non-clients by the hour, half day or full day, Monday through Friday from 8 to 5. Contact our office for pricing and availability.

How long has the firm been practicing?

The firm’s practice began in Centralia, Missouri in 1946 and later expanded into Columbia. Elton W. Fay joined the firm in 1973 and Andrew C. Kopp in 2005.
