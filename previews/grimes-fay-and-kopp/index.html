Estate planning

A clear, three-step plan for your estate

We help clients with both modest estates and those with substantial net worth, and we work to make the process as painless and affordable as possible.

  1. Consultation

    Discuss your goals and expectations, and learn the options available to accomplish them.

  2. Documents & drafts

    Gather what’s needed and review drafts until you understand and are comfortable with every part of your plan.

  3. Signing & funding

    Formal signing, plus help with any asset transfers your plan requires.
