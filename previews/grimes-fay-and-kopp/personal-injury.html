Personal Injury

When you or a loved one is hurt because of someone else’s negligence, our attorneys investigate what happened and pursue the compensation you are entitled to.

Grimes, Fay & Kopp has the skill, experience and resources to help with cases involving serious personal injury or wrongful death. We understand what families go through when a loved one is injured or killed because of someone else’s negligence, carelessness or wrongful conduct, and we work to be the best possible advocates for you as you rebuild after an accident.

Injury claims we handle

Motor vehicle accidents

Car, truck and other vehicle collisions, usually the first thing people think of as personal injury.

Premises liability

Injuries caused by unsafe conditions on someone else’s property.

Workers’ compensation

Injuries that happen on the job and the benefits that should follow.

Wrongful death

Claims on behalf of families who have lost a loved one because of another’s wrongful conduct.

Why it helps to have an attorney

Financial hardship from lost income and medical bills comes with every kind of injury. Insurance companies are not always quick to offer compensation, and when they do, the offer generally serves their interests more than yours. Our attorneys analyze the different avenues of liability, investigate the damages you have suffered, and work to get you the compensation you are entitled to receive.

What to bring to your consultation

In general terms, it helps to bring anything you already have about the incident: accident or incident reports, photos, the names of witnesses, medical records and bills, records of missed work, and any letters from insurance companies. If you don’t have these yet, call anyway; your attorney can explain what is needed.

Injury claims have legal deadlines, and some are shorter than people expect. Calling early gives your attorney the most room to protect your claim.
