Real Estate

Real estate transactions can be complex and involve significant amounts of money. We help protect your financial interests from contract to closing, and in disputes.

Whether you are a buyer, seller, developer, landlord or tenant, Grimes, Fay & Kopp can help protect your financial interests. Our attorneys have legal and practical experience in all aspects of real estate transactions, from drafting and reviewing contracts to litigation over contracts or boundary disputes.

Who we represent

Buyers & sellers

Reviewing and drafting purchase contracts and guiding the transaction.

Developers

Legal support for development projects and the agreements behind them.

Landlords & tenants

Leases and the disputes that can arise under them.

Property disputes

Litigation over real estate contracts and boundary lines.

Why have a real estate contract reviewed

In general, a real estate contract sets deadlines, contingencies and obligations that are hard to undo once signed. Having an attorney review the terms before you commit helps make sure the agreement says what you expect and that your interests are protected.

Connected to the rest of your plans

Real estate often ties into estate planning, estate administration and business matters, all of which our firm handles. A beneficiary deed, a property held in trust or real estate owned by a business can each be part of a larger plan.
