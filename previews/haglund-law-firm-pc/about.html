About the firm

A trusted advisor since 1975

Integrity, insight, loyalty and expertise: the values Wayne Haglund built the practice on.

Built on long-term relationships

The Haglund Law Firm serves clients throughout the state of Texas with a commitment to excellence and a dedication to client-focused representation. Its practice is built on advocating for the communities, school districts and individuals it serves, and the firm prides itself on establishing and maintaining long-term relationships with its clients.

In 1975, Wayne Haglund established his practice with the commitment of becoming a trusted advisor to his clients and not merely a provider of legal services. Integrity, insight, loyalty and expertise are the fundamental values of the practice.

Three levels of advice

The firm responds to clients with legal, practical and political advice. It guides clients through difficult and challenging situations and provides seasoned media and crisis management support whenever a client faces tough decisions.

Who the firm has served

Wayne Haglund and the firm have provided legal services to more than 60 Texas school districts, a community college, a MHMR community center and various other governmental entities, as well as businesses, employers and individuals.

The staff

The firm is proud of a support staff with a wide array of legal experience, training and expertise, and more than 75 years of combined legal support experience. The firm understands the importance of giving clients the highest level of service and response.
