FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the firm and how to reach it.

Where is the Haglund Law Firm located?

The office is at 207 E. Frank Ave., Suite 106, Lufkin, Texas 75904. Mail goes to P.O. Box 713, Lufkin, Texas 75902-0713.

How do I contact the firm?

Call (936) 639-0007, fax (936) 639-0016, or email contactus@haglundlaw.com.

Does the firm only serve clients in Lufkin?

No. The firm serves clients throughout the state of Texas, and its family law practice includes representation of non-Texas residents.

What areas of law does the firm practice?

School law, employment law, public law, estate planning, probate, corporate and business law, immigration, mediation, real estate law and family law.

Does the firm represent school districts?

Yes. The firm currently represents and acts as general counsel for more than 30 school districts throughout Texas, and over the years it has provided legal services to more than 60 Texas school districts, a community college, a MHMR community center and other governmental entities.

Can the firm train our school board and staff?

Yes. Staff, administration and board training is part of the firm’s school law practice, and Mr. Haglund is certified by TEA to conduct board training. The firm also provides sexual harassment training for employers.

How long has the firm been practicing?

Wayne Haglund established his practice in Lufkin in 1975.

Does the firm handle immigration cases?

Yes, including family-based and employment-based immigration, adjustment of status, naturalization, DACA, waivers, and defense in immigration court and before the Board of Immigration Appeals.

Does the firm offer mediation?

Yes. The firm provides mediation services in civil law, employment law and family matters, including custody issues.

Can the firm help me start a business or nonprofit?

Yes. The firm handles business formation (LLCs, LLPs, sole proprietorships, for-profit and nonprofit corporations), IRS 501(c)(3) applications, family limited partnerships and business contracts.

Does contacting the firm make me a client?

No. Contacting the firm does not create an attorney-client relationship. No attorney-client relationship is established until a written engagement or agreement has been signed and accepted.

Should I send confidential details by email?

No. Please do not send information you consider confidential or privileged by email before the firm has agreed to represent you. Until then, information you send is not treated as confidential.
