The Historic Home

A 1928 landmark, restored

The only known Bulger-designed building in deep East Texas.

The 1928 Bulger House

The building was designed by Clarence Bulger and is on the National Register of Historic Places. The architectural firm of C.W. Bulger & Son (Clarence C.) designed homes, churches, theaters, courthouses and other buildings in many Texas towns, including Dallas and Galveston, and in 20 states. The Praetorian Building in Dallas, also designed by the Bulger firm, was the first modern skyscraper built in Texas. The Bulger House in Lufkin is the only known structure designed by the Bulger firm in deep East Texas.

The families who lived here

  1. 1928Designed for Mr. and Mrs. W.A. Boynton. Mr. Boynton was a lumberman in East Texas.
  2. The Perry yearsAfter a few years the home was sold to Mr. and Mrs. Roscoe Perry. Mr. Perry was one of the brothers who founded the Perry Brothers Five and Dime stores known throughout East Texas, and the residence was the site of many social events. Mrs. Perry lived in the home for a number of years after her husband’s death.
  3. The AlgoodsAfter sitting empty for a few years, the house was bought by Mr. and Mrs. Perry Algood, who moved their large, active family in.
  4. 1980Dr. Charles and Beverly Kent bought the house and renovated it for Dr. Kent’s orthodontic practice. Dr. Kent retired in 2005, turning his practice over to Dr. John Studer.
  5. 2006Wayne and Lisa Haglund purchased the building, knowing Dr. Studer planned to move to a new facility in 2008. Under the guidance of Dallas architect Jacquelyn Block, the Haglunds removed the dental equipment and restored the house to near its original state for the Haglund Law Firm, P.C., joining the previous owners in honoring its history.
