The firm

Legal, practical and political advice, in one place.

In 1975, Wayne Haglund established his practice with the commitment of becoming a trusted advisor to his clients, not merely a provider of legal services. Integrity, insight, loyalty and expertise are the values that form the foundation of the practice.

The firm provides representation on three levels, responding to clients with legal, practical and political advice. It guides clients through difficult and challenging situations and provides seasoned media and crisis management support whenever a client faces tough decisions.

Through experience, education and active state and national association involvement, the firm keeps a close understanding of its clients’ changing legal challenges, and it prides itself on long-term relationships.

The Historic Home

A Lufkin landmark, restored

The 1928 Bulger House is on the National Register of Historic Places and is the only known structure by the Bulger firm in deep East Texas. In 2006 Wayne and Lisa Haglund bought the building and, with Dallas architect Jacquelyn Block, restored it to near its original state for the Haglund Law Firm.
