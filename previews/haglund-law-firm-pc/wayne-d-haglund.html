Background

Wayne D. Haglund was born in Galveston, Texas, and attended the public schools of Galveston and Beaumont. He received his B.A. from the University of Texas at Austin in 1974 and his J.D. from the University of Texas School of Law in 1975.

Mr. Haglund’s statewide practice concentrates primarily on the representation of governmental entities. He serves as attorney for a number of Texas school districts, Angelina Community College and the Burke Center, a MHMR community center serving twelve counties in East Texas. He has practiced school law since 1975 and is a frequent speaker on school and public law issues.

Leadership and memberships

  • School Law Section, State Bar of Texas: long-time member; served as Director and Chair
  • Texas Association of School Boards Council of School Attorneys: Chairman and Executive Committee member (Board of Directors and Chair)
  • Texas Association of Community College Attorneys: founding Director and President
  • Education Law Association: member of the Board of Directors and elected President
  • Director, TASB Legal Assistance Fund (TASB-LAF)
  • Member, National School Board Association Council of School Attorneys
  • Texas Association of School Boards Construction Contracts Committee: member for more than five years
  • Sustaining Life Member, Texas Bar Foundation; member, College of the State Bar of Texas
  • Past President, Angelina County Bar Association and Angelina County Young Lawyers Association
  • Certified by TEA to conduct school board training

Recognition

  • 2023: M.A. McGhehey Award for distinguished service to the field of education law, Education Law Association
  • 2019: Kelly Frels Lifetime Achievement Award, the highest honor of the State Bar of Texas School Law Section
  • Since 2004: selected as a Texas Super Lawyer in education law by Law & Politics and Texas Monthly
  • Since 2004: peer rated AV-Preeminent by Martindale-Hubbell
  • 2002: named a “Top Notch Lawyer” in Education Law by Texas Lawyer’s Go-To Guide, one of five lawyers selected in that area
