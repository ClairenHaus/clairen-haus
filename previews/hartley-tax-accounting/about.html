About the firm

A small firm, by design.

Hartley Tax & Accounting is a small business in Fort Wayne, Indiana, recognized as a ministry tax specialist and known for serving pastors, churches, non-profits, small businesses and individuals.

Owner & founder

Jean Hartley, EA

“My name is Jean Hartley and I established this firm in order to provide ministers with expert tax consultation concerning ministry tax.” Jean is a tax accountant specializing in ministry tax and accounting, and an Enrolled Agent with the IRS, with 37 years of experience in the accounting field and 35 years specializing in ministry tax.

She has consulted with hundreds of pastors all over the country, and with hundreds of non-ministry individuals and many small businesses. “I feel very blessed to be able to assist those who serve at home and in the mission fields with their tax obligations.”

And in her spare time, Jean is an Indiana State Champion powerlifter.
