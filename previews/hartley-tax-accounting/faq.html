FAQ

Questions we hear often.

Short answers, from how we work. Anything else, just call.

Do you only work with ministers?

No. We are ministry tax specialists, and we also serve churches, non-profits, small businesses and individuals with tax preparation, planning and bookkeeping.

I don’t live in Fort Wayne. Can you still help?

Yes. We have clients in most of the states in the U.S. and in over 25 different countries, including ministers serving in the mission fields.

How do I become a client?

Call (260) 749-0444 or email secretary@hartleytaxaccounting.com. We will set up a phone consultation, which is free for those becoming clients.

Can I call with questions during the year?

Yes. Clients may call during the year for tax advice, tax estimate updates or consultation on housing allowance adjustments.

How do I send you my documents?

Use our secure file portal at hartleytaxaccounting.securefilepro.com, or drop them off at the office at 5936 East State Blvd.

Do I need to give my birthdates and Social Security numbers every year?

No. After the first year, birthdates and Social Security numbers are not required unless there are additions.

Can you check my withholding?

Yes. If you would like a withholding check-up after tax season, call in for an appointment.

Can you help our church treasurer?

Yes. If a minister becomes a client, we help the treasurer with pay package, withholding and reimbursement issues.

Do you offer an annual tax update?

Yes. Ask for the annual tax update when you contact us; it covers the most current tax law.
