Representation before the IRS and the state

During our many years of experience dealing with many taxing authorities, we have achieved a level of competence that ensures our clients are properly represented before the various federal and state tax agencies.

Jean Hartley is an Enrolled Agent with the IRS. Enrolled Agents are federally authorized tax practitioners who can represent taxpayers before the IRS.
