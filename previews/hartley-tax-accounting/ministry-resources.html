Current tax notes

  • The 2026 business mileage rate is 72.5 cents per mile (70 cents for 2025).
  • Gift tax exclusion: up to $19,000 for 2026.
  • There are seven federal tax rates in 2025: 10%, 12%, 22%, 24%, 32%, 35% and 37%. Long-term capital gains rates remain 0%, 15% and 20%.
  • Child tax credit: $2,200 for 2025, for children 16 and younger.
  • Indiana clients: an Indiana resident who is a member of a health care sharing ministry may be entitled to an adjusted gross income tax deduction (IN.gov).
