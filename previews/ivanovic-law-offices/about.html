About the firm

A Milwaukee family law firm built on responsiveness, honest advice and a team approach.

Our story

Dedicated to family law since 1995

Linda Ivanovic was admitted to practice in Wisconsin in 1993 and has dedicated her practice exclusively to family law since 1995. Ivanovic Law Offices has grown into a team of four attorneys and four staff members in downtown Milwaukee, serving clients in Milwaukee, Waukesha, Ozaukee, Washington and Racine counties and the surrounding area.

Each attorney was chosen for their ambition, dedication and drive. The team works together on legal issues, brainstorming creative solutions and furthering each client’s position.
