Adoption, Guardianship & Grandparent Visitation

Families form and change in many ways. The firm handles adoptions, guardianships and grandparent visitation petitions.

What the firm handles

  • Adoption
  • Guardianships
  • Grandparent visitation

Adoption

An adoption creates a permanent legal parent-child relationship. Wisconsin law sets out who may petition to adopt and the steps required, and the details matter. In 1998, Linda Ivanovic represented a child’s godparents in a contested Milwaukee County adoption case that was covered by the Milwaukee Journal Sentinel and featured on Dateline NBC.

Guardianship

A guardianship gives a person legal authority to care for someone who cannot fully care for themselves, such as a minor child whose parents are unable to. The process runs through the court and requires a petition and a hearing.

Grandparent visitation

In certain circumstances, Wisconsin law allows grandparents to ask the court for visitation with a grandchild. Whether a petition is possible depends on the family’s situation.

This page explains general concepts and is not legal advice. Every case is different.
