Child Support & Maintenance

Support numbers have long-term consequences. The firm prepares detailed child support, maintenance and family support calculations and advises you on the tax effects of each option.

What the firm handles

  • Shared placement support calculations
  • Split placement (children on different schedules)
  • High-income payer child support reductions
  • Imputed earnings for underemployed spouses
  • Hidden and cash income cases
  • Cohabitation support reductions
  • Maintenance tax analysis
  • Lump-sum buy-outs of maintenance
  • Head of household filing status and tax exemptions

Child support in Wisconsin

Wisconsin calculates child support using percentage-of-income standards in the state’s administrative code, with separate formulas for shared placement, split placement, low-income and high-income payers. Getting the inputs right, especially income, matters as much as the formula itself.

Maintenance

Maintenance is Wisconsin’s term for spousal support or alimony. Whether it is awarded, how much and for how long depends on factors such as the length of the marriage, each spouse’s earning capacity and the property division.

The tax side of support

Linda Ivanovic has completed extensive specialized training in tax-advantaged maintenance, child support and family support calculations. The firm will advise you on whether child support, maintenance or family support gives you the best tax result, and on your best filing status and tax exemptions.
