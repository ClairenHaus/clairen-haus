Custody & Placement

Decisions about your children matter more than anything else in a family case. The firm represents parents in custody and placement disputes during a divorce or paternity case and after a judgment is entered.

What the firm handles

  • Legal custody (decision-making) disputes
  • Physical placement schedules
  • Cases involving psychological issues
  • Cases involving alcohol and drug issues
  • Moves out of state
  • Modification of custody and placement after judgment
  • Enforcement of placement orders

Custody and placement in Wisconsin

Wisconsin separates two ideas that many people group together as “custody.” Legal custody is the right to make major decisions for a child, such as education, non-emergency health care and religion. Physical placement is the time a child spends with each parent. The court decides both based on the best interests of the child.

When the facts are difficult

Some placement cases involve mental health concerns, substance use, or a parent who wants to move out of state with the children. These cases often involve guardians ad litem, social workers and psychologists. The firm is familiar with the judges, social workers, psychologists and other professionals involved in Milwaukee-area family cases.

After the judgment

Children grow and circumstances change. If an existing order no longer works, or the other parent is not following it, the firm handles post-judgment motions to modify or enforce custody and placement.

This page explains general concepts and is not legal advice. Every case is different.
