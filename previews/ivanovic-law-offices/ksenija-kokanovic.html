From the chessboard to the courtroom

Ksenija was born in Belgrade, Serbia (former Yugoslavia). She began playing chess at age 7 and became a chess master at 16. She won first place in the former Yugoslav youth championship, competed in world championships in Singapore and Santiago, Chile in 1990, and after moving to the United States in 1992 was one of the highest-ranked female chess players in the country.

Today she uses that intellect and quickness to outmaneuver her opponent in the courtroom. She enjoys complicated, problem-solving cases and takes a personal approach with clients, addressing their concerns promptly.

Areas of experience

  • Divorce, legal separation, custody, paternity and post-judgment motions
  • Consumer bankruptcy and Chapter 128
  • Criminal, personal injury and traffic/OWI cases
  • Business and family immigration law, asylum and deportations

Background and service

In law school, Ksenija was Lead Articles Editor of the Indiana International and Comparative Law Review and received pro bono work recognition and a scholarship award. She interned with the Capital Area Immigrants’ Rights Coalition in Washington, D.C., and worked as a special projects analyst at JP Morgan Chase. She volunteers with Legal Action, the Milwaukee Justice Center, the Eastern District Bankruptcy Help Desk and the Marquette Volunteer Legal Clinic.
