Comfortable in the courtroom

“I enjoy being in court; the courtroom is my playground,” Linda says. She attends court hearings every week and regularly tries contested cases. Many of her clients come to her with difficult cases that cannot be resolved without a trial, and she also receives referrals from other attorneys on cases with complex legal issues. That experience lets her advise clients on when to settle an issue and when to fight.

“Some issues, such as who gets the frying pans, are not worth fighting for. Other issues, such as your children and financial future, are worth the fight.”

Support, tax and retirement expertise

Linda has completed extensive specialized training in tax-advantaged maintenance, child support and family support calculations, including shared placement, split placement and high-income payer schedules. She advises clients on whether child support, maintenance or family support gives the best tax result, and on filing status and tax exemptions.

She has represented business owners, physicians, and city, county and state employees, including police officers, firefighters and teachers, and their spouses. She handles the division of retirement plans through government employers, the military and private companies, and advises on the right order for each, such as a QDRO.

How she works

  • Dependable. Linda returns phone calls within 48 hours and keeps clients updated on their matter.
  • Honest. She tells clients whether they have a strong or a weak case, and when to settle and when to litigate.
  • Real, not theatrical. “You don’t need an actor, you need a real attorney.”
  • Current on the law. She reviews Court of Appeals family law decisions weekly and attends family law update seminars where local judges share their views.
  • Relentless and professional. She describes her style as determined, aggressive and relentless, without being needlessly rude. “Rudeness does not obtain goals. Knowledge is power.”
