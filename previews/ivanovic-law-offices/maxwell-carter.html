Family law from the start

In law school, Max volunteered with the Marquette Volunteer Legal Clinic and the Milwaukee Justice Center Family Law Forms Clinic, working with family law attorneys on a wide variety of family law concerns. Through Marquette’s internship program he interned at Centro Legal, gaining experience with divorce, paternity and post-judgment cases.

At Ivanovic Law Offices

Since joining the firm, Max has gained valuable legal and courtroom experience as part of a team of attorneys who collaborate on issues. He brings eagerness and drive to every case and will go the extra mile for his clients.

Outside the office

After stops in North Carolina, Ohio, Illinois, Michigan and Pennsylvania, Max is happy to call Milwaukee home. He enjoys cooking and is an avid football and basketball fan.
