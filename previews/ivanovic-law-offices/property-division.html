Property, Assets & Retirement

Dividing a marriage’s assets takes careful investigation and valuation. The firm handles everything from retirement plan orders to tracing hidden income and wasted marital assets.

What the firm handles

  • Retirement account valuations and orders
  • Qualified Domestic Relations Orders (QDROs) and Domestic Relations Orders
  • Asset and marital waste investigation
  • Bank record and credit card analysis
  • Home valuations and quit claim deeds
  • Valuation of college degrees
  • Business valuations and buy-out agreements
  • Tax analysis

Property division in Wisconsin

Wisconsin is a marital property state. In a divorce, property is generally presumed to be divided equally, though the court may deviate from an equal division after considering factors set out in Wisconsin law. Identifying what is marital, what it is worth, and what may have been hidden or wasted is often the hardest part.

Retirement plans

Linda Ivanovic handles the division of retirement plans through city, county, state and federal government employers, the military and private companies. She advises clients on the right type of order for each plan, such as a Qualified Domestic Relations Order or a Domestic Relations Order, and on which plans are better to keep through a buy-out and which to divide.

Business owners and professionals

The firm has represented business owners, physicians and their spouses. That includes scrutinizing business evaluations, reviewing buy-out agreements, and working through pay structures, profit-based income formulas and partnership interests.
