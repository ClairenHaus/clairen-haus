Tom Schneck

Tom Schneck has practiced law in southeast Wisconsin for more than 30 years. Practical and approachable, he works across family, criminal and bankruptcy matters and gives clients a “big picture” view of their case.

Experience across practice areas

Given a legal challenge, Tom does not hesitate to dive in head first. He has appeared in courts all over Wisconsin and in the federal courts in Milwaukee and Madison. Because he knows family, criminal and bankruptcy law, he can see how a matter in one area affects another and advise clients on broader outcomes.

Cool, calculated decisions

Clients often come to Tom stressed, anxious or angry about their situation. Tom believes rash decisions made in anger rarely work out well; cool and calculated decisions based on the law and the facts drive the best outcomes.

  • Chapter 7 bankruptcy

  • General civil matters and landlord-tenant

Outside the office

Tom mentors the Wauwatosa West “We the People” team through the National Center for Civic Education. He has been married for over 30 years, has three adult children, and enjoys cycling.
