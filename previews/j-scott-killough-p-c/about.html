About the Firm

J. Scott Killough, P.C. is a Tyler based law firm, founded in 1990, serving clients across nine East Texas counties.

A Tyler law firm since 1990

Scott Killough moved to Tyler in 1989 and launched his own practice, J. Scott Killough, P.C., in 1990, when he began specializing in estate planning and probate. In 1993 he became Board Certified in Estate Planning and Probate Law by the Texas Board of Legal Specialization.

The firm concentrates on estate planning, including the use and preparation of wills, trusts, durable powers of attorney and medical powers of attorney; probate and administration of decedents’ estates; trust creation and administration; business transactions, including organization, business purchases, sales and a broad range of contracts; guardianship administration, including contested guardianships; litigation involving estates, trusts and guardianships; tax planning; and real estate transactions.

Attorney, accountant and mediator

Mr. Killough was a Certified Public Accountant before he practiced law, and he remains one. He also serves as a credentialed mediator in the same areas of practice. With his varied professional credentials, he is able to provide clients with a more complete approach to their legal concerns.
