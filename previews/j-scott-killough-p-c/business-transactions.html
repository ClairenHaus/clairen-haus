Business Transactions

Organizing a business, buying or selling one, and the broad range of contracts a business relies on, from an attorney who is also a CPA.

What the firm handles

The firm’s business transactions work includes:

  • Organization: setting up a business in the form that fits its owners and its plans.
  • Business purchases and sales: the agreements and documents involved when a business changes hands.
  • A broad range of contracts: the agreements a business depends on in its day-to-day dealings.

Where business and estate planning meet

For many business owners, the business is also the largest part of what they will one day pass on. Because Scott Killough’s practice covers business transactions, estate planning, tax planning and real estate, those questions can be considered together rather than one at a time.

An accountant’s perspective

Scott is a Certified Public Accountant and holds an M.B.A. from Southern Methodist University and a B.S.B.A. in Accounting from Drake University. His accounting years in the Tax Department of Arthur Andersen & Co. included corporate and individual income tax, real estate, and oil and gas work, and as a young lawyer he handled a wide variety of commercial matters.
