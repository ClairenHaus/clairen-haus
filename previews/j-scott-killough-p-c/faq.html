Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers drawn only from the firm’s own published information. For advice about your situation, call the office.

Where is the office?

The office is at 5520 Old Bullard Rd, Suite 113, Tyler, Texas 75703. The phone number is (903) 561-8280 and the fax number is (903) 561-8363.

What areas of law does the firm practice?

Estate planning (wills, trusts, durable powers of attorney and medical powers of attorney); probate and administration of decedents’ estates; trust creation and administration; business transactions, including organization, business purchases, sales and a broad range of contracts; guardianship administration, including contested guardianships; litigation involving estates, trusts and guardianships; tax planning; and real estate transactions. Scott Killough also serves as a mediator in these same areas.

Which counties does the firm serve?

Anderson, Cherokee, Gregg, Henderson, Rusk, Smith, Upshur, Van Zandt and Wood Counties.

Is Scott Killough board certified?

Yes. He has been Board Certified in Estate Planning and Probate Law by the Texas Board of Legal Specialization since 1993.

Is he also a CPA?

Yes. He has been a Certified Public Accountant in Texas since 1986. He began his career in 1980 in the Tax Department of Arthur Andersen & Co. in Dallas, working in corporate and individual income tax, estate and gift tax, non-profit organizations, real estate, and oil and gas.

Does he serve as a mediator?

Yes. He has been a credentialed mediator since 2009, serving in the same areas he practices: estates, probate, trusts, guardianships, business and related matters.

Does the firm handle contested guardianships?

Yes. The firm’s guardianship work includes guardianship administration and contested guardianships, as well as litigation involving estates, trusts and guardianships.

How long has Scott Killough practiced law?

He began his legal career in 1985 in Lubbock, moved to Tyler in 1989, and launched his own practice, J. Scott Killough, P.C., in 1990.

Which courts is he admitted to?

He was admitted to the Texas bar in 1986, and is admitted to practice before the U.S. District Courts for the Northern, Eastern and Western Districts of Texas, the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit, and the U.S. Tax Court.

Where did he go to school?

He received his J.D. from Texas Tech University in 1985, an M.B.A. from Southern Methodist University in 1980, and a B.S.B.A. in Accounting from Drake University in 1979.

How do I reach the firm?

Call the office at (903) 561-8280. The office is at 5520 Old Bullard Rd, Suite 113 in Tyler.

These answers are general information about the firm and are not legal advice.
