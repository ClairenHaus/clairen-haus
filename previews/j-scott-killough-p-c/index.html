Common questions

Answers before you call.

Short answers from the firm’s own information. See all questions.

Is Scott Killough board certified?

Yes. He has been Board Certified in Estate Planning and Probate Law by the Texas Board of Legal Specialization since 1993.

Is he also a CPA?

Yes. He has been a Certified Public Accountant in Texas since 1986. He began his career in 1980 in the Tax Department of Arthur Andersen & Co. in Dallas, working in corporate and individual income tax, estate and gift tax, non-profit organizations, real estate, and oil and gas.

Which counties does the firm serve?

Anderson, Cherokee, Gregg, Henderson, Rusk, Smith, Upshur, Van Zandt and Wood Counties.

Does the firm handle contested guardianships?

Yes. The firm’s guardianship work includes guardianship administration and contested guardianships, as well as litigation involving estates, trusts and guardianships.
