A credentialed mediator since 2009, for disputes in the same areas Scott Killough has practiced for decades.

What mediation is

Mediation is a confidential meeting in which a neutral mediator helps the people in a dispute talk through their differences and look for a resolution they can all accept. The mediator does not decide who wins. Agreements reached in mediation are the parties’ own, and mediation is often quicker, less costly and less adversarial than taking a dispute all the way to trial.

A mediator who knows the subject matter

In 2009, Scott Killough augmented his practice by becoming a credentialed mediator for the types of disputes his practice covers. He serves as a mediator in these same areas: estates, probate, trusts, guardianships, business and the related tax and real estate questions.

That background matters in mediation. Disputes over an estate, a trust or a guardianship turn on documents, accounts and family history, and a mediator who has spent his career with those issues, as a Board Certified estate planning and probate attorney and a Certified Public Accountant, can help the parties focus on what actually needs to be resolved.
