Real Estate Transactions

Real estate transactions, handled by a practice that also understands how property fits into estates, trusts, businesses and taxes.

Real estate as part of the practice

Real estate transactions are one of the areas listed in J. Scott Killough, P.C.’s practice. Buying, selling or transferring real property involves documents that need to be right the first time, because they affect title to the property for years to come.

Where real estate overlaps with the rest of the firm’s work

Real estate often sits at the center of other matters the firm handles: a family home or land passing through an estate, property held in a trust, real estate owned by a business, or the tax consequences of a sale. Having one attorney who works in all of those areas helps the pieces fit together.

Background

Scott Killough’s accounting years at Arthur Andersen & Co. included real estate and oil and gas tax work. He is a member of the Real Estate, Probate and Trust Law Section of the State Bar of Texas.
