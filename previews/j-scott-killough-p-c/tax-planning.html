Tax Planning

Tax planning from an attorney who is also a Certified Public Accountant, with a career that began in a major accounting firm’s tax department.

A practice built on tax experience

Scott Killough entered the workforce in 1980 in the Tax Department of Arthur Andersen & Co. in Dallas. During his years in accounting he gained experience in a broad range of tax areas, including:

  • corporate and individual income tax
  • estate and gift tax
  • non-profit organizations
  • real estate
  • oil and gas

He completed the requirements to practice as a Certified Public Accountant while in law school, and has been a CPA in Texas since 1986.

Tax planning alongside the rest of your plan

Tax questions rarely stand alone. They come up when you plan an estate, create a trust, settle an estate, organize or sell a business, or buy and sell real estate. Because tax planning sits inside the same practice as all of that work, the tax side can be considered from the start rather than after the fact.

Courts

Scott is admitted to practice before the U.S. Tax Court, as well as the U.S. District Courts for the Northern, Eastern and Western Districts of Texas and the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit.
