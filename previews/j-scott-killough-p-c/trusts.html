Trust Creation & Administration

Help creating a trust that fits your plan, and help for trustees carrying out the trust they have been given.

Creating a trust

A trust is an arrangement in which a trustee holds and manages property for the benefit of the people the trust names. Trusts are used for many reasons: to provide for children or other family members over time, to set terms for how and when property is distributed, or to plan for the management of assets if the person who created the trust can no longer manage them. The trust document sets the rules, so careful drafting matters.

Administering a trust

Serving as a trustee comes with real responsibilities: managing the trust property prudently, keeping records, communicating with beneficiaries, and making distributions according to the trust’s terms. Trustees often need guidance on what the document requires and how to carry it out properly.
