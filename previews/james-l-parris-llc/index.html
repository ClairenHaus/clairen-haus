What changes

What to fix first.

The current site is short and clear about what the firm does. The problems are underneath it, in how the pages load and who can open them.

Access
Pages visitors can actually open

The “About Us” and “Family Asset Preservation” pages on the current site answer with “Authorization Required,” so a visitor clicking either one hits an error. This concept puts the practice and contact facts on one open page.

Security & mobile
Secure and readable on a phone

The site loads over plain HTTP, which browsers flag as “Not secure,” and it has no mobile viewport, so it renders at desktop width on a phone. The rebuild serves over HTTPS and reflows at every width.

Answers
Structured for search and AI assistants

The firm, its address and practice areas are marked up as structured data (LegalService, Person, FAQ). The current site has none, and its table layout and ©2001 footer read as unmaintained.
