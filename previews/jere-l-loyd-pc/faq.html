FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the office, the firm and the matters we handle.

Where is the office?

The office is at 3715 Beck Road, Bldg. A, Suite 104, St. Joseph, Missouri 64506.

What are your office hours?

Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Saturday appointments are available.

How do I reach the firm?

Call (816) 364-3020 or email loydpc@swbell.net.

What areas do you serve?

The firm serves businesses and individuals throughout the greater St. Joseph, Kansas City, and northwest Missouri areas.

What kinds of legal matters does the firm handle?

Business organization, civil litigation, estate planning, probate, real estate transactions, creditors’ remedies, personal injury and negligence, wrongful death, and Social Security disability.

Who will I work with?

You work with attorney Jere L. Loyd, supported by legal assistant Cynthia Davis.

What estate planning services do you offer?

Trusts, wills, durable powers of attorney, health care powers (directives), Medicaid planning and non-probate transfers.

Can you help me set up or sell a business?

Yes. The firm handles corporations, limited liability companies, partnerships and limited partnerships, as well as mergers and consolidations, dissolution and liquidation, and business purchases and sales.

What probate matters do you handle?

Decedent estates, guardianship and conservatorship, determination of heirship, and probate litigation.

What real estate matters do you handle?

Contracts for sale, leases, quiet title suits, subdivisions, homeowners’ associations, boundary disputes, partition, foreclosure, easements and condemnation.

Which courts is Jere L. Loyd admitted to practice in?

The Missouri Bar, the U.S. District Court for the Western District of Missouri, the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Eighth Circuit, and the U.S. Tax Court.

Does using this website make me a client?

No. Use of this web site and the information on it does not create an attorney-client relationship, nor does it substitute for obtaining legal advice from an attorney.
