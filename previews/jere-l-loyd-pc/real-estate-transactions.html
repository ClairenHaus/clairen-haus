Practice area

Real Estate Transactions

Contracts for sale, leases, subdivisions, easements, boundary disputes, quiet title, partition and more.

Real estate is often the largest asset a family or business owns, and a mistake in a contract, deed or title can be costly to unwind. Careful legal work protects buyers, sellers, landlords, tenants and neighbors alike.

Jere L. Loyd, P.C. handles both transactions and disputes involving real property in the St. Joseph area and northwest Missouri.

Real estate matters we handle

  • Contracts for Sale
  • Leases
  • Quiet Title Suits
  • Subdivisions
  • Homeowners’ Associations
  • Boundary Disputes
  • Partition
  • Foreclosure
  • Easements
  • Condemnation
