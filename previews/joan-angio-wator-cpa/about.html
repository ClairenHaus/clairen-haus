About the firm

Personalized service, by design

JAW

Joan Angio Wator, CPA

Certified Public Accountant

Our firm offers a wide range of services to individual and business clients. Because the firm is relatively small, clients benefit from personalized, quality service, and our experienced staff can develop tax strategies that take advantage of new tax laws and legislation.

Our dedication to client service brought us online, and we continue to provide the highest quality professional service and guidance. Providing financial information to our clients in a timely and accurate manner is a commitment we feel cannot be compromised.

What the firm does

Tax planning and preparation for individuals and businesses; accounting, bookkeeping, payroll, compilations and financial statements; and consulting on business entity selection, business succession and estate planning. See every service.

Where to find us

The office is at 21142 Washington Parkway, Frankfort, IL 60423. The firm is a member of the Frankfort Chamber of Commerce.

Please feel free to contact us with any questions or comments you may have. We’d love to hear from you.
