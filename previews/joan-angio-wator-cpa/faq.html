Frequently asked questions

Where is your office?

We are at 21142 Washington Parkway, Frankfort, Illinois 60423.

How do I reach the office?

Call (815) 469-6225. Our fax number is (815) 469-0608.

Do you work with individuals, businesses, or both?

Both. The firm serves individual and business clients, from an individual return to a multi-tiered partnership.

Do you prepare business tax returns?

Yes. Our accounting services include business tax return preparation, including Sales & Use and Business Property filings.

Can you handle payroll and bookkeeping?

Yes. We offer computerized payroll services and monthly, quarterly or annual bookkeeping, along with general ledger and financial statement preparation.

We're starting a new business. Can you help?

Yes. We set up accounting systems for new businesses and can help you weigh the pros and cons of each entity type, such as an S corporation, sole proprietorship or limited liability company.

Is a compilation the same as an audit?

No. Compilations are based on information the client provides and are used primarily for internal purposes; no assurance is expressed. Reviews and audits are different services.

I don't see the service I need. Should I still call?

Yes. Our list of services is by no means all-inclusive, so please ask about a service even if you don't see it listed.
