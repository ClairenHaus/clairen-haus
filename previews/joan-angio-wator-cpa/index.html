What we do for our clients

A wide range of services for individual and business clients. The list is by no means all-inclusive, so please ask if you don’t see what you need.

Tax Services

  1. 01Tax Planning & PreparationTax planning and return preparation for individuals and businesses, from a single return to a multi-tiered partnership.

Accounting Services

  1. 02Accounting ServicesGeneral ledger, financial statements, bookkeeping, new-business setup, payroll and business tax filings.
  2. 03Bookkeeping / Write-UpDay-to-day bookkeeping handled by qualified staff, so your records stay organized.
  3. 04CompilationsFinancial statements compiled from the information you provide, primarily for internal use.
  4. 05Financial StatementsStatements prepared in accordance with professional standards, as a tool for management decisions.

Consulting Services

  1. 06Business Entity SelectionWeighing the pros and cons of each entity type to find the most advantageous form for your business.
  2. 07Business Succession PlanningPlanning the transition of your business to your successors.
  3. 08Estate PlanningGetting your financial affairs in order for the orderly transfer of assets to your beneficiaries.
