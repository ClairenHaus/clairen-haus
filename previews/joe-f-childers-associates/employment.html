Practice area

Employment

Representing public and private employees in harassment, discrimination, retaliation and wrongful termination cases, disciplinary hearings, and wage and hour claims.

Who the firm represents

The firm represents employees, in both public and private workplaces. In its words: “We have represented numerous public and private employees who have been wrongfully harassed, been subjected to discrimination or retaliation, and wrongfully terminated from employment. We also represent employees in disciplinary hearings, and wage and hour claims.”

Matters the firm handles

  • Workplace harassment
  • Discrimination
  • Retaliation, including retaliation for speaking out
  • Wrongful termination
  • Disciplinary hearings
  • Wage and hour claims

Employment claims often come with short filing deadlines, some measured in days or months rather than years. If you think you have a claim, it is worth talking to a lawyer early, and keeping copies of anything you have in writing.

Public employees

Public employees have protections that private employees may not, including constitutional protections for speech and due process. The firm also litigates these claims as civil rights cases; see Civil Rights.

Common questions

Does the firm represent employees or employers?

The firm’s site describes representing public and private employees.

Can the firm help with a disciplinary hearing?

Yes. The firm represents employees in disciplinary hearings.
