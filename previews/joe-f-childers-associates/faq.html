FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about the firm, drawn from its own published information.

What kinds of cases does Joe F. Childers & Associates handle?

The firm is a civil litigation firm. Its practice areas are environmental and land use, property (including coal, oil and gas royalties), employment, civil rights, personal injury, commercial litigation, and probate, estate litigation and estate planning.

Where is the office?

The Lexington Building, 201 West Short Street, Suite 300, Lexington, Kentucky 40507.

What areas does the firm serve?

The firm serves central and eastern Kentucky from its Lexington office.

Which courts does the firm practice in?

The U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Kentucky, the United States Court of Appeals for the Sixth Circuit, trial courts across central and eastern Kentucky, the Kentucky Court of Appeals, and the Kentucky Supreme Court.

Who will I talk to about my case?

The firm is small, and its relationship with clients is paramount. In its words: “If you call us to talk about your case, we will be here to talk to you.”

How long has Joe Childers practiced law?

Joe Childers was admitted to the Kentucky bar in 1981. He started his career with the Appalachian Research and Defense Fund (AppalRed) and has spent about forty years in private practice.

Does the firm represent landowners against coal, oil and gas companies?

Yes. The firm represents persons harmed by the coal, oil and gas industries, and litigates cases to recover coal, oil and gas royalties owed to landowners.

Does the firm represent employees?

Yes. The firm has represented numerous public and private employees in harassment, discrimination, retaliation and wrongful termination cases, and represents employees in disciplinary hearings and wage and hour claims.

Does the firm help with wills and estate planning?

Yes. The firm assists individuals with wills, powers of attorney, trusts and personal directives, and with probate and estate litigation.
