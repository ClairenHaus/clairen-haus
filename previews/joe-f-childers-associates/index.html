The people behind the practice

Meet Joe F. Childers.

From the firm’s current attorney and about pages.

Joe F. Childers

Attorney

Joe Childers has appeared before dozens of state and federal courts throughout Kentucky, handled federal trials and appeals, and resolved matters through mediation and settlement. Through published decisions he has helped shape Kentucky law on the taxation of unmined coal, surface owners’ rights under broad form deeds and against natural gas companies, and constitutional free-speech protections.

He has served on the Board of Regents of Kentucky State University and as Chairman of the Kentucky Mine Safety Review Commission.

  • University of Kentucky School of Law, J.D., 1981
  • University of Kentucky, B.A. with distinction, 1977
  • Admitted 1981, Kentucky; U.S. Court of Appeals, Sixth Circuit; U.S. District Courts, Eastern and Western Districts of Kentucky
  • Member: Fayette County and Kentucky Bar Associations
About the firm

Economical service, successful results, extraordinary client relationships.

The Lexington Building · 201 West Short Street, Ste. 300 · Lexington, Kentucky 40507

After practicing for several years as one of two members of the nine-lawyer firm Getty & Childers, PLLC, Joe F. Childers opened his own practice on January 1, 2012.

The firm’s stated values are dedication, determination, integrity and accessibility. It litigates with the experience and creativity of a “big firm,” with the personal attention and value of a small one.

Representative clients listed on the firm’s site include Tee Engineering Company, Stoner Mill Farm, LLC, Sierra Club, The Nature Conservancy, Pikeville Energy, LLC and Warehouses KY, LLC.
