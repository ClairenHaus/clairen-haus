Practice area

Probate, Estate Litigation & Estate Planning

Administering an estate correctly, estate and trust litigation, and individualized estate plans.

Probate

“We help ensure that you carry out the process of administering an estate correctly, and that all the legalities are followed according to estate laws.”

Probate is the court-supervised process of settling a person’s estate: proving the will (if there is one), gathering assets, paying debts and taxes, and distributing what is left to the people entitled to it.

Estate and trust litigation

The firm assists in estate and trust litigation matters related to power of attorney, loss of capacity, transfer of wealth, recovery of assets transferred by undue influence, financial abuse, or resulting trusts. In its words: “We have expertise and the understanding of the sensitivity around estate matters.”

Estate planning

The firm assists individuals with personal estate planning, including wills, powers of attorney, trusts and personal directives. “We understand the importance of creating an individualized estate plan concerning the transfer of our clients’ wealth to their intended beneficiaries. We advise each client based on their unique circumstances to create a customized and practical estate plan.”

Common questions

Does the firm prepare wills and powers of attorney?

Yes. The firm assists individuals with wills, powers of attorney, trusts and personal directives.

Can the firm help recover assets taken by undue influence?

Yes. Its estate and trust litigation work includes recovery of assets transferred by undue influence and financial abuse.
