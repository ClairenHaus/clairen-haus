Practice area

Property & Mineral Rights

Trespass and nuisance claims, quiet title actions, royalty recovery for landowners, and real estate purchase and sale disputes.

What this practice covers

In the firm’s words: “We litigate claims for trespass and nuisance, quiet title actions, and cases to recover coal, oil, and gas royalties owed to land owners. We also litigate issues related to the purchase and sale of real estate.”

  • Trespass and nuisance. Claims when someone enters or damages your land without a right to, or interferes with your use and enjoyment of it.
  • Quiet title. A court action to settle who owns land, or an interest in it, when the record is unclear or disputed.
  • Royalties owed to landowners. Cases to recover coal, oil and gas royalties that are owed and unpaid.
  • Real estate purchase and sale. Disputes that come out of buying or selling property.

A long history with Kentucky land and minerals

Joe Childers represented landowners in some of Kentucky’s most important mineral and tax litigation, which led to major reforms in the property tax of unmined minerals and to the passage of the Broad Form Deed Amendment to the Kentucky Constitution in 1988. Through published decisions he has helped write Kentucky law on surface owners’ rights under broad form deeds and against natural gas companies.

Common questions

Can the firm help a landowner recover unpaid coal, oil or gas royalties?

Yes. The firm litigates cases to recover coal, oil and gas royalties owed to landowners.

What is a quiet title action?

It is a court case that settles who owns a piece of land or an interest in it. The firm litigates quiet title actions.
