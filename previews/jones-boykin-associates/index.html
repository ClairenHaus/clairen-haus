Helping folks in Savannah for over 40 years.

Jones, Boykin & Associates, P.C. is a law firm primarily devoted to assisting people seriously injured through the negligence or wrongdoing of others. The firm reports handling thousands of personal injury claims through settlement, trials and jury verdicts, and routinely handles appeals before the Court of Appeals of Georgia, the Georgia Supreme Court and the Eleventh Circuit.

About the practice

Experienced, effective representation for injured clients.

Admitted in all State and Superior Courts of Georgia, the U.S. District Courts for the Southern, Middle and Northern Districts of Georgia, the Eleventh Circuit, the U.S. Supreme Court and the Federal Court of Claims

The firm says its clients have received millions of dollars in settlements and verdicts over the years, and that it has handled scores of cases with verdicts and settlements of a million dollars or more, while noting that not every case will yield such a recovery.

It routinely serves clients in Savannah and Chatham County, including Pooler, Bloomingdale, Tybee Island, Thunderbolt, Port Wentworth and Garden City, as well as Effingham, Bryan, Bulloch, Screven and Glynn counties.

Common questions

Answers from the firm’s own site.

Each answer below is drawn from facts the firm already publishes, marked up so search engines and AI assistants can cite it. General information only, not legal advice.

What kinds of cases does Jones, Boykin & Associates handle?

Serious personal injury and death claims, including motor vehicle and tractor-trailer collisions, injuries caused by drunk drivers, slip and fall injuries, medical malpractice, defective products, maritime and railroad injuries, and employment discrimination and civil rights claims.

Where is the firm located and what are its hours?

213 E 38th St., Savannah, GA 31401. Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM; closed Saturday and Sunday. Home and personal visits are available.

What areas does the firm serve?

Savannah and Chatham County, plus Effingham, Bryan, Bulloch, Screven and Glynn counties, and the firm travels throughout Georgia and the southeast for seriously injured clients.

What are Noble L. Boykin, Jr.’s credentials?

He has been in private practice since 1981, is AV rated by Martindale-Hubbell, is listed in its Bar Register of Preeminent Lawyers, and earned his J.D. cum laude from Mercer University in 1979.
