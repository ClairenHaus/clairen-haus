About the firm

A focused practice in Bloomfield Hills

Joshua D. West, PLC is one attorney with support staff, with its principal office on Woodward Avenue in Bloomfield Hills.

Joshua D. West provides a wide range of legal services to his clients in Oakland and surrounding counties in Michigan.

The firm

The Joshua D. West Law Firm keeps its principal office in Bloomfield Hills, Michigan. The firm is one attorney with support staff, so clients work directly with Mr. West, and he has handled cases throughout the State of Michigan.

How the firm works with clients

  • Around the clock. The firm has 24-hour support at (248) 259-5562.
  • Office hours. Monday to Friday, 8:00am to 5:00pm, at (248) 645-5100.
  • Email. Answered within 24 hours during regular business hours; all email correspondence is kept confidential.
  • Honest referrals. If the firm cannot address your specific legal need, it will gladly refer you to an attorney who can.

Rooted in Oakland County

Mr. West is a lifelong resident of Oakland County. Before private practice he clerked for the Chief Judge of the 52nd District Court, and today he teaches for the Institute of Continuing Legal Education. He is a member of the State Bar of Michigan and the Oakland County Bar, serves as President and Chairman of the Board of the Northfield Hills Condominium Association, and has coached and taught swimming in the Troy School District.
