Business & Corporate Law

Business law, corporate law and contracts, from a lawyer who has run a small business himself.

Most of a business’s legal life happens long before any dispute: how it is set up, who owns it, and what it promises in writing.

What business and corporate law covers

  • Choosing and forming an entity, such as an LLC or corporation
  • Operating agreements, bylaws and ownership terms
  • Contracts with customers, vendors and partners
  • Reviewing an agreement before you sign it
  • Questions that come up as the business grows or changes
  • Buying, selling or winding down a business

Contract law

A clear contract sets out who does what, by when, for how much, and what happens if something goes wrong. Contract law is part of Joshua D. West’s practice alongside business and corporate law.

A business owner’s perspective

Before practicing law, Mr. West was a small business owner and a sales manager for the Tandy Corporation. He also teaches for the Institute of Continuing Legal Education and has been a guest lecturer at Walsh College.
