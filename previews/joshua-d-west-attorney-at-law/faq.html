Frequently asked questions

Answers drawn from the firm’s own published information. General information only, not legal advice.

What kinds of cases does Joshua D. West handle?

The firm lists criminal defense (felonies and misdemeanors), all traffic matters, divorce, child custody and support, family law, business law, corporate law, contract law, and residential and commercial real estate.

Where is the office?

The firm's principal office is at 39520 Woodward Avenue, Suite 230, Bloomfield Hills, Michigan 48304.

What are the office hours?

The office is open Monday to Friday, 8:00am to 5:00pm Eastern.

Can I reach the firm outside office hours?

Yes. The firm keeps a 24-hour support line at (248) 259-5562. During business hours the office number is (248) 645-5100.

How quickly will the firm answer an email?

The firm responds to email within 24 hours during regular business hours. The address is joshwest@jdwestlaw.com.

Is email to the firm kept confidential?

The firm states that all email correspondence with it is kept completely confidential. Contacting the firm does not by itself create an attorney-client relationship.

Which areas does the firm serve?

Joshua D. West serves clients in Oakland and surrounding counties in Michigan, including Macomb and Wayne counties, and has handled cases throughout the State of Michigan.

What if my legal problem is outside the firm's practice?

If the firm cannot address your specific legal need, it will gladly refer you to an attorney who can assist you.

Does the firm handle both felonies and misdemeanors?

Yes. The firm's criminal defense practice covers both felonies and misdemeanors, along with all traffic matters.

What is Joshua D. West's background?

He is a graduate of the University of Michigan (B.A., Political Science, minor in Sociology) and Wayne State University Law School (J.D.). He served as judicial law clerk for Chief Judge William E. Bolle of the 52nd District Court, teaches for the Institute of Continuing Legal Education, and has been a guest lecturer at Walsh College.

How large is the firm?

Joshua D. West, PLC is one attorney with support staff, so clients work directly with Mr. West.

Does the firm handle real estate for businesses as well as homeowners?

Yes. The firm lists both residential real estate and commercial real estate among its practice areas.
