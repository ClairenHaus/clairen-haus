Meet Joshua D. West

A lifelong resident of Oakland County, Joshua D. West is a graduate of the University of Michigan and Wayne State University Law School.

Before starting his private practice in Bloomfield Hills, he served as judicial law clerk for Chief Judge William E. Bolle of the 52nd District Court, where he mediated cases and advised the judge. He is a former small business owner, teaches for the Institute of Continuing Legal Education, and has been a guest lecturer at Walsh College.
