What changes

Specific fixes, drawn from the current site.

  • A secure, phone-ready site

    The current site loads over plain HTTP with no viewport tag, so a phone renders it about 760px wide on a 390px screen. The concept is HTTPS-ready and responsive.

  • Modern code instead of an .asp template

    The site is built on 18 layout tables, <font> tags and Flash leftovers. The concept rebuilds the same content as semantic HTML.

  • Practice first, not a weather widget

    The homepage sidebar carries a weather forecast and links to CNN and Google. Here a visitor sees practice areas, credentials and the phone number first.

  • Structured data and one contact path

    No schema markup exists today, and the site lists two different email addresses. The concept adds LegalService, Person and FAQPage JSON-LD and one consistent contact block.

About the practice

The people behind the practice

Meet the attorney.

Joshua D. West’s background, as published on the current site.

  • Joshua D. WestAttorney and Counselor at Law

    A lifelong resident of Oakland County and a graduate of the University of Michigan (B.A. in Political Science, minor in Sociology) and Wayne State University Law School (J.D.). He is also a graduate of Dale Carnegie training and of a fellowship in political leadership from Michigan State University.

    He served as judicial law clerk for Chief Judge William E. Bolle of the 52nd District Court, where he mediated cases and advised the judge. A former small business owner and sales manager for the Tandy Corporation, he started his private practice in Bloomfield Hills and has handled cases throughout Michigan. He teaches for the Institute of Continuing Legal Education and has been a guest lecturer at Walsh College.

    He serves as President and Chairman of the Board of the Northfield Hills Condominium Association and has coached and taught swimming in the Troy School District for over fourteen years. Member of the Michigan State Bar, the Oakland County Bar and the University of Michigan Alumni Association.
