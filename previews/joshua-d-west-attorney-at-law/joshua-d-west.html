Attorney

Joshua D. West

Attorney and Counselor at Law · Joshua D. West, PLC, Bloomfield Hills

Joshua D. West is a lifelong resident of Oakland County. He is a graduate of both the University of Michigan and Wayne State University Law School.

Education

He holds a Bachelor of Arts in Political Science, with a minor in Sociology, from the University of Michigan, and a Juris Doctor from Wayne State University Law School. Mr. West is also a graduate of Dale Carnegie training, and a fellowship graduate in political leadership from Michigan State University.

Judicial clerkship

Mr. West was a judicial law clerk for Chief Judge William E. Bolle of the 52nd District Court. During his tenure as clerk, he not only mediated cases but also consulted with and advised the judge, while handling communications within the office.

Practice

A former small business owner and sales manager for the Tandy Corporation, Mr. West started a private law practice in Bloomfield Hills. His areas of practice include criminal defense (both felonies and misdemeanors), all traffic matters, divorce, child custody and support, business law, commercial and residential real estate, contract and corporate law. He has handled cases throughout the State of Michigan.

Teaching

Mr. West teaches for the Institute of Continuing Legal Education and has been a guest lecturer at Walsh College.

In the community

He is active within his community. He is a former judge of the Miss Clawson pageant and has served as treasurer and vice-chairman of the Royal Oak Missionary Church. He serves as President and Chairman of the Board of the Northfield Hills Condominium Association, and has coached and taught swimming within the Troy School District for over fourteen years. Mr. West is a former candidate for County Commissioner and State Representative.
