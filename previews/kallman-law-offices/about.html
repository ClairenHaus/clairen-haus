About the office

A personal, small-town law office in downtown Ipswich.

The law office of Richard M. Kallman is dedicated to providing the highest quality legal services to its clients while maintaining a personal, small-town attention to detail.

The practice

Richard M. Kallman is an experienced attorney who takes pride in bringing quality and care to his clients’ legal needs. The office concentrates in commercial and residential real estate, corporate law, zoning and land use, and also handles estate planning and probate.

Its strengths derive from the ability to address a variety of legal issues, a commitment to new technology, and specialized knowledge of complex areas of law.

Rooted in Ipswich

Attorney Kallman managed the Ipswich office of Whipple & King, P.C. from 1990 to 2001 and has long been active in the town’s business community: on the Board of Directors of the Ipswich Business Association, as its President (1994–1996 and 2003), on the Board of the Ipswich Rotary Club, and as a Corporator of the Institution for Savings.
