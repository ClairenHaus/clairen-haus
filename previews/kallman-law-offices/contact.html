Directions

Finding Ipswich River Place

The office is in downtown Ipswich next to the historic Choate Bridge. In every case, go to the rear of the building and enter through the rear entrance; Attorney Kallman is on the third floor, Suite 11.

From Route 95

Take the exit for Route 133 East (toward Rowley) and follow 133 East until it merges with Route 1A. Turn right onto Route 1A South / 133 East and follow it into Ipswich, where Route 1A becomes Central Street. At the intersection with Market Street (monument on the left, Choate Bridge Pub on the far right corner), go straight and cross the Choate Bridge. At the bottom of the bridge, turn left into the driveway at Ipswich River Place.

From Route 1 via Ipswich Road

Ipswich Road crosses Route 1 just north of Route 97. Head east on Ipswich Road for about 6 miles, past Bradley Palmer State Park and Mill Road. Just past the Ipswich Commuter Rail station it becomes Market Street. Stay on Market Street to Route 1A (Central Street), turn right and cross the Choate Bridge. At the bottom of the bridge, turn left into the driveway at Ipswich River Place.

From the south via Route 128 and Route 1A

Follow Route 128 North toward Gloucester and take the exit onto Route 1A North (toward Hamilton). Stay on 1A through Wenham and Hamilton into Ipswich, past the turnoff to Essex via Route 133. After a few blocks, Route 1A bends sharply left and continues past the Town Hall and a gas station on the right. Turn right into Ipswich River Place just past the gas station. If you cross the Choate Bridge, you have gone too far.
