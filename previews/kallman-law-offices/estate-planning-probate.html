Wills, trusts and the administration of estates.

Planning ahead for your family and property, and help for the person who has to settle an estate.

Planning ahead

A will says who receives your property and who will be in charge of settling your estate. A trust can hold property for the people you choose and may allow some assets to pass without going through probate court. For many people, real estate is the largest asset in the plan.

Settling an estate

When someone dies, the person named to settle the estate gathers the assets, pays valid debts and expenses, and distributes what remains. In Massachusetts, estates are generally handled through the Probate and Family Court.
