Frequently asked questions

Answers about the office, its work and how to reach it.

Short answers drawn from the office’s own information.

Where is the office?

At 4 South Main Street, Suite 11, Ipswich, MA 01938, in Ipswich River Place on the Ipswich River, next to the historic Choate Bridge in downtown Ipswich. Enter through the rear entrance of the building; Attorney Kallman is on the third floor, Suite 11.

What areas of law does the office handle?

Residential and commercial real estate, lender representation and title work, zoning and land use, business and corporate law, and estate planning and probate (wills, trusts and estate administration).

Does the office represent lenders as well as buyers and sellers?

Yes. The office represents lending institutions in loan transactions, workouts, foreclosure actions and collections, and represents borrowers and residential and commercial property owners in sales and loan transactions, closings and workouts.

Is the office a title insurance agent?

Yes. The office is an agent for Chicago Title Insurance Company and Old Republic Title Insurance Company, and it conducts title examinations and equity searches.

Can Attorney Kallman help with a zoning or permitting matter in Ipswich?

Yes. The office handles municipal board work and zoning, planning and subdivision law, and Attorney Kallman has handled zoning and land use permitting before Ipswich municipal boards.

Does the office handle landlord and tenant matters?

Yes. The office represents commercial and residential landlords and tenants, including in summary process actions, and prepares lease agreements.

Does the office handle condominium formation and like-kind exchanges?

Yes. Both are listed among the office’s real estate services.

Can the office help me form a business?

Yes. The office plans and forms domestic and foreign businesses, including corporations, limited liability companies, partnerships and business trusts, and handles stock issuance and transfer and asset purchase agreements.

Who will I work with?

Richard M. Kallman, the office’s sole practitioner.

Should I email details of my matter?

E-mail is not a secure form of communication. If you need to speak with the office about a sensitive issue, please call (978) 356-2934.

What are the office’s phone, fax and email?

Phone (978) 356-2934, fax (978) 356-9663, email rich@kallmanlaw.com.

Still have a question?

Call the office or send an email. For a sensitive matter, please call rather than email.
