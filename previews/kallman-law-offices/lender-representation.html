Loan transactions, workouts and title work for lending institutions.

Lending institutions are among the clients the office represents, alongside the title work every closing depends on.

Representing lenders

When a bank or other lender makes a loan secured by real estate, its counsel prepares or reviews the loan documents, confirms title, and closes the loan so the lender’s mortgage is properly recorded. When a loan runs into trouble, counsel helps the lender with a workout, a foreclosure or collection.

How the office helps lenders

  • Representing lending institutions in loan transactions.
  • Loan workouts.
  • Foreclosure actions.
  • Collections.
  • Preparing promissory notes, mortgages and closing documentation.

Title examination and title insurance

Before a property is sold or mortgaged, the title is examined to confirm who owns it and what liens or restrictions affect it. Title insurance then protects the owner or lender against covered defects. The office conducts title examinations and equity searches, prepares title opinions and certifications, and issues title insurance as an agent for:

  • Chicago Title Insurance Company
  • Old Republic Title Insurance Company
