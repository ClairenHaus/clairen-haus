What real estate counsel does

A real estate transaction runs on documents: the offer and the purchase and sale agreement, the loan papers, the deed, the title work and the closing statement. A real estate attorney prepares and reviews those documents, makes sure title is in order, and handles the closing so that ownership and funds change hands correctly.

How the office helps

  • Representing borrowers and residential and commercial property owners in sales and loan transactions, closings and workouts.
  • Representing commercial and residential landlords and tenants, including summary process actions.
  • Preparing purchase and sale agreements, promissory notes, mortgages, lease agreements, title opinions and certifications, title insurance and closing documentation.
  • Conducting title examinations and equity searches.
  • Representing developers and contractors on the subdivision of land and the resale of developed land.
  • Condominium formation.
  • Like-kind exchanges.

Leases and summary process

In Massachusetts, “summary process” is the court procedure a landlord uses to recover possession of a residential or commercial property. The office represents both landlords and tenants in leasing and in summary process actions.

The office is a member of the Real Estate Bar Association of Massachusetts and an agent for Chicago Title Insurance Company and Old Republic Title Insurance Company.
