Richard M. Kallman

Sole practitioner. Experience in commercial and residential real estate, commercial lending, and commercial and corporate law.

Richard M. Kallman practices from his office at Ipswich River Place in downtown Ipswich. The office’s strengths, in its own words, derive from its ability to address a variety of legal issues, its commitment to new technology and its specialized knowledge of complex areas of law.
