What zoning and land use law covers

What can be built on a piece of land, and how it can be used, is governed by local zoning bylaws, subdivision rules and the state laws behind them. Changing a use, dividing a lot or building something new often means applying to a municipal board, such as a zoning board of appeals or a planning board, and presenting the project at a public hearing.

How the office helps

  • Municipal board work.
  • Zoning, planning and subdivision law.
  • Zoning and land use permitting before Ipswich municipal boards.
  • Representing developers and contractors on the subdivision of land and the resale of developed land.
