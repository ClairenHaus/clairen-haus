About the firm

A tradition of providing quality legal services.

Founded in 1913. Based in Hackensack since 1993. Still serving the families and businesses who first walked through the door generations ago.

Our history

Since our founding in Union City, New Jersey in 1913 by John Platoff, the firm earned a reputation as one of the few apolitical, quality firms in Hudson County. We have maintained that reputation as we grew and transitioned to Bergen County in 1993.

It is a history that continues to maintain relationships with second- and third-generation clients and family-owned businesses.

What we hold to

  • Communication with our clients.
  • Commitment to positive results by efficient means.
  • Personal attention to detail. Every case is different.
  • Intense investigation, research and evaluation.
Our legal community

A strong presence, over more than a century.

Public service

Attorneys at the firm have held prominent positions, including first general counsel to the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, trial judges of the Superior Court of New Jersey, a presiding judge of the Appellate Division and counsel to a former New Jersey Governor.

Ethics and pro bono

Many of our past and present attorneys have served on the New Jersey Supreme Court Ethics Committee and have been commended for pro bono and volunteer service.

Shaping the law

Our attorneys serve on the Community Associations Institute NJ Legislative Action Committee and the New Jersey State League of Municipalities Technical Review Committee, and lecture to lawyers, boards and property managers.
