The firm

A tradition of quality legal services, kept since 1913.

Founded in Union City, New Jersey in 1913 by John Platoff, the firm earned a reputation as one of the few apolitical, quality firms in Hudson County, and has kept it since moving to Bergen County in 1993. Our history continues with second- and third-generation clients and family-owned businesses.

Attorneys of the firm have served as first general counsel to the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, as trial judges of the Superior Court of New Jersey, as a presiding judge of the Appellate Division and as counsel to a former New Jersey Governor.
