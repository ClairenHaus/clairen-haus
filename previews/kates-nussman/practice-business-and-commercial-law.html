Business & Commercial Law

General counsel to companies, partnerships, nonprofits and real estate associations across New Jersey.

Our New Jersey business lawyers offer general legal counsel to small companies, large corporations, partnerships, nonprofit organizations and real estate associations throughout the state. That commercial experience, combined with the reputation of a firm that has practiced for more than a century, is what clients rely on.

We are a full-service business and commercial law firm based in Hackensack and serving clients across New Jersey, particularly Bergen, Hudson, Union, Essex, Middlesex and Passaic counties. We have experience in multimillion-dollar, complex real estate transactions and refinancing, and our attorneys focus on the litigation and mediation of commercial disputes.

Common questions

What kinds of businesses does the firm represent?

Small companies, large corporations, partnerships, nonprofit organizations and real estate associations throughout New Jersey, with particular depth in Bergen, Hudson, Union, Essex, Middlesex and Passaic counties.

Does the firm handle both transactions and disputes?

Yes. The practice covers transactional work such as buy-sell agreements, commercial leasing, refinancing and succession planning, as well as the litigation and mediation of commercial disputes.

Can the firm help with a family-owned business transition?

Yes. Generational transfers of business interests (succession planning) are part of the practice, and partner Bruce L. Nussman has long represented family-owned businesses on these transitions.
