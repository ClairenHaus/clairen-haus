Business Litigation

Partner, customer, vendor, union and government disputes, in court or at the table.

Being a business owner is hard enough without disputes with partners, customers, vendors, unions or the government. Those problems are inevitable; when you cannot solve them yourself, you need a lawyer who is experienced, takes the time to understand your business and knows how to solve problems.

Our goal in commercial disputes is to protect a client's overall business interests, looking at the big picture. Sometimes it makes better business sense to settle a claim; at other times a client has to fight. We handle business disputes in state and federal courts, and in arbitration or mediation.

How we can help

  • Breach of contract, written and verbal
  • Corporate and partnership disputes
  • Emergency relief: temporary restraining orders and preliminary injunctions
  • Restrictive covenant litigation and counseling
  • Employment disputes
  • Complex business transaction disputes
  • Trade secret litigation
  • Real estate and land use litigation
  • Intellectual property disputes
  • Insurance claims
  • Business torts

Common questions

Where does the firm litigate business disputes?

In New Jersey state and federal courts, and in arbitration or mediation settings, depending on what best protects the client's overall business interests.

Can the firm obtain emergency relief?

Yes. The practice includes all types of emergency relief, including temporary restraining orders and preliminary injunctions.

Does every dispute go to trial?

No. The firm's stated goal is to look at the big picture; sometimes it makes better business sense to settle, and the firm uses mediation and arbitration where they are the more cost-effective route.
