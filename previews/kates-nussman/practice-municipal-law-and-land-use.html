Municipal Law & Land Use

Zoning, planning, redevelopment and condemnation, for municipalities, boards, developers and neighbors.

The firm represents municipal governing bodies and land use boards in municipal and regional zoning and planning, redevelopment and condemnation. We also represent developers, property owners and environmental groups in the same fields, as well as neighbors opposing harmful development.

Michael B. Kates and the firm have a statewide reputation in municipal and land use law. The firm serves as special counsel to municipalities in Bergen, Hudson and Essex counties and litigates throughout New Jersey. Mr. Kates sits on the Technical Review Committee of the New Jersey State League of Municipalities, is a contributor to New Jersey Zoning and Land Use Administration (Cox & Koenig), and is General Counsel Emeritus to New Jersey Planning Officials.

How we can help

  • Counsel to municipal governing bodies and land use boards
  • Zoning and planning applications and appeals
  • Redevelopment
  • Condemnation
  • Special litigation counsel in development disputes
  • Representation of developers, property owners and objectors
  • Environmental and neighborhood group representation

Common questions

Who does the firm represent in land use matters?

Municipal governing bodies and land use boards, as well as developers, property owners, environmental groups and neighbors in opposition to development.

Where does the firm practice municipal law?

It serves as special counsel to municipalities in Bergen, Hudson and Essex counties and litigates land use matters throughout New Jersey.

Has the firm served as counsel to specific towns?

Yes. Mr. Kates has served as corporation counsel for the City of Hoboken, municipal attorney for the Borough of Fair Lawn, zoning board attorney in Alpine and Planning Board attorney in the City of Englewood, among other roles listed on the firm's site.
