Real Estate Transactions

Purchases, sales, leasing, financing, tax appeals and closings for associations, owners and developers.

Whether you are a community association, homeowner or developer, buying, selling or leasing real estate requires skilled guidance. The law in this area changes constantly; by staying informed, our firm can save clients from expensive litigation down the road.

Founded in 1913, the firm has a long history of results in real estate matters. We are creative negotiators and litigators, and we also offer alternative dispute resolution where it is the less costly and more effective way to settle real estate and community association issues.

How we can help

  • Purchase and sale agreements
  • Contract formulation
  • Environmental issues
  • Commercial leasing and refinancing
  • Real estate financing
  • Real estate tax appeals
  • Closing documents for residential and commercial transactions
  • Lender disputes and mortgage foreclosure
  • Title disputes and claims
  • Condominiums and co-ops
  • Municipal law and land use
  • Short sales

Common questions

Does the firm handle residential closings?

Yes. Preparing closing documents in connection with residential and commercial transactions is part of the practice.

Can the firm appeal my property tax assessment?

Yes. Real estate tax appeals are listed among the firm's real estate services, and partner Joel M. Ellis has represented both municipalities and property owners in tax appeals.

What if a real estate deal turns into a dispute?

The firm litigates lender, title and foreclosure disputes, and offers alternative dispute resolution where it is the less costly route.
