Sexual Harassment & Discrimination

Advocacy for workers facing hostile work environments, and policy counsel for employers.

Every employee should expect a safe and respectful work environment. Some employers allow conduct that violates workers' legal rights: physical touching, inappropriate comments or jokes, excessive yelling, less desirable job assignments or other offensive conduct.

Our lawyers represent individual workers and businesses dealing with real and potential hostile work environments and on-the-job discrimination. That includes analyzing whether legal action, or preventive action, makes sense, and whether filing a lawsuit, settling or another path is the best choice. Every person and situation is different.

How we can help

  • Hostile work environment claims
  • Sexual harassment claims
  • Sex, gender and other discrimination claims
  • Negotiated settlements
  • Workplace policy counsel for employers

Common questions

What is a hostile work environment claim?

A hostile work environment is a form of unlawful discrimination that can arise from harassment based on a protected characteristic such as gender. Whether particular conduct qualifies depends on the facts; this is a general description, not legal advice.

Does the firm only represent employees?

No. The firm represents individual workers and businesses, including advising employers on sexual harassment and anti-discrimination policies.

Does every case go to court?

No. The firm's site describes weighing whether a lawsuit, a settlement or another option is the best choice for each person and situation.
