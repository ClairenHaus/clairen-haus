About the firm

A Freeland family law practice founded in October 2013, built on confidentiality and communication.

A full service family law practice

Katie Marie Law, PC is a full service family law practice, providing expertise in the complex matters of the law that affect you and your family. The firm is dedicated to giving you the information and communication you need to navigate a complicated legal system.

The firm will work diligently to ensure every case is handled with the utmost care, compassion and communication, and to provide the best and quickest resolution to your case.

In the community

Rooted in Freeland

Katie has been a member of the Freeland Chamber of Commerce since 2014, an active participant in Saginaw County Law Day, and served as President of the Saginaw County Bar Association for 2019–2020.
