Adoption & Stepparent Adoption

Michigan adoptions, including stepparent adoption, handled as smoothly and transparently as possible.

From the firm: KatieMarieLaw has an extensive relationship with Juvenile and Family Court and our firm is dedicated to helping through Adoption proceedings as smoothly and transparently as we are able. KatieMarieLaw has performed MI adoptions for many years.

Stepparent adoption

A stepparent adoption lets a stepparent become the legal parent of a spouse’s child. Because a child can have only two legal parents, the other birth parent’s rights must end first, either because that parent consents or because the court terminates those rights under Michigan’s adoption code.

What the process involves

  • Filing a petition for adoption with the court
  • Consent or termination of the other birth parent’s rights
  • Any investigation or report the court requires
  • A final hearing and an order of adoption

Why it matters

A completed adoption gives the adoptive parent the same rights and responsibilities as any other legal parent, and a new birth certificate can be issued.
