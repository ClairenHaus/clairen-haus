Child Support

Family law where minor children are involved is the heart of this practice.

From the firm: KatieMarieLaw specializes in Family Law, especially Family Law where there are minor children involved. Our Firm has extensive training and expertise in Child Custody cases throughout Mid Michigan and we are up-to-date on the latest laws and rulings that affect our clients.

How support is calculated

Michigan sets child support using the Michigan Child Support Formula. The formula looks mainly at each parent’s income, the number of children, and how many overnights the children spend with each parent, and it also accounts for costs such as health insurance and child care. A court can depart from the formula only in limited circumstances and must explain why.

The Friend of the Court

In most Michigan cases, the county Friend of the Court office helps administer support: it can recommend amounts, keep payment records and enforce orders when payments are missed.

Reviewing or changing support

Support is not fixed forever. When incomes, parenting time or a child’s needs change, either parent can ask for a review or file a motion to modify the order.
