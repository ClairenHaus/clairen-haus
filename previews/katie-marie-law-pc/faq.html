FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the firm, the office and how to get started.

What kinds of cases does Katie Marie Law handle?

The firm is a family law practice. Its practice areas are divorce (with or without children), custody and parenting time, child support, adoption including stepparent adoption, guardianship, conservators and powers of attorney, and juvenile law, plus trial and litigation in those cases.

Where is the office?

The office is at 5798 Midland Road, Freeland, MI 48623, just west of the Freeland SportZone and MidMichigan Health.

How do I schedule a consultation?

Call the office at (989) 573-8330 or email kmlaw@katiemarielaw.com to schedule a consultation and discuss your specifics.

Who will I be working with?

Attorney Katherine M. Baluha, the firm’s principal and owner, and paralegal Bridgette Remainder, who has been with the firm since July 2016.

Does Katie handle cases that go to trial?

Yes. Trial and litigation is part of the practice, and Katie has been admitted to practice in the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan since 2015.

Is Katie a mediator?

Yes. Katie is a Certified Domestic Relations Mediator.

Does the firm handle stepparent adoptions?

Yes. Stepparent adoption is one of the firm’s listed services, and the firm has performed Michigan adoptions for many years.

Can I get a document notarized at the office?

Yes. The firm offers free notarization of documents. Stop by any time during business hours; no appointment is needed.

What forms of payment does the firm accept?

The firm accepts Mastercard, Visa, American Express and Discover.

Is the firm hiring?

The firm is always interested in hearing from exceptional professionals. Send a résumé to Attn: HR, Katie Marie Law, PC, 5798 Midland Road, Freeland, MI 48623, or email kmlaw@katiemarielaw.com.
