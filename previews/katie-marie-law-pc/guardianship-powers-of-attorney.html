Guardianship, Conservators & Powers of Attorney

Legal tools for caring for a child or adult who needs help, and for planning ahead.

From the firm: alongside custody cases, Katie Marie Law also specializes in guardianship, conservators and powers of attorney.

Guardianship

A guardian is appointed by the probate court to care for a minor child whose parents cannot, or for an adult who can no longer make personal decisions on their own. A guardian handles decisions such as where the person lives and the care they receive.

Conservators

A conservator is appointed to manage money and property for someone who cannot manage it themselves. A conservator reports to the court on how the person’s assets are handled.

Powers of attorney

A power of attorney is a document you sign while you are able, naming someone you trust to act for you. In Michigan, a durable power of attorney can cover financial matters, and a patient advocate designation covers medical decisions if you cannot make them. Planning ahead this way can avoid the need for a court-appointed guardian or conservator later.
