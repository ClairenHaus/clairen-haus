Meet Katherine M. Baluha

Katie grew up in Freeland, graduating from Freeland High School before earning a B.A. in English at Michigan State University and her law degree at Thomas M. Cooley Law School. She founded Katie Marie Law, PC in October 2013.

  • Certificate in Family Law from the Institute of Continuing Legal Education and the Family Law Section of the State Bar of Michigan (March 2018)
  • Certified Domestic Relations Mediator
  • Past President of the Saginaw County Bar Association (2019–2020)
  • Admitted to the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Michigan (2015)
