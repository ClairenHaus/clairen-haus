Juvenile Law

Matters heard in the juvenile side of the family court.

From the firm: juvenile law is one of the firm’s listed practice areas, and Katie held a contract as a court-appointed attorney for the Saginaw County Juvenile Court from 2017 to 2018.

What juvenile court handles

In Michigan, juvenile cases are heard in the family division of the circuit court. They generally fall into two groups:

  • Delinquency cases, when a young person is accused of breaking the law
  • Child protective proceedings, when the state is concerned about a child’s care at home

Why experience with the court matters

Juvenile proceedings have their own rules and their own focus on the child’s welfare. Katie’s time as a court-appointed attorney for the Saginaw County Juvenile Court gives her direct experience of how these cases run.
