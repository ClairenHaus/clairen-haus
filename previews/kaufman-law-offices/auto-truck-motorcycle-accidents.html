Auto, Truck & Motorcycle Accidents

Injuries from car, truck and motorcycle crashes on Ohio roads.

After a crash

A collision can leave you with medical bills, time off work, a damaged vehicle and an insurance company asking questions before you have had time to think. Kaufman Law Offices handles injury claims that come out of automobile, truck and motorcycle accidents.

How these cases generally work in Ohio

Ohio is an at-fault state: the driver who caused a crash, and that driver’s insurer, are generally responsible for the harm it caused. A claim usually looks at who was at fault, how badly you were hurt, and what the injury has cost you and will cost you, including medical care, lost income, and pain and suffering.

Ohio uses a comparative fault rule. If you were partly at fault, your recovery can be reduced by your share of the blame, and it is barred if you were more at fault than the other side combined.

Truck accidents

Crashes involving commercial trucks can involve more than one responsible party, such as the driver, the trucking company or the company that loaded the cargo, along with federal safety rules on driving hours and vehicle maintenance. Records that matter can be lost if no one asks for them early.

Motorcycle accidents

Riders are often hurt more seriously than drivers in the same crash, and they sometimes face assumptions about how they were riding. A careful look at the facts of the collision matters.

Time limits

Ohio generally allows two years from the date of an injury to file a personal injury lawsuit. Some situations have shorter deadlines or exceptions, so it is wise to talk with a lawyer well before then.

What to bring to a first meeting

  • The crash report, if you have it, and the other driver’s insurance information
  • Photos of the scene, the vehicles and your injuries
  • Medical records and bills, and anything from the insurance companies
  • Names and phone numbers of witnesses

This page is general information about Ohio law, not legal advice. Every situation is different, and deadlines can have exceptions.
