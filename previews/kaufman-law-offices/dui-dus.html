DUI & DUS

Defense for drunk-driving (OVI) and driving-under-suspension charges.

Charged with DUI or driving under suspension

A drunk-driving or driving-under-suspension charge can put your license, your job and your record at risk. Kaufman Law Offices defends people charged with DUI and DUS.

DUI in Ohio is called OVI

Ohio law calls the offense OVI, operating a vehicle while impaired. It covers driving under the influence of alcohol or drugs, and driving with a blood, breath or urine level over the legal limit. Penalties generally increase with prior offenses within a set period and with higher test results.

Refusing a chemical test, or testing over the limit, usually leads to an administrative license suspension that starts right away, separate from the criminal case. There are limited driving privileges in some cases.

Driving under suspension (DUS)

Driving while your license is suspended is its own offense, and the consequences depend on why the license was suspended. Many people do not realize their license was suspended, for example over an unpaid fine or an insurance problem.

What a defense looks at

  • Whether the stop and the arrest were lawful
  • How field sobriety and chemical tests were given and recorded
  • Your driving record and the status of your license
  • Options for limited driving privileges

Bring your citation and any paperwork you were given to your first meeting.
