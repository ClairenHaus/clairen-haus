Elder Law

Planning and legal help for older adults and their families.

Planning for later life

Elder law covers the legal questions that come with aging: who will make decisions if you cannot, how to plan for the cost of long-term care, and how to protect an older family member. Kaufman Law Offices handles elder law matters for clients and their families.

Common elder law topics

  • Powers of attorney: naming someone you trust to handle finances, and a health care power of attorney for medical decisions.
  • Living wills: putting your wishes about end-of-life care in writing.
  • Long-term care planning: understanding how nursing home and in-home care can be paid for, including Medicaid rules.
  • Guardianship: when an adult can no longer manage their own affairs and did not plan ahead, the probate court can appoint a guardian.
  • Wills and estates: making sure property passes the way you want.

Why planning early matters

Most of these tools only work if they are signed while a person can still make their own decisions. Planning ahead usually costs less, and is far easier on a family, than a court process later.
