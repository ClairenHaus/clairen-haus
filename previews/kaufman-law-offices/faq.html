Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the offices, appointments and how to reach Kurt A. Kaufman.

Where are the offices?

The main office is in Eastgate Plaza, 2100 Harding Highway, Lima, OH 45804. The second office is at 215 West High Street, St. Marys, OH 45885, inside Alan Davis Insurance.

Can I walk in to the St. Marys office?

The St. Marys office is open by appointment only. Call (419) 394-8004 to set a time.

Is there a toll-free number?

Yes. You can reach the office toll-free at (800) 852-2371. The Lima office number is (419) 222-1395.

How can I contact the office by email?

Email info@kaufmanlawoffice.com.

Who is the attorney?

Kaufman Law Offices is the law practice of Kurt A. Kaufman, Attorney at Law.

What kinds of cases does the office handle?

Automobile, truck and motorcycle accidents; wrongful death; injuries by products; malpractice; elder law; wills, estates, powers of attorney and trusts; workers’ compensation; DUI and DUS; and divorce and dissolution.
