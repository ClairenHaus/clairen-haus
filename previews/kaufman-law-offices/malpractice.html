Malpractice

Claims when a professional’s negligence causes harm.

When a professional falls short

Doctors, hospitals and other professionals are expected to meet the standard of care of their field. When a professional’s negligence causes an injury, the person harmed may have a malpractice claim. Kaufman Law Offices handles malpractice matters.

How medical malpractice claims generally work in Ohio

A medical claim usually turns on three questions: what a reasonably careful provider would have done, whether the provider failed to do it, and whether that failure caused the injury. Ohio generally requires that a medical malpractice complaint be supported by an affidavit of merit from a qualified medical expert, so these cases depend on a careful review of the medical records.

Time limits are short

Ohio generally allows only one year to bring a medical malpractice claim. In some situations that time can be extended by sending a written notice to the provider before the year runs out, and there are other rules and outer limits. Because the window is so short, it is important to talk with a lawyer promptly.

What helps at a first meeting

  • A timeline of the care you received and the providers involved
  • Any medical records, bills and letters you already have
  • Notes about what you were told before and after the treatment
