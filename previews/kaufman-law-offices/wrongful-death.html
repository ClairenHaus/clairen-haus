Wrongful Death

Claims for families who lost someone because of another’s negligence.

When a death was caused by someone else

When a person dies because of another party’s wrongful act or negligence, such as a crash, a dangerous product or a medical error, Ohio law allows a claim on behalf of the family. Kaufman Law Offices handles wrongful death matters.

How wrongful death claims generally work in Ohio

In Ohio, a wrongful death action is brought by the personal representative of the person’s estate, for the benefit of the surviving spouse, children, parents and certain other next of kin. Because the estate is involved, these cases often run alongside the probate court.

A claim may seek compensation for losses such as the support and services the person would have provided, loss of companionship and guidance, the family’s mental anguish, and funeral and burial costs. A related claim, called a survival action, can cover the person’s own injuries and suffering before death.

Time limits

Ohio generally requires a wrongful death action to be filed within two years of the date of death, with some exceptions. Families are usually grieving during that time, which is one reason to get advice early.
