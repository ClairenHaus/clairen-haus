About us

Our business

A Lake of the Ozarks business since October 1986.

Since October 1986

K&T Seamless Guttering and Siding is located in Sunrise Beach, Missouri. We started in business in October 1986, and have been installing gutters, soffit, fascia, siding and underdecking throughout the Lake of the Ozarks area ever since.

K&T Morris Enterprises, Inc., d/b/a K&T Guttering and Siding, installs and re-installs seamless aluminum guttering, vinyl siding, soffit and fascia, and underdecking. We started in the snow removal business in 1987, too.

Who we are

Running a small business isn’t just about gutters and siding. It’s about people, trust and community. Every job we do helps support our family, our employees’ families, and lets us give back right here at the Lake.

Questions? Call Paul and the K&T crew at (573) 374-8726.
