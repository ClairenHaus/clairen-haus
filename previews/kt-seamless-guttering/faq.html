FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about gutters, siding, underdecking and more.

What services do you offer?

We install and re-install seamless aluminum guttering, leaf guard, vinyl siding, soffit and fascia, and underdecking. We also provide snow removal for parking lots, driveways and subdivision roads.

Where do you work?

We are based off Highway 5 in Sunrise Beach, Missouri, serving the entire Lake of the Ozarks area: Sunrise Beach, Camdenton, Osage Beach, Lake Ozark, Laurie, Versailles, Eldon and Stover.

Are estimates free?

Yes. Estimates are free. Call (573) 374-8726 or use the contact form.

Are your gutters really seamless?

Yes. The gutter is usually made at your location, cut to the size needed to fit.

What sizes and colors do your gutters come in?

Gutter is available in both 5" and 6" to handle the water flow, in several colors with baked-on enamel that will not peel or rust.

Do you test the gutters?

Yes. Each gutter run is water tested to make sure the water runs properly.

What kind of leaf guard do you install?

Our leaf guard is available in aluminum or black coated. It comes in 3-foot sections and is screwed to the gutter to hold it in place.

What is underdecking?

If you have a deck above and a deck or patio below that doesn’t stay dry, we install metal sheeting below the upper deck, with gutter for drainage, so the lower level becomes a dry, usable space.

Does your siding come with a guarantee?

Our vinyl siding comes in several color choices, and the siding material comes with the manufacturer’s guarantee. Soffit and fascia material is also available in aluminum.

Do you plow snow?

Yes. We provide snow removal for parking lots, driveways and subdivision roads, and we’ve been in the snow removal business since 1987.

How long have you been in business?

We started in business in October 1986.

Are you insured?

Yes. We are fully insured, and we offer a satisfaction guarantee.
