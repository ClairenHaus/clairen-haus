Leaf guard for your gutters

Less debris, less clogging and less climbing up a ladder.

Our leaf guard

Available in aluminum or black coated, our leaf guard comes in 3-foot sections and is screwed to the gutter to hold it in place.

You will see lots of advertising for many types of leaf guard. We have found this to be a great product for a good value.

  • Aluminum or black coated
  • 3-foot sections
  • Screwed to the gutter to hold it in place
  • Installed on new or existing gutters

Get ahead of fall

Fall leaves have one plan: move into your gutters. Leaf guard means less debris, less clogging, and less climbing up a ladder.
