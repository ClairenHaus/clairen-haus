About K&T

A Lake business since 1986

We started in business in October 1986, and have been installing gutters, soffit, fascia, siding and underdecking throughout the Lake of the Ozarks area ever since.

We’re a small, family business based off Highway 5 in Sunrise Beach. Every job helps support our family, our employees’ families, and our community.
