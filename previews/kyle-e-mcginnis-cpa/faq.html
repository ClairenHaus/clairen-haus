Questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the firm and its services. Anything else, call the office.

Where is the office?

The main office is at 200 East Pine St., Springfield, IL 62704.

How do I reach the firm?

Call the main number, (217) 753-3377, email shelley@kyleemcginnis.com, or fax (217) 753-8922.

Do you work with individuals or businesses?

Both. The firm offers a wide range of services to individual and business clients, and as a relatively small firm it is able to offer personalized, quality service.

What kinds of tax returns do you prepare?

Returns for individuals, corporations, partnerships, trusts, estates and not-for-profit organizations, prepared with computerized tax preparation and research software.

Can you help if I am being audited or get a letter from a tax agency?

Yes. IRS and state audit representation is one of the services listed in the firm profile, and the firm represents clients before federal and state tax agencies.

Do you offer bookkeeping and payroll?

Yes: monthly bookkeeping and write-up, financial statements, payroll services and payroll tax returns, and sales tax returns.

Can you help with QuickBooks?

Yes. QuickBooks® setup, support and training are part of the firm’s services.

I am starting a business. Can you help?

Yes. The firm lists business startup services, and can help you weigh the pros and cons of each entity type, such as an S corporation, sole proprietorship or limited liability company.

Is the firm peer reviewed?

Yes. Kyle E. McGinnis, CPA received a peer review rating of pass for its accounting and auditing practice for the year ended March 31, 2021 (report dated November 18, 2021).

How do I send documents securely?

Clients can use the firm’s secure online document sharing application, Client File Share, to access and exchange files with a username and password.

What if the service I need isn’t listed?

The list of services is by no means all-inclusive. Please call or email the office and ask about it.
