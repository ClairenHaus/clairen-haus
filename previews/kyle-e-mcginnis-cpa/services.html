Client services

Services for individuals and businesses

Being a relatively small firm, we are able to offer our clients personalized quality service. The list below is by no means all-inclusive, so please ask about any service you don’t see.

Tax services

Tax Preparation

Returns for individuals, corporations, partnerships, trusts, estates and not-for-profits.

Tax Planning

Planning built into your overall business strategy, current year and future years.

IRS & State Audit Representation

Representation before federal and state tax agencies, including audits.

Estate & Trust Tax Preparation

Returns for estates and trusts, with an eye on the orderly transfer of assets.

Payroll Services

Payroll systems, controls and payroll tax returns, prepared on time.

Sales Tax Services

Compiling the information and preparing sales tax returns, efficiently and on time.

Accounting services

Bookkeeping & Financial Statements

Monthly bookkeeping and write-up, and financial statements that are timely and accurate.

QuickBooks® Setup, Support & Training

Getting QuickBooks set up right, and help using it.

Auditing & Accounting

An accounting and auditing practice with a 2021 peer review rating of pass.

Consulting services

Business Entity Selection & Startup

Choosing the right form of business, S corporation, sole proprietor or LLC, from the start.

Business Succession Planning

A sound plan for handing your business to your successors.

Estate Planning

The orderly transfer of assets, security for your spouse, continuity for your business.

Financial Planning

A plan for each stage of life, built around your goals.

Retirement Planning

Financial independence in your working years and through retirement.
