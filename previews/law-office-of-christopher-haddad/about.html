About the firm

Founded in 1999 and located in West Palm Beach, the firm provides aggressive representation in criminal defense and civil rights cases.

A champion of the underdog

Christopher A. Haddad’s practice focuses on criminal defense, civil rights litigation, DUI and juvenile law. The phrase “champion of the underdog” best describes his beliefs and values.

The firm is committed to the belief that the American justice system is the finest in the world and remains a model for other nations to follow. It values its clients’ rights and is passionate about the pursuit of justice.

The firm seeks to know each client well, so that it can present the client’s case aggressively and in a sincere voice. If the firm takes your case, it will work with you toward a favorable result.

What every client can expect

  • Personal attention, detailed case analysis and persuasive advocacy in every case the firm takes on
  • A thorough investigation, with experts and trial consultants where they help tell your side
  • A frank and honest assessment of your case and your options

Supporting the wrongfully convicted

The firm is a proud sponsor of the Innocence Project of Florida, which works to free the wrongfully convicted, helps exonerees rebuild their lives, and seeks reforms that prevent future wrongful convictions.

Federal and state courts

From the Palm Beach County courthouse to the federal courts, the firm defends people charged with federal and Florida crimes and represents people whose civil rights were violated by police. Mr. Haddad is admitted in the Eleventh Circuit and the Southern and Middle Districts of Florida, and the firm handles federal cases nationwide.
