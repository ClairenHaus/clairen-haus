Attorney profile

Christopher A. Haddad

Founding attorney of The Law Office of Christopher A. Haddad, P.A., practicing law in Palm Beach County for over 30 years.

West Palm Beach criminal defense attorney

Christopher Haddad was born in New York City and raised in New Jersey. He has practiced law in Palm Beach County for over 30 years, and has said he feels privileged to live and practice in such a beautiful and diverse part of the country.

Before founding his own firm in 1999, he was an assistant public defender in Palm Beach County, where he handled everything from misdemeanors to first-degree murder and gained trial and appellate experience. He has tried over 100 jury trials to verdict, has defended more than 100 DUI charges since 1990, and has tried over 50 cases in juvenile court. Over the years he has built a diverse network of lawyers, trial consultants and other experts who can help strengthen a client’s case.

His core belief in justice for all

Mr. Haddad was a young child in the 1960s, an era that included the Kennedy presidency, the founding of the Peace Corps, Dr. Martin Luther King Jr.’s “I Have a Dream” speech, the Civil Rights Act of 1964 and the first moon landing. Too young then to grasp their significance, he has come to see how those events shaped what he stands for.

“I believe fundamentally in the importance of equality and fairness for all members of society.”

He has come to value the fundamental rights built into the American legal system, such as the right to a fair trial and to legal counsel, and the Miranda rule. In his own words, he will use his legal skills, knowledge and decades of courtroom experience to do everything in his power to make sure each client is treated fairly and receives the best representation possible.
