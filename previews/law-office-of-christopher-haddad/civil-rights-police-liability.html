Civil rights and police civil liability

False arrest, excessive force, discrimination and misuse of weapons by police, under federal and Florida law.

When police cross the line

The Civil Rights Act of 1964 transformed the country, and the progress made since is undeniable. The firm is here to fight for and protect your civil rights, and concentrates in police civil liability.

The firm recognizes the important role police play, and the risks and responsibilities that come with the job. No one is expected to be perfect, but everyone is expected to do the job with respect for the individual rights we all share. When police arrest someone without a legal basis, or act from motives beyond their duties, they should be held accountable.

Cases the firm handles

  • False arrest, including arrest without probable cause
  • Excessive or unreasonable use of force
  • Discrimination
  • Liability for misuse of weapons
  • Physical and emotional injuries caused by abuse of power or a failure to use care

Federal and Florida remedies

Federal and state laws provide remedies for people whose rights were violated by law enforcement. Where the facts show an agency acted with deliberate indifference to a person’s rights, a federal cause of action may exist. Other cases involve more traditional claims such as false arrest or unreasonable force in making an arrest. As with any area of law, each case needs a careful look at its merits.

Mr. Haddad has tried these cases and obtained monetary judgments for clients for wrongful police conduct involving excessive force and false arrest, and has negotiated monetary settlements with police agencies.
