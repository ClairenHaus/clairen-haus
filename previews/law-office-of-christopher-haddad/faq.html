Frequently asked questions

Straight answers drawn from the firm’s own information. For advice about your situation, call the office.

Where is the Law Office of Christopher A. Haddad located?

The office is at 319 Clematis Street, Suite 812, West Palm Beach, Florida 33401. The phone number is 561-832-1126 and the email is chris@chrishaddad.com.

What kinds of cases does the firm handle?

Federal criminal law, Florida state criminal law, DUI, juvenile law, and civil rights cases involving police misconduct, such as false arrest, excessive use of force, discrimination and misuse of a weapon.

Does the firm take federal cases outside Florida?

Yes. Mr. Haddad is admitted to the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit and the U.S. District Courts for the Southern and Middle Districts of Florida, has been admitted in other U.S. District Courts, and the firm handles federal cases nationwide.

How much trial experience does Christopher Haddad have?

He has tried over 100 jury trials to verdict, has handled state criminal cases since 1990, has defended more than 100 DUI charges since 1990, and has tried over 50 cases in juvenile court.

What was his background before founding the firm?

Before founding the firm in 1999, Mr. Haddad was an assistant public defender in Palm Beach County, where he handled cases ranging from simple misdemeanors to first-degree murder and gained trial and appellate experience.

When was the firm founded?

The Law Office of Christopher A. Haddad, P.A. was founded in 1999 in West Palm Beach.

Is a DUI in Florida a misdemeanor or a felony?

Under Florida law a DUI is usually a misdemeanor, but repeat offenders can face felony charges. Every case depends on its facts, so call the office to discuss yours.

Can the firm represent my child if the case was filed in adult court?

Yes. Mr. Haddad has handled hundreds of juvenile cases, both in juvenile court and in adult court where a young person’s case has been direct filed.

What can I do if police falsely arrested me or used excessive force?

Federal and Florida law provide remedies for people whose rights were violated by law enforcement. Mr. Haddad has obtained jury verdicts and judgments for clients in excessive force and false arrest cases and has negotiated settlements with police agencies. Each case needs a careful look at its merits, so call to arrange a consultation.

Where did Christopher Haddad go to school?

He earned his J.D. from Loyola University, New Orleans, in 1990 and his undergraduate degree from Bentley College in Boston in 1987.

Is Mr. Haddad board certified in criminal law?

The firm’s own disclaimer states that, unless otherwise indicated, Florida attorneys are not certified by the Florida Board of Legal Specialization in the areas of practice listed.

Does contacting the firm make me a client?

No. Emailing or contacting the firm through the website does not by itself create an attorney-client relationship, and information sent before the firm represents you may not be treated as privileged or confidential.
