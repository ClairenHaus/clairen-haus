Federal criminal defense

From pre-indictment investigation through trial or sentencing, a careful defense in federal court, in Florida and nationwide.

Defending federal charges takes a methodical approach

Federal criminal law involves the defense of people accused of violating a law of the United States. These cases often begin long before anyone is charged, and a carefully devised, methodical defense is essential.

How the firm approaches a federal case

  1. Before an indictmentFederal cases often involve intense pre-indictment investigation. The firm works proactively, whenever possible, to gain information and organize the defense early.
  2. Release before trialIf charges are filed, the firm works to secure your release under the Bail Reform Act where possible. The district court has many alternatives to consider when deciding on pretrial release, and the firm explores all of them.
  3. Grand jury testimonyIf you are expected to testify before a grand jury, you will be prepared so your account is presented truthfully and in a positive light.
  4. DiscoveryThe firm uses the rules of discovery to obtain the evidence the government intends to use, so the defense can be built to refute the allegations.
  5. Challenging illegal searchesWhere appropriate, the firm files challenges to illegal searches and seizures, covering physical items such as drugs, firearms or papers, and in some cases statements that were illegally obtained.
  6. Trial or pleaOne of the most important decisions in a federal prosecution is whether to go to trial or accept a plea agreement. The firm’s systematic approach, with Mr. Haddad’s counsel, helps you make an informed choice.

In building a defense, the firm also addresses any mental health issues or other evidence that may help exonerate you.

Admitted in federal court

Mr. Haddad is admitted to the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit and the U.S. District Courts for the Southern and Middle Districts of Florida. He has also been admitted in other U.S. District Courts and has handled cases in districts throughout the country, and the firm can handle federal cases nationwide.

Representative federal cases

Federal drug charges

A client originally facing a five-year mandatory minimum for trafficking in over 300 marijuana plants received a sentence of one year and one day under a plea agreement to reduced charges.

Possession of cocaine with intent to distribute

After a traffic stop, a trooper searched the client’s vehicle and found two kilos of cocaine in a rear panel. Mr. Haddad argued a motion to suppress all evidence on Fourth Amendment grounds.

All charges dismissed

Conspiracy to distribute hashish

A client charged with conspiracy to import hash from the Middle East. Mr. Haddad filed a motion to dismiss based on a Speedy Trial violation under the U.S. Constitution.

Case dismissed

Conspiracy to distribute marijuana

A client with no prior record faced a mandatory minimum prison sentence. Mr. Haddad negotiated the dismissal of several counts and persuaded the court to impose probation with home confinement.

Conspiracy to distribute cocaine

Mr. Haddad persuaded the court, over the government’s objection, to apply the safety valve provision of the Federal Sentencing Guidelines, reducing the sentence.

Cultivation of marijuana

A client charged with operating a grow house with more than 100 plants received home confinement instead of incarceration.
