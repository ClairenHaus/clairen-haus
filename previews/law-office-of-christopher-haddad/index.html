What changes

A site that works on the phone people are holding when they need a lawyer.

Two specific things the current site gets in the way of, and what this concept does about them. The firm's own words stay; the way they are delivered changes.

  • Today: no mobile layout
    Built for the phone first

    The current site has no viewport tag, so on a phone it shows a shrunken desktop page, and Google indexes sites by their mobile version. This concept is a single responsive page that reads cleanly at 390 pixels wide, with the phone number one tap away.

  • Today: no structured data, no FAQ
    Answers machines can read

    The current site has no schema markup, three H1 headings and no alt text on its images. This page carries LegalService, Person and FAQPage structured data and a short FAQ answered from the firm's own pages, so search engines and AI assistants can confirm who the firm is, where it is and what it does.

Where is the Law Office of Christopher A. Haddad located?

The office is at 319 Clematis Street, Suite 812, West Palm Beach, Florida 33401. The telephone number is 561-832-1126 and the fax is 561-418-6862.

What kinds of cases does the firm handle?

Federal and Florida state criminal law, DUI, juvenile law, and civil rights violations involving police misconduct, including false arrest, excessive use of force and misuse of a weapon.

Does the firm take federal cases outside Florida?

Mr. Haddad is admitted to the 11th Circuit Court of Appeals and the U.S. District Courts for the Southern and Middle Districts of Florida, has been admitted in other U.S. District Courts, and the firm states it can handle federal cases nationwide.

How much trial experience does Christopher Haddad have?

The firm states that Mr. Haddad has tried over 100 jury trials to verdict, has handled state criminal cases since 1990, and has defended more than 100 DUI charges since 1990.

When was the firm founded?

The Law Office of Christopher A. Haddad, P.A. was founded in 1999 and is located in West Palm Beach.

About the practice

The information clients need before they call.

Loyola University, New Orleans, 1990 · Bentley College, 1987

Founded 1999 · 319 Clematis Street, Suite 812, West Palm Beach

Christopher A. Haddad's practice focuses on criminal defense, civil rights litigation, DUIs and juvenile law. The phrase "champion of the underdog" is how the firm describes his beliefs and values, and the firm states a commitment to the view that the American justice system is the finest in the world.

Each case is approached from the perspective that a rigorous pursuit of the facts and understanding of the law are the essential components of a strong defense. The firm combines detailed case investigation with the use of experts and trial consultants, and describes a network of lawyers, trial consultants and other experts available to strengthen a case.
