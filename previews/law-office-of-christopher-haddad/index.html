“The best defense is a good offense.”

How Christopher A. Haddad sums up his approach to criminal law

A rigorous pursuit of the facts

Each case is approached from the perspective that a rigorous pursuit of the facts and an understanding of the law are the essential components of a strong defense.

The firm combines a thorough, detailed case investigation with the use of experts and other trial consultants to convey the essential message of your case and refute the other side’s arguments.

  • Personal attention to every client
  • Detailed case analysis
  • Persuasive advocacy, in and out of the courtroom
Trial experience

A cornerstone of our system is the right to a trial by jury

Before founding his own firm in 1999, Mr. Haddad was an assistant public defender in Palm Beach County, where he gained trial and appellate experience on cases ranging from simple misdemeanors to first-degree murder.

He has since tried over 100 jury trials to verdict, defended more than 100 DUI charges, and tried over 50 cases in juvenile court.

Your attorney

Christopher A. Haddad

Born in New York City and raised in New Jersey, Christopher Haddad has practiced law in Palm Beach County for over 30 years. He earned his law degree from Loyola University, New Orleans, and his undergraduate degree from Bentley College in Boston.

He is admitted to the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit and the U.S. District Courts for the Southern and Middle Districts of Florida, and the firm handles federal cases nationwide.

Yes. Mr. Haddad is admitted to the Eleventh Circuit and the Southern and Middle Districts of Florida, has been admitted in other U.S. District Courts, and the firm handles federal cases nationwide.
