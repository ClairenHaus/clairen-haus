About the office

A small, personal practice in Harvard, Massachusetts, focused on estate planning and on planning for every ability.

The practice

The Law Office of Erin McBee, PLLC assists clients with their estate planning needs, including elder and disAbility law. Erin opened the practice in North Andover, Massachusetts, and the office is now located in Harvard.

Since opening the office, Erin has expanded her practice to include elder and disAbility law, including long-term care planning and Medicaid planning. The office also handles probate and trust administration, and a focused set of real estate, small business and family law matters.

Erin is licensed in Massachusetts and Texas and offers virtual, online estate planning for clients in both states.

The “A” in disAbility

“The A is capitalized on purpose to reflect a focus on abilities.”

It is a small detail that says a lot about how Erin approaches her work: plans built around what people can do, and around the support that helps them do it.
