Elder law & disAbility law

Long-term care and Medicaid planning, special needs trusts and ABLE accounts, folded into your estate plan.

Planning ahead for care

If you or a loved one has a disAbility, or needs or will likely need long-term care, Erin can help you build planning for those issues into your estate plan.

Long-term care, whether at home, in assisted living or in a nursing home, can be one of the largest costs a family faces. Planning ahead gives you more choices about how that care is paid for.

Long-term care and Medicaid planning

In Massachusetts, Medicaid is called MassHealth, and it can pay for long-term care for people who meet its eligibility rules. Planning for the cost of care and for Medicaid eligibility may involve tools such as:

  • Irrevocable trusts
  • A life estate in your home

The rules are detailed and change over time, so the right approach depends on your family, your assets and your timing. Erin gained experience with Medicaid applications and long-term care planning working with the Boston elder law firm of Cohen & Oalican, LLP.

Protecting a loved one with a disability

A Special or Supplemental Needs Trust can protect the inheritance of a loved one with a disability, and an ABLE (529A) account is another important part of special needs planning. Read more about special needs planning.

Erin is a member of the National Academy of Elder Law Attorneys (NAELA) and its Massachusetts chapter.
