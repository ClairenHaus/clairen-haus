Why plan now

Have your estate plan drawn up sooner rather than later

An estate plan can give you peace of mind, knowing that everything and everyone is taken care of. With a durable power of attorney and a health care proxy you can make your wishes known, and keep some control over your finances and health care if you ever become incapacitated.

If you have minor children, or adult children who still need your care, you can name a guardian for them in a will and set up a trust to control how their inheritance is paid out. Careful planning can also reduce probate costs and estate taxes.
