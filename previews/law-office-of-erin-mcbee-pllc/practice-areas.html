Practice areas

Estate planning is the heart of the practice, along with elder and disAbility law, estate and trust administration, and a focused set of real estate, business and family matters.

Estate Planning

Wills, powers of attorney, health care proxies, living wills and trusts, planned together so your wishes are clear.

Elder Law & DisAbility Law

Planning for long-term care, its cost and Medicaid (MassHealth) eligibility, built into your estate plan.
