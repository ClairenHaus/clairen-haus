Probate, estate & trust administration

Help with the paperwork, organization and distribution of a loved one’s estate, whether they had a will, a trust or no plan at all.

When someone you love has died

“Settling an estate and administering a trust can be an emotional and overwhelming process.”

Erin’s office can help you with the paperwork, organization and distribution of the assets of your loved one, whether they had a will, a trust or no estate plan at all.

Probate and estate administration

Probate is the court process for settling an estate: confirming who has authority to act, gathering what the person owned, paying debts and taxes, and passing what remains to the people entitled to it. In Massachusetts, estates are handled through the Probate and Family Court.

Trust administration

When a trust is part of the plan, the trustee follows the trust’s terms instead of a court process. Trustees still have real duties, including keeping records, managing assets carefully and distributing them as the trust says. Erin can guide trustees through each step.

If there was no will

When a person dies without a will, state law decides who inherits. Erin can help the family understand what that means and handle the estate from start to finish.
