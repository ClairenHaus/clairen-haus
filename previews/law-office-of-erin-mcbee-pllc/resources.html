Resources

Helpful links & resources

Trusted outside resources Erin recommends for families planning ahead, caring for a loved one, or protecting themselves as consumers.

Estate planning, elder & disAbility law

  • AARP: estate planning

    Articles from AARP on wills, trusts and common estate planning mistakes.

  • Aging Life Care Association

    Formerly the National Association of Professional Geriatric Care Managers. Helps families find care managers and resources for caring for older adults.

  • Family Caregiver Alliance (Caregiver.org)

    Practical information on end-of-life decision making and other issues for people with chronic, disabling conditions and the people who care for them.

  • ABLE National Resource Center

    Information about ABLE (529A) accounts for people with disabilities.

Consumer protection

  • Consumer Financial Protection Bureau

    The federal agency for consumer financial protection, with guides and a complaint portal.

  • Massachusetts Office of Consumer Affairs and Business Regulation

    The Commonwealth’s consumer protection office.

Links to other websites are provided for convenience and should not be considered sponsorship, endorsement or approval of the materials on those sites.
