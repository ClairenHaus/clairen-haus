Practice area

Small business

Help forming your business and reviewing the contracts it runs on.

Getting your business set up

  • Incorporation. Forming a corporation for your business.
  • Limited liability companies (LLCs). A flexible structure that can separate business and personal liability.
  • Partnerships. Setting out how partners share decisions, profits and responsibilities.
  • Review of contracts. A careful read of an agreement before you sign it.

Thinking about what comes next

A business is often a large part of what a family owns. Planning for what happens to it if you retire, become ill or die belongs in your estate plan as well.
