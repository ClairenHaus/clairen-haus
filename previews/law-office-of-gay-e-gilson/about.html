About the firm

A Corpus Christi employment law office representing employees since 1996.

A sole-proprietor practice

Gay E. Gilson is the sole proprietor of the Law Office of Gay E. Gilson, founded in 1996. The office represents employees with employment issues such as discrimination (race, gender, sexual harassment, age, disability, pregnancy), whistleblower, constitutional, retaliation, qui tam and Merit System Protection Board matters, and administrative hearings in Corpus Christi and South Texas.

Courts and agencies

  • State and county courts in Corpus Christi and south and central Texas
  • United States District Courts within the Southern District of Texas
  • Merit System Protection Board, for federal employees
  • Equal Employment Opportunity Commission, for federal employees
  • Office of Special Counsel complaints
