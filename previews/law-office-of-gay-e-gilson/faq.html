Frequently asked questions

Answers drawn from the office’s own information.

Do you offer a free consultation?

Yes. You may call the office for a free telephone consultation at (361) 887-0552. Office consultations are by appointment only.

Can I contact the office by email?

Yes, at gegilson@gilsonlaw.com. If you are an employee, do not use an email associated with your employer or use a work computer to communicate with an attorney about your legal problems.

Who will handle my matter?

Gay E. Gilson is the sole proprietor of the firm, which she founded in 1996.

What kinds of cases does the firm handle?

Examples include discrimination (sex, race, color, national origin, age, disability, pregnancy, religion, genetic information, transgender status and sexual orientation), sexual harassment, constitutional claims, whistleblower claims, qui tam/False Claims Act, federal employment issues, the Family Medical Leave Act, veteran employment issues, wage, hour and benefits, and workers’ compensation retaliation.

Which courts does the firm appear in?

The firm serves clients before state and county courts in Corpus Christi and south and central Texas, and in the United States District Courts located within the Southern District of Texas. Ms. Gilson is also admitted to the Fifth Circuit and Federal Circuit Courts of Appeals.

Do you represent federal employees?

Yes. Ms. Gilson represents federal employees before the Merit System Protection Board and the Equal Employment Opportunity Commission, responds to proposed disciplinary and performance actions, and handles complaints to the Office of Special Counsel.

Is there a deadline to bring a claim?

Legal claims have deadlines and statutes of limitations in which a timely claim must be made or your legal rights will be lost. You should seek the opinion of an attorney about the merits of your potential claim and the deadlines that apply.

Does reading this website make me a client?

No. The information on this website is provided for educational and informational purposes, is not legal advice, and does not create a lawyer-client relationship.

Where is the office?

The Enclave, 5525 S. Staples St., Suite B3, Corpus Christi, Texas 78411. Turn onto Cain Drive from Staples at A’s Cleaners; look for the American flag. It is the last building on the right in The Enclave.
