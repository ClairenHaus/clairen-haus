Practice areas

The office represents employees. Examples of cases handled by the firm include discrimination, sexual harassment, constitutional claims, whistleblower claims, qui tam/False Claims Act, federal employment issues, the Family Medical Leave Act, veteran employment issues, wage, hour and benefits, and workers’ compensation retaliation.
