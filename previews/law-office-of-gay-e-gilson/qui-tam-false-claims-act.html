Qui Tam / False Claims Act

The federal False Claims Act creates liability for fraud on the government and protects employees, contractors and agents who act to stop it.

The federal False Claims Act, 31 U.S.C. § 3729 et seq., provides for liability for damages and a penalty per claim, established by statute, for anyone who knowingly submits or causes the submission of a false or fraudulent claim to the United States. The False Claims Act has many detailed and complex requirements that must be met in order that a claim may be successful.

The False Claims Act includes an anti-retaliation provision, 31 U.S.C.§ 3730(h) which states:

  • (1) In general, - Any employee, contractor, or agent shall be entitled to all relief necessary to make that employee, contractor, or agent whole, if that employee, contractor, or agent is discharged, demoted, suspended, threatened, harassed, or in any other manner discriminated against in the terms and conditions of employment because of lawful acts done by the employee, contractor, or agent on behalf of the employee, contractor, or agent or associated others in furtherance of other efforts to stop 1 or more violations of this subchapter.
