About the firm

About the Office

A Chattanooga law office built around one attorney, his clients, and doing things the right way.

Honor, integrity and representation

The law firm of James L. Henry, Jr. handles every case for the client with the utmost honor, integrity and representation. When you call the office, the attorney you are hiring is the attorney who does the work.

Jim was born and raised in Chattanooga, studied business, accounting and law at the University of Tennessee at Knoxville, and is both an attorney and a CPA. That combination shapes the practice: estate plans, property questions and business matters often turn on the numbers as much as on the law.

What the office does

  • Estate planning & probate: revocable trusts, wills, powers of attorney, probate resolution and conservatorships
  • Real estate law: complex boundary line disputes and real estate in estates and trusts
  • Business law: formation, contracts, startups, collections, joint ventures and nonprofits

A problem-solver’s approach

Jim works every matter the same way: identify the problems and needs, present ideas toward a solution, and act on them until the solution is accomplished. Whether the obstacle is a business opportunity that needs vision, a collection account that needs a creative resolution, an estate dispute that needs mediation, or a 150-year-old boundary line, the goal is the same: to guide you over, around, or through it to a winning solution for you, your business and your family.

Tennessee and Georgia

Jim is licensed in Tennessee. For cases in Georgia, and for most litigation, he collaborates with a Georgia attorney colleague.
