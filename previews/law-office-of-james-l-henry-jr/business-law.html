Practice area

Business Law

Corporation and LLC formation, contracts, startups, collections, joint ventures and nonprofits, from Chattanooga attorney and CPA James L. Henry, Jr.

Business advice from an attorney who is also a CPA

Jim studied business and accounting before law, and he is licensed as both an attorney and a CPA. For more than 33 years he has represented two large material supply companies in Chattanooga, giving them business legal advice and resolving and collecting their customers’ past-due accounts.

Corporation and LLC formation and operation

Choosing the right structure, forming the company, and keeping it running properly afterward: the agreements, the records and the decisions that come up as the business grows.

Contracts, transactional and employment matters

Drafting and reviewing the contracts a business runs on, handling transactions, and employment questions.

Representing startup companies

New companies need a sound legal footing from the first day. Jim represents startups as they get organized and begin doing business.

Collections and litigation

When customers fall behind, Jim works to resolve and collect past-due accounts, with creative resolutions where they make sense. For most litigation, and for cases in Georgia, he collaborates with a Georgia attorney colleague.

Joint ventures and nonprofits

Ventures between businesses, and organizations formed to serve the community, each with their own legal setup.

Questions about business law

Can you help me form a corporation or an LLC?

Yes. Business law work includes corporation and LLC formation and operation, as well as representing startup companies.

Does the office collect past-due accounts?

Yes. Collections and litigation are part of the business practice. Jim has represented two large material supply companies in Chattanooga for more than 33 years, providing business legal advice and resolving and collecting their customers’ past-due accounts.

Do you work with nonprofits and joint ventures?

Yes. Both are listed in the business law practice, along with contracts and transactional and employment matters.
