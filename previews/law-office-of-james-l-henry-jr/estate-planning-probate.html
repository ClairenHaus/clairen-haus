Estate Planning & Probate

Revocable trusts, wills, powers of attorney, probate and conservatorships for Chattanooga families, from attorney and CPA James L. Henry, Jr.

Planning that takes care of your family

A good estate plan does two things: it makes sure your goals are met, and it makes sure your family is cared for. Jim helps clients put the right documents in place and, when a loved one has passed, helps the family settle the estate.

Revocable trusts

A revocable trust holds your property during your life under terms you can change, and names who manages and receives it afterward. Many people use one to keep their affairs private and organized for the people who come after them.

Wills

A will says who receives your property and who you trust to carry out your wishes. It is the foundation of most estate plans, including plans built around a trust.

Healthcare and financial power of attorney

These documents name the people who can make medical decisions and handle your finances if you cannot. Putting them in place ahead of time spares your family uncertainty at a hard moment.

Probate resolution

Probate is the court process for settling a person’s estate. Jim helps families work through it to a resolution, including estates that hold real estate.

Conservatorships

When an adult can no longer manage their own affairs, a conservatorship gives a trusted person the court’s authority to help. Jim handles conservatorship matters as part of this practice.
