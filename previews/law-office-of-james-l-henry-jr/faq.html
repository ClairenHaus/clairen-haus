Frequently asked questions

Questions & Answers

Straight answers about the office and its practice areas, drawn from the firm’s own information.

The practice

What kinds of legal matters does the office handle?

Three areas: estate planning and probate (revocable trusts, wills, healthcare and financial powers of attorney, probate resolution and conservatorships), real estate law (complex boundary line disputes, real estate that is part of a probate estate, and real estate in estate planning and trusts), and business law (corporation and LLC formation and operation, contracts, transactional and employment matters, startup companies, collections and litigation, joint ventures and nonprofits).

Who will handle my matter?

Attorney James L. Henry, Jr. The office is his own practice, and the firm handles every case for the client with the utmost honor, integrity and representation.

Is Jim Henry also an accountant?

Yes. Jim graduated from the University of Tennessee at Knoxville in business (accounting) and law, and he is both an attorney and a CPA.

Can the office help with a matter in Georgia?

Jim is licensed in Tennessee. For cases in Georgia, and for most litigation, he collaborates with a Georgia attorney colleague.

How does Jim approach a legal problem?

With a three-step method he repeats until the problem is solved: identify, present, act upon. He identifies the problems and needs, presents ideas toward a solution, and acts on those ideas until a solution is reached.

Estate planning & probate

What estate planning documents can the office prepare?

Revocable trusts, wills, and healthcare and financial powers of attorney.

Does the office handle probate and conservatorships?

Yes. Probate resolution and conservatorships are part of the estate planning and probate practice.

Can you help when family members disagree about an estate?

Jim lists an estate dispute requiring mediation between the parties among the obstacles he helps clients get over, around or through.

Real estate

Do you handle boundary line disputes?

Yes, including complex boundary line disputes. Jim names a 150-year-old boundary line or surveyor error as one example of the obstacles he works through for clients.

What if the property is part of an estate or a trust?

The real estate practice covers real estate subject to probate and estates, and real estate in estate planning and trusts.

Business

Can you help me form a corporation or an LLC?

Yes. Business law work includes corporation and LLC formation and operation, as well as representing startup companies.

Does the office collect past-due accounts?

Yes. Collections and litigation are part of the business practice. Jim has represented two large material supply companies in Chattanooga for more than 33 years, providing business legal advice and resolving and collecting their customers’ past-due accounts.

Do you work with nonprofits and joint ventures?

Yes. Both are listed in the business law practice, along with contracts and transactional and employment matters.
