Common questions
Where is the office?

At 412 Broad Street, Chattanooga, TN 37402, in downtown Chattanooga. The telephone number is (423) 756-3898 and the fax is (423) 756-3899.

Is James L. Henry, Jr. also a CPA?

Yes. He graduated from the University of Tennessee at Knoxville in business (accounting) and law, and is both an attorney and a CPA.

Can he handle a matter in Georgia?

He is licensed in Tennessee and collaborates with a Georgia attorney colleague for cases in Georgia and for most litigation.

What estate planning documents does he prepare?

Revocable trusts, wills, and healthcare and financial powers of attorney. He also handles probate resolution and conservatorships.

Does he work with businesses and startups?

Yes. His business practice covers corporation and LLC formation and operation, contracts, transactional and employment matters, startup companies, collections and litigation, joint ventures and nonprofits.
