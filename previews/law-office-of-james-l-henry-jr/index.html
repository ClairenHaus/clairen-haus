Common questions

Quick answers.

What kinds of legal matters does the office handle?

Three areas: estate planning and probate (revocable trusts, wills, healthcare and financial powers of attorney, probate resolution and conservatorships), real estate law (complex boundary line disputes, real estate that is part of a probate estate, and real estate in estate planning and trusts), and business law (corporation and LLC formation and operation, contracts, transactional and employment matters, startup companies, collections and litigation, joint ventures and nonprofits).

Is Jim Henry also an accountant?

Yes. Jim graduated from the University of Tennessee at Knoxville in business (accounting) and law, and he is both an attorney and a CPA.

Can the office help with a matter in Georgia?

Jim is licensed in Tennessee. For cases in Georgia, and for most litigation, he collaborates with a Georgia attorney colleague.

Do you handle boundary line disputes?

Yes, including complex boundary line disputes. Jim names a 150-year-old boundary line or surveyor error as one example of the obstacles he works through for clients.
