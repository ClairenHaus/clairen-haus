About Jim

Attorney James L. Henry, Jr. was born and raised in Chattanooga. A graduate of the University of Tennessee at Knoxville in business (accounting) and law, he is both an attorney and a CPA.

Jim is licensed in Tennessee and collaborates with a Georgia attorney colleague for cases in Georgia and for most litigation.

Jim is a visionary leader who loves taking on challenges and discovering creative solutions that nurture his family, enrich his clients, impact his community, and glorify his God. He and his wife, Beverly, have six grown children.

How he practices

Identify, present, act upon; identify, present, act upon.

Jim practices law, in his own words, the way General Patton fought battles. He attacks each approaching scenario with an unyielding three-step advance: identifying problems and needs, presenting ideas toward a solution, and acting on those ideas until a solution is accomplished.

He loves uncharted territory. Where most people see an impenetrable barrier, Jim looks for a clear path through, using positioning and timing to gain the advantage, and when a counterattack comes, working the problem again from another angle.

Experience

  • Represents two large material supply companies in Chattanooga, and has provided them business legal advice and resolved and collected their customers’ past-due accounts for more than 33 years.
  • Wrote Tennessee legislation in the 1980s legalizing home schooling in Tennessee.
  • Wrote Tennessee legislation in the 1980s requiring regulation of Tennessee abortion clinics.
  • Wrote Tennessee legislation regulating and restricting adult book stores in Tennessee.
