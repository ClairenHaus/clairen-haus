Practice area

Real Estate Law

Complex boundary line disputes, real estate in probate and estates, and real estate in estate planning and trusts, in Chattanooga, Tennessee.

Property questions that need careful work

Jim’s real estate practice focuses on the hard cases: lines that were drawn wrong generations ago, and land that is tied up in an estate or a trust.

Complex boundary line disputes

Where one property ends and the next begins can depend on old deeds, old surveys and how the land has actually been used. Jim works through disputes like these, including the kind that trace back to a 150-year-old boundary line or a surveyor’s error.

Real estate subject to probate and estates

When the owner of a home or land passes away, the property usually has to move through the estate before it can be kept, divided or sold. Jim handles the real estate side of that process.

Real estate in estate planning and trusts

Land and homes are often the largest part of an estate. Jim helps clients decide how property should be held and passed on, including property placed in a trust.
