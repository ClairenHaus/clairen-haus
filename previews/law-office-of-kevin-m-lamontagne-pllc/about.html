About the office

A solo practice, built on access

The Law Office of Kevin M. LaMontagne, PLLC has served clients from Boynton Beach since 1991. When you call, you reach the attorney who will handle your matter.

Our history

Palm Beach County roots.

Kevin M. LaMontagne first practiced in Boca Raton and Palm Beach, then established his own office in Boynton Beach in 1991. The practice has stayed local ever since, at 1375 Gateway Boulevard.

The office handles both personal and business matters, with a wide range of transactional and litigation experience: estate plans and trusts, contracts and leases, real estate closings, and the disputes that sometimes follow.
