Common questions

Answers from the firm.

Short answers drawn from the firm's own published information.

Does a living trust automatically avoid probate?

No. According to the firm, a living trust does not automatically avoid probate; if it is not properly structured and funded, probate may still be required. Post-signing planning and guidance matter too.

Do I need a lawyer to buy or sell real estate in Florida?

Florida law does not require a lawyer at the closing table, but the firm considers it prudent to have legal representation for a sale or purchase, whether using a standard FAR/BAR contract or a tailor-made one.

Does the office handle bankruptcy?

Kevin is experienced in Chapter 7 representation and bankruptcy litigation. At present he is limiting his bankruptcy practice to litigated matters.

Can Kevin meet outside the office?

Upon special request he has met clients outside the office, including visits to independent and nursing care facilities and hospitals when an exigency exists.
