All questions
Does a living trust automatically avoid probate?

No. A living, revocable trust is one of several tools for avoiding probate, but it has to be carefully planned, drafted and funded. If a living trust is not properly structured and funded, probate may still be required. Post-signing planning, guidance and legal advice matter as much as the signing itself.

Do I need a lawyer to buy or sell real estate in Florida?

Florida law does not require a lawyer at the closing table, but Kevin’s view is that it is always prudent to have legal representation in a matter as important as a sale or purchase, whether the deal uses a standard FAR/BAR contract or a tailor-made one.

Does the office handle bankruptcy cases?

Kevin has represented clients in Chapter 7 bankruptcy proceedings and is experienced in bankruptcy litigation. At present, he is limiting his bankruptcy practice to litigated matters.

Can Kevin meet outside the office?

Upon special request, Kevin has made special appointments to meet prospective or existing clients outside the office, including visits to independent and nursing care facilities and hospitals when an exigency exists.
