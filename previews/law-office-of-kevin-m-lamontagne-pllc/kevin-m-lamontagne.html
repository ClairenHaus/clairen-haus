Kevin M. LaMontagne

Attorney at Law · Boynton Beach, Florida

Background

Kevin M. LaMontagne is a graduate of Duke University School of Law. He has practiced in Boynton Beach, Florida since 1991, when he established his office there after first practicing in Boca Raton and Palm Beach.

He has a wide range of transactional and litigation experience and has helped clients with a wide array of personal and business situations and problems. He represents clients of all ages in important legal contracts, transactions and disputes.

Practice

  • Estate planning: wills, trusts, durable powers of attorney, health care surrogates, ladybird deeds and pre-need declarations of guardian
  • Business matters: commercial leases, business contracts, entity formation, LLCs for rental property owners, shareholder and operating agreements
  • Real estate: residential and commercial transactions and closings
  • Litigation: real estate contract, business law and condominium disputes, and appellate work
  • Bankruptcy: Chapter 7 experience and bankruptcy litigation; currently litigated matters only

Education

  • J.D., Duke University School of Law
  • B.A., University of South Florida, magna cum laude, completed in three years
  • Cardinal Gibbons High School, Fort Lauderdale

Admissions and memberships

  • Member of The Florida Bar since 1982
  • U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Florida
  • U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of Florida
  • Real Property, Probate & Trust Law Section, The Florida Bar
  • Trial Lawyers Section, The Florida Bar
  • Business Law Section, The Florida Bar
  • Florida licensed real estate broker since 1998; principal of Sellers Only Real Estate Brokerage Corp.

Serving seniors

Kevin understands the special needs of seniors and elder law issues. For 13 years he offered free consultations to seniors at the Boynton Beach Senior Center, taking over from attorney Allen William Martincavage, who had asked Kevin to fill in for him on what both of them expected would be a short-term basis. On special request, Kevin has met prospective and existing clients outside the office, including at independent and nursing care facilities and hospitals when an exigency exists.

From the sciences to the law

Kevin’s undergraduate studies were more pre-med than pre-law, including the three-course calculus sequence required of math and science majors, in which he earned a 4.0 average. His interest in science goes back to Cardinal Gibbons High School in Fort Lauderdale, where he received the four-year science award, given to one male and one female student in each graduating class.

Late in his undergraduate years, after a business law class and a logic class he thoroughly enjoyed, he chose law instead. He is happy with that decision to this day: he loves the practice of law and helping people with their legal needs, including many who find themselves in extremely difficult legal predicaments. In his spare time he still loves reading about the newest scientific discoveries and breakthroughs.
