Practice area

Litigation, disputes & appeals

Alongside his transactional work, Kevin litigates the disputes that grow out of contracts, property and business, in court, in arbitration and on appeal.

Disputes Kevin handles

  • Real estate contract disputes
  • Business law disputes and commercial litigation
  • Condominium disputes, including in arbitration
  • Appellate work, including an appeal in an environmental law case
  • Evictions
  • Foreclosure defense
  • Litigated bankruptcy matters (see Bankruptcy)

Transaction experience, applied to the dispute

Many disputes come down to what a contract, a lease or a set of condominium documents actually says. Kevin drafts and negotiates those documents in his transactional practice, and brings that same close reading to a dispute.

He is a member of the Trial Lawyers Section of The Florida Bar and is admitted to the U.S. District Court and the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of Florida.

Landlords and tenants

Kevin reviews residential leases and handles evictions, and prepares commercial leases with the default and remedy provisions that matter if a relationship breaks down.

Every dispute turns on its own facts. Call the office to talk through yours before deciding on next steps.
