How Kevin can help

Kevin represents clients of all ages in personal and business matters: the contracts, transactions and disputes they are involved in, from an estate plan to a commercial lease.

Estate Planning, Trusts & Wills

Wills, living trusts, durable powers of attorney, health care surrogates, ladybird deeds and pre-need declarations of guardian, planned around probate avoidance.

Business & Contracts

Commercial leases, business contracts, entity formation, LLCs for rental property owners, shareholder agreements and LLC operating agreements.

Real Estate

Residential and commercial transactions and closings, on standard FAR/BAR or tailor-made contracts, from an attorney who is also a licensed broker.

Litigation & Disputes

Real estate contract, business and condominium disputes, arbitration, appeals, evictions and foreclosure defense.
