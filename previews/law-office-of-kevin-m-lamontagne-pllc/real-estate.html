Real estate transactions & closings

Kevin M. LaMontagne has represented buyers and sellers of real estate for over 35 years, in both residential and commercial transactions and closings.

An attorney who also knows the brokerage side

Kevin is a Florida licensed real estate broker, and has been since 1998. He is the principal of Sellers Only Real Estate Brokerage Corp., his wholly owned real estate brokerage company, through which he has assisted sellers in their brokerage transactions for decades.

Why representation matters

Whether a transaction uses a standard FAR/BAR contract or a tailor-made one, it is always important to have the representation and advice of a skilled real estate attorney.

Real estate salespersons and brokers sometimes tell a buyer or seller that legal representation isn’t needed in Florida. Florida law does not require a lawyer at the closing table, as some states do, but it is always prudent to have legal representation in a matter as important as a sale or purchase of real estate.

About title insurance. Title insurance is only as good as the worst title exception in the title policy. Don’t be persuaded that title insurance alone is a reason to skip hiring a real estate attorney.

Questions about real estate

Do I need a lawyer to buy or sell real estate in Florida?

Florida law does not require a lawyer at the closing table, but Kevin’s view is that it is always prudent to have legal representation in a matter as important as a sale or purchase, whether the deal uses a standard FAR/BAR contract or a tailor-made one.

Isn’t title insurance enough?

Title insurance is only as good as the worst title exception contained in the policy. Having title insurance is not a reason to go without a real estate attorney.

Can the office help landlords who own rental property?

Yes. Kevin forms LLCs to protect clients who own and hold rental properties, and he reviews residential and commercial leases and handles evictions.
