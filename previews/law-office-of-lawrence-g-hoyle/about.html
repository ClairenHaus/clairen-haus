About the office

A Wellesley law office built on personal service

The Law Office of Lawrence G. Hoyle is at 40 Washington Street in Wellesley Hills, where Attorney Hoyle provides personal service to each client.

The practice

Attorney Hoyle began his own law practice in 1995 in Canton, Massachusetts, and moved it to Wellesley in 2008. The office provides legal services in estate planning, trusts and estates, probate law, asset protection, elder law, tax law, real estate law, business law, and litigation relating to those areas of practice.

Clients receive personal service, a thorough review of their circumstances, practical legal advice and knowledgeable representation.
