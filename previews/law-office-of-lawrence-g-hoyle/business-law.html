Business law

Attorney Hoyle helps owners create and maintain the right legal form for their business, and handles the legal matters that come up as it is bought, run, sold or passed on.

How Attorney Hoyle helps

Types of business organization include:

  • Sole proprietorships
  • Corporations
  • Limited liability companies
  • General and limited partnerships
  • Nominee and realty trusts
  • Other forms of organization

Services include assistance with legal matters that arise from:

  • Creating or purchasing a business
  • Operating a business
  • Selling a business
  • Planning for the succession of a business on an owner’s incapacity or death
  • Tax matters, including tax return issues, audits and appeals

In plain terms

The form you choose for a business affects personal liability, taxes and what happens when an owner leaves or dies. Those questions connect directly to estate planning, which is why succession planning sits naturally beside Attorney Hoyle’s estate and tax work.
