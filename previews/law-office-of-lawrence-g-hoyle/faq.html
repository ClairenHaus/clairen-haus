Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about the office, a first meeting and the work Attorney Hoyle does, drawn from the firm’s own information.

Where is the office?

The Law Office of Lawrence G. Hoyle is at 40 Washington Street, Suite 200, Wellesley Hills, MA 02481. The mailing address is P.O. Box 81342, Wellesley Hills, MA 02481.

How do I reach Attorney Hoyle?

Call 781.489.1489 or email larry@attorneyhoyle.com.

Do I need to meet Attorney Hoyle in person?

Yes. A consultation in person with Attorney Hoyle is necessary before he can consider representing a client.

Can I send details of my situation by email?

Please do not send confidential information by email or through the website. Sending information and receiving a response does not create an attorney-client relationship and is not an agreement to represent you.

What happens at the first meeting?

Attorney Hoyle reviews your present estate planning documents, if any, along with your assets, family circumstances and wishes. He then explains the laws that affect your plan, answers questions, sets out the alternatives and recommends a course of action.

Which documents are in a typical estate plan?

A general durable power of attorney, a health care proxy and a will are customarily included in every estate plan. A living will is added for people who wish to make a declaration about life-sustaining measures.

Can Attorney Hoyle serve as trustee or personal representative?

If a client asks, he may agree to serve as a personal representative or trustee, alone or together with a family member, friend, bank or trust company.

Does the office help with MassHealth applications?

Yes. Attorney Hoyle assists clients with completing MassHealth applications, and explains how estate planning documents and the form of property ownership can affect MassHealth eligibility.

Which courts and counties does Attorney Hoyle handle probate in?

He prepares and submits petitions to the Massachusetts Probate and Family Court, primarily in Norfolk, Middlesex, Suffolk, Essex, Bristol and Plymouth counties.

Which towns does the office serve?

Wellesley, Needham, Weston, Lincoln, Newton, Lexington, Concord and other cities and towns in Massachusetts.

Does Attorney Hoyle handle tax matters?

Yes. He holds an LL.M. in taxation from the Boston University School of Law Graduate Tax Program, represents individual and business clients in tax disputes at the local, state and federal levels, and assists with tax planning and return preparation.

How long has Attorney Hoyle practiced?

He has been an attorney for forty-three years. He opened his own practice in Canton in 1995 and moved it to Wellesley in 2008.
