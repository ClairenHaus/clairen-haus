How it works

A careful, four-step process

The way Attorney Hoyle approaches an estate plan, in his own description of his services.

  1. Initial conference

    A review of your present documents, if any, your assets, your family circumstances and your wishes.

  2. Advice and a recommendation

    He explains the laws that affect your plan, answers your questions, sets out the alternatives and recommends a course of action.

  3. Drafts, then signing

    He prepares the documents, sends you drafts, and schedules a conference to review and sign them.

  4. Putting it in place

    He recommends changes to asset ownership and beneficiary designations, and helps you make them on request.
