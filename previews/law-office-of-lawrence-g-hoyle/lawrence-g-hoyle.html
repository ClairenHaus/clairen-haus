Attorney profile

Lawrence G. Hoyle

Attorney at Law · Law Office of Lawrence G. Hoyle, Wellesley Hills

Lawrence Hoyle has been an attorney for forty-three years. He has extensive experience in estate planning, the preparation of estate planning documents, trust and estate administration, probate law, trust law, tax law and elder law.

Attorney Hoyle provides personal service, a thorough review of each client’s circumstances, practical legal advice and knowledgeable representation.

Experience

Attorney Hoyle began his own law practice in 1995 in Canton, Massachusetts, and moved his office to Wellesley in 2008. Before that, his experience included:

  • Associate at a pension and employee benefit consulting firm in Providence, Rhode Island
  • Estate and trust administrator at a national bank in Providence, Rhode Island
  • Associate attorney at a Boston law firm specializing in estate planning
  • Associate attorney in the estate planning department of a Providence law firm
