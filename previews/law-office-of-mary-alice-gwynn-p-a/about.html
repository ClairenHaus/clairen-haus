Firm overview

Personal attention and passionate advocacy

Estate planning law confronts the hardest questions at the end of a life. You want a lawyer who will take every care to get you the right answers.

Here for you at every step

At the Estate Planning Law Office of Mary Alice Gwynn, P.A., that is the central focus. The firm’s lead attorney is a former nurse who has seen up close how not having an estate plan can lead to devastation, and that is why the firm does this work.

Serving families throughout Florida

Families are the people most affected by estate planning and probate law. Going it alone is an option, but the consequences of navigating the probate system without help can be serious, including:

  • Probate disputes that pull families apart over confusing issues
  • Financial losses to taxes and the other hidden costs of losing a loved one
  • Days and weeks lost to paperwork, meetings and court dates
  • The stress of answering questions you never considered

The firm works to address these problems before they grow. Early planning is always the best way forward.

Real estate closings and contracts

Mary Alice Gwynn has a long, active history in Delray Beach, which gives her a real understanding of your real estate needs. She can assist with your closing and write contracts that prioritize your needs.
