Estate planning attorney in Delray Beach

An estate plan is a map that can lead your family through the difficult issues they will face when you pass on.

What is estate planning?

Your estate plan decides how your assets will be preserved, managed and distributed after you’re gone, and who will speak for you if you can’t. You don’t want to leave your family without guidance at that moment, so choose a lawyer with a history of personal care and compassion to help the people you love move forward.

Your life experience is unique, which means you need an estate plan unlike everyone else’s: one written specifically for your needs and your family. There is no one-size-fits-all approach, so the firm gets to know your story first.

The difference that care can make

Many people see estate planning as simply transactional: make an appointment, fill out some forms, leave with a plan. It should be more personal than that. When you work with the firm, the goal is to:

  • Address every question and concern right away, with the information laid out in a way you understand.
  • Give honest, transparent information about the realities of estate planning and the difficulties you and your loved ones may face.
  • Provide comfort and assist you with compassion, taking your wishes and needs into account.
  • Give you peace of mind that your family will not be lost when you are no longer there to guide them.

Mary Alice Gwynn’s history as a nurse and a lifelong member of the Delray Beach community is why every plan gets a human face.

Common tools in a Florida estate plan

In general terms, a plan may include a will, a revocable living trust (often with a “pour-over” will as a safety net), beneficiary designations, a durable power of attorney and health care documents. Which of these fit you depends on your family, your assets and your goals. The FAQ has Mary Alice’s own answers on living trusts, wills and probate.
