Common questions

Answers from the firm.

Drawn from the firm's current site.

Do living trusts go through probate?

Generally, no. One benefit of a living trust is avoiding probate. But when an asset is not retitled in the name of the trust, or an account is opened outside the trust, a pour-over will acts as a safety net: it picks that asset up and, through probate, pours it into the trust.

Do I need an attorney to handle an estate in Florida?

The firm’s answer is always yes. It is best to work with a professional who knows this area of law, and depending on the size of the estate and whether other beneficiaries are involved, the Florida legislature requires that an attorney assist with the administration.

How are fees determined for the personal representative and the attorney?

Generally in two ways: a statutory fee the legislature has set as reasonable for the personal representative and the attorney, or a separate contract with the attorney stating what the fees will be during the administration.
