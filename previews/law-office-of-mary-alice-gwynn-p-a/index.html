About Mary Alice

A nurse before she was a lawyer

Mary Alice Gwynn spent 11 years as a registered critical care nurse before opening her own law firm in 1992. She saw up close how not having an estate plan can devastate a family, and that experience shapes how she works with every client.

She is a lifelong member of the Delray Beach community, and her commitment to compassionate, personal care is what sets the firm apart. When you step into the office, you become part of the family.
