Mary Alice Gwynn spent 11 years working as a registered critical care nurse before opening her own law firm in 1992. Her commitment to compassionate, top-notch care for every client sets her apart.

As a nurse she saw up close how not having an estate plan can devastate a family, and she learned that prevention is the better part of treatment. She brings both lessons to her estate planning, elder law and probate work, and she is passionate about helping people through their difficulties so they can move forward with confidence.

A lifelong member of the Delray Beach community, she also has a long, active history with local real estate, helping clients with closings and writing contracts that put their needs first.
