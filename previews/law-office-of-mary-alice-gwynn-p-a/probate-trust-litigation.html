Probate & trust litigation in Delray Beach

If there is one thing Mary Alice Gwynn learned in 11 years as a critical care nurse, it is that prevention is the better part of treatment.

That lesson matters for anyone facing probate or trust litigation. Every client gets a focused, attentive team that handles their concerns according to Florida law, and if a matter must go to court, it receives the same level of attention.

Focus on the cause, not the effect

A well-drafted estate plan is the best defense against future litigation, but it is not a guarantee. Common reasons an estate plan is challenged include:

  • Undue influence: a concern that someone close to the person who died used their access to influence the decisions in the plan.
  • Lack of testamentary capacity: a concern that the person did not have the mental capacity to understand what the will included.

When clients request changes to their wills, the firm takes every possible step to avoid these complications, including conferring with the client’s physician and handling the request ethically.

Breach of fiduciary duty in a trust

A trustee has a duty to act according to the terms of the trust and in the best interests of the beneficiaries. If a trustee acted otherwise, or the final accounting is off, action may need to be taken.
