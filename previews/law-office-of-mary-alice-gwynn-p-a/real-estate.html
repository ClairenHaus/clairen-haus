Real estate attorney in Delray Beach

Buying or selling a home is exciting, until the paperwork and legalities pile up. An attorney can take that weight off your shoulders.

The firm helps with legal issues in real estate, from reviewing contracts to overseeing what happens at the closing table, so your purchase or sale can go smoothly from start to finish.

Attorney Mary Alice Gwynn has a long, active history in the Delray Beach community, which gives her a real understanding of local real estate needs.

How the firm can help

  • Draft and review contracts and documents for the purchase or sale of a home
  • Write contracts that put your needs first
  • Take charge of the closing process

Why work with an attorney?

It may not seem necessary, but having a legal professional in your corner is a real advantage: informed advice, careful review of important documents, and peace of mind that the terms you’re agreeing to are actually in your best interests.

The decisions you make about your real estate today can affect you tomorrow, which is why every matter is taken seriously, so you can focus on the joy of buying or selling a home.

Talk it through

If you’re ready to close on a property or need help with a contract, schedule a free consultation. The firm serves clients in Delray Beach, Boca Raton, West Palm Beach, Ft. Lauderdale, Palm Beach County and Broward County.
