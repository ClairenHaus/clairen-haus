About the firm

Committed to you.

A personal injury practice in downtown White Plains, led by trial lawyer Robert B. Marcus.

Your lawyer matters

The Law Office of Robert B. Marcus P.C. represents people who have been seriously injured: construction workers hurt in falls, drivers and pedestrians hit by vehicles, and people harmed by slip and fall accidents and medical or pharmacy errors.

Many of its clients are union electricians, carpenters and laborers. The firm has taken cases to verdict at trial, used expert witnesses and video animation to prove what happened, and recovered many awards in the one-million-dollar range and above. See the results.

An office in downtown White Plains

The office is at One North Broadway, Suite 412, on the corner of Main Street and North Broadway in the White Plains Plaza. There are building entrances on Main Street and on North Broadway.
