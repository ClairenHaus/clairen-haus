Directions and parking

One North Broadway is on the corner of Main Street and North Broadway in the White Plains Plaza, downtown White Plains. There are building entrances on Main Street and on North Broadway.

Parking

  1. Park in the garage for Walmart, 275 Main Street, 445 Hamilton and One North Broadway, on your right on North Broadway just past Hamilton Avenue. Take a ticket.
  2. Take the elevator down to the plaza “P” level.
  3. Cross the plaza and enter the back of One North Broadway.
  4. Check in at the front desk for elevator access. The office is on the 4th floor, Suite 412.

From I-287 (Cross Westchester Expressway) westbound

Take exit 6 and turn right at the light onto North Broadway (Route 22 south). Go through nine traffic lights, passing the American Red Cross and Pace University on the left. The garage is on your right just after Hamilton Avenue.

From I-287 eastbound

Take exit 6, turn left at the exit light, then left onto North Broadway (Route 22 south). Go through ten traffic lights, passing the American Red Cross and Pace University on the left. The garage is on your right just after Hamilton Avenue.

From Long Island

Take the Throgs Neck or Whitestone Bridge to the New England Thruway north or the Hutchinson River Parkway north, then I-287 west. Take exit 6 and follow the eastbound directions above.

From upstate New York

Take the New York State Thruway across the Hudson (the former Tappan Zee Bridge, now the Governor Mario M. Cuomo Bridge). Take exit 8 at Elmsford onto I-287 and follow the westbound directions above.

From New York City

Take the FDR Drive north to the Third Avenue Bridge and the Major Deegan Expressway north, which becomes the New York State Thruway. Take exit 4 (Cross County Parkway) east, then the immediate left exit onto the Hutchinson River Parkway north. Take I-287 west to exit 6 and follow the westbound directions above.
