Frequently asked questions

Questions and answers.

Straight answers about the firm, drawn from its own information.

What kinds of cases does the firm handle?

Personal injury cases of all kinds, including construction accidents (falls from ladders, scaffolds, roofs and cranes, and falling objects), car accidents, truck accidents, pedestrian accidents, slip and fall accidents, and medical and pharmaceutical malpractice.

Who handles the cases?

The office is led by Robert B. Marcus, a personal injury trial lawyer admitted to the New York bar in 1980.

Does the firm represent construction workers?

Yes. Most of the results on the firm’s site are for workers hurt on the job, including union electricians, carpenters and laborers, a window washer, an HVAC worker and a non-union cable technician.

Does the firm only represent union members?

No. Many clients are union workers, but the firm’s results also include a non-union cable technician, an account executive, a New York City supervisor and others.

Does Robert take cases to trial?

Yes. The firm’s results include a $6.0 million verdict at trial for two construction laborers hit by a vehicle, where expert witnesses and video animation were used to prove the driver was at fault. Robert was named one of the Top 100 Trial Lawyers by the American Trial Lawyers Association (2007–2012) and The National Trial Lawyers (2013–2018).

Can I still have a case if I had an earlier injury?

Several of the firm’s results involved clients whose accident made an earlier injury worse, including a $2.2 million result after a minimal-impact rear-end crash and a $1.9 million result for an HVAC worker with a prior knee injury. Every case depends on its own facts.

What results has the firm obtained?

Results on the firm’s site include $7.0 million for a union electrician who fell from a ladder, $6.0 million at trial for two construction laborers hit by a vehicle, $3.1 million for a window washer whose ladder broke, and $3.0 million for a union carpenter. Prior results do not guarantee a similar outcome.

Does the firm handle cases outside New York?

The firm’s results include a truck accident in Massachusetts that was pending in federal court in Boston and was resolved before trial.

Does the office speak Albanian?

Yes. The office serves clients in Albanian (shqip) as well as English, and offers free consultations to Albanian-speaking clients.
