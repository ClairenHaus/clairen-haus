What changes

Specific fixes this concept makes to the current site.

Each point below comes from a check of the firm’s live site, not from a template.

  • 01The current site’s footer reads ©2018 and its headlines, phone number and address are baked into images, so search engines and screen readers cannot read them. Here every word is real text.
  • 02The homepage title is just “Home,” with no meta description, no H1 and no street address in text. This page carries a descriptive title, description and the full office address.
  • 03There is no structured data or FAQ on the current site. This page adds LegalService, Person and FAQ markup from the firm’s own facts, so assistants and search engines can confirm what the firm does and where.
  • 0424 of the 27 homepage images have no alt text today; every image here is labelled, and the badge wall is condensed into one readable credentials list.

Common questions
What kinds of cases does Robert B. Marcus handle?

His site reports personal injury matters: falls from ladders and scaffolds on construction sites, worksite accidents, vehicle collisions and other serious-injury claims, many on behalf of union workers.

What are some of the firm’s reported results?

The current site lists results including $7.0 million for a union electrician who fell from a ladder, $6.0 million at trial for two construction laborers hit by a vehicle, and $3.1 million for a window washer whose ladder broke. The site notes that prior results do not guarantee a similar outcome.

Does the office speak Albanian?

Yes. The current site has a dedicated Albanian (Shqip) page stating that the office speaks Albanian.

Where is the office and how do I get there?

One North Broadway, Suite 412, White Plains, NY 10601, on the corner of Main Street and North Broadway in White Plains Plaza. The site’s directions page notes parking in the One North Broadway ramp and check-in at the front desk for elevator access. Phone: 914.831.9911.

About the practice

The information clients need before they call.

  • Pace University School of Law, J.D., 1979 · Associate Editor, Pace Law Review
  • Admitted in New York (1980), U.S. District Courts for the Southern and Eastern Districts of New York, and the U.S. Court of Appeals, Second Circuit (1991)
  • Distinguished Service Award, Pace University School of Law, 2008
  • Board of Directors, New York State Trial Lawyers Association and New York State Academy of Trial Lawyers

Robert Marcus completed a B.A. in French at Lehman College in 1975 and his J.D. at Pace University School of Law in 1979, where he was Associate Editor of the Pace Law Review and a Moot Court semifinalist. He was admitted to the New York bar in 1980.

He has been a member of the American Association for Justice, the New York State Bar Association and the American Bar Association, is a longstanding member of the Million Dollar Advocates Forum and a Life Fellow of the American Bar Foundation, and was named to New York Super Lawyers for twelve consecutive years, 2005 through 2017. The site also lists Westchester’s Best Lawyers (2012–2014), Long Island Legal Eagles (2010–2012), The Best Lawyers in America (2016) and Lawyers of Distinction (2016–2018).
