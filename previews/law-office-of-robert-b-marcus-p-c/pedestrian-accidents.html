Pedestrian accidents

People on foot who are hit by a vehicle, whether crossing the street or working on a job site.

Hit by a vehicle

The firm represents pedestrians injured by drivers. One of its largest results, $6.0 million at trial, was for two construction laborers who were hit by a vehicle. One lost his right leg above the knee; the other suffered crush injuries to his right foot.

Proving what happened

The driver in that case claimed he had a green light. Using expert witnesses and video animation, the firm proved at trial that he failed to keep a proper lookout and was responsible for the collision.

In plain terms

General information about New York law, not legal advice for your situation.

  • Drivers must watch for pedestrians. New York law requires drivers to use due care to avoid hitting anyone on foot.
  • No-fault benefits. A pedestrian hit by a car in New York is generally covered for basic medical bills and lost earnings by no-fault insurance, usually under the vehicle’s policy if the pedestrian has none of their own.
  • Serious injury. As with car accidents, a claim for pain and suffering against the driver generally requires a “serious injury” under New York law.
  • Time limits. Most lawsuits must be filed within three years; claims against public bodies usually need a notice of claim within 90 days.
