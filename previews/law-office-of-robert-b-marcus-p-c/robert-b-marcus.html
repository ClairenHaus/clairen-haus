Personal injury trial lawyer. Admitted to the New York bar in 1980.

For Robert Marcus, law has been his one true calling. The tireless pursuit of justice for his clients has always been his abiding goal.

He has obtained many awards for his clients in the one-million-dollar range, some well in excess of that amount. See his case results.
