Slip and fall accidents

Injuries from slipping or tripping and falling because of a dangerous condition on someone else’s property.

When a fall isn’t your fault

Slip and fall accidents are among the personal injury cases the office handles. A fall can cause fractures, head injuries and back injuries that keep someone out of work for a long time.

If you were hurt in a fall, call the office to talk about what happened. Photos of the spot where you fell, the names of anyone who saw it and your medical records can all help.

In plain terms

General information about New York law, not legal advice for your situation.

  • The owner’s duty. Property owners in New York must keep their property reasonably safe.
  • Notice. A claim usually depends on showing that the owner created the dangerous condition, or knew or should have known about it and didn’t fix it in time.
  • Public property. Falls on city, town or county property often have extra requirements, including a notice of claim within 90 days and, in many places, proof that the government had prior written notice of the defect.
  • Time limits. Most personal injury lawsuits must be filed within three years.
