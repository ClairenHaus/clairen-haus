Practice area

Truck accidents

Collisions with tractor-trailers and other commercial trucks, which often cause serious injuries and involve more than one responsible party.

Big vehicles, serious injuries

The firm handles truck accident cases, including cases that happen outside New York. In one of its results, a New York City supervisor was rear-ended by a tractor-trailer in Massachusetts. He had been treated for neck and back injuries just a month earlier, and after the crash he needed surgery. The case was pending in federal court in Boston and was resolved before trial.

In plain terms

General information about New York law, not legal advice for your situation.

  • Federal safety rules. Interstate trucks and their drivers must follow federal rules on driving hours, rest breaks, vehicle inspection and maintenance. Records kept under those rules can matter in a case.
  • More than one responsible party. Depending on the facts, a claim may involve the driver, the trucking company, the owner of the truck or trailer, or others.
  • Where the case is heard. A crash in another state, or between parties from different states, may be heard in that state’s courts or in federal court.
  • Time limits depend on where the crash happened and who is involved, and can be short.
