About the firm

About us

Thorough, effective and timely service, with a high degree of expertise.

Our firm specializes in residential and commercial real estate law. We understand every client has different concerns, and we individualize our services based on the needs and goals of each client.

We have closing locations throughout the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, so you may choose the one that will be most convenient for you. We are committed to providing excellent, cost-effective legal services, and we have a reputation for being highly ethical, meticulous, and responsive to our clients.

Our office represents all parties involved in real estate transactions, for both residential and commercial properties. Whether you are buying commercial real estate as an investment property, buying or selling a home, or refinancing your existing property, we will work to protect your interests.

Our mission

To provide thorough, effective and timely service with a high degree of expertise and customer satisfaction.
