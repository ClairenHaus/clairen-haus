FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about representation, title insurance and closings.

Can you represent me if you’re already closing for my lender?

Yes. It is not the responsibility of lender’s counsel to advise you on your rights and obligations in your purchase. Our office can represent you, even if we are handling the closing for your lender, for a reasonable fee.

Doesn’t the lender’s title insurance protect me?

No. The lender’s policy protects the lender’s interest up to the amount of the mortgage, even though you pay its premium at closing. Your ownership interest is insured only by an owner’s title insurance policy, which stays in effect for as long as you and your heirs own the property.

What is the Eagle policy?

The Eagle policy is the best owner’s policy now available. It adds protection for problems such as zoning and building permit violations, restrictive covenant violations and encroachments, including those that occur after your purchase. We plan to issue an Eagle owner’s policy unless you tell us otherwise.

Where are closings held?

We have closing locations throughout the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, so you may choose the one that will be most convenient for you.

Do you handle commercial property?

Yes. We handle purchases, sales and refinances of both residential and commercial properties, including investment property.

Do you offer e-closings?

Yes. Our services include e-closings with digital signatures and electronic transfer of funds.

Do you work with mortgage lenders and brokers?

Yes. We work with a variety of local and national mortgage brokers and lenders, and we are approved title agents for First American Title Insurance Company.
