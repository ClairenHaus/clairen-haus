About the office

A Brunswick law office with deep roots in Glynn County

Trusted legal counsel for individuals, businesses and local governments across Coastal Georgia.

How Tom works

Tom Lee is an experienced attorney with an acute legal eye. He delivers insight and guidance to help clients avoid lengthy entanglements and reduce the overall cost of their matter, whether that is settling an estate, buying or developing property, running a business, or dealing with a local government.

Clients work with Tom directly. He has practiced law in Glynn County since 1970, formerly as a partner at Lee and MacMillan, and today from his office on St. Andrews Court in Brunswick.

Who the office serves

The office serves Brunswick, Glynn County, St. Simons Island and the Golden Isles, and clients across Coastal Georgia. In real estate matters Tom also represents developers and investors from across the country.

  • Individuals and families planning their estates
  • Families settling an estate through probate
  • Small businesses and their owners
  • Real estate buyers, sellers, developers and investors
  • Counties and cities, and individuals dealing with them
