Attorney

Thomas J. Lee

Attorney at Law in Brunswick, Georgia. Practicing in Glynn County since 1970.

Background

Tom Lee, formerly a partner at Lee and MacMillan, received his undergraduate degree from Mercer University and earned his law degree from the Walter F. George School of Law, Mercer University, in 1964. During his senior year in law school he served as Chief Justice of the Honor Court.

Shortly after graduating, he entered the United States Army as a Judge Advocate General officer. He was assigned to Fort Leonard Wood, Missouri, where he served for two years as a legal assistant attorney and military prosecutor, and then to Fort Benning, Georgia, where he taught military justice at the Infantry School until his discharge.

Tom has practiced law in Glynn County, Georgia since 1970. For approximately 16 years he served as Glynn County Attorney, and he has also represented other governmental units including Brantley County, the City of Kingsland and the City of Woodbine.
