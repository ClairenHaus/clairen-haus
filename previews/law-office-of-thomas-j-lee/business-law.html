Business law for Coastal Georgia companies

Tom serves as outside general counsel for a diverse range of small businesses in Brunswick, Glynn County and St. Simons, from the day a company is formed to the years after.

What Tom helps with

  • Choosing the right type of entity for a new business
  • Drafting the documents a new company needs
  • Outside general counsel for small businesses
  • Ongoing legal advice and strategy for established companies

Starting a business

Tom helps entrepreneurs set up their organizations, guiding them in selecting the appropriate entity and drafting the required legal documents. The type of entity you choose (for example a corporation or a limited liability company) affects how the business is owned, managed and taxed, and how the owners are protected, so it is worth getting right at the start.

Counsel for established companies

For seasoned business owners who already have a company, Tom provides experienced legal guidance and strategy as their outside general counsel, so they can focus on running the business. Having one attorney who already knows your company means questions get answered with the full picture in mind.

Many business owners in the area also own commercial property or need to plan for the future of the company; Tom’s real estate and estate planning work sits alongside his business practice.
