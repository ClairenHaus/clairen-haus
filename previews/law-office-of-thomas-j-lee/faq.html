Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about the office, the practice areas and how to reach Tom Lee.

Where is the Law Office of Thomas J. Lee?

The office is at No. 9, St. Andrews Court, Suite 101, Brunswick, GA 31520.

What areas does the office serve?

Brunswick, Glynn County, St. Simons Island and the Golden Isles, and clients across Coastal Georgia. In real estate matters Tom also represents developers and investors from across the country.

What kinds of legal matters does Tom Lee handle?

Estate planning, elder care and probate administration; business law; local government law; and residential and commercial real estate law.

How long has Tom Lee practiced law?

Tom has practiced law in Glynn County, Georgia since 1970. He earned his law degree from the Walter F. George School of Law at Mercer University in 1964 and then served as a Judge Advocate General officer in the U.S. Army.

Does Tom do more than wills?

Yes. Along with drafting wills, he works with revocable and irrevocable trusts, life insurance planning, powers of attorney, family partnerships, and charitable trusts and foundations.

Can Tom help with probate after a family member passes away?

Yes. Tom assists clients with probate and estate administration.

Can Tom help me start a business?

Yes. He helps entrepreneurs set up their organizations, guiding them in selecting the appropriate entity and drafting the required legal documents. He also serves as outside general counsel for established small businesses.

What real estate matters does Tom handle?

Residential and commercial transactions, subdivision development and condominium law, and negotiating and documenting agreements for private residences, office, industrial, multi-family, retail and hotel properties.

Does Tom represent cities and counties?

Yes. He served as Glynn County Attorney for approximately 16 years and has served as legal counsel for Brantley County, the City of Kingsland and the City of Woodbine.

I have a problem with a city, county or state agency. Can Tom help me as an individual?

Yes. Tom can help individuals faced with legal issues involving city, county and state governments.

Which courts is Tom admitted to practice before?

The Supreme Court of the United States, the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit, the federal district courts for the Middle and Southern Districts of Georgia, the Supreme Court of Georgia, the Court of Appeals of Georgia and the Superior Courts of Georgia.

How do I reach the office?

Call (912) 261-2929 or email ssilaw@bellsouth.net.
