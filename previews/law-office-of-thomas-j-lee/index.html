Your attorney

Work directly with Tom Lee.

A Mercer law graduate, former Army JAG officer and longtime Glynn County Attorney who lives on St. Simons Island.

Tom earned his undergraduate and law degrees at Mercer University, graduating from the Walter F. George School of Law in 1964, where he served as Chief Justice of the Honor Court. He then served in the U.S. Army as a Judge Advocate General officer before settling in Glynn County, where he has practiced since 1970.
