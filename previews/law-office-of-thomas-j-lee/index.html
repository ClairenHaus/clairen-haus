The people behind the practice

Meet Tom Lee.

The firm’s attorney, as described on its About page.

  • Thomas J. LeeAttorney at law

    Formerly a partner at Lee and MacMillan, Tom Lee has practiced law in Glynn County since 1970 and served about 16 years as Glynn County Attorney. After law school he served in the U.S. Army as a Judge Advocate General officer, as a military prosecutor at Fort Leonard and teaching military justice at the Infantry School at Fort Benning. He served eight years on the Glynn County Airport Commission and as President of the Brunswick-Glynn County Bar Association.

    J.D., Walter F. George School of Law, Mercer University, 1964 (Chief Justice of the Honor Court) · Undergraduate degree, Mercer University · State Bar of Georgia, Real Property Law Section · Admitted before the U.S. Supreme Court, the Eleventh Circuit, the Supreme Court and Court of Appeals of Georgia, and the Middle and Southern Districts of Georgia

Common questions

Answers from the firm.

Drawn from the firm's current site.

What estate planning services does Tom Lee provide?

A full range: drafting wills; planning with life insurance and with revocable and irrevocable trusts; family partnerships; powers of attorney; charitable trusts and foundations; and probate and estate administration.

Does the firm represent local governments?

Yes. Tom Lee served as Glynn County Attorney for sixteen years and has served as legal counsel for Brantley County and the City of Kingsland, as well as the City of Woodbine. He also helps individuals with legal issues involving city, county and state governments.

What areas does the firm serve?

Brunswick, St. Simons and the Golden Isles of Georgia, including Glynn County. For real estate, Tom also represents developers and investors across the country.

How do I get to the office?

The office is at No. 9, St. Andrews Court, Suite 101, Brunswick, GA 31520. Follow Glynn Ave./Highway 17 toward downtown Brunswick, turn right on Gloucester Street, right onto Newcastle Street, then left onto F Street, which turns right and becomes St. Andrews Court.
