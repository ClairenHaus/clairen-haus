Practice area

Local government law

Extensive experience representing counties and cities in Coastal Georgia, and help for individuals facing legal issues with city, county and state governments.

Experience on both sides of the table

Tom Lee has extensive experience in local government law at both the county and city level. He served as Glynn County Attorney for approximately sixteen years and has served as legal counsel for Brantley County and the City of Kingsland. He has also provided legal services for the City of Woodbine, Georgia.

  • Glynn County: County Attorney for about 16 years
  • Brantley County: legal counsel
  • City of Kingsland: legal counsel
  • City of Woodbine: legal services for the city

For cities and counties

A local government attorney advises elected officials and staff, reviews contracts and ordinances, and handles the legal questions that come with running a city or county. Tom’s background lets him get right to the heart of a matter and take care of it with thorough attention to the specific needs of the city or county involved, saving the government time and expense.

For individuals

Tom can also help individuals faced with legal issues involving city, county and state governments. Someone who has spent years advising local governments understands how those offices work and what they need to see.
