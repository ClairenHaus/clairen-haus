For developers and investors

Tom represents real estate developers and investors in Brunswick, Glynn County and the St. Simons area of Coastal Georgia, as well as across the country. He counsels them on a wide variety of business development, lender, operational and management issues.

Real estate law, in plain terms

A real estate attorney reviews and drafts the contracts and closing documents in a purchase, sale or lease, makes sure the terms protect you, and helps resolve issues that come up before closing. For developments and condominiums, the legal work also covers how property is divided, the documents that govern owners, and the agreements with lenders and partners.

Tom is a member of the Real Property Law Section of the State Bar of Georgia.
