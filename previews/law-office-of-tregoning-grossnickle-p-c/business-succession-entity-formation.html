Business Succession & Entity Formation

Forming a business entity and planning how a business passes on as part of an owner’s estate plan.

What the firm does

The firm’s areas of practice include business entity formation, and its estate planning services include business succession planning and entity formation. Daniel K. Tregoning and Jacquelyn M. Grossnickle both focus their practices on estate and trust planning, business succession planning and estate administration.

Entity formation

Choosing and forming the right type of business entity, such as a corporation or limited liability company, sets the ground rules for how a business is owned and run. The choice can affect liability, taxes and how ownership can later be transferred.

Business succession planning

For many owners, the business is one of the largest parts of their estate. Succession planning looks at what happens to the business if the owner retires, becomes unable to work or dies: who will own it, who will run it, and how the transition will be handled and paid for.

Part of the larger plan

Because a business interest passes like other property, succession planning works best alongside the owner’s will, trusts and tax planning.

This page explains the subject in general terms only and is not legal advice. Every situation is different; please talk with an attorney about yours.
