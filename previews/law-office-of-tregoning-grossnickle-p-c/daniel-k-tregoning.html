Biography

Daniel K. Tregoning focuses his practice on estate and trust planning, business succession planning and estate administration.

Danny graduated magna cum laude from Salisbury State University with a B.A. in Political Science, and cum laude from the University of Baltimore School of Law in May 1994. He is a member of the Maryland State Bar Association (Estates and Trusts Section), the Bar Association of Frederick County and the Estate Planning Council of Frederick County, which he served as President in 2001.

Danny is actively involved in Good Shepherd Lutheran Church and the Community Foundation of Frederick County, and previously assisted with his children’s youth sports. He is married with three adult children.
