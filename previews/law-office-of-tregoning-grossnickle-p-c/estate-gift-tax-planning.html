Estate, Gift & Generation-Skipping Tax Planning

Estate tax, gift tax and generation-skipping transfer tax planning, gifting programs and asset protection.

What the firm does

The firm helps clients develop an estate plan while taking advantage of the appropriate estate and gift tax planning. Its services include estate tax planning, gift tax planning and generation-skipping transfer tax planning, along with gifting programs and asset protection.

Estate tax

Estate tax is a tax on the transfer of property at death. In addition to the federal estate tax, Maryland has its own estate tax, and it also has an inheritance tax that can apply depending on who receives the property. Which taxes apply depends on the size of the estate, how property is owned and who the beneficiaries are, so planning is specific to each family.

Gift tax and gifting programs

Gifts made during life can be part of an estate plan. A gifting program sets out how and when gifts are made, and gift tax rules determine how those gifts are reported and treated.

Generation-skipping transfer tax

The generation-skipping transfer tax can apply when property passes to grandchildren or others more than one generation below the person giving it, either directly or through a trust. Plans that benefit grandchildren usually take it into account.

Asset protection

Asset protection planning looks at how property is owned and held so that it is better positioned for the future, within what the law allows.

This page explains the subject in general terms only and is not legal advice. Every situation is different; please talk with an attorney about yours.
