FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers about the firm, the office and how to get in touch.

What kind of law does the firm practice?

The Law Office of Tregoning & Grossnickle, P.C. is an estate planning and administration law firm. Its areas of practice include estate planning (wills, trusts, powers of attorney and advance medical directives), trust and estate administration, and business entity formation.

Where is the office?

The office is at 352 West Patrick Street, Frederick, Maryland 21701.

Where can I park?

There is un-metered on-street parking along Patrick Street in front of the building. There is also parking, including one handicap-accessible space, immediately behind the office off Steiner Way. Please note that the spaces along Steiner Way itself are permitted spaces for the apartments, and cars parked there will be towed.

How do I reach the office?

Call 301-668-5310, or email Jacquelyn M. Grossnickle at jmg@tregoninglaw.com or Daniel K. Tregoning at dkt@tregoninglaw.com. The office fax number is 301-668-5311.

Who are the attorneys?

The firm’s principals are Daniel K. Tregoning, admitted in Maryland in 1994, and Jacquelyn M. Grossnickle, admitted in Maryland in 2011. Both focus their practices on estate and trust planning, business succession planning and estate administration.

Does the firm help after a family member dies?

Yes. The firm’s trust and estate administration services include all aspects of probate and administration of decedents’ estates, trust administration and trust dissolution.

Can the firm serve as trustee or help a trustee?

Yes. The firm’s trust services include serving as trustee and assisting trustees, including working with accountants to prepare tax returns.

Does the firm do tax planning?

Estate planning at the firm is done while taking advantage of the appropriate estate and gift tax planning. Services include estate tax planning, gift tax planning and generation-skipping transfer tax planning.

Does the firm work with business owners?

Yes. Services include business succession planning and business entity formation.

Does reading this website make me a client?

No. The information on this website is for general informational purposes only and is not legal advice. Reading it, or sending information to the firm, does not create an attorney-client relationship.
