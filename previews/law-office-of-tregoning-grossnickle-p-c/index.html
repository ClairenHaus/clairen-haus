Estate planning · Frederick, Maryland

Plan for your family. Settle an estate with care.

The Law Office of Tregoning & Grossnickle, P.C. is an estate planning and administration law firm in Frederick, Maryland, with more than 30 years of experience representing clients in the State of Maryland. The firm prepares wills, trusts, powers of attorney and advance medical directives, guides families through trust and estate administration, and helps business owners with entity formation and succession.

How the firm helps

A plan that fits the client, with the tax picture in view

The attorneys have a broad range of experience in helping clients develop an estate plan that meets each client’s needs, while taking advantage of the appropriate estate and gift tax planning.

When a family member dies, the firm assists with all aspects of probate and the administration of the estate, and with trust administration, including working with accountants to prepare tax returns.
