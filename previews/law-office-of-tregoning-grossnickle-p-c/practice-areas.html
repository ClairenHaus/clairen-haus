Estate planning

Planning ahead

The firm’s estate planning services include preparation of wills, trusts, powers of attorney and health care decision-making documents, asset protection, gifting programs, business succession planning and entity formation, estate tax planning, gift tax planning, and generation-skipping transfer tax planning.

Trust & estate administration

When a loved one dies

Trust and estate administration services include all aspects of probate and the administration of decedents’ estates, and trust services including trust administration and trust dissolution, serving as trustee, assisting trustees, and working with accountants to prepare tax returns.
