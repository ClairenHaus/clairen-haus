Trust Administration & Trustee Services

Trust administration and dissolution, serving as trustee, and help for trustees, including work with accountants on tax returns.

What the firm does

The firm’s trust services include trust administration and trust dissolution, serving as trustee, and assisting trustees, including working with accountants to prepare tax returns.

What a trustee does

In general terms, a trustee holds and manages trust property for the beneficiaries according to the terms of the trust document and the law. That can include keeping records, investing prudently, making distributions, communicating with beneficiaries and filing the trust’s tax returns.

When a trust ends

Many trusts are written to end at a certain time or event, such as a beneficiary reaching a certain age. Dissolving a trust involves making the final distributions, settling any remaining obligations and closing out the trust’s accounts and records.

Serving as trustee, or helping one

Some clients ask the firm to serve as trustee. Others have named a family member, who may want guidance on their duties. The firm offers both.

This page explains the subject in general terms only and is not legal advice. Every situation is different; please talk with an attorney about yours.
