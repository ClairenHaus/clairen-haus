About the firm

A boutique law firm in downtown Delray Beach, focused solely on Florida estate planning, probate, trust and guardianship matters.

Close to the courthouse

The Law Offices of Christopher P. Taylor, P.A. are located in downtown Delray Beach, only a couple of blocks from the South Palm Beach County Courthouse. Attorney Christopher P. Taylor is the founding member and managing partner.

The firm provides simple and more complex estate planning for individuals and families in South Florida, and represents beneficiaries and fiduciaries in the administration of probate estates, trusts and guardianships, as well as in the litigation and mediation of Florida probate, trust and inheritance disputes, in Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach Counties.

How the firm works

Mr. Taylor opened his own practice so he could offer more personalized, goal-oriented representation. The firm’s commitment is to honest, experienced and professional counsel, and to representation that is reasonably priced.

Litigation between family members is often highly emotional, can last several years, and can leave lasting divisions. Where litigation is needed to protect an inheritance, the firm pursues it; where it is not, exploring pre-suit settlement through negotiation or mediation can save the parties thousands of dollars in fees and costs.

The same experience drives the firm’s planning work. Far too many estate and trust disputes begin with no plan at all, or with do-it-yourself documents and inexpensive planning that end up costing families far more later.

Clients the firm has represented

Individual, corporate and professional clients in matters including will contests, trust reformation and termination, fiduciary breach and removal, compensation disputes, elective share and surviving spouse rights, elder exploitation, guardianships, probate and trust administration, and inheritance disputes.
