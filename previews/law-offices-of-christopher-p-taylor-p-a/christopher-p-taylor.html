Background

Christopher P. Taylor grew up in South Florida. Born in Miami-Dade County, he attended grade school in Broward County before going to the University of Florida in Gainesville, where he earned his Bachelor of Science in Business/Finance with honors, followed by his Juris Doctor from the University of Florida’s Levin College of Law. In law school he worked as a certified legal intern with the Gainesville Public Defender’s Office, representing clients through the judicial process.

Courtroom experience

After law school, Mr. Taylor returned to South Florida and spent four years as an Assistant Public Defender for the 15th Judicial Circuit in Palm Beach County. He represented clients in more than 40 jury and non-jury trials, in cases ranging from misdemeanors to first-degree felonies, and gained the comfort and knowledge needed to present a case to judges and juries.

Probate, trust and guardianship

In early 2006 he left the Public Defender’s Office to focus on civil litigation, specifically probate, estate, trust and guardianship litigation, and spent nearly seven years with a Broward County boutique firm handling those cases throughout Florida. He then opened his own practice to give clients more personalized, goal-oriented representation in South Florida, where he was born and raised and still lives.

Mr. Taylor has been involved in well over a hundred estate and trust-related disputes. That work gave him insight into the issues that lead to these cases, and into the common pitfalls of poor estate planning, which now inform how he plans for clients.
