Florida probate guides

Articles Mr. Taylor has written on the questions families bring to a probate and trust lawyer. Each card quotes his opening and summarizes what the article covers.

These articles were written between 2013 and 2015 and describe Florida law as of that time. Laws and local court contacts change, so please call the office before relying on any of them for your situation.

Florida statutes

The Florida Probate Code (Chapters 731 to 735) and the Florida Trust Code (Chapter 736) govern most of what these guides discuss. Both are published by the Florida Legislature at leg.state.fl.us.
