Common questions

Answers from the firm’s own pages.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. Mr. Taylor provides a free one-hour consultation to discuss your Florida probate, trust or guardianship concerns and the most efficient, cost-effective strategy for your circumstances.

Does a personal representative in Florida need a lawyer?

Generally, yes. In Florida every personal representative must be represented by an attorney admitted to practice in Florida, unless the personal representative is the sole interested person or is a licensed Florida attorney.

Does trust administration in Florida require court approval?

Generally not. The trustee takes control of trust assets when the person who made the trust dies, and this generally does not require court approval. The trustee still owes fiduciary duties under Chapter 736 of the Florida Statutes.

Which counties does the firm serve?

Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach Counties. The office is in downtown Delray Beach.
