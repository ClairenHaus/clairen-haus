Common questions

Before you call.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. Mr. Taylor provides a free one-hour consultation to discuss your Florida probate, trust and guardianship concerns and to determine the most efficient and cost-effective strategy based on your facts and circumstances.

Does every Florida estate have to go through probate?

No. Not all Florida estates require probate, and not all property a person owns at death is subject to it. Generally, if a person dies owning property in his or her name alone, probate is required. How property is titled, and the estate planning done in advance, can avoid unnecessary probate costs.

Does a personal representative need a lawyer in Florida?

In Florida, every personal representative must be represented by an attorney admitted to practice in Florida, unless the personal representative is the sole interested person or is a licensed Florida attorney.
