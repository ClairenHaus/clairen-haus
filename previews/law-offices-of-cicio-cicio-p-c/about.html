About the firm

A Birmingham general practice founded in 1958 and carried on by a second generation.

Our history

Two generations on 14th Avenue South

The law office was founded by attorney Anthony L. Cicio, Sr. in Birmingham, Alabama in 1958. In 1994, his son, attorney Anthony L. Cicio, Jr., joined him, and the Law Offices of Cicio & Cicio, P.C. was established.

Today Cicio & Cicio is a general practice law firm representing plaintiffs, defendants, individuals and corporations, primarily in Jefferson County and some of its surrounding counties. It is a family-owned and operated firm, and clients are welcome to call for a free initial consultation.

Founder · 1926–2021

Anthony L. Cicio, Sr.

The firm’s founder and senior partner practiced law in Birmingham for more than 60 years.

During World War II, Mr. Cicio served with the Army Air Force’s 20th Bomber Command in the South Pacific, on Saipan and Guam, as a crew member and flight engineer on a B-29. He was president of his law school graduating class.

Over his career he was a member of the Birmingham Bar Association, the Alabama State Bar, the American Bar Association and the American Trial Lawyers Association, and served on the Executive Committee of the Alabama Trial Lawyers Association. He chaired the Alabama State Bar’s Committee on Media Relations, served on the Judicial Legal Reform Committee, was appointed by the Mayor of Birmingham to the Birmingham Planning and Zoning Committee, and by the Mayor of Mountain Brook to the Metro Regional Planning Commission. He held a Martindale-Hubbell AV Preeminent rating.
