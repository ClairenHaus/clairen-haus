Wills and estates

Do I need a Last Will & Testament, Power of Attorney or Health Care Proxy / Living Will?

Yes. These documents will guide your representative in carrying out your wishes regarding the distribution of assets, make medical and legal decisions, and will help reduce the cost of probating your estate at the time of your death.

I already have these documents. Do I need to update them or have them reviewed?

Yes. You should update them when circumstances change (divorce, marriage, children, etc.), and some documents could be ineffective due to changes in legal requirements. Estate planning should not be considered permanent, as conditions and your desires may change.

Injuries

What should I do if I just had a vehicle accident?

If you have been injured, get immediate medical attention; report the accident to your insurance company as soon as possible; get names and contact information for any witnesses; take photos of your injuries promptly and about twice a month until the physical signs are gone; and contact an experienced lawyer at the Law Offices of Cicio & Cicio, P.C.

Workers’ compensation

What is the statute of limitations in Alabama for a workplace injury?

Two years from the date of injury or two years from the date of last compensation payment.

Can an employer refuse to file a workers compensation claim because there was not a witness to the accident?

No. Just because an accident was not witnessed, it does not mean it did not happen. A first report of injury must be filed by the employer.

Am I required to see the doctor my company has selected for my workers compensation injury?

Yes. The employer is only liable to pay medical benefits for an injured employee who has been treated by a doctor the employer has either chosen or approved.

I settled a prior workers compensation claim and signed an agreement not to file for future injuries. Can I file a new injury claim?

Yes. If your new claim is not for the same body part as your previous claim, you can file a new claim.

Social Security disability

When should I file for Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI) or Supplemental Security Income (SSI)?

You should file for SSDI or SSI as soon as you are aware that you have a disability according to Social Security accepted disabilities.

Real estate

What does real property mean?

Real property is the land and improvements such as garages and other buildings (excludes mobile homes) as well as gas, oil, minerals and other substances located below the land.
