Common questions

Answers from the firm.

Short answers drawn from the firm's own published information.

Do I need a Last Will & Testament, Power of Attorney or Health Care Proxy/Living Will?

Yes, according to the firm. These documents guide your representative in carrying out your wishes about distributing assets and making medical and legal decisions, and help reduce the cost of probating your estate.

Should these documents be reviewed over time?

Yes. The firm advises updating them when circumstances change (divorce, marriage, children) because some documents could become ineffective due to changes in legal requirements.

What is the statute of limitations in Alabama for a workplace injury?

Per the firm's FAQ: two years from the date of injury, or two years from the date of the last compensation payment.

Can an employer refuse to file a workers' compensation claim because nobody witnessed the accident?

No. Per the firm's FAQ, an unwitnessed accident still happened, and the employer must file a first report of injury.
