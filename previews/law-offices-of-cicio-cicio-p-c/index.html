Our story

Founded by a WWII flight engineer turned Birmingham lawyer.

Anthony L. Cicio, Sr. served in the Army Air Force’s 20th Bomber Command in the South Pacific as a B-29 flight engineer, then founded his Birmingham law practice in 1958. He practiced for more than 60 years. In 1994 his son, Anthony L. Cicio, Jr., joined him, and the Law Offices of Cicio & Cicio, P.C. was established.

In September 2000 the firm was featured in a small-business spotlight under the headline “Family-owned business thrives on respect.”
