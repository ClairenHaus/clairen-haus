Personal Injury

If you were hurt because someone else was careless, you may be entitled to compensation. Cicio & Cicio represents injured people in Jefferson County and some of its surrounding counties.

Cases the firm handles

The firm’s personal injury work includes injuries caused by the negligence of others, such as:

  • Automobile accidents
  • Tractor-trailer and 18-wheeler accidents
  • Motorcycle accidents
  • Slip and fall, trip and fall
  • Animal and dog bites
  • Wrongful death

Automobile accidents

If you were injured in a car accident, you may be entitled to compensation not only for the damage to your vehicle, but also for your injuries, scarring, pain and suffering, lost income, and past and future medical expenses. The firm helps clients through the legal process that follows an accident.

Tractor-trailer and motorcycle accidents

Crashes involving large trucks or motorcycles often mean serious injuries and more parties involved, such as trucking companies and their insurers. An attorney can help you understand who may be responsible and what your claim may include.

What to do after a vehicle accident

From the firm’s own guidance: if you have been injured, get immediate medical attention; report the accident to your insurance company as soon as possible; get names and contact information for any witnesses; and take photos of your injuries promptly, then about twice a month until the physical signs are gone. Then talk with an experienced lawyer.

After an injury, the best way to know whether you have a claim is to have it evaluated. The firm offers a free initial consultation: call 205-939-1327.

Questions clients ask
