About the firm

A solo practice, by design.

The Law Offices of David J. Reed is the solo law practice of David J. Reed, Esq. When you call, you work with the attorney himself.

Who the firm serves

Individuals and small businesses in metro Atlanta, from the firm’s office in Roswell. The services include incorporation, contract preparation and review, civil litigation, real estate and estate planning.

What the practice is built around

Civil litigation is the most highly developed part of the practice, with a focus on litigation related to real property. A significant part of the practice surrounds real property transactions and litigation, informed by Mr. Reed’s own work as a licensed broker and investor.

Not your typical attorney website

From the start, the firm’s website set out to compile useful information for the public: guides to the stages of a lawsuit, small claims in Magistrate Court, incorporation, estate planning and real estate transactions. Clients wrote in to say they appreciated that it was written in plain English rather than legal jargon. This site keeps that spirit.

Since 1993

David Reed graduated from Emory Law School in 1988, practiced with two Atlanta law firms, and opened his own practice in 1993.
