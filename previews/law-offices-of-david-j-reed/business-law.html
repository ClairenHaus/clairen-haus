Forming the right entity

The firm incorporates businesses and assists with creating business entities of all types, including limited liability companies (LLCs), limited liability partnerships (LLPs), partnerships and limited partnerships. A business can be started as a corporation, or an existing business can be converted to one.

Why incorporate

One of the most important advantages of incorporating is limited liability: in a partnership, the partnership’s debts are generally also the partners’ personal responsibility, while a corporation’s debts generally are not the personal responsibility of its shareholders. Some businesses also incorporate for tax reasons, which vary from business to business.
