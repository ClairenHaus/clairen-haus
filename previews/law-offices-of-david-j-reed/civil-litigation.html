Civil cases only

Litigation is another word for a lawsuit. There is civil litigation and criminal litigation; the firm practices civil litigation.

The six phases of a lawsuit

  1. Preparation. It usually begins with a call to the lawyer. Evaluation of the case starts right in that first conversation.
  2. Filing. A Summons and Complaint are filed with the Clerk. The complexity is in preparing those papers.
  3. Answer & default. Once served, a defendant typically has 30 days to file an Answer, or risks a default judgment.
  4. Discovery. The heart of a lawsuit: depositions, interrogatories, requests for documents and requests for admission.
  5. Motions. Requests asking the judge to make an order, such as motions to compel discovery or for summary judgment.
  6. Trial. The phase most people know from the media or jury service.
