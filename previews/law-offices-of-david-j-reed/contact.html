Getting here

From I-285, take GA-400 north to exit 8 and turn left onto Mansell Road. Go about 2 miles and turn right onto Alpharetta Highway (Hwy 9). In 7/10 of a mile, turn left onto Elkins Road, then left onto Bombay Lane into the Oakbrook office condominium complex. 1503 Bombay Lane is the last building on the right.
