Contract Preparation & Review

When a business or individual is asked to sign a formal written contract for a substantial ongoing relationship, having it reviewed first is an efficient way to avoid problems, and even litigation, later.

Call 770-751-0900

Contracts the firm typically reviews

  • Leases
  • Purchase and sale agreements
  • Building contracts
  • Employment contracts

What the review looks for

  • Restrictive covenants in employment or sale-of-business contracts, which may limit competition or solicitation of customers
  • Jurisdiction clauses that decide where a lawsuit must be filed, sometimes in a distant state
  • Variance from the norm: terms outside the typical, ordinary and customary provisions for that market
  • Who is bound: signing as a company officer is very different from signing personally, which can amount to a personal guaranty
