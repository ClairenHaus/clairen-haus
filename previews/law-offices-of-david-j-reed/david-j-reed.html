Background

David Reed is a 1988 graduate of the Emory Law School. He began his solo practice in 1993 after practicing with two Atlanta law firms. Today the firm provides legal services to individuals and small businesses in metro Atlanta, with civil litigation as the most developed part of the practice.

Real estate experience

Mr. Reed is a licensed real estate broker, the owner of Renee Taylor Realty, LLC, and an active real estate investor. He has experience buying, selling, refurbishing and leasing single-family homes, for clients and for his own portfolio.
