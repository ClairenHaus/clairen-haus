FAQ

Quick answers

Who does the firm represent?

Individuals and small businesses in metro Atlanta. The Law Offices of David J. Reed is the solo practice of David J. Reed, Esq.

Does the firm handle real estate closings?

No. The firm does not do closings, but it handles nearly everything else related to real estate: entities and trusts, contract review, and real property litigation.
