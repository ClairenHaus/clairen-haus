What changes

Specific fixes, drawn from the current site.

  • A secure site

    The current site loads only over plain HTTP; an HTTPS connection fails, so browsers flag it “Not secure.” The rebuild ships on HTTPS from day one.

  • A site that works on a phone

    The pages use a fixed table layout with no mobile viewport, and every page footer reads “Last modified: December 05, 2006.” The concept is a fast, mobile-first page.

  • Current directions, current tools

    The Directions page still points visitors to Mapblast! and MapQuest. The concept gives a tap-to-call number and a plain address.

  • Schema that says “law firm”

    There is no structured data on the site. The concept adds LegalService, Person and FAQPage markup so search engines and AI assistants can read the firm’s name, address, phone and services.

About the practice

One attorney, start to finish.

Emory Law School, 1988 · Member, State Bar of Georgia, Atlanta Bar and the Federalist Society

David Reed has been licensed to practice law in Georgia since 1988. He is admitted before the Georgia Supreme Court, the Georgia Court of Appeals, all other courts of the State of Georgia, the Eleventh Circuit Court of Appeals, and the U.S. District Courts for the Northern and Middle Districts of Georgia.

A significant part of the practice surrounds real property transactions and litigation. The firm does not do closings, but handles nearly everything else related to real estate: setting up corporations, trusts and partnerships, reviewing contracts, and litigation for investors, closing attorneys, builders and homeowners.

Estate planning follows a simple three-step process: an initial meeting of about an hour and a half, drafting and review of the documents, and the signing.

Common questions

Answers from the firm’s own site.

Each answer below is drawn from facts the firm already publishes, marked up so search engines and AI assistants can cite it. General information only, not legal advice.

Who is the attorney at the Law Offices of David J. Reed?

David J. Reed, Esq., a 1988 graduate of Emory Law School, licensed in Georgia since 1988. He began his solo practice in 1993 after practicing with two Atlanta law firms.

Does the firm handle real estate closings?

No. The firm does not do closings, but handles nearly everything else related to real estate, including entity formation, contract review and real property litigation.

What kind of litigation does the firm handle?

Civil litigation, with a focus on litigation related to real property. The practice also includes commercial cases, business disputes including “business divorce,” and personal injury.

Which courts is David Reed admitted to?

The Georgia Supreme Court, the Georgia Court of Appeals, all other Georgia state courts, the Eleventh Circuit Court of Appeals, and the U.S. District Courts for the Northern and Middle Districts of Georgia.
