How the firm can help

General legal services for individuals and small businesses in metro Atlanta: incorporation, contract preparation and review, civil litigation, real estate and estate planning.

Real Estate Law

Entities, trusts, contract review and litigation for investors, builders, homeowners and anyone with an interest in real property. No closings.

Civil Litigation

Business Disputes & Business Divorce

Personal Injury

Automobile accidents, slip and fall, medical malpractice and product liability cases.
