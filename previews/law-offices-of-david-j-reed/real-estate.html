Real Estate Law

A focus of the practice is service to the real estate investor. David Reed is himself a licensed real estate broker and an active investor, so he approaches real property from more than one side of the table.

Call 770-751-0900

Who the firm works with

Real estate investors, closing attorneys, builders, homeowners, and anyone with an interest in real property who has been sued or is considering legal action.

What the firm handles

  • Setting up corporations, LLCs, partnerships and trusts to hold property
  • Reviewing and drafting real estate contracts, including purchase and sale agreements and leases
  • Litigation over real property
  • Conventional and non-conventional (“subject to”) residential transactions

Please note: the firm does not handle real estate closings. It handles nearly everything else related to real estate.

Conventional and creative transactions

In a conventional residential transaction, the buyer gets financing from a lender and pays off the seller’s existing loan. In a non-conventional transaction, the buyer purchases the property “subject to” the seller’s existing debt. The firm assists clients with both kinds, and with the contractual relationships between sellers, buyers, agents and brokers that come with them.

A broker’s and investor’s perspective

Mr. Reed is a licensed real estate broker, the owner of Renee Taylor Realty, LLC, and has experience buying, selling, refurbishing and leasing single-family homes for clients and for his own portfolio.

FAQ

Common questions

Does the firm handle real estate closings?

No. The firm does not do closings, but it handles nearly everything else related to real estate, including setting up entities and trusts, reviewing contracts, and real property litigation.

Can the firm help with a “subject to” purchase?

Yes. The firm assists with both conventional and non-conventional real property transactions, including purchases made “subject to” the seller’s existing loan.
