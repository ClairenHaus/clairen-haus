About the practice

The information clients need before they call.

Hon. Anthony J. DePanfilis · Georgetown University, J.D., 1974

The firm’s site says that since 1977 DePanfilis & Vallerie has enjoyed a well-deserved reputation in the legal community as well as the respect of the courts of Fairfield County, Connecticut. It is counsel to local and national institutions including Fairfield County Bank Corp., Norwalk Bank & Trust, The Norwalk Redevelopment Agency, First American Title Insurance Company, Chicago Title Insurance Company and United General Title Insurance Company.

The firm is divided into separate practice groups tailored to the expertise of the lawyer and the needs of each client. Managing Partner Marc J. Grenier heads civil litigation, joined by Anthony DePanfilis, Joseph DaSilva, Jr. and Thomas L. McKirdy, Jr.; Senior Partner David W. Stergas, who holds an LL.M. in Taxation from New York University, leads estates and trusts.

The office is on Route 1, adjacent to the Norwalk Courthouse, and easily accessible from I-95, Route 7 and the Merritt Parkway.
