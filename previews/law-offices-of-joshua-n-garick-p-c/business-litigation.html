Business Litigation

Carefully planned, creative and cost-effective strategies for pursuing, defending or resolving business disputes, for companies large and small.

Commercial disputes, handled with the business in mind

The Law Offices of Joshua N. Garick, P.C. advises and represents businesses, both large and small, in many areas of complex commercial litigation. The firm prides itself on preparing carefully planned, creative and cost-effective strategies for the successful pursuit, defense or resolution of business disputes.

The right strategy is not always the one that maximizes legal remedies. It may be guided more by business concerns: a relationship worth preserving, the cost of a long fight, or the need for a quick answer.

Types of disputes

  • Breach of contract
  • Breach of fiduciary duty
  • Business torts
  • Antitrust
  • Unfair and deceptive trade practices
  • Trade secrets and non-competition agreements
  • Shareholder derivative lawsuits
  • Franchise and distribution conflicts
  • Other commercial disputes

Before a dispute becomes a lawsuit

Many business disputes can be narrowed or resolved through a demand letter, negotiation or mediation. When they cannot, the firm litigates in the state and federal courts, and in arbitration where a contract calls for it.
