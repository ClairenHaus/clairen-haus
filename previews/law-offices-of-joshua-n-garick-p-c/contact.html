Getting here

The office is on Salem Street (Route 129) in downtown Reading. Off-street parking is available in front of the building.

From Boston or the north: take I-93 to the Reading/Wilmington exit for Route 129 and head toward Reading on Lowell Street (Route 129 East). Approaching downtown, Route 129 becomes Salem Street. Number 34 is the third building on the left after you cross Route 28, about 2 miles from I-93.

From the south, west or central Massachusetts: take Route 128 (I-95) North to the Wilmington/Wakefield Center exit for Route 129, then take Route 129 West toward Wilmington. This is Salem Street; number 34 is about a mile on the right, just before Reading Square.
