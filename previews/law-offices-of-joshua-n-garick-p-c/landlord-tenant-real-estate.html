Practice area

Landlord/Tenant & Real Estate Litigation

Representation for tenants and landlords in residential and commercial lease disputes, and for owners in a wide range of real estate litigation.

Both sides of the lease

The firm represents both tenants and landlords in residential and commercial lease disputes, and has prosecuted and defended summary process (eviction) actions for non-payment of rent and other material breaches of a lease.

Landlord/tenant matters

  • Covenant of habitability
  • Covenant of quiet enjoyment
  • Equal housing and housing discrimination
  • Foreclosures
  • Lead paint and mold
  • Lockouts
  • Negligence by a property owner
  • Public housing issues
  • Retaliatory evictions
  • Sanitary and building code violations
  • Security deposit statute violations
  • Unfair and deceptive business practices

The firm has also brought class actions against residential landlords whose leases include terms that violate landlord/tenant law.

For landlords: Massachusetts landlord/tenant law can be difficult for residential landlords. Advice before a tenancy begins can reduce exposure, or prevent litigation altogether.

Other real estate litigation

  • Adverse possession
  • Condominium disputes
  • Easements and rights-of-way
  • Eminent domain
  • Foreclosure and loan modification
  • Petitions for partition
  • Purchase and sale litigation
  • Title disputes
  • Tenant association claims
