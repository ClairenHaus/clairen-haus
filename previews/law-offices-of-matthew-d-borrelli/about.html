About the firm

A Needham practice built on customer service and a personal touch.

A practice in Needham Center

The Law Offices of Matthew D. Borrelli is at Chestnut Green, 105 Chestnut Street, Suite 28, just off the center of Needham, across the street from the Fire Station.

Most of the firm’s work is residential real estate, for buyers, sellers and lenders. It also prepares estate plans, converts multi-family homes into condominiums, defends criminal cases in District Court and handles civil litigation.

“It is this element of customer service and personalized touch that separates us from other attorneys.”

From the firm’s condominium conversion practice

Who we work with

A network that serves clients

Legal work rarely happens alone. The firm works alongside the other professionals a client needs.

Lenders and brokers

Solid working relationships with a variety of lenders and brokers help home purchases and sales go smoothly.

Fidelity National Title

The firm is a title insurance agent for Fidelity National Title Insurance Company.

Financial planners

For estate planning clients, the firm works closely with financial planners who can offer life insurance protection or help with financial growth.

Architects and engineers

Condominium conversions are coordinated with an architect and an engineer or land surveyor, who prepare the floor plans and site plan.

Banks and mortgage companies

The firm represents banks and mortgage companies in title matters and mortgage closings.

Young families

The firm takes particular pride in drawing basic estate plans for young families and couples to protect and provide for their children.
