Civil Litigation

Guiding clients through the court process, with a clear view of the strategy.

“Our objective is to most effectively meet your legal needs and relieve your anxieties, and to provide you with appropriate, expeditious and positive resolutions.”

Litigation requires knowledge, sensitivity, innovation, flexibility and determination. The firm handles a wide variety of cases, including:

  • Personal injury
  • Real estate
  • Business & commercial law
  • Contracts
  • Debtor/creditor
  • Discrimination
  • Motor vehicle accidents (plaintiff)
  • Sexual harassment

Kept informed at every stage

A lawsuit moves through stages: the complaint and answer, the exchange of information known as discovery, motions, and settlement discussions or trial. As the firm works toward a favorable result, clients are guided through the judicial process and kept up to date on both the process and the strategy.

Motor vehicle accidents

In motor vehicle accident cases the firm represents the injured person, the plaintiff.
