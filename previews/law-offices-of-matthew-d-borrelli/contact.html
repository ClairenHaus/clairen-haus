Directions

From Route 128, north or south: take the Highland Avenue exit toward Needham (Exit 35, numbered 19 before the 2021 renumbering) and merge onto Highland Avenue. Go through 5 traffic lights and bear right at the gas station onto Chapel Street. Go through the next intersection, across Great Plain Avenue, onto Chestnut Street. 105 Chestnut Street (Chestnut Green) is 100 yards on your right, across from the Fire Station.

From Wellesley: take Route 135 west toward Needham Center. When you enter the center of town, turn right on Chestnut Street. 105 Chestnut Street (Chestnut Green) is 100 yards on your right, across from the Fire Station.
