Criminal Defense

Defending the rights of people who have been accused, arrested or charged.

“We are devoted to defending the rights of persons who have been accused, arrested or charged with criminal offenses. We are committed to zealous advocacy for our clients along with a creative, realistic and result-oriented approach.”

The firm handles criminal defense in a wide variety of District Court cases, including:

  • DUI
  • Drug possession
  • Theft
  • Fraud
  • Assault
  • Battery
  • Felonies
  • Misdemeanors

If you are arrested

Before police question someone in custody, they should be told that they have the right to remain silent, that anything they say may be used against them, that they have the right to have a lawyer present during questioning, and that a lawyer will be appointed if they can’t afford one.

You can be questioned without a lawyer only if you voluntarily give up those rights. If you agree to questioning and then change your mind, the questioning must stop when you say so or ask for a lawyer.

When to call a lawyer

If you are arrested, particularly for a serious charge, contact a lawyer as soon as possible. A lawyer can advise you on what to say and what not to say, and can help you and your family with the bail process.

What to tell your lawyer

Everything. Your lawyer is held to a strict duty of confidentiality, so there is no such thing as giving your lawyer too much information.

Bail and arraignment

Bail lets a person who has been arrested be released while promising to come back to court for every scheduled date. At the arraignment, the first court appearance, the charges are read, the defendant is asked about having a lawyer, a plea is entered, and the court can revisit bail and set the next dates in the case.
