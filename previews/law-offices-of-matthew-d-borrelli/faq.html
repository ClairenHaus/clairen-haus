Real estate

Does the firm represent lenders?

Yes. The firm represents banks and mortgage companies in title matters, and lenders and brokers can send closing documents to closingdocs@borrellilegal.com.

Can the firm provide title insurance?

Can I convert my multi-family home into condominiums?

The firm represents owners converting a multi-family home or investment property into condominiums. Estate planning

What does a will do?

A will directs how your assets are distributed after your death and names the executor who manages your estate. Most importantly for parents, it can name a guardian for children who are still minors.

Does my will control all of my assets?

Generally not. A will controls assets titled in your name alone. Retirement plans, jointly owned property, “payable on death” accounts and life insurance usually pass by their beneficiary designations or ownership terms instead.

Is a handwritten will valid in Massachusetts?

A will written by hand and signed but not witnessed is generally not valid in Massachusetts. A will needs to be in writing, signed, and witnessed by two people.

Does a living trust save estate taxes?

Not by itself. A living trust can contain provisions that postpone, reduce or eliminate estate taxes, but the same provisions can be written into a will. Creating a living trust also has no effect on your income tax during your lifetime.

Criminal defense

What kinds of criminal cases does the firm handle?

A wide variety of District Court cases, including DUI, drug possession, theft, fraud, assault, battery, felonies and misdemeanors.

What should I tell my lawyer?

Everything. Your lawyer is held to a strict duty of confidentiality, so you should never be concerned about giving your lawyer too much information.
