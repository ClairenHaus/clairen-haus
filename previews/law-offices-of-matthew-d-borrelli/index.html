Buying or selling a home
“We realize buying or selling a home can be a time consuming, stressful and expensive experience. Our approach is geared to making your experience as enjoyable as it should be.”

From the firm’s real estate practice

We advise buyers and sellers on contract and inspection issues, financing options, title insurance, mortgage closings and deeds. We have developed solid working relationships with a variety of lenders and brokers, which helps keep the time, stress and cost of a move down.

The firm also represents banks and mortgage companies in title matters, and acts as a title insurance agent for Fidelity National Title Insurance Company.
