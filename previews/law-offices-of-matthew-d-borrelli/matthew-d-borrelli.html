Attorney

Matthew D. Borrelli

Attorney and Counselor at Law, Needham, Massachusetts.

Matthew D. Borrelli is the attorney behind the Law Offices of Matthew D. Borrelli, at Chestnut Green on Chestnut Street in Needham Center.

His practice is centered on residential real estate. He guides buyers and sellers through home purchases and sales, advising on contracts, inspections, financing, title insurance, mortgage closings and deeds, and represents banks and mortgage companies in title matters. The firm is a title insurance agent for Fidelity National Title Insurance Company.

He also represents owners converting multi-family homes and investment properties into condominiums, prepares estate plans (with particular pride in basic plans for young families and couples), defends clients in a wide range of District Court criminal cases, and handles civil litigation.
