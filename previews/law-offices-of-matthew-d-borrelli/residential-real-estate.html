Residential Real Estate

Guidance for buyers and sellers, from the offer to the deed.

“We have an active, customer service oriented real estate practice focusing primarily on conveyancing transactions. We guide consumers to a successful home purchase and/or sale experience.”

Buying or selling a home is one of the largest transactions most people make. The firm represents buyers and sellers through the whole process and advises on:

  • Contract issues
  • Inspection issues
  • Financing options
  • Title insurance
  • Mortgage closings
  • Deeds

Buyer representation

For a buyer, the attorney’s work usually starts when an offer is accepted: reviewing the purchase and sale agreement, making sure inspection and financing deadlines protect you, examining the title to the property, and explaining the loan and closing documents before you sign them.

Seller representation

For a seller, it means reviewing the agreement, preparing the deed, clearing up title questions and arranging for the existing mortgage to be paid off at the closing.

Working relationships that help

The firm has developed solid working relationships with a variety of lenders and brokers. That helps keep a purchase or sale on schedule and keeps the time, stress and cost of the process down.

Title insurance

The firm is a title insurance agent for Fidelity National Title Insurance Company, so title insurance can be arranged as part of the same transaction. See Lender representation & title.
