About the firm

Rooted in Alpharetta

A North Fulton firm built by two attorneys who share a common-sense approach to estate planning.

Firm history

After practicing in Alpharetta for 14 years, Chris Miller opened his own law firm with the help of Marianna Chaet in 2014. Eight years later, Chris and Marianna formed a partnership, and the Law Offices of Miller & Chaet was born.

  1. 2014Chris Miller opens his own firm with the help of Marianna Chaet, after 14 years practicing in Alpharetta.
  2. 2020The firm is named “Best of North Atlanta” in the Attorneys category.
  3. Eight years onChris and Marianna form a partnership: the Law Offices of Miller & Chaet.

Mission

Our mission is to guide clients with our legal knowledge and experience.

We strive to do so in a welcoming, friendly atmosphere. We believe that how we spend our time, and how we perform our jobs, has an impact far beyond the walls of our office, and can change people’s lives for the better.

What we do

Estate planning, start to finish.

We guide surviving spouses and other family members through the grief of losing a loved one, and work on transferring assets both inside and outside of probate. Our skill set lets us form trusts and business entities to lend structure to the plans we recommend.

Whether starting a business or grappling with a transition, our goal is to make sure clients have the information they need to make sound decisions.
