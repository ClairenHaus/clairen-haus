Practice area

Estate Administration

We help surviving spouses and family members through the grief of losing a loved one, and make the legal process as smooth as possible during an already difficult time.

When there is a will

The person named as executor or personal representative is responsible for probating the will: settling the estate and making the distributions its terms prescribe.

When there is no will

When someone passes intestate, the court appoints an administrator to settle the estate. The administrator has many of the same responsibilities as an executor.

How the firm helps

  • File original wills with the probate court
  • Locate witnesses
  • Notify and obtain consents from heirs
  • Publish notices to debtors and creditors of the estate
  • File returns
  • Settle accounts
  • Petition for year’s support
  • File affidavits of survivorship

The Law Offices of Miller & Chaet help executors, personal representatives and estate administrators navigate the maze that is estate administration.

Questions about estate administration

I was named executor. Can the firm help?

Yes. The firm helps executors, personal representatives and court-appointed administrators through estate administration, from filing the original will with the probate court and notifying heirs to publishing notices to creditors, filing returns and settling accounts.

Who settles an estate when there is no will?

The court appoints an administrator, who has many of the same responsibilities as an executor or personal representative. The firm helps administrators through the process too.
