Common questions
Do I need a will in Georgia?

The firm explains that if someone dies without a will, Georgia’s intestacy laws decide who inherits, distributing assets to the closest living relatives as assigned by state law. A will lets you name an executor and a guardian for minor children and decide who receives what.

Do I need a trust?

Not always. The firm says trusts are expensive to create and manage and it does not use them for everybody. It advises clients on whether a trust is beneficial, for example to avoid ancillary probate on out-of-state property or to control when beneficiaries receive funds.

What is a Special Needs Trust?

A place to hold gifts or inheritances for a family member with a disability so they do not count against eligibility for benefits such as Medicaid. The assets are meant for needs beyond what benefits cover, such as therapies, coaching and activities.
