About Chris

J. Christopher Miller focuses his time in the areas of trusts and estates, taxation and business law. His experience with estate administration informs his common-sense style of practicing law and helps him craft estate plans that suit each client’s situation.

Chris has helped hundreds of families navigate probate courts around Georgia, with wills of all types and with no wills at all. He has guided clients through estate tax filings and audits, and brings a caring approach to guardianship and conservatorship cases.

Chris enjoys his work in the special needs community, regularly discussing the use of Special Needs Trusts at the seasonal conferences of the Down Syndrome Association of Atlanta. He has spoken at several CLE seminars on Georgia’s probate code and its Limited Liability Company Act, and in 2014 he wrote the chapter “Asset Protection Planning” for a guidebook for physicians and healthcare professionals.

He is active in the Greater North Fulton Chamber of Commerce, where he served for several years on the committee organizing the Emerging Leaders Program for high school students. In 2022 he began leading the Board of Trustees of the Chamber’s Foundation, which sponsors Leadership North Fulton and the NextGen Leaders groups.

Chris lives in Johns Creek with his wife, Sharon, and their daughter.
