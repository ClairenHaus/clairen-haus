About Marianna

Marianna I. Chaet assists clients in estate planning, estate administration, special needs planning and business law. She found a love for estate planning in her first trusts and estates class at the University of Georgia School of Law, and went on to take every estate planning class the school offered.

She enjoys this work because she can help people whether they are planning for the future or dealing with the loss of a loved one. Her goal is to make a difficult time less cumbersome.

In law school Marianna worked at the school’s family violence clinic, helping clients obtain temporary and permanent restraining orders. She also interned for the legal department of an Atlanta corporation and for the Honorable Judge Christopher Ballar of the Gwinnett County Probate Court.

Marianna has published articles in North Fulton Family Life and the Milton Neighbor, has been an invited speaker for the Alpharetta New Comers Club, and has presented on estate planning topics for many groups. She volunteers on the Advisory Council for The Drake House and has volunteered with the Furkids animal shelter.

She grew up in North Fulton, attended Chattahoochee High School, is fluent in Russian and conversational in Spanish, and is a loyal Falcons, Hawks and Braves fan. She lives in Roswell with her husband, their young son and a cat who sometimes thinks he is a dog.
