About Skyler

Skyler Steckler was born on Long Island, New York, but grew up in the North Fulton area of Georgia for most of his life. He attended Alpharetta High School and graduated from the University of Georgia in 2014 with a Bachelor of Arts in Political Science, with minors in History and Philosophy.

After a few years of restaurant and tutoring work, Skyler continued his education at the Georgia State University College of Law, graduating cum laude in 2021. There he earned a Certificate in Environmental and Land Use Law while also studying subjects including Wills, Trusts, and Estates.

Away from the office, Skyler spends as much time as he can with family and friends. He enjoys movies and television, all kinds of music, reading and video games, and is an avid New York sports fan (though he does love Atlanta United). His dedication to his teams is matched only by his dedication to his work.
