About the firm

Over 40 years of personalized legal service in South Florida.

A Boca Raton law firm serving individuals and businesses, with two attorneys who have practiced Florida law since 1981 and 1983.

Firm overview

The Law Offices of Weintraub & Weintraub, P.A. has provided personalized legal services in the South Florida area for over 40 years, from its office at 7700 Congress Ave., Suite 1110 in Boca Raton.

The firm handles legal matters in real estate, commercial law, commercial litigation, bankruptcy, business law, contracts, construction law, trusts and estates, wills and probate, landlord and tenant, and general practice.

“Our goal is to provide the highest quality legal services to you and your business in a timely fashion.”

The attorneys

Peter B. Weintraub was admitted to The Florida Bar in 1981 and is admitted to the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Florida, the U.S. Bankruptcy Court and the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit. About half of his practice is commercial litigation.

Toni R. Weintraub was admitted to The Florida Bar in 1983. Her practice includes commercial litigation, real estate, business law, contracts and general practice.
