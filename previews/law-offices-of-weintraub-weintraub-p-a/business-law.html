Business Law

Corporate and LLC formation and ongoing business counsel for owners who want a lawyer who knows their company.

What business law covers

Business law covers the life of a company: choosing an entity, forming a corporation or limited liability company, documenting how owners make decisions, and handling the legal questions that come up as the business grows or changes hands.

The right structure at the start can make later questions simpler, from bringing in a new owner to separating personal and business obligations. Business counsel also reviews the contracts a company signs and helps owners plan for disputes before they happen.

Matters this can include

  • Corporation and LLC formation
  • Operating agreements and shareholder arrangements
  • Business contracts and transactions
  • Advice for owners on day-to-day legal questions
  • Disputes between owners or with other businesses
