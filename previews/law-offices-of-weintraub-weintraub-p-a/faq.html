FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Answers about the firm, its attorneys and how to reach the office.

Where is the firm’s office?

The Law Offices of Weintraub & Weintraub, P.A. is at 7700 Congress Ave., Suite 1110, Boca Raton, Florida 33487.

How do I contact the firm?

Call 561-988-6411 or email PBW@weintraublawfirm.com. The firm’s fax number is 561-988-6011.

What practice areas does the firm handle?

Real estate, commercial law, commercial litigation, bankruptcy, business law, contracts, construction law, trusts and estates, wills and probate, landlord and tenant, and general practice.

Who are the attorneys?

Peter B. Weintraub and Toni R. Weintraub, both members of the firm and members of The Florida Bar.

How long has the firm been serving South Florida?

The firm has provided personalized legal services in the South Florida area for over 40 years.

Which courts is Peter B. Weintraub admitted to?

Florida (1981), the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Florida and the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit (1981), the U.S. Bankruptcy Court (1985), and the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit (1986).

Can I get information about the attorneys’ qualifications before I decide?

Yes. The hiring of a lawyer is an important decision that should not be based solely upon advertisements. Before you decide, you can ask the firm to send you free written information about its qualifications and experience.

Is the information on this website legal advice?

No. This website is designed for general information only. It should not be construed to be formal legal advice or the formation of a lawyer/client relationship.
