What changes

Three fixes the current site needs.

Drawn from a review of weintraublawfirm.com as it stands today. The firm’s content stays the same; how it reaches people changes.

Phones
A layout that fits a phone

The current pages have no mobile viewport and use a table layout, so on a phone they load as a shrunken desktop page. This version reflows to any screen.

Security
A secure address

The site serves over plain HTTP and its HTTPS certificate is invalid, so browsers label it “Not secure.” The rebuild ships on HTTPS.

Clarity
Credentials people (and search tools) can read

About the firm

A South Florida practice for more than four decades.

7700 Congress Ave., Suite 1110 · Boca Raton, Florida 33487

Weintraub & Weintraub, P.A. has provided personalized legal services in the South Florida area for over 40 years, serving individuals and businesses from its Boca Raton office.

The firm also publishes a Real Estate Newsletter on practical questions owners, landlords and tenants run into: security deposits, “as is” clauses in residential contracts, the attractive nuisance doctrine, whether an owner has a right to a view, and lead hazards in homes built before 1978.
