Peter B. Weintraub

A Florida attorney since 1981 whose practice centers on commercial litigation, commercial law and real estate.

Biography

Peter B. Weintraub is a member of the Law Offices of Weintraub & Weintraub, P.A. He was admitted to The Florida Bar in 1981, the same year he earned his J.D. from Nova Southeastern University, and he is admitted to practice before the U.S. District Court for the Southern District of Florida, the U.S. Bankruptcy Court and the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit.

About half of his practice is commercial litigation. The rest spans commercial law, real estate, landlord and tenant law, estate planning, corporate and LLC formation, and bankruptcy law.

A Miami Beach native, he earned his B.A. from the University of Miami in 1978. In law school he was a member of Phi Alpha Delta and the Moot Court Society, and champion of the Freshman Moot Court Competition.
