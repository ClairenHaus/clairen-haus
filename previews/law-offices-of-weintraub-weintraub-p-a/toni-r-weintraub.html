Toni R. Weintraub

A Florida attorney since 1983 whose practice includes commercial litigation, real estate, business law and contracts.

Biography

Toni R. Weintraub is a member of the Law Offices of Weintraub & Weintraub, P.A. She was admitted to The Florida Bar in 1983, the year she earned her J.D. from Nova Southeastern University.

Her practice includes commercial litigation, real estate, business law, contracts and general practice. She earned her B.A. from the University of Florida in 1977.
