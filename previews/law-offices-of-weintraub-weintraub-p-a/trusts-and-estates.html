Trusts & Estates

Estate planning and trusts for individuals and families, from an attorney who is a member of The Florida Bar’s Real Estate and Probate Section.

What trusts and estates covers

Estate planning is the process of deciding, in advance, how your property will be managed and passed on, and who will make decisions if you cannot. A trust is one of the main tools for doing that: an arrangement in which property is held and managed for the people you name.

An estate planning attorney helps you understand the options, prepares the documents that put your plan in place, and helps trustees and family members carry out their responsibilities when the time comes.

Matters this can include

  • Estate planning for individuals and families
  • Trusts and trust administration questions
  • Planning for property you own in Florida
  • Guidance for trustees and family members
  • Coordination with your will and probate questions
