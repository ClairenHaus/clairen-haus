What changes

A site that tells machines it is a law firm, with answers on the page.

Two specific things the current site gets in the way of, and what this concept does about them. The firm's own content stays; how it is described to search engines and AI assistants changes.

  • Today: schema never says "law firm"
    Typed as a legal service

    The current site's structured data describes a generic Organization and WebPage (BreadcrumbList, CollectionPage, ImageObject) and never a LegalService, Attorney or LocalBusiness, so AI assistants cannot confirm what the firm does or where. This page carries LegalService with the full address, Person entries for both attorneys and a FAQPage.

  • Today: no FAQ, no address on the homepage
    Answers and location up front

    Six of thirteen images on the current homepage lack alt text and the street address is absent from it. Here the Scottsdale address, phone and email sit in the page, every image is described, and a short FAQ answers the questions the firm's pages already answer.

Common questions

Answers before the first call.

Each answer below is drawn from what the firm already publishes, written so a person or an AI assistant can quote it directly.

Where is Lotzar Law Firm located?

The office is at 8687 E. Via de Ventura, Suite 115, Scottsdale, Arizona 85258. The main number is (480) 905-0300 and the email is info@lotzar.com.

What kinds of clients does the firm represent?

Corporations, nonprofits, investors, developers and entrepreneurs, including real estate developers, contractors and property managers. Representative clients named on the site include the Industrial Development Authority of the City of Tucson, Adolfson & Peterson Construction, Native American Connections and Chicanos Por La Causa.

Does the firm work on affordable housing and tax credits?

Yes. The firm lists affordable housing, tax-credit finance and syndication among its four core areas, including Low-Income Housing Tax Credits and New Markets Tax Credits, and Mary Sue Lotzar practices in Low-Income Housing Tax-Credits and real estate development.

Which states does the firm serve?

The firm states that it represents clients throughout Arizona, Texas, Utah and Michigan.

When was the firm founded and by whom?

Charles W. Lotzar founded Lotzar Law Firm, P.C. in August 2005 after serving as a senior partner at Kutak Rock LLP.

About the practice

The information clients need before they call.

Founded August 2005 by Charles W. Lotzar

Best Lawyers since 2013 · Super Lawyers since 2014 (Charles W. Lotzar, as listed on the firm's site)

Lotzar Law Firm describes its greatest attribute as the ability to approach projects from a business perspective: rather than focusing on obstacles, it seeks alternatives that create a better path for clients. Its goal is to be a preferred business partner at every phase of growing a company, advising on rights and responsibilities, evaluating risks and opportunities, and working toward the client's long- and short-term vision.

The firm is a team-based environment. Its two attorneys, Charles W. and Mary Sue Lotzar, have practiced together at the firm since 2005, and the firm describes a staff that is accessible and eager to answer questions whether a matter is small or large in scope.
