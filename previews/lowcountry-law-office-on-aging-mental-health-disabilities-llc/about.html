About the office

Guidance, counsel and, ultimately, peace of mind.

We dedicate ourselves to the citizens of the Lowcountry: individuals and families dealing with aging spouses and parents, mental and physical disabilities, and children with special needs.

Focused entirely on one kind of family

At the Lowcountry Law Office on Aging, Mental Health & Disabilities, we focus entirely and exclusively on the needs of individuals and families who are struggling with aging spouses and parents, mental and physical disabilities, and children with special needs.

Our services go well beyond those of a traditional estate planning and probate practice. We help clients successfully age in place, collect government benefits to pay for care or for expenses when working is no longer possible, plan for the future of a child with special needs, and cope with the effects of a loved one’s mental illness. We also prepare wills, trusts and powers of attorney for those who are able to plan ahead.

We come to you

We understand that it is often too difficult to bring a loved one to a law office. As a courtesy to clients and their families, we can go to the home, hospital room, nursing home room or assisted-living apartment of our clients. In short: we will help your loved one, no matter where they are.

A team around your loved one

We work with your doctors, your accountants, your hospice nurses and social workers, your professional caregivers and, most importantly, your entire family, to make sure your loved one’s health, safety and well-being are protected.

Free initial consultations

Your first consultation is free. If we are unavailable or out of the office when you call, please leave a detailed voicemail, including your phone number, and we will make every attempt to return your call as soon as possible.

Lunch-n-Learn seminars

The office offers small-group Lunch-n-Learn seminars on the last Wednesday of each month (dates may change in November and December for the holidays). Seating is very limited, so please call (843) 416-1102 to reserve a spot, or email Alyson Fudge at acf@lowcountrylawofc.com or Misty McCann at mmm@lowcountrylawofc.com.

If you or your company is hosting or sponsoring an event for seniors or people with mental or physical disabilities, let us know and we’ll share it.
