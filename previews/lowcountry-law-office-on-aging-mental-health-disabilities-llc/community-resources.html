Community resources

Local resources we trust.

This is not meant to be a complete list of local resources. It is a list of organizations our clients have had repeated positive experiences with, and that we feel comfortable referring others to.

Non-profit, municipal and government resources

For seniors and people with disabilities in the Charleston area:

Independent Transportation Network (ITN) Charleston TridentDignified transportation for seniors 65+ and people with visual impairments.
Lowcountry Senior NetworkA local network serving seniors. Alyson Fudge is a past board member.
South Carolina Aging in Place CoalitionAlyson Fudge is its Education Committee Chair and a past board member.
Association for the BlindIn-home comprehensive vision rehabilitation.
Alzheimer’s Association, South Carolina ChapterRespite assistance grants, resource information and care consultations.
Center for Heirs’ Property PreservationPreserving family-owned land.
City of Charleston Mayor’s Office on AgingAdvocating for policies that protect the dignity and independence of seniors.
Charleston Habitat for HumanityHome repairs for homeowners.
Dorchester County DSS / Adult Protective ServicesSafety and well-being of older adults and adults with disabilities.
East Cooper Meals on WheelsDaily nutrition delivered to seniors.
Lowcountry Senior CenterHolistic wellness to keep you engaged, energized and connected.
Hospice of CharlestonComfort and end-of-life care.
Mount Pleasant Police DepartmentAn advocate for senior crime victims.
MUSC Center on AgingResearch, service and education.
Respite Care MinistriesSupportive services for people with memory loss.
