Directions

We’re in the eWall Street Office Park, just over the Ravenel Bridge off Highway 17 North, about two and a half traffic lights from the base of the bridge.

From Summerville, Goose Creek, North Charleston, Johns Island or Daniel Island

  1. Take I-26 East toward Charleston, then the exit for Mt. Pleasant.
  2. On the Ravenel Bridge, stay in one of the two left lanes and follow the signs for Highway 17 North.
  3. At the second traffic light (Mathis Ferry Road on the left, Houston Northcutt Blvd on the right), turn left onto Mathis Ferry Road.
  4. At the first light, turn right onto the frontage road, which runs parallel to Highway 17 North.
  5. Turn left on the first street after the Palmetto Boat Store. This is eWall Street, marked by a street sign and a brick “eWall Street Office Park” sign.
  6. Drive past the tree-lined island and take the first left into the office park lot. We’re in the one-story building in the far back right corner; our sign is visible from the lot.

From Downtown Charleston, West Ashley or James Island

Depending on where you start, you may prefer the route above. Otherwise, follow Highway 17 North to the Ravenel Bridge, stay in one of the two left lanes, follow the signs to remain on Highway 17 North, and continue with step 3 above.
