Planning ahead

Can my adult children sign a Medicaid application for me?

Not in South Carolina unless they have been appointed under your power of attorney. The same is true for spouses.

Will my power of attorney or living will from another state work here?

It may not. South Carolina’s rules are very specific, and banks and hospitals here may not be obligated to accept out-of-state documents.

What is a special needs trust?

A trust that holds property for a child or adult with a disability, designed to supplement, not replace, government benefits such as SSI.

Can the person with a disability be the trustee of their own special needs trust?

No. The premise of the trust is that the beneficiary does not control its principal or income.

Benefits

How long must a disability last to qualify for Social Security?

At least a year (365 days), or it must be expected to result in death. You can apply before the year has passed.

How long do I have to appeal a Social Security disability denial?

Appeals must be filed within 60 days of the denial.

Does Medicaid pay for care at home?

South Carolina Medicaid pays for Home and Community-Based Waiver services that help people who need a nursing-home level of care remain at home, as well as nursing facility care.

Guardianship and probate

What is the difference between a guardian and a conservator?

A guardian makes day-to-day living, placement and medical decisions. A conservator handles finances, pays bills and applies for benefits. One person can serve as both, but they are separate appointments.

Is an attorney required for guardianship?

In Charleston County, yes. In Berkeley and Dorchester counties an attorney is not required, but the Court strongly urges families to have one.

Why does an estate need to be probated?

Estates must be probated whether or not there is a will, so property, especially real estate, can legally pass to someone living.

How long does probate take?

About one year. Creditors have eight months from the published notice to make a claim.

What should I bring to start probate?

A certified copy of the death certificate with a raised seal, a copy of the funeral program, and the names and addresses of all living blood relatives.
