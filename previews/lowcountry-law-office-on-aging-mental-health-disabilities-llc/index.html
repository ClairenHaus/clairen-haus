How we help

More than wills and probate.

We help clients age in place, collect government benefits to pay for care or replace income when working is no longer possible, plan for the future of a child with special needs, and cope with a loved one’s mental illness. And of course, we prepare wills, trusts and powers of attorney for those who are able to plan ahead.

Estate & Pre-Planning

Wills, trusts, durable powers of attorney and health care directives, drafted to meet South Carolina’s specific rules.

If they can’t come to us

We can, and will, come to you.

It is often too difficult to bring a loved one to a law office. As a courtesy to clients and their families, Alyson Fudge will meet your loved one at home, in a hospital room, in a nursing home room or in an assisted-living apartment.

ACFAttorney & Founder
Your attorney

Alyson C. Fudge

Alyson founded the firm to focus on the needs and concerns of seniors, people living with mental illness, injury or physical disabilities, and children with special needs and their families. Before private practice she served as a judicial clerk in the Charleston County Probate Court, working in its Estate, Guardian and Conservatorship, and Mental Health Commitment divisions.

She speaks regularly to the public and to other lawyers, and was a featured speaker on estate planning and Medicaid at the 2013 South Carolina Bar Annual Convention.
