Common questions

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes. The firm offers free initial consultations.

Can an attorney come to us?

Yes. The attorneys can go to the home, hospital room, nursing home room or assisted-living apartment of a client when it is too difficult to bring a loved one to the office.

Why is it necessary to probate an estate?

Estates must be probated whether or not there is a will. Probate is necessary to legally transfer ownership of property, especially real estate, so the deed is in the name of someone living and the property can be sold.

What should I bring to start the probate of an estate?

A certified copy of the death certificate (with raised seal), a copy of the funeral program, and a list of the names and addresses of all living blood relatives.

How long does probate take?

About one year. When an estate is opened, a notice to creditors is published in a local newspaper, and potential creditors have eight months to make a claim.
