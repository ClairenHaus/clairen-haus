Practice areas

Focused entirely on aging, mental health & disabilities.

Our services go well beyond a traditional estate planning and probate practice. Each area below has its own page explaining the basics in plain language.

Estate & Pre-Planning

Wills, trusts, durable powers of attorney and health care directives, drafted to meet South Carolina’s specific rules.

Learn more →

Medicaid & Long-Term Care

Help applying for Medicaid to pay for care in a nursing facility or at home through South Carolina’s waiver services.

Learn more →

Social Security Disability (SSDI & SSI)

Applications and appeals for people who can no longer work because of a medical or mental health condition.

Learn more →

Special Needs Planning & Trusts

Planning for two generations: special needs trusts that protect a child’s quality of life and benefits.

Learn more →

Guardianship & Conservatorship

Probate Court petitions when a loved one has lost capacity and can no longer make decisions safely.

Learn more →

Mental Health Law

Advocacy for adults and children with mental illness, including commitment proceedings and capacity issues.

Learn more →

Probate & Estate Administration

Guiding families through the probate process after a loss, so they can focus on each other.

Learn more →
