About

Meet Lecrecia Williams

Owner and tax preparer of LSW Taxes, a family owned and operated tax preparation service.

My name is Lecrecia Williams

Owner/Tax Preparer of LSW Taxes. LSW Taxes is a family owned and operated income tax preparation service. The company was formed in Chicago, Illinois, and is now based in Hazel Crest, Illinois, offering virtual tax preparation to clients nationwide.

Experience

Lecrecia concentrated in accounting at DeVry’s Chicago campus, and she has held various positions within the accounting field since 2003.

In 2002 she joined Jackson Hewitt as an office supervisor and tax preparer. She exceeded expectations, was promoted to a management position and began floating between multiple tax offices. Her love for income tax preparation increased daily.

She began teaching the Basic Income Tax Course in 2006. Ninety-eight percent of her students passed and were employed by a company as tax preparers. The same year, she taught a class on customer service skills. She is also an authorized IRS e-file provider.
