FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers about working with LSW Taxes.

Can I have my taxes prepared virtually?

Yes. LSW Taxes offers virtual tax preparation and serves clients nationwide, so you don’t need to come to an office.

Where is LSW Taxes based?

LSW Taxes was formed in Chicago, Illinois, and is now based in Hazel Crest, Illinois.

Who do you prepare taxes for?

Individuals, the self-employed and small businesses.

Can you prepare prior-year or amended returns?

Yes. LSW Taxes prepares prior-year returns and amended returns as well as current-year returns.

Are you only open during tax season?

No. Tax preparation is available year round.

I got a letter from the IRS. Can you help?

LSW Taxes offers assistance with IRS communication. Call (888) 407-6796 or email lswtaxes@yahoo.com and have the letter handy.

Do you file electronically?

Yes. Lecrecia Williams is an authorized IRS e-file provider, so your return is filed electronically.

Do you offer refund advances?

Ask about refund advance options when you book. Refund advances depend on the bank’s approval.

Who will prepare my return?

Lecrecia Williams, owner and tax preparer of LSW Taxes. She joined Jackson Hewitt as an office supervisor and tax preparer in 2002 and began teaching the Basic Income Tax Course in 2006.

How do I get started?

Call (888) 407-6796 or email lswtaxes@yahoo.com to book your appointment.
