Choosing a preparer

Choose a preparer you can reach

“Choose a preparer you will be able to contact after the return is filed and one that will be responsive to your needs.”The IRS’s advice, as quoted by LSW Taxes

Taxpayers are legally responsible for what’s on their returns, even if someone else prepares them. At LSW Taxes you work with a trained tax preparer who understands your situation and can answer your tax questions with friendly, professional service.
