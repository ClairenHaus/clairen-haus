Self-employed and business owners welcome

Whether you freelance, drive for an app, run a side business or own a small company, your taxes look different from a W-2 employee’s. LSW Taxes prepares returns for the self-employed and small business owners as well as individuals.

In general, self-employment income and business expenses are reported with your return, and self-employed people may owe self-employment tax and need to make estimated tax payments during the year. Good records make all of it easier.

  • Freelancers and contractors
  • 1099 income
  • Business income and expenses
  • Small business owners

Records worth keeping

Keep your 1099s, a record of what you earned, receipts or statements for business expenses, and any estimated payments you made. Bring what you have and ask about the rest.
