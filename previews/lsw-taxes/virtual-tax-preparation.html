Filing season basics

In general, the IRS opens the tax filing season in late January, and the deadline for most people to file and pay is in mid-April. An extension gives you more time to file, typically until mid-October, but not more time to pay any tax owed. Tax preparation at LSW Taxes is available year round.

What to have ready

Your W-2s and 1099s, records of other income, records of business income and expenses if you’re self-employed, documents for any deductions or credits, and last year’s return if you have it.
