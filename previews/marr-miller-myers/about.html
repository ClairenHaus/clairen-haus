About the firm

A full-service firm, built on relationships

Marr, Miller & Myers, PSC serves businesses and individuals throughout Southeastern and Central Kentucky from its office on Summit Drive in Corbin.

Who we are

Marr, Miller & Myers, PSC is a full-service accounting firm. We provide audit, review and compilation, tax, bookkeeping, payroll and consulting services to business and individual clients across Southeastern and Central Kentucky.

What we believe

We believe in the value of relationships. We view every client relationship like a partnership, and truly believe that our success is a result of your success.

We are committed to close, personal attention, and we take pride in giving you the assurance that the help you receive comes from years of advanced training, technical experience and financial acumen. We pride ourselves on being proactive and responsive to our clients’ questions and suggestions.

Experience you can trace

  • Five partners, every one a CPA; eight CPAs on the team
  • Partner Rebecca Myers, a CPA since 1979, served on the Kentucky Society of CPAs Board of Directors (1996–1999) and was appointed to the State Board of Accountancy
  • Partner Nancy Logan has been in public practice with the firm since 1986
  • Office Manager Hazel Carnes has run the office since 1993
  • Transactions from small to very complex, with values in the hundreds of millions of dollars

In Corbin

Rebecca Myers advises the local economic development boards and the Southeast Kentucky Chamber of Commerce, and chaired the Corbin Industrial Commission from 1997 to 2007.
