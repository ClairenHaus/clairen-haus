About Archie

Archie is a 1976 graduate of Indiana University with a Bachelor of Science degree in Accounting. After graduation he received his CPA certification in both Indiana and Kentucky, and he has worked in public accounting his entire career.

He has served as an instructor, discussion leader and lecturer for the AICPA, the University of Kentucky Management Center, the Indiana Society of Certified Public Accountants and the Kentucky Real Estate Education and Research Center.

Areas of experience

  • Acquisition and valuation strategies
  • Business and financial planning
  • Management advisory services
  • Income tax matters and representation before taxing authorities
