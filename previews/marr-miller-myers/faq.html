FAQ

Questions clients ask

Straight answers about the firm, our services and how to reach us.

Where is your office?

Our office is at 500 Summit Drive, Corbin, Kentucky 40701. We serve clients throughout Southeastern and Central Kentucky. Call (606) 528-2454 for directions or to set a time.

What services do you offer?

Audits, reviews and compilations; tax preparation and tax planning; estate and trust tax returns; IRS representation; bookkeeping, write-up and payroll; business consulting, entity selection and succession planning; buying and selling a business, mergers and acquisitions; and estate and retirement planning. The list isn’t all-inclusive, so ask if you don’t see what you need.

Who are the partners?

Archie Marr, CPA (Managing Partner), Rebecca Myers, CPA, Anthony Combs, CPA, Nancy Logan, CPA, and Eric Jones, CPA. You can read each partner’s background on the Our Team page.

Which industries do you work with?

Construction, mining, manufacturing, utilities, colleges and universities, restaurants and hospitality, professional services, and not-for-profit organizations. If your field isn’t listed, call us anyway.

What kinds of tax returns do you prepare?

Returns for individuals, corporations, partnerships, trusts, estates and not-for-profit organizations, prepared with computerized tax preparation and research software.

Can you represent me before the IRS or the state?

Yes. We represent clients before federal and state tax agencies. Bring the notice you received and call the office before you respond to it.

What’s the difference between an audit, a review and a compilation?

An audit is an intensive examination of your company’s systems and records and gives the highest level of assurance. A review involves some analytical procedures and gives limited assurance. A compilation is based on information you provide and is used primarily for internal purposes.

How do I request an appointment?

Call (606) 528-2454, email office@marrmillermyers.com, or use the request form on the Contact page and tell us who you’d like to meet with.
