Client services

What we do for our clients

A wide range of services for business and individual clients, each with personalized, quality attention. If you don’t see a service listed, ask.

  1. 01Audits, Reviews & CompilationsThree levels of assurance on your financial statements, from a compilation for internal use to a full audit.
  2. 02Tax PreparationReturns for individuals, corporations, partnerships, trusts, estates and not-for-profit organizations.
  3. 03Tax PlanningTax planning as part of your overall business strategy, to minimize current and future liabilities.
  4. 04IRS RepresentationRepresentation before federal and state tax agencies, backed by years of dealing with taxing authorities.
  5. 05Bookkeeping & PayrollDay-to-day bookkeeping and write-up, a reliable payroll system and timely payroll tax returns.
  6. 06Business ConsultingA sounding board for management, entity selection and succession planning that is practical and technically sound.
  7. 07Buying, Selling & MergersStructuring a purchase, sale, merger or acquisition, from small transactions to very complex ones.
  8. 08Estate & Retirement PlanningOrderly transfer of assets to your beneficiaries, and a financial plan that carries you through retirement.
