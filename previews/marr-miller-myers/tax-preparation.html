Estate and trust returns

Effective estate and gift planning facilitates the orderly transfer of assets to your beneficiaries and can reduce or eliminate the tax due on that transfer. We prepare the estate and trust returns that go with it, and for business owners we keep business continuity and succession in view.

Planning comes first

Tax planning is an essential element of the tax preparation process. A conversation before year end often does more for your return than anything done in April. More on tax planning.
