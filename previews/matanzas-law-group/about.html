About the firm

Caring about you, your family, your business, your legacy.

That phrase reflects the underlying mission of all the legal work Matanzas Law Firm, P.A. performs for its clients.

Our mission

At Matanzas Law Firm, P.A., we vow to represent you with the compassion, determination, knowledge and skill you deserve. We pride ourselves on being zealous advocates for our clients and on providing exceptional personal service.

Understanding that consulting a lawyer is never easy, often intimidating and involves life-changing events, Matanzas Law Firm’s mission is not simply to provide outstanding legal services, but to ensure that our clients have a complete understanding of the legal process. We will take the time to explain the process, answer your questions and, hopefully, calm your fears. Working together, we will address your legal, financial, personal and family needs, beginning with your initial consultation to assess your specific case and legal options.

Your goals are our goals, and we will fight for you until we achieve them.

Above all, Matanzas Law Firm, P.A. is committed to bringing the highest principles of ethics and a zeal for justice to you and your case.

Our history

Matanzas Law Firm, P.A. was formerly Tance E. Roberts, P.A. of Clark & Roberts, PLC. Today it is St. Augustine’s law firm for estate planning, tax law and business law, led by Tance E. Roberts, Esquire.

Where we serve

The firm serves the legal needs of St. Johns, Putnam and Flagler Counties. Its main office is at 200 Malaga Street, Suite 9 in St. Augustine, and clients can also meet with the firm in Palatka and Bunnell by appointment.

Rooted in the community

Ms. Roberts is a Master of the Bench of the St. Augustine Inns of Court, chairs the St. Augustine Professional Advisory Council for the Community Foundation of Northeast Florida, and is on the St. Johns County Legal Aid Pro Bono Honor Roll. She is a Paul Harris Fellow of the St. Augustine Rotary Club and serves on the Capital City Bank Community Board.
