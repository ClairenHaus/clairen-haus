FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about the firm, its offices and how to prepare. For anything about your own situation, call the St. Augustine office.

What does Matanzas Law Firm, P.A. handle?

The firm concentrates on estate planning, tax law and business law. It also handles wills, trusts and estates; estate and trust administration; probate and probate litigation; guardianship; elder law and Medicaid planning; real estate; and mediation.

Where are the firm's offices?

The main office is at 200 Malaga Street, Suite 9, St. Augustine, FL 32084, phone 904-826-1772. The firm also meets clients in Palatka (386-325-7705) and Bunnell (386-437-5686). Those two offices are by appointment only.

Which counties does the firm serve?

St. Johns, Putnam and Flagler Counties.

How do I prepare for an estate planning consultation?

About two weeks before your appointment, the firm sends you an Estate Planning Questionnaire. It gives you time to gather documents and information about your assets, liabilities and estate, so the attorney is better informed and you can get more precise answers about your options.

What should I bring to a tax or business appointment?

Bring all documentation pertaining to your tax matter, contract or other business transaction, along with valid ID.

What should I bring for a probate, guardianship or elder law matter?

Bring original wills and other estate planning documents, asset and liability information, family contacts, death certificates and medical information, as appropriate. Anything else needed is discussed at the initial consultation.

Do I need to bring identification?

Yes. Please bring valid ID to every appointment; the firm will need to make a copy.

Is Tance E. Roberts board certified?

Yes. She is Florida Bar Board Certified in Tax Law. She also holds an LL.M. in Taxation from the University of Florida College of Law and is an Accredited Estate Planner® through the National Association of Estate Planners & Councils.

Why talk to a tax attorney early?

Early intervention by a Florida Bar Board Certified Tax Attorney is critical to preventing damage and securing a favorable resolution of tax matters with the IRS and state tax agencies. Consulting before you enter into contracts and business transactions can also help avoid adverse tax consequences.

Does the firm offer mediation?

Yes. Tance E. Roberts is a Circuit Civil Mediator, and mediation is one of the firm's practice areas.

What happens after the initial consultation?

At the first meeting the firm gathers the information needed to assess your situation and options. A plan of action is then formulated, and further engagement happens as needed from there.

How quickly will I hear back if I send a message?

The firm's contact page says that, if needed, you will hear back within 48 to 72 hours. For anything time-sensitive, call the St. Augustine office at 904-826-1772.

Was the firm known by another name?

Yes. Matanzas Law Firm, P.A. was formerly Tance E. Roberts, P.A. of Clark & Roberts, PLC.
