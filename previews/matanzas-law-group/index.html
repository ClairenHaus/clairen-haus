Common questions

Before you call

What does Matanzas Law Firm, P.A. handle?

The firm concentrates on estate planning, tax law and business law. It also handles wills, trusts and estates; estate and trust administration; probate and probate litigation; guardianship; elder law and Medicaid planning; real estate; and mediation.

Where are the firm's offices?

The main office is at 200 Malaga Street, Suite 9, St. Augustine, FL 32084, phone 904-826-1772. The firm also meets clients in Palatka (386-325-7705) and Bunnell (386-437-5686). Those two offices are by appointment only.

How do I prepare for an estate planning consultation?

About two weeks before your appointment, the firm sends you an Estate Planning Questionnaire. It gives you time to gather documents and information about your assets, liabilities and estate, so the attorney is better informed and you can get more precise answers about your options.

Is Tance E. Roberts board certified?

Yes. She is Florida Bar Board Certified in Tax Law. She also holds an LL.M. in Taxation from the University of Florida College of Law and is an Accredited Estate Planner® through the National Association of Estate Planners & Councils.

Does the firm offer mediation?

Yes. Tance E. Roberts is a Circuit Civil Mediator, and mediation is one of the firm's practice areas.
