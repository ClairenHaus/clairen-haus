About the practice

Taking the time to explain the process.

Serving St. Johns, Putnam and Flagler Counties

Offices in St. Augustine, Palatka and Bunnell

Understanding that consulting a lawyer is never easy, often intimidating and involves life-changing events, the firm’s mission is not simply to provide outstanding legal services, but to ensure clients have a complete understanding of the legal process.

The firm will take the time to explain the process, answer your questions, and address your legal, financial, personal and family needs, beginning with an initial consultation to assess your specific case and options.

Common questions

How do I prepare for an estate planning consultation?

About two weeks before your appointment, the firm sends an Estate Planning Questionnaire so you have time to gather documents and information about your assets, liabilities and estate. Please bring valid ID.

What should I bring for a tax or business matter?

Bring all documentation pertaining to your tax matter, contract or other business transaction, and valid ID.

What should I bring for probate, guardianship or elder law?

Original wills and other estate planning documents, asset and liability information, family contacts, death certificates and medical information, as appropriate, plus valid ID.

Is Tance E. Roberts board certified?

Yes. She is Florida Bar Board Certified in Tax Law and holds an LL.M. in Taxation from the University of Florida College of Law.
