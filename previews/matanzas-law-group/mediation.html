Mediation

Mediation

A confidential, structured way to resolve a civil dispute with the help of a neutral mediator, where the parties keep control of the outcome.

How mediation works

In mediation, a neutral mediator meets with the parties to a dispute and helps them talk through the issues, understand each other’s positions and explore possible solutions. The mediator does not decide the case. Any agreement is the parties’ own, and it is usually written down and signed at the end of a successful session.

Why parties choose it

  • It is generally faster and less expensive than taking a case through trial.
  • Discussions are confidential under Florida law, with limited exceptions.
  • The parties shape the result, rather than leaving it to a judge or jury.
  • It can preserve family and business relationships that a courtroom fight would strain.

Florida courts frequently refer civil cases to mediation, and parties may also agree to mediate before a lawsuit is filed.
