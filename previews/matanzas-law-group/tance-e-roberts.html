Attorney

Tance E. Roberts, Esquire

LL.M. in Taxation · Florida Bar Board Certified in Tax Law · Accredited Estate Planner® · Circuit Civil Mediator

Tance E. Roberts concentrates her practice in the areas of federal and state taxation, estate planning, estate and trust administration, probate and guardianship, business and corporate law, tax planning, contracts, and mediation.

In addition, she has extensive experience in the formation, operation, reorganization, purchase and sale and dissolution of business entities; Medicaid planning; real estate law; and municipal and local government law.

She is Florida Bar Board Certified in Tax Law, holds an LL.M. in Taxation from the University of Florida College of Law, is an Accredited Estate Planner® through the National Association of Estate Planners & Councils, and is a Circuit Civil Mediator.
