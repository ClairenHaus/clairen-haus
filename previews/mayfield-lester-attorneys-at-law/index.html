About the practice

Let us do the hard part.

Creditor representation only in bankruptcy cases · Hamilton and surrounding counties

Mayfield & Lester is a Chattanooga, Tennessee based law firm focused on creditors’ rights and debt collections. Its work is representing creditors trying to safeguard against unpaid debt, protecting creditors’ interests in bankruptcy cases, and handling landlord-tenant issues.

From the time an account enters the office, the firm’s priority is to collect payments in full, regardless of the balance. For landlords, it guides the proper procedure to regain possession of the property and recover money owed. When a customer, client or renter files bankruptcy, its detail-oriented staff makes sure the claim is filed correctly and on time, through filing, the ongoing case, and dismissal or discharge.

Common questions

Answers from the firm’s own site.

Each answer below is drawn from facts the firm already publishes, marked up so search engines and AI assistants can cite it. General information only, not legal advice.

How does Mayfield & Lester charge for collections?

Debt collections are handled on a contingent fee: the firm does not get paid unless you get paid. For the majority of its bankruptcy services, it uses a flat fee billing structure.

Can the firm help a landlord with an eviction in Chattanooga?

Yes. The firm handles detainers (evictions) in the Chattanooga area, whether for possession of the property or a money judgment on back rent and damages.

Does the firm represent debtors in bankruptcy?

No. In bankruptcy cases the firm represents creditors only, from the debtor’s initial filing through the ongoing case to dismissal or discharge.

How can clients check on accounts they have placed?

Through the firm’s creditor access website, MLCAW.com, where clients can search accounts, see detailed collection activity, and submit new accounts with scanned PDF documents.

Can I email the firm about an account placed for collection?

The firm cannot reply to emails about accounts placed for collection. Call the office at 423-622-2021 between 8:30 and 5:00 to discuss the account.
