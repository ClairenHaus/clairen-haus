About the firm

Rooted in downtown Concord since 1953.

Medlin & Medlin, P.A. was founded in 1953. Today Steve Medlin practices from the firm’s office on Union Street South, in the old section of downtown Concord.

A hometown practice

A lawyer who knows this community.

Steve grew up walking these streets and learned to appreciate and love the community. When he graduated from law school he moved downtown and walked to work every day, and the local business people and neighbors he met became lifelong friends and clients.

That is still the practice today: one-to-one attention, phone calls returned, and custom legal work instead of templates, all aimed at keeping clients out of the very expensive courtroom.

In the community

Decades of service to Concord.

Steve has spent most of his life in Concord’s civic organizations and has spoken up for many local causes.

Rotary

25+ years

Active in the Rotary Club for more than 25 years, and a speaker to Rotary clubs across the area on community affairs and charitable work.

Toastmasters

Since the 1970s

A Toastmasters member most of his life and still active, he has taught public speaking to local students and adults.

Lions & Jaycees

Service clubs

Ten years in the Lions Club, whose work includes eye banks and blindness research, and years with the Concord Jaycees.

Downtown

Local causes

A public speaker for causes such as preserving historic downtown Concord, keeping county government downtown, and school bond funding.

Historic Concord

An amateur historian of downtown.

Concord is home to 197 houses and buildings on the national historic register, and Steve walks year-round to visit them. He still hikes the North Carolina mountains, and has recorded short videos about downtown landmarks such as the James William Cannon House and the Concord National Bank and Hotel Concord.

Client and community videos are on the firm’s YouTube channel.
