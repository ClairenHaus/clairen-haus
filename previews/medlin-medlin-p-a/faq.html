Frequently asked questions

Questions people ask before they call.

Straight answers about the firm, the practice areas and how to reach Steve Medlin.

Where is the office?

Medlin & Medlin, P.A. is at 43 Union Street South, Concord, NC 28025, in the old section of downtown Concord.

What kinds of legal matters does the firm handle?

The firm focuses on four areas: estate planning, estate settlement, business contracts and incorporations.

How long has the firm been in Concord?

Medlin & Medlin was founded in 1953. Steve Medlin has practiced law with the firm since 1977.

Will I work directly with Steve Medlin?

Yes. Steve works with clients one to one, listens to their situation and returns their phone calls.

What does “keeping you out of the courtroom” mean?

Steve’s approach is to do the legal work right the first time, so clients avoid expensive court proceedings and save money in the long run.

Which communities do you serve?

Clients come from many communities, including Concord, Kannapolis, Harrisburg, Mt. Pleasant, Richfield, China Grove, Granite Quarry, Mint Hill, Charlotte, Gold Hill, Stanfield and Landis.

What can an estate plan include?

An estate plan can include a will, trusts, a durable power of attorney, a health care power of attorney and a living will. Steve writes each plan for the client’s own family and situation.

I’ve been named executor of an estate. Can the firm help?

Yes. Steve helps executors and administrators settle estates, including the filings with the court, tax returns, bills and claims, and the paperwork with heirs and the government.

Which business structures can you help me set up?

The firm helps clients choose and form a sole proprietorship, partnership, C or S corporation, or limited liability company (LLC).

Can you help a church incorporate?

Yes. Steve has taught many churches in the greater Concord and Cabarrus County area how incorporating can help limit a church’s liability.

Does Steve speak to groups?

Yes. Steve speaks to churches, businesses, schools and civic groups, with seminars on estate planning, estate settlement, incorporation and business contracts.

How do I get in touch?

Call the office at (704) 786-8173 or email info@stevemedlinlaw.com.
