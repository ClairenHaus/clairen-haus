Incorporations · Business formation

Start your business on the right legal footing.

Sole proprietorships, partnerships, C and S corporations and LLCs: Steve helps you choose the form that fits, and forms it correctly.

Forming a business is a legal matter

A corporation or LLC is meant to protect your personal assets from the business’s liabilities. Forming one is complex: miss a paper or do one thing wrong, and that protection can fail, leaving you personally liable for business debts.

Four basic ways to operate a business

  • Sole proprietorship. You operate alone, with no corporation or LLC, and carry the risks personally.
  • Partnership. Others own the business with you, which brings questions of shared liability and what happens in a breakup.
  • Corporation. Including the choice between an S corporation and a C corporation.
  • LLC (limited liability company). There are several types of LLC, each with pros and cons.
