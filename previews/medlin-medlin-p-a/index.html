Meet Steve Medlin

Recognition

Honored by the profession and his hometown.

Steve Medlin has been awarded the Chief Justice’s Professionalism Certificate, and in 2023 he received the Liberty Bell Award.

Away from the office he has spent decades in Concord’s civic life: the Rotary Club, Toastmasters, the Lions Club and the Jaycees, along with years of public speaking for local causes.
