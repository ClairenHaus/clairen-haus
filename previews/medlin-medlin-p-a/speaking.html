Speaking & seminars

A speaker for your church, business or civic group.

Steve Medlin is an accomplished public speaker with experience before all kinds of audiences. His seminars help people understand estate planning, estate settlement, incorporation and business contracts.

Seminar topics

  • Estate planning: wills, trusts and powers of attorney
  • Estate settlement: what executors and administrators need to know
  • Incorporation, including church incorporation and limiting a church’s liability
  • Business contracts

Steve has presented to civic groups, churches, schools and businesses throughout the Metrolina area. Past hosts include area United Methodist, Presbyterian, Baptist and Reformed churches, Lions and Optimist clubs, and the Retired School Personnel Association.

An experienced speaker

  • Active with Toastmasters International since the mid-1970s, competing for national and international awards, including as a finalist in the World Championship of Public Speaking.
  • Won the Jaycees state speech contest four times and competed at the national finals.
  • Has spoken to Rotary clubs across the area on community affairs and charitable work.
  • Holds basic and advanced lay speaker certifications in the United Methodist Church and has taught classes and given sermons for churches of many denominations.
