Steve L. Medlin

Concord’s homegrown lawyer, practicing with Medlin & Medlin in downtown Concord since 1977.

Call (704) 786-8173

Steve Medlin grew up in Concord, North Carolina. He went to Mt. Pleasant High School, then to UNC Charlotte, and took his law degree at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. Then he came right back to his hometown to practice law at 43 Union Street South, in the old section of downtown Concord.

“I am the hometown country lawyer. I return phone calls. I listen to people. I am involved in my community, and I do custom work for people with unique legal problems.”Steve L. Medlin

Steve does not believe in templates or the cookie-cutter approach to law. Each client’s needs are unique, so he listens and works with them one to one to settle each situation in its own way. He believes in saving clients money in the long run by doing the work right and avoiding expensive court proceedings.

Knowing his community is part of how he does it. Through decades of civic work he knows the people and the system around Concord, and how things get done here.
