About the firm

On the Avenues, serving greater Richmond.

5600 Grove Avenue · Richmond, Virginia 23226

Meyer, Baldwin, Long & Moore, LLP handles an array of sophisticated legal matters for a diverse client base, from real estate closings and estate plans to business transactions and litigation.

From its office at 5600 Grove Avenue in the near West End, the firm serves clients throughout the greater Richmond metropolitan area and the surrounding counties of Chesterfield, Goochland, Hanover, Henrico and Powhatan.

Common questions
What areas of law does Meyer Baldwin Long & Moore practice?

Residential and commercial real estate, estate planning and administration, business, bankruptcy and creditors’ rights, commercial litigation and collections, employment litigation and civil rights, family law, education law, disability, criminal defense and traffic, and personal injury.

Where is the firm located?

On the Avenues at 5600 Grove Avenue, Richmond, Virginia 23226, in the near West End.

Which areas does the firm serve?

The greater Richmond metropolitan area and the surrounding counties of Chesterfield, Goochland, Hanover, Henrico and Powhatan.

Does the firm handle real estate closings?

Yes. The firm handles residential and commercial real estate transactions, including settlement, recording, escrow and disbursement of funds, and is licensed and bonded under the Virginia Consumer Real Estate Settlement Protection Act.
