About the firm

A different type of law firm, since 1998.

After working at large New York firms, Colette Meyer missed connecting with and helping the people behind the legal documents. She envisioned a more personal lawyer relationship, and built Meyer Law Firm on it.

How the firm works

Five principles behind every matter.

Focused

The firm concentrates on the areas that affect a family’s personal and financial future. That focus has built the depth of knowledge and experience critical to helping you with life’s legal matters.

Experienced and dedicated

Colette has served clients in Palm Beach County and Martin County since founding the firm in 1998, after years at large law firms.

Personal attention

Estate planning is not an easy topic to discuss. As life’s events happen, the team guides you through the legal questions that arise with compassion, and clients speak directly with their attorney.

Quality

The firm takes pride in its work: estate planning documents that give comfort to your loved ones, with trusts fully funded, wishes clearly stated and, most importantly, legally valid.

Life-long representation

The team serves as a resource throughout your life, there to guide you as circumstances and laws change.
