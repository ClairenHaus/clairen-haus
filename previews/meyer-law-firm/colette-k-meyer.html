Attorney profile

Colette K. Meyer, Esq.

Founder of Meyer Law Firm, focused on guiding families through the complicated and often emotional work of estate planning.

Perhaps more than any other legal area, estate planning involves intensely personal issues. Colette focuses on guiding clients through the complicated and often emotional maze of balancing family protection, wealth preservation and cherished family values.

She designed her practice in 1998 with a dedication to life-long legal representation complemented by personal attention. As life’s events happen or laws change, Colette helps you refine your goals and wishes and adjust your estate plan as needed.

Experience

Colette built her legal foundation at renowned New York-based firms. She was counsel in the Palm Beach office of the international firm Donovan, Leisure, Newton & Irvine, and before that an attorney at Mudge, Rose, Guthrie, Alexander & Ferdon and at Nixon, Hargrave, Devans & Doyle. While in law school she worked for the Wisconsin Attorney General.

She has been granted membership in the AARP Legal Services Network, has held memberships in the Jupiter-Tequesta-Hobe Sound Association of Realtors, the National Network of Estate Planning Attorneys and The Family Wealth Planning Institute, and has participated in the adjunct professor program at the University of Miami School of Law.

Community

Colette has a strong commitment to the community where she lives and works, and supports the arts as President of the Lighthouse ArtCenter in Tequesta. Through her immigrant parents she holds U.S. and Swiss dual citizenship, which sparked her love of travel. Her personal interests include downhill skiing and whitewater rafting.
