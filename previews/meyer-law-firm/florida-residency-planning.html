Florida Residency Planning

Beyond the sunny days, Florida offers residents strong asset protection and tax laws. To benefit, your actions must show that Florida is your permanent home.

Florida defines a permanent residence as “that place where a person has his or her true, fixed, and permanent home and principal establishment to which, whenever absent, he or she has the intention of returning.” Meyer Law Firm helps you take the steps needed to establish Florida domicile.

Factors that show intent

Factors that may be considered in determining your intent to establish Florida residency include:

  • A formal Declaration of Domicile
  • Your designated mailing address and informal statements about residency
  • Your place of employment
  • Ending your previous residency in another state or country
  • Registering to vote in Florida
  • A Florida driver’s license, and Florida tags on all your vehicles
  • Using your Florida address on your federal income tax forms

The advantages of Florida residency

Lower property taxes

If you establish homestead, the assessed value of your home for property taxes is reduced, and the “Save Our Homes” cap limits how much that assessment can rise each year.

Home protection

Florida’s homestead rules limit who a home can pass to, for example when there is a surviving spouse or minor children. Without a well thought-out plan, your home may not go to the person you intend.

Creditor protection

Set up correctly, the homestead law can also provide protection against creditors.
