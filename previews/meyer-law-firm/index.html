Meet the founder

A more personal kind of law firm.

After working at large law firms, Colette Meyer found she missed connecting with the people behind the legal documents. In 1998 she opened Meyer Law Firm to offer clients a better, more personal lawyer relationship, built on seeing beyond a set of documents to the real needs of a person or family planning for the future.

Clients speak directly with their attorney. And as life’s events happen or laws change, Colette helps refine your goals and adjust your estate plan, as a legal resource for life.
