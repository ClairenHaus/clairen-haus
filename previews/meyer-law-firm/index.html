What changes

Specific fixes, drawn from the current site.

  • A secure site

    The current site loads over plain HTTP; its HTTPS certificate fails validation, so browsers show “Not secure” to every visitor. The rebuild ships on HTTPS from day one.

  • A current footer and a current platform

    The footer still reads © 2012. The concept replaces the 2012 WordPress theme with a fast, maintainable static page.

  • Schema that says “law firm”

    The current structured data is limited to BreadcrumbList, ImageObject and ListItem. The concept adds LegalService and Person markup with the firm’s full name, address and phone.

  • FAQs that machines can read

    The site already answers client questions on its practice pages, but without FAQPage schema. The concept marks them up so AI assistants can cite them.

Common questions

Answers from the firm’s own site.

Each answer below is drawn from facts the firm already publishes, marked up so search engines and AI assistants can cite it. General information only, not legal advice.

Who founded Meyer Law Firm?

Colette K. Meyer founded the firm in 1998. She earned her J.D. from the University of Wisconsin–Madison in 1985 and is a member of the Florida Bar and the State Bar of Wisconsin.

What are the firm’s office hours?

Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. Saturday, Sunday, before- or after-hours and in-home consultations are available by appointment.

Does the firm help with Florida residency planning?

Yes. The firm helps clients take the steps needed to clearly show intent to establish Florida domicile, so they can benefit from the state’s asset protection and tax laws.

Does the firm handle real estate closings?

Yes. The firm handles real estate transactions and represents the client from contract to closing, and also lists tax appeals.
