Probate and Trust Litigation

Disputes over a will or trust divide families. Meyer Law Firm represents beneficiaries or fiduciaries, working to resolve the dispute as efficiently and effectively as possible.

Probate and trust litigation can arise from many issues surrounding a decedent’s will or trust, including:

  • Claims that the person who signed was not mentally competent
  • Claims of undue influence over the person
  • Concerns about the notarizing or witnessing of the document
  • A breach of fiduciary duty by the personal representative or trustee

Whatever the reason, the team at Meyer Law Firm is sensitive to how difficult contested proceedings are, knowing that financial and emotional issues are intertwined and can divide families.
