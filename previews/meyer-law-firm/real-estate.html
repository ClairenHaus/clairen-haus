Real Estate

For many people, buying a home is the largest financial transaction of their lives. Meyer Law Firm represents you through the entire process, from contract to closing.

The purchase, ownership, development, management and transfer of real estate can have serious implications if not planned and carried out properly. The firm helps you avoid expensive and time-consuming mistakes.

Purchase and sale

After the offer is signed, the firm confirms that title is in proper legal condition and that the necessary contingencies and requirements are satisfied. If you are selling, the firm prepares the deeds and other closing documents. On new construction, it helps set up escrows for uncompleted items. And the firm attends the closing with you.

Title insurance

A thorough title search traces ownership and checks for issues such as construction liens, unpaid real estate taxes, court judgments, forged deeds or undisclosed heirs. Even so, not every issue is apparent. Title insurance protects you: if an issue arises, the title insurer must defend your case, resolve the problem or cover the financial consequences.

Real estate tax appeals

If your Notice of Proposed Property Taxes looks wrong, contact the firm as soon as you receive it, because there are strict deadlines for appeals. Meyer Law Firm has assisted clients in avoiding tax increases and, in certain cases, lowering their assessments.

Common questions about real estate

Answers drawn from the questions new clients ask the firm most often. They are general information, not legal advice for your situation.

What is a title?

A title is the rights of ownership to the property.

What can a title search uncover?

A title search can uncover many issues with title to property including the following:

  • Construction liens.
  • Court judgements.
  • Unpaid property taxes.
  • Improperly executed will.
Why do I need title insurance?

Unfortunately, not all issues can be found even with a thorough title search. Title insurance protects you in these situations since, if issues arise, the title insurer is required to defend your case, resolve the problem, or cover any financial implications.

What does title insurance cost?

The title insurance premium is based on the purchase price and is paid at closing.

Why do I need to have an attorney to help sell or buy my house?

An attorney will protect your interests and ensure proper title and treatment of closing costs. An attorney’s advice can prove invaluable in the following circumstances:

  • The contract terms are not in your favor.
  • Discovering that someone else has claim over the property.
  • Title issues that cause problems for you when you try to sell the property.
  • Breach of contract due to unclear terms or a lack of understanding.
  • A contract that isn’t legal.
