What is a Florida Registered Paralegal?

The Florida Bar created the designation to set high professional standards and give the public added assurance about the quality of legal services. Registered paralegals must meet minimum education, certification or work-experience requirements, follow the Bar’s code of ethics, and complete 30 hours of continuing education every three years, the same requirement Florida Bar attorneys meet.
