Common questions

Answers, drawn from the firm's own site.

  • Which kind of bankruptcy should I file?

    The firm’s site says this is hard to answer without a consultation. Generally, it describes Chapter 7 as a better way to deal with only unsecured debt or to start over, and Chapter 13 as more complex, with plan payments for up to five years, but able to save a home from foreclosure and protect other assets.

  • Will filing bankruptcy stop creditor harassment?

    According to the firm, once the required paperwork is submitted the bankruptcy court issues an automatic stay, which prevents creditors from contacting you to recover money, seize property or place liens on your home.

  • Is the first consultation free?

    Yes. The firm offers a free, private and confidential consultation at its Southwest Florida offices.
