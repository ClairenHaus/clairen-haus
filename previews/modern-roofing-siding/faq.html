FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our work, warranties and service area.

What services do you offer?

We install steel and asphalt roofing, rubber roofing, vinyl siding, vinyl windows, insulated storm and exterior doors and cellulose insulation. We also build decks, garages, sheds and additions, and take on flooring, plumbing, heating and cooling, and all indoor renovations or remodels.

Where do you work?

Our home base is in Nashville, Maine. We have finished jobs from Fort Kent and Allagash to Patten and Benedicta, and if you live in between, we will be happy to help you.

Are consultations free?

Yes. We offer free consultations, and quotes are free.

What warranty comes with a metal roof?

Our metal roofing has a limited 40-year factory warranty on the prepainted metal panels. The strapping and aluminum trim around the fascia board is included in our free quote.

What shingles do you use?

We use the Landmark Series asphalt roofing from CertainTeed. It comes with an exclusive 10-year streak-free warranty, and you can choose a 30, 40 or 50-year warranty that transfers if you sell your home.

What siding do you install?

Mastic vinyl siding (also known as Alcoa), with all the accessories: shutters, mantels, casings, moldings, corner posts, vents, blocks and columns.

Where are your windows made?

Our vinyl windows come from Paradigm Windows, made in Portland, Maine.

Do you clean up after the job?

Yes. We offer a no-mess guarantee and a satisfaction guarantee.

What payment do you accept?

We accept VISA, MasterCard and Discover.

How long have you been in business?

We have been beautifying Northern Maine since 1948, for two generations of the Nelson family.

How do I reach the owner?

You can reach Otis at (207) 227-0700. The office number is (207) 435-8233. Please leave a message if no one is there to take your call, and we will get back to you as soon as we can.

Are you hiring?

Modern Roofing & Siding Plus is an Equal Opportunity Employer. Fill out our job application and mail it to Modern Roofing & Siding, PO Box 157, Ashland, ME 04732.
