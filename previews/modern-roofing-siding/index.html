About Modern Roofing & Siding

A business rich with stories

It started with founder Robert Nelson Sr. driving around Northern Maine, looking for those tell-tale long icicles hanging off the roof edges, then going door to door to sell insulation jobs. Now, we still sell those insulation jobs, and so much more.

Today the business is run by Otis Nelson, owner, with Ginger Nelson in the office.
