About the firm

Our roots run deep in our community.

Joseph Morton started practicing law in St. Joseph in 1894. More than a century later, the firm he founded still serves St. Joseph and Northwest Missouri from an office on Village Drive.

From the Morton firm to today

  1. 1894Joseph Morton begins practicing law in St. Joseph. The Chicago Times describes the city as “a modern wonder — a city of 60,000 inhabitants, eleven railroads, 70 passenger trains each day, 170 factories, thirteen miles of the best paved streets, [and] the largest stockyards west of Chicago.”
  2. Two more generationsJoseph’s son, William M. Morton, Sr., works at the firm his whole career, apart from two years in the Army during the First World War. Grandson David joins the firm after rising to the rank of Captain in the U.S. Navy during World War II.
  3. 1968Jim Counts becomes the first person outside the Morton family to be associated with the Morton firm. He became the first non-family member to make partner there.
  4. 1970The Morton family firm merges with the well-known Reed family firm, and the new firm is called Morton, Reed & Counts.
  5. TodayMorton Reed Counts Briggs & Robb: five attorneys at 2301-B Village Drive, St. Joseph.

The Reed family firm

Ronald S. Reed, Jr. was another second-generation lawyer whose career was delayed, this time by the Korean War. In civilian life he was politically active: he was the youngest member of the Missouri delegation that helped nominate John Kennedy for President. As a state representative, Ron was instrumental in passing the legislation that established Missouri Western State College, now Missouri Western State University.

What the firm does

The firm has served its clients in real estate, estate planning, education law, civil litigation, banking, construction and business and transactions for decades. While most of its work has, as its objective, keeping clients out of court, when necessary the firm is prepared to defend its clients’ interests in court.

There are no longer 70 passenger trains, nor are there any Mortons or Reeds on the staff today. Their legacy still guides the firm’s values and its commitment to clients.

“Our values have deep roots. David Morton and his father before him, William Morton, Sr., were committed to principles of integrity and quality service to clients, and giving back to the community.”

Steve Briggs
