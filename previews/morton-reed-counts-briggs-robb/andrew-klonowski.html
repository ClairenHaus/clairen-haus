About Andrew

Andrew was raised in Southeast Kansas and earned a bachelor’s degree in political science at Pittsburg State University. He then attended law school at Washington University in St. Louis, where he met his wife, Allison. After law school he worked for firms in the Kansas City area, focusing first on complex civil litigation and then on representing businesses in employment-related disputes.

In 2018 Andrew joined the firm and relocated to St. Joseph. He has devoted his practice to helping clients with all aspects of their estate planning and business needs: preparing estate plans, assisting in the administration of estates and trusts, and representing clients in many types of litigation, including employment, business, property and estate disputes. In all of these, his focus is client-focused, effective and nuanced counsel that responds to the unique factors of each situation.

Throughout his practice, Andrew has focused on guiding clients through the complexities of litigation, and on providing a steady hand during what for many is their first and only interaction with the legal system.

In his spare time Andrew enjoys time with his wife and three children, reading, and his passion for food and cooking. He has served as President of the Downtown Rotary Club #32 and as a judge for the Cotillion for Excellence, and supports numerous other local charitable causes.
