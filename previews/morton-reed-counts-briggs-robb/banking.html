Practice area

Banking

The financial institutions the firm represents play a vital role in the lives of their communities, and facilitating that role is important to the financial well-being of everyone in the community.

Representation for financial institutions

  • Secured lendingProper documentation of perfected security interests in real or personal property, including promissory notes, deeds of trust, security agreements and other financing documents.
  • WorkoutsAssisting financial institutions that are working with their customers to restructure debt and avoid foreclosure by modifying existing loan terms.
  • ForeclosuresSeeing the process through from demand and acceleration of the note balance to sale on the courthouse steps, in both judicial and non-judicial foreclosure.
  • CollectionsHelping financial institutions recover on bad loans by obtaining judgments and garnishments.
  • BankruptcyRepresenting lenders as creditors in Chapter 7, 11 or 13 bankruptcy cases filed by their borrowers.
