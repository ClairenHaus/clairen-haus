Practice area

Civil Litigation

Running a sound business doesn’t make anyone immune from lawsuits. To protect your assets and reputation, and sometimes to encourage other parties to settle, you must be willing to enter into litigation.

Most of the firm’s work has, as its objective, keeping clients out of court. When it is necessary, the firm is prepared to defend its clients’ interests in court.

Disputes we handle

  • Business disputes
  • Trust and will disputes
  • Real estate
  • Personal injury and wrongful death
  • Employment

Personal injury and wrongful death matters are among the practice areas of Nicholas K. Robb; employment disputes are a focus for Andrew Klonowski, and Blair Merrigan handles business litigation including landlord/tenant matters.
