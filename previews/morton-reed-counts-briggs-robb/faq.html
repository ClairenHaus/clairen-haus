Questions & answers

Common questions

Straight answers about the firm, drawn from what the firm itself says about its work. For anything about your own situation, please call.

How long has the firm been in St. Joseph?

Since 1894, when Joseph Morton started practicing law in St. Joseph. His son and grandson followed him into the firm, and in 1970 the Morton family firm merged with the Reed family firm. Read our history.

Where is the office?

Please note our new address: 2301-B Village Drive, St. Joseph, Missouri 64506. Call 816-232-8411 to set up an appointment.

What kinds of matters does the firm handle?

Seven practice areas: estate planning, real estate, business and transactions, civil litigation, banking, construction and educational institutions.

My situation doesn’t fit any of those areas. Can you still help?

Although each attorney tends to focus on certain practice areas, the firm welcomes inquiries and is happy to advise you no matter what your situation. The easiest way to find out whether the firm can represent you is to call.

Do you help family farms and family businesses plan for the next generation?

Yes. Representing multi-generational family farms and businesses, and working with them to ensure continuity of their operations and preservation of their assets, is a benchmark of the firm’s practice. Business and farm succession planning is part of estate planning, led by Stephen J. Briggs.

When should I update my estate plan?

Because estate tax laws change, a plan is the beginning of planning, not the end. Review it when significant events occur: a change in marital status, births or deaths in the family, serious illness or disability, changes in your charitable giving, changes in state or federal tax laws, a move to a different state, or any significant change in your financial status.

Do you go to court?

Most of the firm’s work has, as its objective, keeping clients out of court. When it is necessary, the firm is prepared to defend its clients’ interests in court, in business disputes, trust and will disputes, real estate, personal injury and wrongful death, and employment matters.

Do you represent banks and other lenders?

Yes. The firm represents financial institutions in secured lending, workouts, foreclosures, collections and bankruptcy cases filed by their borrowers. More on banking.

Do you work with school districts?

Yes. The firm serves as outside general counsel and special counsel for a number of educational institutions, including on contracts, employment law, Missouri Sunshine Law compliance, litigation, and bonding and construction. More on educational institutions.

Can you help with a business or a matter in Kansas?

The firm helps make sure your Missouri or Kansas enterprise has a solid legal foundation. Nicholas K. Robb and Andrew Klonowski are both admitted to the Kansas Bar.

Do you handle 1031 exchanges and development financing?

Yes. The firm’s real estate work includes tax-deferred 1031 exchanges, tax increment financing and tax credit financing for developers rehabilitating buildings into senior or affordable housing.

Can I email the details of my matter before we meet?

Please don’t send any confidential information until an attorney/client relationship has been established. Sending an email to the office does not create an attorney-client relationship, and the firm can’t represent you on a new matter until it determines that there is no conflict of interest. Call or email to set up an appointment first.

Call the office and the firm will point you to the right attorney.
