Our history

Our roots run deep in our community.

Joseph Morton started practicing law in St. Joseph in 1894, when the Chicago Times described St. Joe as “a modern wonder”, with eleven railroads and seventy passenger trains a day. His son and grandson followed him into the firm. In 1970 the Morton family firm merged with the well-known Reed family firm.

There are no longer 70 passenger trains, nor any Mortons or Reeds on staff today. Their legacy still guides the firm’s values and its commitment to clients.
