About Jim

Jim Counts was raised in Bunker, Missouri, a small town deep in the Mark Twain National Forest. Although he knew from a young age that he wanted to be a lawyer, he first qualified as a teacher and taught at schools in Iron County and St. Louis County before studying law at the University of Missouri in Columbia.

He came to St. Joseph in 1968 to interview with the Morton Law Firm, and became the first non-family member to make partner there. After a merger, the firm became Morton, Reed & Counts.

Over the years Jim has volunteered time, and sometimes legal counsel, to the United Way, the St. Joseph Performing Arts Association, the Noyes Home for Children and the Ladies Union Benevolent Association.
