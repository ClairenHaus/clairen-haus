About Nick

Nick is another Central High School alumnus. At the University of Nebraska he first studied architecture, then, drawn to the engineering and construction side rather than design, switched majors and graduated with a Bachelor of Science in Construction Management from the engineering school. When he excelled in a class on law for engineers, his professor suggested law school, and Nick enrolled at the University of Missouri–Kansas City School of Law.

At UMKC he was a member of the National Trial Team, on the staff of the Urban Lawyer law journal and a student emissary. In his third year he worked as a “Rule 13” Assistant Prosecutor in the Clay County, Missouri Prosecuting Attorney’s office. After graduation he spent two years with a Kansas City litigation firm before returning home to St. Joseph.

Given his education, Nick enjoys construction law and civil litigation. “I tend to approach problems as if I’m solving an equation,” he says. His analytical background serves him well in other areas of the law too, in particular business, farm and estate planning.

He has served on the boards of the St. Joseph Family YMCA and InterServ, and is past President of the Pony Express Council of the Boy Scouts of America and the Central High School Booster Club. He currently serves as Vice-President of Endowment of the Pony Express Council, Trustee and Secretary of the Mount Mora Cemetery Association, Chair of the Morton Fund for the Arts, Chair of the Calla Varner Scholarship Fund, and Board Member and Treasurer of the Missouri Western State University Foundation.

He enjoys traveling with his wife and spending time with his three children at their athletic and school activities. When time permits, a round of golf may find its way onto the agenda.
