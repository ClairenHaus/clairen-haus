About Steve

Steve decided he was going to be a lawyer while he was still a student at Central High School. He went on to study political science at Macalester College in St. Paul, where he was a member of the Phi Beta Kappa honor society, and completed his law degree at the University of Minnesota.

He came straight back to St. Joseph and joined the firm right away. Today, Steve takes the lead on most of the firm’s multi-generational estate and succession planning and real estate development work.

“We have deep roots in the St. Joseph community and greater northwest Missouri, as well. As a result, we are privileged to represent multi-generational family farms and businesses. Working with these families to ensure continuity of their operations and preservation of their assets is a benchmark of our practice. That work involves a diverse practice from estate and succession planning to real estate to administration of trusts.”

Following the legacy of William Morton, Sr. and David Morton in community service, Steve has served as member and President of the St. Joseph Library Board; on the boards of the Missouri Western State University Foundation and the Albrecht-Kemper Museum of Art; as chair of the St. Joseph Library tax levy campaign and co-chair of the citizens’ St. Joseph School District levy campaign; as co-chair of the 2013 levy for public safety; on the 2022 Committee for a police tax and the 2023 citizens committee for the renewal of the Capital Improvements Projects tax; on the St. Joseph City Charter Commission that wrote the city charter in 1980–81; and as chair of the 2008–09 Citizens Charter Review Committee.

Steve is currently a member and Chair of the St. Joseph Land Bank Board, a member and former chair of the Board of First Christian Church, and a member and secretary of the Board of the Northwest Missouri Community Foundation.
