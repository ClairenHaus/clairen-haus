About

An East Texas practice built on the federal courthouse

Andy Tindel, Attorney & Counselor at Law, P.C., is the Tyler law practice of Andy Tindel, the Tyler-based partner of the MT2 Law Group.

How the practice began

Andy graduated from Baylor University School of Law in 1982 and spent the next three years as law clerk to U.S. District Judge William M. Steger in the Eastern District of Texas. Since leaving the court in 1985 he has practiced as a trial and appellate lawyer, primarily in federal court.

What it does today

The practice handles commercial damage claims and intellectual property litigation, securities fraud and False Claims Act cases, insurance disputes, and selected catastrophic personal injury and wrongful death cases. For more than 25 years Andy has also served as advisor and local counsel for clients and attorneys with high-stakes cases in the Eastern District of Texas and in state courts throughout East Texas, and he mediates civil disputes.

MT2 Law Group

Andy is the Tyler, Texas based partner of the MT2 Law Group. News on this site refers to the firm as Mann Tindel Thompson.
