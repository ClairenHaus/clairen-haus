Profile

Andy Tindel

A native East Texan, federal law clerk, and trial and appellate lawyer since 1985.

Andy Tindel is a native East Texan. After graduating from Baylor University with a B.B.A. in Accounting, he completed his legal education at Baylor University School of Law in 1982. He then served for three years as law clerk to the Honorable William M. Steger, United States District Judge for the Eastern District of Texas. Since leaving the court in 1985, Andy has concentrated his practice on the trial and appeal of complex commercial litigation and personal injury cases, primarily in federal court.

Andy is certified by the Texas Board of Legal Specialization in Civil Trial Law, Civil Appellate Law and Personal Injury Trial Law. He is the Tyler, Texas based partner of the MT2 Law Group.

Courts

Andy is admitted to practice before the Supreme Court of the United States, the United States Court of Appeals for the Federal Circuit, the United States Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit, the United States District Courts for the Eastern, Southern, Western and Northern Districts of Texas, and the Eastern District of Oklahoma.

Leadership in the bar

  • Chairman of the Litigation Section of the State Bar of Texas, the largest section of the State Bar (2004–2005 term)
  • President of the Bar Association of the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, 2001–2002
  • President of the East Texas Chapter of the American Board of Trial Advocates
  • President of the T. John Ward Chapter of the American Inns of Court, 2012–2013
  • Life Fellow of the Texas Bar Foundation; board of directors, Smith County Bar Foundation
  • Local Rules Committee for the Eastern District of Texas, and other court-appointed committees and task forces on federal practice

Memberships

State Bar of Texas, Smith County Bar Association, Fifth Circuit Bar Association, Texas Trial Lawyers Association, American Association for Justice, East Texas Trial Lawyers Association, College of the State Bar of Texas, Bar Association of the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas, American Board of Trial Advocates, and the T. John Ward Chapter of the American Inns of Court.

Recognition

  • Named in 2007 by Texas Lawyer as one of four “Top-Notch” plaintiff’s personal injury trial lawyers in Texas
  • Texas Super Lawyer® every year since the recognition began in 2003
  • Rated AV Preeminent® by Martindale-Hubbell
  • The Best Lawyers in America®, Litigation – Intellectual Property (28th Edition)

Outside the office

Andy is married to the former Sandy Roberts, also a Baylor graduate and a registered nurse. They are members of Green Acres Baptist Church in Tyler and board members of Christian Foundations of Faith. In his spare time Andy enjoys running, weight lifting and oil painting.
