Legal Services

Appellate Services

Appeals for plaintiffs and defendants in the federal courts of appeals, the Texas courts of appeals and the Texas Supreme Court.

Andy has enjoyed reading and studying the law since entering law school in 1979, and that interest led him to build an active appellate practice alongside his trial work. In 1992 he was certified as a specialist in Civil Appellate Law by the Texas Board of Legal Specialization.

Over the years Andy has prosecuted and defended numerous appeals for both plaintiffs and defendants in the federal appellate courts, the Texas courts of appeals and the Texas Supreme Court. He has appeared before the United States Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit in New Orleans more than a dozen times, including arguing and winning two en banc appeals before the full court:

  • Bartley v. Euclid, 180 F.3d 175 (5th Cir. 1998)
  • Coggin v. Longview I.S.D., 337 F.3d 459 (5th Cir. 2003)

Early advice for trial teams

Clients and law firms often retain Andy early in a case for advice on developing causes of action, defense strategy, discovery planning, preparing or responding to dispositive motions, preparing and arguing the jury charge, drafting or answering post-trial motions, and legal research.

Co-counsel and lead counsel on appeal

Andy routinely assists retaining attorneys as co-counsel on appeal, helping with brief preparation and argument strategy, or acts as lead appellate counsel at the request of the client or the trial lawyers.

Past results do not guarantee a similar outcome in any other matter.
