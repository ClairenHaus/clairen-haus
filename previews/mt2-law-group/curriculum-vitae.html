Curriculum Vitae

Curriculum Vitae of Andy Tindel

An East Texas lawyer specializing in the preparation, trial and appeal of major civil damage claims.

Andy Tindel is an East Texas lawyer specializing in the preparation, trial and appeal of a wide variety of major civil damage claims, including intellectual property and technology matters, patent infringement suits, copyright and trademark disputes, securities fraud litigation, false claims act cases, class actions, unfair competition and related business tort matters, as well as catastrophic personal injury cases. He frequently serves as an advisor, attorney and local counsel for domestic and overseas corporate clients and national law firms with litigation in the United States District Courts for the Eastern District of Texas, and in state trial and appellate courts throughout East Texas. He is also a frequent author and lecturer at continuing legal education seminars throughout Texas.
