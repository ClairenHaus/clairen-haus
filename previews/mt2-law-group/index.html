Since leaving Judge Steger's court in 1985, Andy has concentrated his practice on the trial and appeal of complex commercial litigation and personal injury cases, primarily in federal court. For over 25 years he has served as an advisor and local counsel for clients and attorneys with high-stakes litigation pending before the United States District Court for the Eastern District of Texas and state courts throughout East Texas.

He has appeared before the United States Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit more than a dozen times, including arguing and winning two en banc appeals, Bartley v. Euclid (5th Cir. 1998) and Coggin v. Longview I.S.D. (5th Cir. 2003). He also serves on the board of directors of the Smith County Bar Foundation and has served on the Local Rules Committee for the Eastern District of Texas.

Andy is married to the former Sandy Roberts, a Baylor graduate and registered nurse. They are members of Green Acres Baptist Church in Tyler. In his spare time he enjoys running, weight lifting and oil painting.

Common questions

Questions people ask before they call.

Answered only from what the current site says. General information, not legal advice.

Does Andy Tindel serve as local counsel in the Eastern District of Texas?

Yes. For over 25 years he has served as an advisor and local counsel for clients and attorneys with high-stakes litigation pending in the Eastern District of Texas, assisting national firms with court protocol, local rules interpretation and trial strategy, and serving as co-counsel at trial and on appeal.

Is he board certified?

Yes. He is certified by the Texas Board of Legal Specialization in three fields: Civil Trial Law, Civil Appellate Law and Personal Injury Trial Law.

Does he handle appeals?

Yes. He maintains an active appellate practice alongside his trial work, including appeals in the federal appellate courts, the Texas courts of appeals and the Texas Supreme Court, and is often retained early in a case to help with dispositive motions, jury instructions and post-trial motions.

Is mediation available, and how is it scheduled?

Yes. He has been a certified mediator since March 1999 and mediates selected civil matters in state and federal court in Northeast Texas, including appellate mediations. Mediations are scheduled through his office at 903-596-0900.
