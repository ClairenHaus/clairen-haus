The practice

Complex cases, from the first pleading through the final appeal.

Andy specializes in the preparation, trial and appeal of commercial damage claims and intellectual property litigation, including patent, trademark and copyright infringement, trade dress and false advertising suits, unfair competition and trade secrets litigation, corporate governance disputes, securities fraud class actions and other business litigation.

He also handles selected catastrophic personal injury cases involving product liability claims, trucking accidents, wrongful death and general negligence, in state and federal courts throughout Texas.
