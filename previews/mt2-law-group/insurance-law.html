Insurance Law

Insurance disputes, from coverage questions to claims that end up in a courtroom or on appeal.

Andy’s experience

Insurance Law is one of the practice areas listed on Andy’s site, alongside his commercial and personal injury trial work.

How these cases generally work

Common insurance disputes

Insurance disputes often involve whether a policy covers a loss, how much is owed, whether a claim was handled properly or paid on time, and whether the insurer must defend its policyholder in a lawsuit.

Texas-specific rules

Texas law has statutes on prompt payment of claims and on unfair claim settlement practices, and many policies carry their own notice, appraisal and deadline requirements. Reading the policy closely is usually the first step.

Where insurance meets other cases

Insurance questions come up inside many other cases, such as personal injury claims, commercial disputes and wrongful death cases, because insurance coverage often decides how a case can be resolved.
