Legal Services

Services for clients, law firms and parties

Beyond his own trial practice, Andy works as local counsel and appellate counsel for other lawyers and their clients, and as a mediator.

Local Counsel Services

Court protocol, local rules interpretation and trial strategy for national firms and companies with cases in any division of the Eastern District of Texas.

Learn more →

Appellate Services

Lead or co-counsel on appeal, and early-case advice to trial teams, from a lawyer board certified in Civil Appellate Law since 1992.

Learn more →

Mediation Services

Mediation of selected civil cases in state and federal court in Northeast Texas, with a specialty in appellate mediations.

Andy began his career in 1982 as law clerk to U.S. District Judge William M. Steger, and has practiced in every division of the Eastern District of Texas since.
