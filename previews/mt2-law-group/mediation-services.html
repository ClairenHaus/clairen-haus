Legal Services

Mediation Services

Mediation of selected civil cases in state and federal court in Northeast Texas, by a certified mediator since 1999.

As an adjunct to his trial and appellate practice, Andy is available to mediate selected civil cases pending in state and federal court in Northeast Texas. He was certified as a mediator in March 1999 and has mediated many disputes since.

His courtroom experience and appellate background give him insight into the strengths and weaknesses of each side’s case, and into the benefits of a negotiated settlement. Andy works to explore every avenue toward a fair resolution and to keep the parties negotiating as long as any reasonable possibility of settlement remains.

Appellate and federal mediations

In 2001 Andy was appointed to the panel of mediators for the Twelfth District Court of Appeals in Tyler, and he has specialized in appellate mediations since. His long federal trial practice, which began in 1982 as briefing attorney to U.S. District Judge William M. Steger, also makes him a frequent choice for parties with complex business or personal injury cases in the Eastern District of Texas.

Fees and travel

Andy will travel to mediate at the parties’ request. Fees for half-day or full-day mediations are competitively priced; fees for multi-party or unusually complex matters are set case by case after consulting with the parties.
