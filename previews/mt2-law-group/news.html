News

Legal news from Andy’s practice

Cases, recognitions and speaking engagements, as published on Andy Tindel’s website.

2022

November 15, 2022

VLSI Wins $948 Million Verdict Against Intel

Irell & Manella LLP and Mark Mann and Andy Tindel of Mann Tindel Thompson secured a verdict of $948,755,789.00, plus running royalties, on behalf of VLSI Technology LLC against Intel Corporation in a suit involving a microprocessor technology patent owned by VLSI. The Austin, Texas jury found that Intel had infringed the asserted claims and had not shown the patent was invalid.

October 26, 2022

Andy Tindel Speaks at 2022 E.D. Tex. Bench/Bar Conference

Andy served as a panelist on “Litigation Latigo – Securing a Plaintiff’s Saddle to EDTX” at the 2022 Eastern District of Texas Bench/Bar Conference in Plano, Texas, alongside Blake Thompson of Mann Tindel Thompson and lawyers from Fabricant LLP, Eichmann, P.C., Cherian LLP and Mayer Brown.

September 21, 2022

Facebook Hit With $175 Million Verdict in Austin

Voxer Inc. received a $174.5 million verdict against Facebook parent Meta Platforms Inc. from jurors in Austin, Texas, who found Meta had infringed two patents related to video streaming and messaging. Voxer was represented at trial by Quinn Emanuel Urquhart & Sullivan, LLP and Mann Tindel Thompson.

June 14, 2022

Andy Tindel Named a Texas Super Lawyer for the 20th Straight Year

Andy was selected as a Texas Super Lawyer® for the 20th straight year, every year since the recognition began in 2003.

May 20, 2022

VLSI Secures $2.3+ Billion Patent Infringement Judgment Against Intel

Irell & Manella LLP and Mark Mann and Andy Tindel of Mann Tindel Thompson persuaded a judge to enter a Final Judgment of $2,337,321,343.00 in favor of VLSI Technology LLC against Intel Corporation in a suit involving two patents on technology used in Intel’s microprocessors, following a Waco, Texas jury verdict the year before.

2021

November 6, 2021

Bright Data Ltd. Prevails in Patent Trial Against Oxylabs

A jury in Marshall, Texas, in the Eastern District of Texas, returned a verdict for Bright Data Ltd. in a patent infringement action against the companies that use the name “Oxylabs.” Bright Data was represented by Cherian LLP, Capshaw DeRieux LLP and Mann Tindel Thompson.

October 14, 2021

Andy Tindel Serves as Panelist for 2021 E.D. Tex. Bench/Bar Conference

Andy served on “The Hindsight Track: What Seasoned Lawyers Will Share with Young Lawyers and Old Alike about Career Missteps and Lucky Breaks” at the 2021 Eastern District of Texas Bench/Bar Conference in Plano, Texas.

September 18, 2021

MT2 Law Group Secures Appellate Victory for Vistra Corp.

On September 17, 2021, the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit affirmed a final judgment in a trespass to try title action brought by a Vistra Corp. subsidiary, Luminant Mining Company, LLC v. Parker, 14 F.4th 375 (5th Cir. 2021). Andy served as lead appellate counsel for Luminant.

August 19, 2021

Andy Tindel Recognized in The Best Lawyers in America® for Intellectual Property Litigation

Andy was recognized in the 28th Edition of The Best Lawyers in America® in the practice area of Litigation – Intellectual Property.

May 7, 2021

Andy Tindel Once Again Named a Texas Super Lawyer®

Andy was named a 2021 Texas Super Lawyer®. He was first recognized in 2003, the first year the honor was given, and has been recognized each year since.

2020

November 6, 2020

Marshall Jury Finds in Favor of Google in Patent Trial Over YouTube

A jury in Marshall’s federal court found that Google’s YouTube service did not infringe patents owned by Personalized Media Communications related to adaptive video streaming. Google was represented by Quinn Emanuel Urquhart & Sullivan LLP and by Mark Mann of the MT2 Law Group.

October 31, 2020

Tyler Jury Returns a $502.8 Million Patent Verdict Against Apple

A federal jury in Tyler returned a $502.8 million verdict for VirnetX, Inc. and Leidos, Inc. on two network security patents, following four days of trial before Judge Robert Schroeder III in the Eastern District of Texas.

May 31, 2020

Andy Tindel Named a Texas Super Lawyer for 18th Straight Year

Andy was named a 2020 Texas Super Lawyer, a recognition he has received every year since it was first given in 2003.

May 27, 2020

Leidos Recovers $84.6 Million From Apple in VirnetX Litigation

Leidos, Inc. announced it had received $84.6 million from VirnetX, Inc., its share of proceeds from VirnetX’s patent lawsuit against Apple Inc. in the Eastern District of Texas.

2018

April 13, 2018

Apple Hit With $502.6 Million Verdict

A jury in the Eastern District of Texas returned a $502.6 million verdict against Apple, Inc. after an eight-day trial in a patent infringement action brought by VirnetX, Inc. and Leidos, Inc.
