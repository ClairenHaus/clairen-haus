How these cases generally work

What these claims are

A personal injury claim seeks compensation when someone is hurt because of another person’s or company’s negligence or wrongdoing, for example in a vehicle or trucking crash, an industrial accident, or because of a dangerous product or property. When the injury causes death, Texas law allows a wrongful death claim by the surviving spouse, children and parents, and a survival claim on behalf of the person’s estate.

Deadlines

In Texas the deadline to file most personal injury and wrongful death lawsuits is generally two years, and claims against government bodies can require written notice much sooner. Some exceptions apply, so it is worth asking a lawyer early.

What compensation can cover

Depending on the case, damages may include medical expenses, lost earnings and earning capacity, pain and suffering, and, in a death case, the loss of companionship and support. Texas uses proportionate responsibility, which can reduce or bar recovery if the injured person is found partly at fault.
