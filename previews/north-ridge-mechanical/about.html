About us

Family owned in Carlisle since 1997

North Ridge Mechanical LLC is an independently owned and operated small business serving Harrisburg’s surrounding areas.

Meet Jason Jancse, owner

Jason Jancse, owner of North Ridge Mechanical, has been building long lasting relationships while providing quality service to the South Central Pennsylvania area since 1997. We are family owned and operated, located right here in Carlisle, Pennsylvania.

Our team is highly experienced in heating and air conditioning. Understanding the importance of listening to what your needs are, and delivering solutions to your particular need, is what we do. We strive to consistently provide high quality workmanship in a timely manner.

Not everyone’s needs in heating and air conditioning are the same. We take pride in listening to our customers and delivering exactly what they need for their unique situation.
