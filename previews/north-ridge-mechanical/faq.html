FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about North Ridge Mechanical, our services and our service area.

Where is North Ridge Mechanical located?

We’re at 7816 Wertzville Road, Carlisle, PA 17013. North Ridge Mechanical is family owned and operated, located right here in Carlisle, Pennsylvania.

What areas do you serve?

Carlisle, Mechanicsburg, Enola, Dillsburg, Newville, Shippensburg, Hummelstown, Hershey, Landisburg, Camp Hill, Boiling Springs, Mt. Holly Springs, Gardners, and surrounding areas in South Central Pennsylvania.

Who owns North Ridge Mechanical?

Jason Jancse is the owner. He has been providing quality service to the South Central Pennsylvania area since 1997.

What services do you offer?

New installations, replacement systems (change outs), geothermal, heat pumps, oil and gas furnaces, boilers, ductless mini splits, radiant heat, central air, humidifiers, air purifiers, dehumidification, duct work, Aeroseal duct sealing, service of existing equipment, preventive maintenance and hot water heaters.

Do you offer free in-home consultations?

Yes. We specialize in in-home consultations, and they’re free. Seeing your home in person helps us find the right solution for your particular needs.

Can I reach you after hours?

Yes. For our customers’ convenience, our answering service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Call (717) 241-5994.

Are you licensed?

North Ridge Mechanical is licensed, certified and trained in everything from preventive maintenance and repairs to replacement systems and new installations.

Do you work on new construction and existing homes?

Yes. Whether your project is new construction or an existing home, we offer many services you can rely on.

Will you service equipment you didn’t install?

Service of existing equipment is one of the services we offer, along with preventive maintenance.

What brands do you install?

Heating and cooling: Bryant, Trane, Payne, Lennox, Climate Master, Mitsubishi, Fujitsu, Honeywell, Aprilaire, EWC, Peerless, Amana and Goodman. Water heaters: State and Ruud.

How much will it cost?

Every home is different, so we start with a free in-home consultation. We offer competitive pricing and try to meet all of our customers’ budget needs.

How do I get in touch?

Call (717) 241-5994 or email nridgemechanical@gmail.com. You can also find us on Facebook.

Planning a new system or a replacement?

Start with a free in-home consultation. We’ll listen, look at your home and put together a plan designed to work for it.
