Heat pumps and geothermal

Heat pumps and geothermal heating and cooling units, installed, replaced and serviced.

What we work on

  • Heat Pumps
  • Geo Thermal Heating & Cooling Units

Both are on the list of services and installations North Ridge Mechanical offers.

How they work, in plain terms

A heat pump moves heat instead of making it: it pulls heat out of the outdoor air to warm your home in winter, and runs in reverse to cool it in summer. A geothermal system works the same way, but trades heat with the ground through buried loops instead of the outside air. Whether either suits your home is something we look at during a free in-home consultation.
