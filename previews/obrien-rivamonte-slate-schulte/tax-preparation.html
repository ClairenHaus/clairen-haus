Tax Services

Tax Preparation

Accurate returns for individuals, businesses, trusts, estates and not-for-profits.

How we help

Our significant investment in computerized tax preparation and research software enables us to accurately and efficiently prepare returns for various types of entities including individuals, corporations, partnerships, trusts, estates, and not-for-profit organizations.

That includes income tax preparation for all types of businesses, and individuals.

In general terms

  • Federal individual returns are generally due April 15, with an extension available to October 15. The extension is for filing, not for paying.
  • Bringing last year’s return, all W-2s and 1099s, and any IRS or state letters makes the first meeting faster.

General information only, not advice for your situation. Call (248) 353-2800 to talk it through.
