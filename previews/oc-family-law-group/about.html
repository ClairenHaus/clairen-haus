Led by Andrea L. Mersel

Lead attorney Andrea Mersel brings more than 35 years of legal experience to the practice, with strong negotiation and litigation skills. Her business degree from the Wharton School and her experience as a negotiation professor help her navigate complex divorces involving businesses, substantial assets and real estate.

Negotiation first, court when needed

The firm aims for amicable resolutions that preserve both time and financial resources, focusing on negotiated agreements and offering mediation as a cost-effective alternative to litigation. When a matter needs to go to court, you will be represented there.

The right experts for the case

Where a case calls for it, the firm works with forensic accountants to build an accurate financial picture, and with psychologists, therapists and other experts on custody questions.
