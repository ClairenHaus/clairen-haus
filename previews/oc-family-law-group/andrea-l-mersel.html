Andrea L. Mersel

With more than 35 years of legal experience, Andrea Mersel brings a litigator’s skill, a negotiator’s training and a business education to family law.

Why family law

After personally experiencing the dissolution process, Andrea shifted the focus of her practice to family law in order to better assist those in need. As a parent, she understands the additional emotions and complexities you may be experiencing with respect to your children.

Litigator, negotiator, teacher

Andrea is a highly experienced litigator and has years of experience as a trial advocacy and negotiations professor. Her undergraduate business degree from the Wharton School makes her particularly well suited to divorces where real estate, businesses and highly valued assets are involved.

Professional background

Before focusing on family law, Andrea held the position of Of Counsel at Latham & Watkins, and taught as an Adjunct Professor of Law at Whittier Law School.
