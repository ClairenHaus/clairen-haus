Common questions about working with the firm

Straight answers about the office, the process and what to expect. For advice about your own situation, call (714) 544-6622.

Where is the office?

The Law Offices of Andrea L. Mersel are at 3090 Bristol Street, Suite 120, Costa Mesa, CA 92626, centrally located in Orange County near South Coast Plaza. The office is open Monday through Friday, 8:30 am to 5:30 pm, by appointment only.

Which areas do you serve?

The firm serves clients throughout Southern California, including Orange, Los Angeles, Riverside, San Diego and San Bernardino Counties, and communities such as Costa Mesa, Irvine, Tustin, Newport Beach, Huntington Beach, Mission Viejo, Laguna Beach, Anaheim, Fullerton and Santa Ana.

What kinds of family law matters do you handle?

Divorce, high net worth divorce, child custody and custody modifications, spousal and child support, post-divorce modifications, prenuptial agreements, paternity, domestic abuse and mediation.

How do I schedule a consultation?

Call the office at (714) 544-6622 to schedule a consultation. You can also send a message through the contact form, and a member of the team will get back to you.

Does my divorce have to go to court?

Not necessarily. The firm aims for amicable resolutions that save time and money, focusing on negotiated agreements and offering mediation as an alternative to litigation. If an agreement can’t be reached, the firm is prepared to represent you in court.

My divorce involves a business or significant assets. Can you help?

Yes. High net worth divorce is a focus of the practice. The firm works with forensic accountants and other financial experts to value real estate, investments, businesses and retirement accounts, and Andrea Mersel’s business degree from the Wharton School helps in understanding business interests.

What is the difference between legal and physical custody?

Physical custody determines where a child lives; it can be sole (the child lives primarily with one parent) or joint (time is split between both parents). Legal custody is the right to make important decisions about the child’s life, and can also be sole or joint.

Can a custody or support order be changed later?

Yes. When circumstances change significantly, such as a parent’s relocation, changes in a child’s needs, or changes in income, an existing order can be modified. The process begins with a petition to modify the order; parents may reach agreement through mediation or negotiation, and otherwise a judge decides.

Do you represent people accused of domestic abuse?

Yes. The firm represents individuals affected by domestic abuse, whether they are seeking protection or have been accused of abuse.

Who will handle my case?

Andrea L. Mersel, the firm’s senior attorney. She has more than 35 years of legal experience, a J.D. summa cum laude from UC Berkeley’s Boalt Hall School of Law and a business degree from the Wharton School, and she previously taught trial advocacy and negotiation.

Does sending a message through the website make me a client?

No. Using the website or its form to contact the firm does not create an attorney–client relationship. Please don’t send confidential or time-sensitive information through the form; call the office instead.
