Where is the Law Offices of Andrea L. Mersel located?

The office is at 3090 Bristol Street, Suite 120, Costa Mesa, CA 92626, centrally located in Orange County near South Coast Plaza. Phone: 714-544-6622.

Which counties does the firm serve?

What family law matters does the firm handle?

Divorce, high net worth divorce, child custody and custody modifications, spousal and child support modifications, post-divorce modifications, prenuptial agreements, paternity, domestic abuse and mediation.
