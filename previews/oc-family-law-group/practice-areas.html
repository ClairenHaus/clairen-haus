Practice areas

Family law, from first questions to final orders

The Law Offices of Andrea L. Mersel is dedicated to serving families across Southern California, providing expert legal guidance and compassionate support in all areas of family law. Our firm proudly serves clients in Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside, San Diego, and San Bernardino Counties. We understand the unique challenges that families face, and we are committed to helping you navigate these issues with care and professionalism.

  • Divorce

    We understand that divorce is a complex and emotionally charged process. Our team listens carefully to your concerns and objectives, ensuring you are well-informed and supported throughout every step of the process.

    Read more →

  • High Net Worth Divorce

    Andrea Mersel’s business expertise uniquely qualifies her to handle the financial complexities of high net worth divorces. We thoroughly evaluate financial statements and forensic reports to protect your interests.

    Read more →

  • Child Custody & Custody Modifications

    Your children’s well-being is our top priority. We work closely with psychologists, therapists, and other experts to determine the best custody arrangements, and we advocate for modifications when necessary to meet the evolving needs of your family.

    Read more →

  • Spousal Support & Child Support

    We conduct thorough financial reviews to ensure that spousal and child support obligations are fair and reflective of both parties’ financial situations. Our goal is to secure a just and equitable arrangement.

    Read more →

  • Post-Divorce Modifications

    If your family’s circumstances change after a divorce, we can assist with post-decree modifications, including adjustments to child support, custody arrangements, and spousal support, ensuring that the agreements remain fair and relevant.

    Read more →

  • Prenuptial Agreements

    Our experienced attorneys are skilled negotiators who will educate you on applicable laws and help you craft a prenuptial agreement that protects your assets and clarifies expectations, providing peace of mind for your future.

    Read more →

  • Paternity

    Establishing paternity is crucial for ensuring parental rights and responsibilities. We represent clients in paternity actions, helping them secure their legal rights and maintain meaningful relationships with their children.

    Read more →

  • Domestic Abuse

    We represent individuals affected by domestic abuse, whether as victims seeking protection or those accused of abuse. Our firm is committed to fully advocating for your rights and ensuring your safety and well-being.

    Read more →

  • Mediation

    Mediation offers a cost-effective and collaborative alternative to litigation. Our firm provides professional mediation services to help families resolve disputes amicably and efficiently, keeping your legal costs to a minimum and preserving relationships.

    Read more →
