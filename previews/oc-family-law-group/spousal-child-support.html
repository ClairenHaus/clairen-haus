Spousal Support & Child Support

Circumstances can change significantly after a divorce, affecting the fairness and feasibility of existing spousal support and child support arrangements. At the Law Offices of Andrea L. Mersel, we help clients modify these agreements to better reflect their current situation, ensuring continued fairness and support for all parties involved. We proudly serve clients throughout Los Angeles, Orange, Riverside, San Diego, and San Bernardino Counties.

Understanding Spousal Support Modifications

Grounds for Modification

  • Significant Income Changes. Adjustments due to substantial increases or decreases in either party’s income.
  • Employment Changes. The impact of job loss, new employment, or career advancements on spousal support obligations.
  • Remarriage or Cohabitation. Changes in spousal support when the recipient spouse remarries or begins cohabitating with a new partner.

Modification Process

  • Filing a Petition. Initiating the modification by filing a legal petition with the court.
  • Providing Evidence. Demonstrating the change in circumstances that justifies the modification.
  • Court Hearing. A judge will review the evidence and decide whether to approve the modification.

Understanding Child Support Modifications

Grounds for Modification

  • Income Changes. Modifications due to significant changes in either parent’s income.
  • Changes in Child’s Needs. Adjustments based on the evolving needs of the child, such as increased medical expenses, education costs, or extracurricular activities.
  • Parenting Time Adjustments. Changes in custody arrangements or parenting time that impact the amount of support needed.

Modification Process

  • Reviewing State Guidelines. Ensuring that the modification request aligns with state child support guidelines.
  • Negotiation and Mediation. Attempting to reach an agreement through negotiation or mediation to avoid a court battle.
  • Court Approval. Submitting the agreed-upon modification to the court for approval, or presenting the case to a judge if no agreement is reached.

The Importance of Legal Assistance

Modifying spousal or child support arrangements can be a complex and emotionally charged process. It’s essential to have accurate documentation, a thorough understanding of your legal rights, and a strong advocate on your side. At the Law Offices of Andrea L. Mersel, we ensure that all financial information and relevant documents are accurately presented, helping you achieve a fair and just modification.
