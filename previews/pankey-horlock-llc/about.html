About the firm

A full service community association law firm

Pankey & Horlock, LLC represents community associations in all the legal issues necessary for the successful operation of their associations.

Who we represent

Our attorneys have extensive experience in community association law and have represented and advised many condominium and homeowners’ associations of all sizes through northern Georgia, in all types of legal issues. We are aggressive, diligent and persistent.

Beyond association work, the firm’s attorneys represent individuals, partnerships and corporations in business and real estate litigation, and collect debts of every kind for clients in Georgia.

Experience since 1991

Our attorneys have been collecting debts from debtors located in Georgia since 1991, the year Laura Horlock began her legal career in civil litigation in Atlanta. Larry Pankey joined the State Bar of Georgia in 1992 and brings extensive trial experience in federal and state court. Lisa Fuerst brings years of work as general counsel and litigator for associations across metro Atlanta and north Georgia.

How we work

What associations can expect

Advice for boards

We advise boards of directors of their legal rights and obligations under the association documents, and help managers carry out the required procedures.

Documents, start to finish

We draft and amend governing documents from start to finish, and draft all relevant documents for association votes.

Collections and court

From efficient collection of assessments to covenant enforcement and contract disputes, we take matters to court when that is the best or only option.
