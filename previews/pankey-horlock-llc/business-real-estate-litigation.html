Practice area

Business & Real Estate Litigation

Trial attorneys for business, employment, injunction and real estate disputes, in state and federal court.

When a dispute becomes litigation

It is not uncommon for community associations to find themselves in a legal dispute with another party. Disputes such as a breach of contract usually fall under civil litigation, which uses the courts or arbitrators to decide the matter.

For plaintiffs, the goal is to right a wrong, honor an agreement or obtain compensation for an injury. Defendants want to protect their rights in opposing the claim. Either way, a qualified trial attorney matters.

Cases we handle

Our attorneys have represented plaintiffs and defendants, as individuals, partnerships and corporations, in these types of cases:

Business litigation

  • Fraud
  • Breach of contract
  • Dissolution or breakup of a business
  • Unfair business practices
  • Business disputes
  • Materialmen’s liens

Employment and injury

  • Employment litigation
  • Employment discrimination
  • Wrongful termination
  • Failure to pay overtime
  • Personal injury
  • Product liability
  • Medical malpractice

Injunctions

  • Preliminary injunctions
  • Permanent injunctions

Real estate litigation

  • Landlord-tenant litigation
  • Construction defects
  • Premises liability

Where we appear

We represent clients before administrative agencies, in mediation and arbitration, in jury and bench trials in state and federal court, and in proceedings before appellate courts. Our attorneys work closely with clients to evaluate their claims or defenses, prepare the case, and explain each step of the process.

A negotiated settlement may be the most appropriate and economical way to resolve some cases. When a dispute cannot be resolved satisfactorily, taking the case to court may be the best or only option. When needed, we work with experts such as investigators, accountants and economic impact analysts.
