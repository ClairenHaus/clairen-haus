Community Association Law

Representation for condominium, homeowners and property owners’ associations in every legal issue of running a community.

The complete spectrum of association legal issues

Condominium and homeowners associations must deal with, and operate under, many complex documents and required procedures: covenants, governing documents, amendments, membership votes, assessments, liens and foreclosures. Pankey & Horlock handles the complete spectrum of legal issues for community associations. As part of our ongoing representation, we also manage related real estate issues such as disputes arising from purchase agreements and construction contracts, and title disputes.

Legal services for community associations

We help directors and managers of condominium, homeowner and property owners’ associations with all of their legal issues. Community association law presents a unique set of challenges, with issues involving real estate law, corporate law, financing, environmental law, insurance and commercial litigation.

  • Interpreting and enforcing covenants and legal documents
  • Advising boards of directors about their rights and obligations under the governing documents
  • Drafting and revising governing documents
  • Coordinating meetings and votes for amendments
  • Collecting assessments and perfecting liens
  • Enforcing covenants, including injunctions and equitable relief
  • Negotiating and enforcing contracts with outside vendors
  • Litigating covenant disputes and vendor contract disputes

How associations work, and where legal help fits

Community associations are governed by a board of directors made up of residents of the community, people from varied backgrounds who share a desire to provide an enjoyable lifestyle and a stable community for their families and neighbors.

Associations typically provide services, regulate activities, impose design standards and levy assessments on property owners. They collect dues from members to cover the community’s common expenses, such as recreational amenities or water and trash service, and may impose special assessments for major improvements. The firm helps homeowners’ and condominium associations protect their bottom line and the quality of life of their residents, from negotiating contracts to preserving property values by enforcing covenants.
