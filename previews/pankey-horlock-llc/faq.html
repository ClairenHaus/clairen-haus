Frequently asked questions

Answers for association boards and managers

Common questions about the firm, community association law, collections and litigation, answered from the firm’s own practice information.

What kinds of associations does Pankey & Horlock represent?

Condominium, homeowners and property owners’ associations of all sizes throughout metropolitan Atlanta and northern Georgia. The firm handles the complete spectrum of legal issues for community associations.

Which counties does the firm serve?

All of metropolitan Atlanta and northern Georgia, including Bartow, Carroll, Cherokee, Clayton, Cobb, Coweta, Dawson, DeKalb, Douglas, Fayette, Forsyth, Fulton, Gwinnett, Hall, Henry, Lumpkin, Paulding and Rockdale counties.

Can you help our association collect unpaid assessments?

Yes. The firm collects community association assessments, fines, charges and attorneys’ fees, and has significant experience in pre-judgment and post-judgment collection, including garnishments, asset location and foreclosure.

What can an association do about delinquent owners before going to court?

Depending on the type of association and what the declaration authorizes, an association may be able to suspend use of common elements such as the pool, tennis courts or parking; in certain circumstances suspend services or utilities paid as a common expense; suspend the owner’s right to vote; and require a tenant of a delinquent owner to pay rent directly to the association. The firm can help identify your association’s rights and set up the proper procedures.

What happens after the association gets a judgment?

The association may garnish the debtor’s wages and bank accounts, place judgment liens on real estate and personal property, and have the sheriff seize and sell property at a public sale. Associations subject to the Georgia Condominium Act or the Georgia Property Owners’ Association Act usually have the right to foreclose on the delinquent property.

Do you draft and amend governing documents?

Yes. The firm drafts and amends governing documents from start to finish, drafts all relevant documents for association votes, and coordinates meetings and votes for amendments.

Can you enforce our covenants?

Yes. The firm interprets and enforces covenants and legal documents, including obtaining injunctions and equitable relief, and litigates covenant disputes when needed.

Do you help with vendor contracts?

Yes. The firm negotiates and enforces contracts with outside vendors and has litigated disputes arising from vendor contracts.

Does the firm handle litigation beyond association matters?

Yes. The attorneys represent plaintiffs and defendants, as individuals, partnerships and corporations, in business litigation, employment matters, personal injury, injunctions and real estate litigation such as landlord-tenant disputes, construction defects and premises liability.

Which courts and forums do you appear in?

State and federal courts, including jury and bench trials, appellate courts, administrative agencies, and mediation and arbitration.

How long have your attorneys been practicing?

The firm’s attorneys have collected debts in Georgia since 1991, when Laura Horlock began her career in civil litigation in Atlanta. Larry Pankey became a member of the Georgia Bar in 1992.
