What we handle for your association

Our attorneys have represented and advised many condominium and homeowners’ associations through northern Georgia in all types of legal issues. We are aggressive, diligent and persistent.

  • Aggressive and efficient collections
  • All forms of contract and legal disputes and litigation
  • Enforcing covenants, including obtaining injunctions and equitable relief
  • Advising boards of directors of their legal rights and obligations under the association documents
  • Drafting and amending governing documents from start to finish
  • Drafting all relevant documents for association votes
  • Negotiating and enforcing contracts with outside vendors

Why associations call us

Association documents are complex. Your counsel shouldn’t be.

Condominium and homeowners associations operate under covenants, governing documents, amendments, membership votes, assessments, liens and foreclosures. The firm handles the complete spectrum of those legal issues, and also manages related real estate matters such as disputes over purchase agreements, construction contracts and title.

Community association law brings together real estate law, corporate law, financing, environmental law, insurance and commercial litigation. Our attorneys help directors and managers work through all of it.

Common questions

Questions boards ask us

What kinds of associations does Pankey & Horlock represent?

Condominium, homeowners and property owners’ associations of all sizes throughout metropolitan Atlanta and northern Georgia, in all of the legal issues they face.

Can you help our association collect unpaid assessments?

Yes. The firm collects community association assessments, fines, charges and attorneys’ fees, with experience in both pre-judgment and post-judgment collection, including garnishments, asset location and foreclosure.

Which counties do you serve?

All of metropolitan Atlanta and northern Georgia, including Bartow, Carroll, Cherokee, Clayton, Cobb, Coweta, Dawson, DeKalb, Douglas, Fayette, Forsyth, Fulton, Gwinnett, Hall, Henry, Lumpkin, Paulding and Rockdale counties.
