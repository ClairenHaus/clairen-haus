Attorney

Larry A. Pankey

Employment discrimination, civil rights litigation, personal injury and business litigation, with extensive trial experience in federal and state court.

Practice

Larry Pankey concentrates his practice in employment discrimination, civil rights litigation, personal injury and business litigation. He has extensive trial experience in both federal and state court, and significant trial and courtroom experience overall.

He became a member of the State Bar of Georgia in 1992 and is also licensed to practice law in Massachusetts and the District of Columbia.

Recognition

  • Named one of the Top 100 Trial Lawyers in the State of Georgia by the American Trial Lawyers Association
  • Member, Million Dollar Advocates Forum

Teaching

Mr. Pankey served as an Adjunct Professor of Law at John Marshall Law School, where he taught courses including Employment Discrimination, Civil Procedure, Constitutional Law, Ethics and Jurisprudence.

Education

  • J.D., Boston University School of Law
  • B.A., University of Georgia

Background

Mr. Pankey grew up on the Fort Berthold Indian Reservation in Mandaree, North Dakota, and is an enrolled member of the Three Affiliated Tribes (Hidatsa).

In his spare time he can be found coaching one of his four children on the football field, the baseball diamond or even the chess board. In 2011, Born 2 Compete named him its Coach of the Year after he coached the 11U Atlanta Colts to the North Metro Football League Championship. In 2013 he coached the 12U Colts to an undefeated NWGYFL Championship.
