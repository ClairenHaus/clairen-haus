Laura C. Horlock

Community association law and litigation, creditors’ rights and collection law, and real estate based litigation, practicing in Atlanta since 1991.

Practice

Laura Horlock began her legal career in civil litigation in Atlanta in 1991. Her focus throughout has been community association law and litigation, creditors’ rights and collection law, and real estate based litigation.

Over her years of practice she has gained broad experience in collection and real estate based litigation through her representation of community associations, banks, mortgage lenders and small businesses, with broad experience in both federal and state courts. Laura represents a large number of community associations in metropolitan Atlanta, big and small, providing the full array of legal services those associations need.

Her litigation experience includes mortgage fraud, real estate title disputes, construction defects, employment discrimination and personal injury, which gives her a broad perspective when representing community associations in litigation.

  • Board of Directors, Junior League of DeKalb County
