Attorney

Lisa B. Fuerst

General counsel and litigator for condominium, property owners and homeowners associations throughout metropolitan Atlanta and north Georgia.

Practice

Lisa Fuerst has extensive experience as both general counsel and litigator for condominium associations, property owners associations and homeowners associations throughout metropolitan Atlanta and north Georgia.

Lisa works closely with community association board members to help them understand their responsibilities under the association’s governing documents, and advises on all phases of covenant enforcement and corporate governance. She has also served associations as a collections litigator, developing cost-effective collection strategies, and has been a pioneer in helping community associations foreclose their liens. She is respected for helping neighbors reach reasonable resolutions of their conflicts, and for advocating for clients when negotiations fail.

Lisa has appeared before Superior, State and Municipal courts throughout the metropolitan Atlanta area. A former criminal defense lawyer, she brings a distinct perspective to helping associations resolve issues with problem owners.

Writing and teaching

Lisa has written articles published in Georgia Commons, a publication of the Community Associations Institute, and is a certified instructor with the Georgia Real Estate Commission. She is an active presenter on community association and collection topics. Her speaking engagements include:

  • “Community Association Finances 101,” Community Associations Institute of Georgia (2008)
  • “Community Association Liens,” LandAmerica Dale P. King Memorial Seminar (2008)
  • “Post Judgment Collections,” Atlanta Volunteer Lawyers Foundation Saturday Lawyer Skills Training (2008)
  • “Homeowner and Condominium Association Assessments: From Origins to Impact,” LandAmerica Dale P. King Memorial Seminar (2006)
  • “Benefits of the POA,” Lorman (2006)
  • Instructor, Community Association Manager Pre-License Course, Georgia Institute of Real Estate (2007–2010)
