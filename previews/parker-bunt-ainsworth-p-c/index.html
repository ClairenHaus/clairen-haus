About the practice

The information clients need before they call.

Formed in 2002By former federal judge Robert M. Parker, who retired in 2016. Shareholders Robert C. (Chris) Bunt and Charles L. Ainsworth continue the practice.

One block from the Tyler Federal CourthouseAdditional building and office space can be arranged for national law-firm clients during trial.

Based in Tyler, Texas, Parker, Bunt & Ainsworth, P.C. is a boutique litigation firm representing individuals and prominent businesses in state and federal courts throughout East Texas. It assists both plaintiffs and defendants in civil litigation, including intellectual property, breach of contract, fraud, antitrust and securities matters, in addition to general tort claims for personal injury.

In addition to its own work as lead trial counsel, the firm regularly acts as local counsel to large national law firms, offering the local expertise to navigate the unique obstacles and customs of litigating in the Eastern District of Texas. The firm describes a team approach to litigation drawing on nearly 90 years of collective experience.

Common questions

Answers drawn from the firm’s own site.

Question-and-answer content, marked up as FAQ schema, is the format search engines and AI assistants quote directly.

Do you serve as local counsel in the Eastern District of Texas?

Yes. Beyond its own work as lead trial counsel, the firm regularly acts as local counsel to large national law firms, guiding them through the customs and obstacles of litigating in the Eastern District of Texas, including jury selection, witness examination and trial strategy.

Do you represent plaintiffs or defendants?

Both. The firm assists plaintiffs and defendants in a wide range of civil litigation matters.

Where is the office?

100 E. Ferguson Street, Suite 418, Tyler, Texas 75702, one block from the Tyler Federal Courthouse. For national law-firm clients, additional building and office space can be arranged for trial.

Who founded the firm?

Former federal judge Robert M. Parker formed the firm in 2002 after over two decades on the Eastern District of Texas bench and the Fifth Circuit Court of Appeals. He retired in 2016; shareholders Robert C. (Chris) Bunt and Charles L. Ainsworth continue the practice.

What kinds of patent cases has the firm handled?

The firm describes patent disputes involving semiconductors, medical devices, software, oil and gas equipment, transmitters, clothing and golf clubs.
