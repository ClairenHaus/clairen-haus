FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about working with our Weymouth office.

Where is your office?

Our office is at 1109 Washington St., Weymouth, MA 02189. Call (781) 337-2066 or email info@cpabarrett.com.

Will my return need an extension?

Not if all of your information is provided. The firm is known for timely tax work, with no extensions when all information is in.

Do you file returns electronically?

Yes. Your return can be filed electronically, so a refund comes back sooner.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. We offer small business accounting (bank reconciliations, income statements, balance sheets, ledger clean-up and unlimited consultations), QuickBooks setup and training, payroll, and part-time CFO services.

My books are behind. Can you still do my taxes?

Yes. We can prepare your bookkeeping for the year, a full Schedule C and your personal return, then help you set up an easy system for next year.

I received a letter from the IRS. Can you help?

Yes. We handle audit representation, unfiled returns, back taxes, payroll tax problems, liens, levies, wage garnishments, payment plans and offers in compromise. Rarely do our clients even have to talk with the IRS.

How do I send you documents securely?

Log in to the secure client portal, or use SecureSend to upload a file directly to a member of the firm.

Can I pay my bill by card?

Yes. Fees can be paid by Visa, MasterCard, Discover or American Express through a secure online payment.

Who will I be working with?

Patrick J. Barrett, CPA, who has owned the firm since 1986.
