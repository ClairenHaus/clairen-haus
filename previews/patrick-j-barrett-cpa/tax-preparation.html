Services

Tax preparation

Today’s tax laws make even a simple return confusing, and it’s easy to miss deductions and credits you’re entitled to. We prepare it for you, carefully.

What you get

  • Your return is checked and rechecked by our software, which flags items the IRS may look at more closely and reviews the math to limit IRS contacts.
  • Your return can be filed electronically, so a refund comes back sooner.
  • We’ll show you how to adjust your payroll withholding, so you aren’t giving the IRS an interest-free loan.
  • We’ll point out potential deductions for next year, and give you a sheet of commonly overlooked deductions.
  • No extensions, as long as all of your information is provided.

Books a mess? No problem.

If you own a small business and haven’t kept up your bookkeeping, don’t worry. We’ll prepare your bookkeeping for the year, prepare a full Schedule C as well as your personal income tax return, and then help you set up an easy system to keep your books in shape next year.

Haven’t filed in a while?

Unfiled returns and back taxes are part of our IRS problem resolution work. The sooner they’re dealt with, the fewer penalties and interest pile up.

Bring last year’s return, your W-2s and 1099s, and records of deductions. Not sure what you need? Call (781) 337-2066.
