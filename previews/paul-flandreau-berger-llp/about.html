About the Firm

A small Media firm, formed in 2009 by partners who had long practiced in Media, dedicated to the service of its clients.

Our history

In 2009, the partners, who had long been practicing in Media, decided to take their practices and create a new firm dedicated to the service of their clients. Paul, Flandreau & Berger is a full-service law firm concentrating on civil litigation, business and real estate in Southeastern Pennsylvania, including Philadelphia, Delaware, Montgomery, Bucks, Chester, Berks and Lehigh counties, for over thirty years. Its services include a well-respected environmental litigation practice, primarily asbestos, representing both national and local companies.

How we serve clients

We serve our clients by carefully reviewing their needs and the issues that confront them, and providing appropriate counsel and advocacy both in and out of court. Because we are a small firm, we can provide personal attention and accessibility to our clients.

Although the firm focuses on litigation, we have the knowledge and experience to assist our clients in other ways and areas. We regularly help disabled individuals obtain the Social Security disability and Supplemental Security Income benefits to which they are entitled, and help injured workers and people hurt in accidents. We represent clients in property tax appeals and in commercial and real estate transactions, and we work with individuals and businesses, helping them succeed and prosper.

When you need someone else

If a client needs specialized representation in a matter that we cannot personally handle, the contacts we have developed through our involvement in the Delaware County and Philadelphia-area legal community let us help that client obtain the quality representation that is needed.

Our office

Our office is in Media, Pennsylvania, the county seat of Delaware County, and we can meet you at various other offices in Southeastern Pennsylvania. The Media office is close to I-476 (the Blue Route) and offers a free parking lot for clients.
