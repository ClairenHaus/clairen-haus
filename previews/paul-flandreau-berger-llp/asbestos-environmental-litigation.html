The firm’s environmental practice

Paul, Flandreau & Berger’s services include a well-respected environmental litigation practice, primarily asbestos, representing both national and local companies. Asbestos litigation is one of the areas the firm’s litigation practice concentrates on.

What asbestos litigation generally involves

Asbestos lawsuits typically arise from claims that exposure to asbestos-containing products or materials caused an illness, often decades after the exposure. Because a person may have worked around many products over a long career, these cases commonly name many companies as defendants.

  • Exposure history. Where, when and how a person is said to have been exposed is examined closely.
  • Product identification. Whether a particular company’s product was actually present is often central to each defendant’s position.
  • Medical and expert evidence. Diagnosis and causation are usually addressed through medical records and expert testimony.
  • Coordinated dockets. Courts with many asbestos cases often manage them through specialized procedures and schedules.
