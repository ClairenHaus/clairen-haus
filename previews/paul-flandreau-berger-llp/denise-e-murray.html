Attorney

Denise E. Murray

Social Security disability, business law and civil litigation.

  • PracticeSocial Security disability and SSI; business law; civil litigation
  • EducationPennsylvania State University, magna cum laude, Phi Beta Kappa; Temple University School of Law
  • AdmittedPennsylvania, 1990

Ms. Murray devotes a significant portion of her practice to Social Security disability and Supplemental Security Income clients. These individuals have physical or mental disabilities, or a combination of disabilities. She is involved in all phases of their cases, including meeting with the applicants, developing the evidence that supports the claims, and representing them at their hearings.

She also works with business clients to help establish a foundation for their continued success. In addition to helping select and form the appropriate entity, she structures businesses by drafting their corporate governance documents, including shareholder agreements and operating agreements.

Ms. Murray also regularly works with the other members of the firm during the litigation of various civil cases.

Education and admissions

Ms. Murray graduated magna cum laude from Pennsylvania State University, where she was elected a member of Phi Beta Kappa. She then graduated from the Temple University School of Law. She was admitted to the Pennsylvania bar in 1990, and is a member of the Delaware County Bar Association.
