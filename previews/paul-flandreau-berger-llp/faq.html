Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about the firm, its practice and how to reach it.

Where is the firm’s office?

The office is at 320 West Front Street, Media, PA 19063, in Media, the county seat of Delaware County. It is close to I-476 (the Blue Route) and has a free parking lot for clients.

Can I meet an attorney somewhere other than Media?

Yes. The firm has the ability to meet clients at various other offices in Southeastern Pennsylvania. Call 610-565-4750 to arrange it.

Which counties does the firm serve?

The firm serves clients in Southeastern Pennsylvania, including Philadelphia, Delaware, Montgomery, Bucks, Chester, Berks and Lehigh counties.

What kinds of cases does the firm handle?

The practice concentrates on litigation, including asbestos litigation, workers’ compensation, Social Security disability and personal injury. The firm also handles business law and transactions, commercial and residential real estate, property tax assessment appeals, commercial litigation, subrogation, zoning and municipal matters.

Does the firm represent injured workers?

Yes. James R. Flandreau has built a regional workers’ compensation practice since 1988 and now uses his experience to protect the rights of employees who are hurt at work. Timothy A. Berger has litigated workers’ compensation cases since 1997.

Can the firm help me apply for Social Security disability or SSI?

Yes. Denise E. Murray devotes a significant portion of her practice to Social Security disability and Supplemental Security Income clients, working with Timothy A. Berger. She is involved in all phases of a case, from the first meeting through the hearing.

Does the firm handle property tax assessment appeals?

Yes. The firm represents clients in property tax assessment appeals for commercial and residential property.

Can the firm help me set up or buy a business?

Yes. The firm handles the formation, operation, management and termination of LLCs, corporations, partnerships and limited partnerships, including operating and shareholder agreements, and the purchase or sale of businesses or business assets.

What if my matter is outside the firm’s practice?

Through its relationships in the Delaware County and Philadelphia-area legal community, the firm can help you obtain quality representation from other well-qualified attorneys.

How do I reach the firm?

Call 610-565-4750. The office is at 320 West Front Street in Media.

These answers are general information about the firm, not legal advice about your situation.
