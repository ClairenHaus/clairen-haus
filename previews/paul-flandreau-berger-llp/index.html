How we work

Careful review, then counsel and advocacy in and out of court

We serve our clients by carefully reviewing their needs and the issues that confront them, and providing appropriate counsel and advocacy both in and out of court. Our goal is to make sure every client receives the individualized attention and quality representation they deserve.

When a client needs counsel in an area in which we do not practice, the relationships we have built in the Delaware County and Philadelphia-area legal community let us work with other well-qualified attorneys to make sure those needs are met.
