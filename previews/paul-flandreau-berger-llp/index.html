About the practice

The information clients need before they call.

Media office near I-476 (the Blue Route), with a free client parking lot

The current site describes Paul, Flandreau & Berger as a full-service firm concentrating on civil litigation, business and real estate in Southeastern Pennsylvania, with a well-respected environmental litigation practice, primarily asbestos, representing both national and local companies.

The firm's stated goal is individualized attention and quality representation for each client. When a client needs counsel in an area the firm does not practice, its relationships with other well-qualified attorneys are used to make sure those needs are met.

Preview content is based on publicly available information and is subject to the firm's review before publication. Images, final copy, domain connection, forms, and accessibility checks are completed during the paid build.

Questions clients ask

Answers drawn from the firm's own site.

Short, direct answers are what people and AI assistants look for first. Each one below comes from information the firm already publishes.

Where is Paul, Flandreau & Berger located?

The office is at 320 West Front Street in Media, Pennsylvania, the county seat of Delaware County. It is close to I-476 (the Blue Route) and has a free parking lot for clients. The firm can also meet clients at other offices in Southeastern Pennsylvania.

Which counties does the firm serve?

The firm serves clients across Southeastern Pennsylvania, including Philadelphia, Delaware, Montgomery, Bucks, Chester, Berks and Lehigh counties.

Does the firm handle Social Security disability claims?

Yes. Social Security disability and Supplemental Security Income are listed practice areas. Denise E. Murray devotes a significant portion of her practice to these claims and works with Timothy A. Berger to develop and present the cases.

Does the firm handle workers' compensation?

Yes. Workers' compensation is a core part of the practice. James R. Flandreau has built a regional workers' compensation practice since 1988, and Timothy A. Berger has litigated workers' compensation cases since 1997.
