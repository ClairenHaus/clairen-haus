Partner

James R. Flandreau

Workers’ compensation and personal injury. A regional workers’ compensation practice serving Southeastern Pennsylvania since 1988.

  • PracticeWorkers’ compensation; personal injury, including motor vehicle accidents
  • EducationBucknell University; The Dickinson School of Law
  • AdmittedPennsylvania, 1983; U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania; Third Circuit Court of Appeals; United States Supreme Court

Since 1988, James R. Flandreau has built a regional workers’ compensation practice serving Southeastern Pennsylvania, including Delaware, Philadelphia, Bucks, Chester and Montgomery counties. He originally worked with both injured workers and employers, and so learned how workers’ compensation insurers try to reduce or cut off an injured worker’s benefits. He now uses that knowledge to protect the rights of employees who are hurt at work.

He and his staff carefully review the facts of each case and gather the documentation and evidence needed to win at trial. Because insurance companies and their lawyers know he is ready and willing to litigate each case to a final decision rather than settle on less than favorable terms, that readiness adds value to his clients’ cases. Over the years, he has taken hundreds of cases to a final decision.

His goal is to make certain that clients receive personal attention and quality representation, so they receive the benefits to which they are entitled and can focus on recovering from their injury and regaining their normal life.

Because of his years of experience, he is well known among the workers’ compensation bar and by the Workers’ Compensation Judges before whom he regularly tries cases. Since 2005, he has been recognized as a workers’ compensation Super Lawyer, a title earned based on the recognition of his peers.

In addition to his workers’ compensation practice, he regularly handles personal injury cases arising from various incidents, including motor vehicle accidents.

Education and admissions

After graduating from Bucknell University, Mr. Flandreau attended the Dickinson School of Law. He was admitted to the Pennsylvania Bar in 1983, and is also admitted to practice before the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania, the Third Circuit Court of Appeals and the United States Supreme Court.
