A founding member of the original firm, Peter Mardinly is now of counsel to the firm. After more than 23 years of private practice, he accepted a position as General Counsel and Chief Financial Officer for a company with significant real estate holdings in Pennsylvania and Florida.

Education and admissions

After graduating from Yale University, he attended Boston University School of Law, and was admitted to the Pennsylvania and Massachusetts bars in 1977. He was additionally admitted to the Florida bar in 2002. In 1981 he received his LL.M. in Taxation from the Temple University School of Law. He was admitted to practice before the U.S. Tax Court in 1978, the United States Court of Appeals for the Third Circuit in 1991, and the United States Supreme Court in 1995.

Teaching and service

  • Adjunct Assistant Professor teaching tax law at Widener University, 1981–1984
  • President of the Delaware County Attorney/CPA Forum, 1989–1991
  • Media Rotary Club member since 1981; President, 1997–1998; Secretary of Rotary District 7450, 1998–1999 and 2000–2001
  • Board of Managers of the Rocky Run YMCA for more than 10 years, including as Vice Chairman, and Chairman of the Building Committee starting in 1998
