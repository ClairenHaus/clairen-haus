A few things buyers often overlook

  • Review the title search early. Ask for a copy as soon as it is available. Closing is not the time to read it for the first time.
  • Title insurance has limits. It does not remove recorded easements or restrictions, and Pennsylvania policies normally do not cover mechanics’ liens that had not been filed when the policy was issued.
  • Planned communities have rules. If a home is in a planned community, you live with its recorded Declaration. Pennsylvania’s Uniform Planned Community Act requires that buyers receive certain documents.

Read more in Mr. Berger’s article, The role of title insurance when buying real estate.
