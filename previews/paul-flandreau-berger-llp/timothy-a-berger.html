Mr. Berger has litigated workers’ compensation cases since 1997, and has given presentations and prepared written materials on the workers’ compensation system for the Delaware County Bar Association and other organizations.

Real estate

He is regularly involved in commercial and residential real estate transactions. He assists clients with the purchase, sale and leasing of commercial real estate, and counsels both buyers and sellers of homes, including homes in planned communities and condominium associations. He has represented several homeowners’ associations, and works with developers during all phases, from planning to construction to sale. He litigates real estate cases, including disclosure issues in home sales, tax assessment appeals and construction issues.

Business clients

He provides a variety of services to help business clients grow and prosper, working with them from startup to windup. He helps choose the most appropriate entity, including LLCs and S corporations, and drafts the governing documents, including partnership, shareholder and operating agreements. He also prepares form and custom contracts and other documents for daily operations. As clients’ businesses grow, he helps with succession planning, and with mergers and acquisitions, helping clients understand the ramifications and structure the transaction appropriately.

Disability and litigation

Mr. Berger also works with disabled individuals seeking Social Security disability and Supplemental Security Income benefits, together with Denise E. Murray, to analyze, develop and present the cases. He maintains a litigation practice in state and federal court, with civil cases ranging from commercial litigation for business clients to personal injury.

Education, admissions and memberships

Mr. Berger graduated with Honors from Swarthmore College and cum laude from the Temple University School of Law, where he served as Executive Editor of the Temple Environmental Law & Technology Journal. He was admitted to the Pennsylvania Bar in 1994, and is also admitted before the U.S. District Court for the Eastern District of Pennsylvania and the Third Circuit Court of Appeals.
