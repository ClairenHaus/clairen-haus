1031 Tax Deferred Exchanges

Pearce Law Firm can help you with 1031 tax deferred exchanges, closing the sale and purchase that make up the exchange.

How a 1031 exchange works, in general terms

Section 1031 of the Internal Revenue Code allows an owner of investment or business property to defer tax on a gain by exchanging it for other like-kind property. The rules are strict: replacement property generally has to be identified within 45 days of the sale and acquired within 180 days, and the sale proceeds are usually held by a qualified intermediary rather than paid to the seller.

Because the timelines are fixed, the closings on both sides of the exchange need careful coordination. Your tax adviser can tell you whether an exchange makes sense for you.

Where the firm fits

The firm handles the real estate closings that make up the exchange and works within the exchange’s timeline. Call the office to discuss your transaction.
