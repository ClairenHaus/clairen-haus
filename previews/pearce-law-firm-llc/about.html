About

A boutique real estate firm in Mount Pleasant

Pearce Law Firm, LLC focuses on real estate closing services and representation in residential and commercial real estate matters, across the river from Charleston.

Firm profile

Pearce Law Firm, LLC was formed in 2007 when Dixon F. Pearce, II decided to leave his former firm, in which he was a partner. Dixon has practiced in the Charleston area since 1992, including more than seven years handling litigation matters before focusing on real estate law in 2000. He estimates he has closed over 10,000 real estate transactions.

The staff at Pearce Law Firm includes real estate closers who prepare and coordinate closings and other matters. Together, the firm’s closers have a combined experience of almost 100 years.

How we work

We strive to make your transaction as smooth as possible, customizing our service to meet the particular needs of your situation. We offer custom fee quotes, pre-ratification contract review and on-site closing services, and we keep secure IT systems and file storage, with back-up internet service so work continues if our provider goes down.

Above all, our goal is to provide you with quality representation and excellent service.
