Born in Charleston. Practicing here since 1992.

Background

Dixon was born in Charleston in 1964 and has lived in Charleston and Mount Pleasant his entire life, with the exception of his college and law school years.

He graduated from the University of South Carolina with a B.S. in Business Administration in 1987, and earned his J.D. from the USC School of Law in 1992. He has practiced in the local area since 1992, and was formerly a partner in Buist, Byars, Pearce & Taylor, LLC.

Practice

Dixon spent more than seven years handling litigation matters before focusing on real estate law in 2000. In 2007 he left his former firm, where he was a partner, and formed Pearce Law Firm, LLC. He estimates he has closed more than 10,000 real estate transactions.

Today he supervises the firm’s residential and commercial closings, working with a staff of real estate closers who prepare and coordinate each closing.
