FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers drawn from the firm’s own information. For anything about your specific transaction, call the office.

Where is Pearce Law Firm located?

The office is at 1476 Ben Sawyer Blvd, Suite 1, Mt. Pleasant, SC 29464, in Mount Pleasant, across the river from Charleston.

What areas do you serve?

The firm serves all areas of the Charleston metro area, including Charleston, Mount Pleasant, Sullivan’s Island, Isle of Palms, Kiawah Island, Seabrook Island, Folly Beach, North Charleston, Moncks Corner, Ridgeville, Holly Hill and Bonneau.

Can you handle a closing outside the Charleston area?

Yes. The firm can accommodate closing needs in counties throughout the State of South Carolina from its office in Mount Pleasant.

What kinds of real estate transactions do you handle?

Residential and commercial real estate closings, builder closing services, refinances and home equity lines, title insurance, second homes and vacation homes, investment property, Power of Attorney closings, Limited Liability Companies, 1031 tax deferred exchanges, construction loans, and acquisition and development work.

Can I get a quote for my closing?

Yes. The firm offers custom fee quotes. Call 843-972-0860 to discuss your transaction.

Will you review a contract before I sign it?

Yes. The firm offers pre-ratification contract review, so a contract can be reviewed before it is final.

Do you offer on-site closings?

Yes. On-site closing services are one of the services the firm offers.

Can I close if I can’t be there in person?

The firm handles Power of Attorney closings, where someone you authorize signs on your behalf. Call the office early so the Power of Attorney can be prepared and reviewed before closing.

Who is the attorney at Pearce Law Firm?

Dixon F. Pearce, II. He earned his J.D. from the University of South Carolina School of Law in 1992, has practiced in the Charleston area since 1992, and has focused on real estate law since 2000.

How long has the firm been in business?

Pearce Law Firm, LLC was formed in 2007, when Dixon F. Pearce, II left his former firm, Buist, Byars, Pearce & Taylor, LLC, where he was a partner.

Who prepares my closing documents?

The firm’s staff includes real estate closers who prepare and coordinate closings, with a combined experience of almost 100 years, under the supervision of Dixon F. Pearce, II.

How do I reach the office?

Call 843-972-0860, fax 843-972-0861, or email closings@pearce-law.com.

Call the office to discuss your needs.
