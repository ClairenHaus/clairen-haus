Common questions
What areas does Pearce Law Firm serve?

All areas of the Charleston metro area, including Charleston, Mount Pleasant, Sullivans Island, Isle of Palms, Kiawah Island, Seabrook Island, Folly Beach, North Charleston, Moncks Corner, Ridgeville, Holly Hill and Bonneau. The firm can also accommodate closings in counties throughout South Carolina from its Mount Pleasant office.

Can the firm review my contract before I sign?

Yes. The firm lists pre-ratification contract review among its services, along with custom fee quotes and on-site closing services.

Does the firm handle 1031 exchanges and power of attorney closings?

Yes. The firm says it can help with 1031 tax-deferred exchanges, power of attorney closings, LLCs, construction loans, and acquisition and development work.

How do I reach the office?

Call 843-972-0860 or email closings@pearce-law.com. The office is at 1476 Ben Sawyer Blvd, Suite 1, Mt. Pleasant, SC 29464.

About the practice

The information clients need before they call.

  • Dixon F. Pearce, II: J.D., USC Law School, 1992; B.S. in Business Administration, University of South Carolina, 1987
  • Practicing in the local area since 1992; formerly a partner in Buist, Byars, Pearce & Taylor, LLC
  • Over 7 years in litigation before focusing on real estate law in 2000

Pearce Law Firm, LLC was formed in 2007 when Dixon F. Pearce II left his former firm, where he was a partner. Born in Charleston in 1964, he has lived in Charleston and Mount Pleasant his entire life apart from college and law school.

The firm’s staff includes real estate closers who prepare and coordinate closings. Its stated goal is quality representation and excellent service, handling each transaction in a professional, trustworthy and responsive manner.
