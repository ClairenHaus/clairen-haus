Questions

Common questions

What areas do you serve?

The firm serves all areas of the Charleston metro area, including Charleston, Mount Pleasant, Sullivan’s Island, Isle of Palms, Kiawah Island, Seabrook Island, Folly Beach, North Charleston, Moncks Corner, Ridgeville, Holly Hill and Bonneau.

Can I get a quote for my closing?

Yes. The firm offers custom fee quotes. Call 843-972-0860 to discuss your transaction.

Will you review a contract before I sign it?

Yes. The firm offers pre-ratification contract review, so a contract can be reviewed before it is final.

Do you offer on-site closings?

Yes. On-site closing services are one of the services the firm offers.
