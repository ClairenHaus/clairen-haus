Refinances & Home Equity Lines

Whether you are refinancing to a new rate or opening a home equity line of credit, the firm handles the closing so the new loan is signed, recorded and funded correctly.

What happens at a refinance closing

A refinance replaces your existing mortgage with a new loan. The closing attorney examines title, obtains the payoff of the old loan, prepares and supervises the signing of the new loan documents, pays off the prior lender, and records the new mortgage.

A home equity line of credit (HELOC) works much the same way: it is a loan secured by your home, and it is closed and recorded so the lender’s interest is properly in place.
