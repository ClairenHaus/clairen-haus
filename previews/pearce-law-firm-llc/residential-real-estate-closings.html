Residential Real Estate Closings

The heart of the practice. Pearce Law Firm focuses on residential real estate closings for buyers, sellers and lenders throughout the Charleston metro area, with the goal of making your transaction as smooth as possible.

What a closing involves

A residential closing is where a home purchase becomes final: the deed is signed and recorded, the loan documents are executed, and funds are disbursed to the right people. Before that day there is work to do, including examining title, coordinating with the lender, preparing the settlement figures and documents, and making sure every condition in the contract has been met.

In South Carolina, a licensed attorney must supervise a real estate closing, so choosing your closing attorney is one of the early decisions in any purchase.

How Pearce Law Firm helps

  • Closers who prepare and coordinate your closing, with a combined experience the firm puts at almost 100 years
  • Custom fee quotes, so you know what to expect
  • Pre-ratification contract review, before you are committed
  • On-site closing services
  • Purchases involving a Power of Attorney or a Limited Liability Company

Every file is supervised by attorney Dixon F. Pearce, II, who has focused on real estate law since 2000 and estimates he has closed more than 10,000 real estate transactions.
