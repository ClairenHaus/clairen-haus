About Peck Ritchey

A boutique firm that concentrates its practice in all aspects of probate, estate planning, guardianship, special needs planning and elder law, with an emphasis on the litigation of contested wills, trusts and disabled persons' estates.

Clients work directly with a professional legal team that uses its experience and creativity to find solutions for personal and business needs, and that makes sure clients understand their options and the steps required to reach their goals.

The firm believes in its responsibility to serve as a leader in both the profession and the community. Its attorneys are involved in professional and civic organizations, speak frequently at continuing legal education programs for other attorneys, and are regularly contacted by news media as a resource on issues affecting older adults and families.

Peck Ritchey's attorneys are frequently involved in drafting new legislation for consideration by the Illinois General Assembly and have been recognized in Best Lawyers in America®, 2024–2026.

  • Boutique practice concentrated in probate, estate planning, guardianship, special needs planning and elder law, with an emphasis on contested wills, trusts and disabled persons' estates.
  • Represents all parties to decedent's estate litigation, trust litigation and contested guardianships.
  • Attorneys are regularly involved in drafting legislation for the Illinois General Assembly, speak at continuing legal education programs and are contacted by news media on issues affecting older adults and families.
  • Kerry R. Peck is a past President of the 22,000-member Chicago Bar Association and was appointed by the Illinois Supreme Court to chair its Commission on Elder Law.
  • The firm was hired by the City of Chicago's Department on Aging to rewrite the Elder Abuse and Neglect Act.
  • All attorneys are members of the Chicago Bar Association and the Illinois State Bar Association.
