Timothy J. Ritchey

Timothy J. Ritchey is a partner at Peck Ritchey, LLC who concentrates his practice in estate and trust litigation, elder law, and guardianship.

(312) 201-0900info@peckritchey.com

Tim has been appointed Special Assistant Attorney General in numerous matters to represent the State's interests in actions relating to Illinois healthcare and disability law. In 2014, Tim wrote legislation and testified as an expert before the Illinois Senate on an amendment to the Illinois Probate Act regarding elder abuse and financial exploitation.

Timothy J. Ritchey was selected to the Illinois Super Lawyers list from 2019 to 2026, Super Lawyers Rising Stars from 2012 to 2018, an honor which is bestowed on up to 2.5% of the attorneys in Illinois annually, and has been named one of the Top 100 Lawyers in Illinois by Super Lawyers. Tim has authored numerous articles addressing elder law issues. In 2014, Tim was selected by the Law Bulletin Publishing Company as one of the 40 Illinois Attorneys Under 40 to Watch, as well as one of the Best Professionals Under Forty by Suburban Life in the western suburbs. Timothy J. Ritchey was also recognized in The Best Lawyers in America® 2026 for his work in Litigation – Trusts and Estates and Trusts and Estates and was recognized as a Leading Lawyer in Elder Law and Trust, Will & Estate Planning Law by Law Bulletin Media's Leading Lawyers Network (2025).

Tim serves as Guardian ad Litem and Court Appointed Counsel before the Probate Court. He works closely with individuals and financial institutions throughout the administration of estates and trusts. He vigorously represents all parties in contested will, trust and guardianship proceedings, in addition to assisting families to plan for disabled children and adults.
