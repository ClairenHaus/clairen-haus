Corporate Law & Family Business Planning

Formation, governance and succession planning for small and family businesses.

Peck Ritchey's corporate attorneys work with businesses of any size, often from the moment a business is only an idea, through formation and on as it grows. The firm offers small and family business planning alongside its estate and trust work, so a business owner's plan and personal plan fit together.

Missed filings and misunderstood obligations can expose an owner to personal liability. The firm's role is to make sure the structure is right from the start.

What we handle

  • Business formation and structure
  • Ongoing corporate counsel
  • Small and family business planning
  • Business succession planning
  • Resolving family disagreements about the management of a family business

Common questions

Do you work with brand-new businesses?

Yes. The firm works with clients from the concept stage and continues to provide legal services once the business is up and running.

How does business planning connect to estate planning?

For family businesses, who runs and who owns the company after the founder is an estate planning question as much as a corporate one. The firm handles both sides, including disputes about the management of a family business.
