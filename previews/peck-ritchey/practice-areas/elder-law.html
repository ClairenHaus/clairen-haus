Elder Law

Health care decisions, long-term care planning, estate planning and guardianship for seniors and their families.

Peck Ritchey provides a full range of services for seniors: planning for health care decisions and end-of-life issues, long-term care planning, estate planning and administration, and guardianship.

The firm's team helps families locate the appropriate type of care, coordinate private and public resources to finance it, and work to ensure the client actually receives quality care. Its attorneys helped rewrite Chicago's Elder Abuse and Neglect Act and have testified before the Illinois House and Senate.

What we handle

  • Health care and end-of-life planning
  • Long-term care planning
  • Coordinating private and public resources to pay for care
  • Estate planning and administration for seniors
  • Guardianship of adults
  • Elder abuse, neglect and financial exploitation

Common questions

What does an elder law attorney do?

Elder law covers the legal issues that come with aging: who makes health care and financial decisions, how long-term care is arranged and paid for, protecting against abuse and exploitation, and planning an estate. Peck Ritchey handles all of these for seniors and the families supporting them.

Can you help pay for long-term care?

The firm helps families coordinate private and public resources to finance the cost of care as part of long-term care planning. Whether a particular program applies depends on the family's facts, which an initial consultation covers.

Where do you handle elder law matters?

The firm handles cases in Cook, Lake, Kane and DuPage Counties from offices in Chicago, Northbrook, Oak Brook and Lake Forest.
