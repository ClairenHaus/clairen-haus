Guardianship Litigation

Contested guardianships, and action against guardians who neglect, abuse or exploit the people in their care.

Guardians hold significant power over another person's life and money. When a guardian breaches that trust, or when family members disagree about who should serve, the matter goes to court. Peck Ritchey's guardianship litigation attorneys represent families on every side of these disputes.

The firm brings and defends contested guardianships and takes action to stop breaches of fiduciary duty, financial exploitation and elder abuse by guardians and agents.

What we handle

  • Contested guardianship proceedings
  • Breach of fiduciary duty by a guardian
  • Citations to recover and discover assets
  • Financial exploitation by a guardian or agent
  • Removal of guardians and agents under power of attorney
  • Elder abuse

Common questions

What if I think my parent's guardian is misusing their money?

A court can be asked to investigate, through a citation to discover and recover assets, and to remove a guardian who has breached their duty. Peck Ritchey brings these proceedings and defends guardians who are wrongly accused.

Can a guardianship be contested?

Yes. Family members can contest who is appointed, or whether a guardianship is needed at all. The firm represents all parties to contested guardianships.

Does the firm also defend guardians?

Yes. The firm represents fiduciaries, including guardians, trustees and agents, as well as the beneficiaries and family members bringing claims against them.
