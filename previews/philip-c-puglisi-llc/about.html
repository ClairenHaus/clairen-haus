Our approach

A small family law office built around one idea: the attorney you meet at your consultation is the attorney who handles your case.

One attorney, start to finish

When Philip Puglisi takes on your representation, a team relationship is created with a truly one-on-one approach. He handles your matter from start to finish, not an associate or another individual.

Many prospective clients ask which lawyer will be in charge of their case. At the Law Offices of Philip C. Puglisi, LLC, the answer is always the same. You should be able to talk to your lawyer as you would talk to a friend or relative, without ever feeling talked down to or ill at ease. The divorce process may be difficult, but your lawyer should be a comfort, not a hindrance.

Every route, explained

There is more than one way through a divorce. Philip explains the litigated, mediated and collaborative routes, the pros and cons of each, and the retainer required, so you can choose the path that fits your family. He can handle your case whichever you choose.

Honest about cost

Every potential client has the absolute right to ask what a divorce might cost. It is hard to give a number without the whole picture: the lawyer is paid for time and advice, so a contested matter that needs more time costs more, and a straightforward, uncontested matter where the spouses agree on most issues costs less.

What you are entitled to

  • Legal advice explained in simple, easy-to-understand terms
  • Competent, diligent representation
  • Honest and accurate legal fees
  • Quick responses to phone calls and written communications
  • Convenient times to meet, centered around your schedule
  • Referrals to competent, experienced experts when needed, such as CPAs and mental health and custody experts
  • Concrete roadmaps of where your case is going
  • A unique one-on-one relationship
