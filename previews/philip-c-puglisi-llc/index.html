Your options

Three paths to a divorce in New Jersey

Are you ready to begin? You could try mediation, file for divorce at the outset, or go the collaborative route. Philip can handle your divorce whichever avenue you choose, and he will explain each one in detail at a free half-hour consultation.

i.

Litigation

The traditional route: a complaint filed in the Family Part of the Superior Court, with Philip advocating for you at every stage, through settlement or trial.

How a divorce works →
ii.

Mediation

You and your spouse work out the terms yourselves, guided by a neutral mediator. Philip is accredited by the New Jersey Supreme Court as a Family Law Mediator.

About mediation →
iii.

Collaborative divorce

Both spouses have attorneys and commit in writing to settling out of court, with the children as the priority.

Your attorney

Philip C. Puglisi

One attorney and one relationship, from your first consultation to the final judgment.

A graduate of Temple University School of Law who clerked for the Arizona Court of Appeals, Philip has spent his career in family and matrimonial law in Bergen and Passaic counties. He is accredited by the New Jersey Supreme Court as a Family Law Mediator, serves on the Bergen and Passaic County rosters of economic mediators, and has sat on the Bergen County Early Settlement Panel for more than 20 years.
