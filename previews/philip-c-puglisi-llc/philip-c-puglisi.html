Divorce can be one of the most trying, difficult and emotional times in a person’s life. The last thing anyone needs is the added stress of the wrong divorce lawyer.

You want someone with extensive divorce and family law experience to guide you through the process, and an attorney who is compassionate and understands what you are going through. Philip C. Puglisi has spent his career handling family law and matrimonial cases in Bergen and Passaic counties and the surrounding communities of North Jersey.

Philip has the patience to truly listen to your concerns, however small or complex. Each client is treated with dignity, care and respect, and over the years he has developed a following based on that treatment. Phone calls, emails and other inquiries are answered promptly and courteously, to reduce your burden as you move through the process.

At his office, you get one lawyer for the entire matter: him. He handles divorces through litigation, mediation or the collaborative process, and as a young attorney he worked on Gayet v. Gayet (1983), one of the New Jersey Supreme Court’s seminal cases on cohabitation and alimony.
