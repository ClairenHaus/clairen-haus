Preparing for your first meeting

At your first meeting, be prepared to give a general overview of your situation, including:

  • Your current living arrangements
  • Your overall financial picture
  • The status of any children
  • A list of assets and who owns them
  • Your debts
  • Your employment history
  • Any domestic violence or substance abuse issues
  • Your immediate and future goals

Philip will explain his background and experience, give an overview of the divorce process and the routes you can choose, litigation, mediation or collaborative divorce, with the pros and cons of each, and tell you the retainer he will require. Bring a written list of questions; you should never hesitate to ask pointed ones.

Start gathering your financial information

If a divorce is filed, you will need to prepare a Case Information Statement covering income, monthly expenses during the marriage and now, and a balance sheet of family assets and liabilities. Reviewing your credit card statements and checks helps pin down monthly expenses.
