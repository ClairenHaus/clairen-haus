About the firm

A general practice in Bergen County, minutes from New York, with an office in lower Manhattan. Serving clients since 1984.

Generations of clients

Deborah L. Pico founded the firm and has been licensed to practice in New Jersey since 1984 and in New York for more than 30 years. Generations of clients have come to the practice over the past 40 years, and the firm wants to be your first stop for any legal matter.

The goal is the highest quality legal services for reasonable fees. The firm stands by its reputation for good service, communication and cost-effective problem solving.

In our firm, the client comes first

Your legal problems are handled as efficiently and effectively as possible. The firm tries to identify all of your legal issues, unlike an attorney who works in only one or two narrow areas. The practice areas are broad because years of experience have shown how different areas of the law affect your primary situation.

The firm will advise you how a step forward in one area might harm you in another, and, when necessary, will co-counsel with experts in the field, with your approval. No problem is too small to review.
