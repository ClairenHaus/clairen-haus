Background

Deborah L. Pico has been a New Jersey resident her entire life. She graduated from Parsippany High School and went on to Montclair State College, earning a Bachelor of Arts in Political Science in 1979, back when it was still a sleepy little state college. She then attended Benjamin N. Cardozo School of Law in New York City and received her Juris Doctor in 1982.

After being licensed to practice in New Jersey and New York, she opened her first law firm with Lois A. Myers, Esq. in 1984. That firm, Myers & Pico, P.C., remained in operation until she opened The Law Offices of Deborah L. Pico, LLC, now located in Fort Lee, New Jersey, with an office in New York City.

Over her years in practice, Ms. Pico has served as an adjunct professor and has taught many adult education classes on the law.

Her practice

The firm is a general practice, concentrating on residential and commercial real estate, including closings and contracts, elder law, estate planning with Wills and trusts, probate and estate litigation. Being licensed in both New Jersey and New York lets her help clients whose assets are in one state while they live in the other.
