Real estate closings, co-ops and condominiums

Real estate has been at the center of the practice for 40 years, for first-time buyers, seasoned investors and commercial clients in New Jersey and New York.

Who the firm represents

Clients range from first-time home buyers to people who buy and sell real estate for a living. The firm represents individual, commercial and business clients in transactions and proceedings involving real property, including:

  • Purchases, sales and contracts
  • Refinances and construction mortgages
  • Leases and tenancies
  • Title examinations and quiet title actions
  • Closings and property management matters
  • Foreclosures and evictions

Co-ops and condominiums

Cooperatives and condominiums are their own area of the law. The firm represents co-op and condo associations and boards, as well as individual buyers and sellers of co-ops and condos in both New Jersey and New York.

A co-op in particular is not real estate in the usual sense: it is shares of stock. When you buy a co-op, you are buying into a corporation and receiving a proprietary lease that gives you the right to live in your unit. You want an attorney who knows how co-op transactions work.

When a deal falls apart

Sometimes a closing is cancelled and you find yourself suing, or being sued, over the contract of sale. The firm handles litigation relating to real estate, including enforcing contracts, recovering your deposit when you cancel, suits for specific performance, foreclosures, evictions and quieting title.

Fees. Real estate matters are usually handled for a flat fee. Some transactions may call for an hourly arrangement.
