About the firm

In the business of client service since 2009.

Quarles Law Firm, LLC provides civil legal services in the Birmingham–Jefferson County, Alabama, area, from offices in Mountain Brook Office Park.

Firm overview

Established in 2009, Quarles Law Firm, LLC is in the business of client service. The firm focuses on the unique needs of the individuals and businesses it represents.

“We always strive for the best results for our clients, but we believe the process is important, too.” Clients’ calls are answered or promptly returned. Updated information is provided on an ongoing basis. The firm employs a team approach, and clients are an essential part of the team.

Two attorneys, over 50 years of experience

The firm consists of two seasoned attorneys, Randy Quarles and Fran Quarles, with over 50 years of combined legal experience. A client’s case is never turned over to a paralegal or a fresh-out-of-law-school associate. Randy and Fran are also married, and through past personal experience they understand the emotional and financial turmoil that divorce, child custody and child support issues can cause.

Before founding the firm, Randy was a partner at Birmingham law firms and clerked for Judge Emmett Ripley Cox of the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Eleventh Circuit. Fran clerked for Judge Robert B. Propst of the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Alabama and served in banking compliance and as Associate General Counsel with HealthSouth Corporation.
