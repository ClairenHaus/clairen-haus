Driving directions

Open in Apple MapsOpen in Google Maps

Coming from the north of Birmingham

Take Highway 280 East through Birmingham (outbound). After Highway 31 and Highway 280 split in Homewood, continue on Highway 280 East. Turn right at the first traffic light onto Office Park Drive, between a Regions branch and a Wells Fargo branch. Proceed up the hill until you see a brown brick sign on the left for Building 300. Turn in the driveway in front of the sign and proceed straight for free surface parking.

Coming from the south of Birmingham

Take Highway 280 West toward Homewood (inbound). Go past the Lakeshore Drive/Brookwood Mall exit to the next traffic light. Turn left onto Office Park Drive. Proceed up the hill until you see a brown brick sign on the left for Building 300. Turn in the driveway in front of the sign and proceed straight for free surface parking.

Finding the door

The entrance is on the long (east) side of the building, next to a circular handicap ramp. The firm is in Suite 100, just inside the door. Please note: the building is built on a hill. If you turn right as you enter the parking lot and park on the west side of the building, you will be on the second floor and need to take the elevator or stairs down one flight to the office.
