About Fran

Fran Quarles has over two decades of experience in general litigation in state and federal court; drafting and reviewing contracts; and advising individuals and companies on a variety of business and regulatory issues. Her background is in banking and healthcare law.

Since joining Quarles Law Firm, LLC, in 2009, she has expanded her practice to include domestic relations law: divorce and child custody and support. She is trained in collaborative law.

As Associate General Counsel with HealthSouth Corporation, she was named among the top ten “Outstanding In-House Counsel to Hospitals/Healthcare Systems” in Nightingale’s Healthcare News. She is Martindale-Hubbell AV® Preeminent Rated.
