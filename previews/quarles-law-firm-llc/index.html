Common questions
Do I need to prove my spouse was at fault to get a divorce in Alabama?

No. Alabama allows a “no-fault” divorce on grounds of incompatibility of temperament or irretrievable breakdown of the marriage, which do not require proof that either spouse did anything wrong.

Are there alternatives to going to trial?

Yes. The firm describes three: an uncontested divorce, mediation, and collaborative divorce, in which the parties resolve disputes away from court with lawyers and other professionals.

I just received a divorce complaint. What do I do now?

Contact a divorce lawyer as soon as possible. You have only 30 days to respond with a motion, answer, or answer and counterclaim; if you do not answer, your spouse may obtain a default judgment.

Where is a divorce filed in Alabama?

When both parties live in Alabama, in the county where the defendant resides or where the parties lived at the time of separation. If the defendant has left Alabama, in the county where the plaintiff resides; by agreement, it may be filed in any county.
