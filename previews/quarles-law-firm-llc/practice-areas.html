Practice areas

Business and family law, under one roof.

Quarles Law Firm, LLC handles a wide range of civil matters in the Birmingham–Jefferson County area. Choose an area to read how it works and how the firm can help.

Business & Commercial

For businesses, lenders and individuals in financial disputes.

  • Banking & FinanceDeposit accounts, commercial loans, special assets, U.C.C. matters and lender liability.
  • Business FormationChoosing and setting up the right entity for a new business.
  • Commercial & General LitigationBusiness disputes and general civil litigation in state and federal court.
  • Contract Drafting & ReviewDrafting, reviewing and negotiating contracts, and resolving contract disputes.
  • Creditor RightsCollecting what is owed: demands, lawsuits, judgments and enforcement.
  • Insurance Coverage & DisputesCoverage questions and insurance disputes.
  • Real Estate DisputesLitigation over real property, contracts and loans secured by real estate.

Family & Individual

For men and women at every stage of divorce, and for families planning ahead.

  • DivorceContested and uncontested divorce, alimony, and alternatives to trial.
  • Child CustodyCustody and visitation arrangements that serve your children’s best interests.
  • Child SupportSetting, changing and enforcing child support under Alabama’s guidelines.
  • Modifications & EnforcementChanging or enforcing custody, support and alimony orders after divorce.
  • PaternityEstablishing parentage, and the custody and support that follow.
  • Stepparent AdoptionsMaking a stepparent a child’s legal parent.
  • Wills & ProbateWills, probate of estates, and will contests.
