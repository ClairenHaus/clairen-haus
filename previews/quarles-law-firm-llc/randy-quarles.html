About Randy

Randy Quarles has represented financial institutions and other businesses in a wide range of litigation, including shareholder derivative actions, class actions and complex commercial disputes.

His experience includes trial and appellate practice in state and federal courts; arbitrations; financial services litigation; insurance defense; and commercial litigation. He is admitted to practice in Alabama, Mississippi and Tennessee.

In addition to his law practice, he served as an adjunct instructor and co-director of the Judicial Externship Program at The University of Alabama School of Law from 2005 to 2021. Randy has received Martindale-Hubbell’s highest rating, AV® Preeminent.
