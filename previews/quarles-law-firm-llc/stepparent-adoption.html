Stepparent Adoptions

Help for blended families when a stepparent wants to become a child’s legal parent.

What this includes

  • Stepparent adoption petitions
  • Consent of the other parent
  • Court hearings and final orders

Making it official

A stepparent adoption makes a stepparent the child’s legal parent, with the same rights and responsibilities as a biological parent. It also ends the legal relationship between the child and the other birth parent, including that parent’s duty of support, so it is a significant step for everyone involved.

What the process generally involves

  • A petition filed in court (in Alabama, adoptions are handled in probate court)
  • The consent of the other legal parent, or a showing that consent is not required
  • A hearing and a final order of adoption

Whether the other parent will consent is usually the biggest question, and it shapes how the case proceeds. A first conversation with the firm can sort out what your family’s situation requires.
