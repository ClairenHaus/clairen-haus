About Raffaele Puppio

A multi-practice law firm based in Media, Delaware County, Pennsylvania.

Decades of experience in Delaware County

Raffaele Puppio is a multi-practice law firm based in Media, Delaware County, Pennsylvania, and one of the largest and most established full-service firms in the county. Its attorneys are known for their legal ability among the bench and bar, and have decades of experience representing school districts, municipalities, businesses and individual clients, helping to solve legal problems while avoiding future legal issues.

The firm’s lawyers have tried cases in Delaware County, Montgomery County, Philadelphia and other areas of Pennsylvania, in Municipal, Common Pleas and Federal courts, and have presented oral arguments before the Supreme Court of Pennsylvania.

A tradition of public service

Founded on a rich tradition of public service and professional excellence, Raffaele Puppio’s lawyers have served as district attorneys, as a Judge Pro Tem of the Court of Common Pleas of Delaware County, and in many leadership roles for the Delaware County Bar Association. Some have served as school district and township solicitors, while another has served as a teacher in the Delaware County public school system.

Recognition

The firm’s lawyers have been consistently recognized among the top lawyers in Pennsylvania, and the firm has been accepted to the Bar Register of Preeminent Lawyers. In 2024, four of its attorneys were named Top Lawyers of Delaware County.
