About Alex

Alexandra (Alex) Junkin joined Raffaele Puppio in 2016 and became an associate in January 2017. She represents clients in all family law matters, including divorce, child custody, child and spousal support, and Protection from Abuse.

Alex graduated from The George Washington University in 2013 with a major in archaeology and a minor in criminal justice, then earned her J.D. at Villanova University’s Charles Widger School of Law, where she received the Leonard Levin Memorial Award.

In law school she interned with the Delaware County District Attorney’s Office, assisting in the prosecution of an attempted murder trial and in Veterans Court and Mental Health Court proceedings. She then focused on family law through an internship with the Honorable Barry C. Dozor, researching, drafting orders and opinions, and observing trials, conferences, family law hearings and Protection from Abuse hearings.

In 2016 she joined Raffaele Puppio as an intern, helping prepare for trials and family law hearings and drafting pleadings and discovery.
