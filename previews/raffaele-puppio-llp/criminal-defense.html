Criminal Law

A criminal charge can affect your freedom, your job and your future. Raffaele Puppio defends clients charged with crimes in Delaware County and across Pennsylvania.

How Raffaele Puppio can help

The firm’s litigation department, headed by J. Patrick Hickey, a former Assistant District Attorney in Berks County, handles criminal defense alongside its civil trial work. Raffaele Puppio’s lawyers have tried cases in Municipal, Common Pleas and Federal courts.

What we handle

  • DUIDefense of driving under the influence charges.
  • Drug crimesDefense of possession, distribution and other drug charges.
  • Simple and aggravated assaultDefense of assault charges at every level.
  • Fraud, theft and receiving stolen propertyDefense of property and financial crimes.
  • Juvenile crimesRepresentation of young people in the juvenile system.
  • Probation violationsRepresentation at violation hearings.
  • Traffic violationsHelp with traffic citations and their consequences.
  • ExpungementsClearing eligible records so a past charge does not follow you.

Every matter is different. The descriptions above are general information, not legal advice about your situation.
