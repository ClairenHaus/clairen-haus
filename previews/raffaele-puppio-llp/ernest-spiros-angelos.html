About Ernie

Ernest Spiros Angelos is a partner with Raffaele Puppio. His goal is to give clients reasonable approaches to complex life and legal situations and to instill confidence through professionalism and diligent representation. He focuses his practice on municipal and educational law, business litigation, real property, contracts and tax assessment appeals, representing taxing authorities and taxpayers.

Growing up, Ernie regularly visited his grandfather’s diner in Northeast Philadelphia and came to appreciate the hard work it takes to build a business. Today he has a passion for helping entrepreneurs and has represented many contractors, construction companies and restaurant owners.

Ernie is a member of the Pennsylvania and Delaware County Bar Associations, where he previously served as event coordinator for the Young Lawyers Section and is active in the criminal defense, education and real property sections. He is a charter member and founding Treasurer of the West Chester Rotary Act Club, a member of the Western Delaware Valley Lions Club, and a member of St. George Greek Orthodox Church in Media.

He earned his B.A. in political science from the University of Delaware in 2003 and his J.D. from Widener University Delaware Law School in 2007. Ernie lives in Thornbury with his wife and son.

Away from the office, Ernie enjoys auto racing events and cheering on a friend who races professionally.
