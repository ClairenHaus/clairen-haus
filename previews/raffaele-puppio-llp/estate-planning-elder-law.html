Practice area

Elder Law, Estate Planning & Administration

Planning ahead protects the people you care about and makes sure your wishes are followed. Raffaele Puppio helps individuals and families plan their estates and guides them through administration and probate.

How Raffaele Puppio can help

Whether you are preparing your first will, planning for care later in life, or settling the estate of someone you have lost, the firm can help you understand your options under Pennsylvania law.

What we handle

  • Wills and trustsDocuments that say who receives your property and how.
  • Power of attorneyChoosing someone to handle financial matters if you cannot.
  • Advance health care directives and living willsRecording your wishes for medical care.
  • Estate planning and administrationPlanning an estate and carrying out its administration.
  • ProbateGuiding executors and families through the probate process.

Every matter is different. The descriptions above are general information, not legal advice about your situation.
