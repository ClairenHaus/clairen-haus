Practice area

Family Law

Family law matters are among the most personal a person will face. Raffaele Puppio’s family lawyers take a problem-solving approach, helping clients avoid the delay and expense of unnecessary litigation whenever possible, and are prepared to go to court when it is needed.

How Raffaele Puppio can help

Founding partner Michael A. Raffaele has dedicated his practice to family law. He has served the Delaware County courts as a Juvenile Court Master and Custody Master and as a Judge Pro Tem in a program to reduce the family law backlog. Associate Alex Junkin represents clients in all family law matters.

What we handle

  • DivorceGuidance through every stage of a Pennsylvania divorce, from filing to final decree.
  • High net worth divorceDivorces involving significant assets, businesses or complex property.
  • Child custody and supportCustody arrangements and child support that put children’s needs first.
  • Alimony and spousal supportSupport during and after a separation or divorce.
  • Prenuptial and postnuptial agreementsAgreements that set expectations before or during a marriage.
  • Separation agreementsAgreements covering the terms of a separation.
  • Settlement agreements in divorceNegotiating and drafting the agreements that resolve a divorce.
  • Protection from AbuseProtection from Abuse petitions and hearings.
  • Grandparents’ rightsCustody and visitation questions for grandparents.
  • Domestic partnerships and same-sex family lawFamily law matters for domestic partners and same-sex couples.

Every matter is different. The descriptions above are general information, not legal advice about your situation.
