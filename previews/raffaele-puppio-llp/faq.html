Frequently asked questions

Questions about Raffaele Puppio

Answers about the firm, its attorneys and how to reach us.

Where is Raffaele Puppio’s office?

The firm’s office is at 19 West Third Street, Media, Pennsylvania 19063, in Delaware County. Onsite parking is available behind the building on Miller Street.

How do I contact the firm?

Call 610.891.6710 or email info@raffaelepuppio.com. You can also contact any of the attorneys directly; each attorney’s profile lists a direct email address. The fax number is 610.891.9985.

What areas of law does Raffaele Puppio handle?

The firm’s attorneys provide counsel in school law and government services, family law, criminal law, general litigation, personal injury, commercial real estate and business transactions, and elder law, estate planning and administration.

Can the lawyers at Raffaele Puppio help me with all of my legal matters, so I only work with one firm?

Raffaele Puppio handles many types of law, and its lawyers have the resources, locally and at the state level, to provide diverse representation. The firm can also connect clients with resources and serve referring attorneys in areas of the law it does not typically handle.

Which courts do Raffaele Puppio’s lawyers appear in?

The firm’s lawyers have tried cases in Delaware County, Montgomery County, Philadelphia and other areas of Pennsylvania, in Municipal, Common Pleas and Federal courts, and have presented oral arguments before the Supreme Court of Pennsylvania.

Does the firm represent school districts and municipalities?

Yes. Raffaele Puppio has decades of experience representing school districts and municipalities. Its attorneys serve as solicitors to school districts, a charter school, boroughs and a zoning hearing board in southeastern Pennsylvania.

Who handles family law matters at the firm?

Founding partner Michael A. Raffaele has dedicated his practice to family law, and associate Alex Junkin represents clients in all family law matters, including divorce, custody, support and Protection from Abuse.

Who handles personal injury and criminal defense?

J. Patrick Hickey heads the firm’s litigation department. His practice includes personal injury, automobile liability, construction accidents, workers’ compensation and criminal defense.

Do Raffaele Puppio’s attorneys have good relationships with other lawyers and judges in Delaware County?

The firm’s lawyers have deep relationships with members of the state and local bar and the judiciary. They have served as district attorneys, as a Judge Pro Tem of the Court of Common Pleas of Delaware County, and in leadership roles with the Delaware County Bar Association.

Does sending a message create an attorney-client relationship?

No. Contacting the firm through the website or by email does not create an attorney-client relationship. Please don’t send confidential information until an attorney has agreed to represent you.
