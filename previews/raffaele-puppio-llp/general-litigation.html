General Litigation

When a dispute cannot be resolved, you need trial lawyers who know the courts. Raffaele Puppio’s attorneys have tried cases in Delaware County, Montgomery County, Philadelphia and elsewhere in Pennsylvania, in Municipal, Common Pleas and Federal courts, and have presented oral arguments before the Supreme Court of Pennsylvania.

How Raffaele Puppio can help

The litigation department is headed by J. Patrick Hickey, who has served as lead counsel in approximately 50 jury trials. Partner Ernest Spiros Angelos handles business litigation, real property and contract disputes, and associate Jennifer M. Ganley brings more than twenty years of experience representing insured and self-insured corporations in Pennsylvania and New Jersey.

What we handle

  • Business and contract disputesLitigation over contracts, business relationships and commercial matters.
  • Real property disputesDisputes involving real estate and property rights.
  • Insurance and corporate defenseDefense of insured and self-insured businesses.
  • AppealsBriefing and argument before the Pennsylvania Superior and Supreme Courts.
