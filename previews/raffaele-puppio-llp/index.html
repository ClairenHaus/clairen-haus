About the firm

Founded on public service and professional excellence

Raffaele Puppio’s lawyers are known among the bench and bar. They have served as district attorneys, as a Judge Pro Tem of the Court of Common Pleas of Delaware County, and in leadership roles with the Delaware County Bar Association. Some have served as school district and township solicitors; another taught in the Delaware County public schools.

The firm has been accepted to the Bar Register of Preeminent Lawyers, and its lawyers have been consistently recognized among the top lawyers in Pennsylvania.

Where is Raffaele Puppio’s office?

The firm’s office is at 19 West Third Street, Media, Pennsylvania 19063, in Delaware County. Onsite parking is available behind the building on Miller Street.

How do I contact the firm?

Call 610.891.6710 or email info@raffaelepuppio.com. You can also contact any of the attorneys directly; each attorney’s profile lists a direct email address. The fax number is 610.891.9985.

What areas of law does Raffaele Puppio handle?

The firm’s attorneys provide counsel in school law and government services, family law, criminal law, general litigation, personal injury, commercial real estate and business transactions, and elder law, estate planning and administration.

Can the lawyers at Raffaele Puppio help me with all of my legal matters, so I only work with one firm?

Raffaele Puppio handles many types of law, and its lawyers have the resources, locally and at the state level, to provide diverse representation. The firm can also connect clients with resources and serve referring attorneys in areas of the law it does not typically handle.
