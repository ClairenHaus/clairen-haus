What changes

A secure, readable site that says what the firm already knows.

Two specific things the current site gets in the way of, and what this concept does about them. The firm's own content stays; the way it is served changes.

  • Today: served over plain HTTP
    Secure by default

    The current site loads over HTTP only and the HTTPS handshake fails, so browsers label it "Not secure." The rebuild ships on HTTPS with a current stack instead of jQuery 1.8.3.

  • Today: FAQ page without FAQ markup
    Answers machines can read

    The firm already publishes FAQs, but with no schema markup, no H1 and no robots.txt. This page carries LegalService, Person and FAQPage structured data so search engines and AI assistants can confirm who the firm is, where it is and what it does.

Common questions

Answers before the first call.

Each answer below is drawn from what the firm already publishes, written so a person or an AI assistant can quote it directly.

Where is Raffaele Puppio located?

The firm's office is at 19 West Third Street, Media, Pennsylvania 19063, in Delaware County. Onsite parking is available in the rear of the building on Miller Street. The main number is 610.891.6710.

What areas of law does Raffaele Puppio handle?

Raffaele Puppio is a full-service firm. Its attorneys provide counsel in education law, government and municipal services, family law, general litigation, personal injury, commercial real estate and business transactions, estate planning and administration, and criminal defense.

Does the firm represent school districts and municipalities?

Yes. The firm describes decades of experience representing school districts, municipalities, businesses and individual clients. Partner Michael V. Puppio, Jr. serves as solicitor for school districts and municipalities throughout southeast Pennsylvania.

Who leads the firm's family law practice?

Founding and managing partner Michael A. Raffaele has dedicated his practice to family law and has served the Delaware County court system as a Juvenile Court Master, Custody Master and Judge Pro Tem in family law cases.

Which courts have the firm's attorneys appeared in?

The firm's attorneys have tried cases in Delaware County, Montgomery County, Philadelphia and other areas of Pennsylvania in Municipal, Common Pleas and Federal courts, and have presented oral arguments before the Supreme Court of Pennsylvania.

About the practice

The information clients need before they call.

Accepted to the Bar Register of Preeminent Lawyers

Office: 19 West Third Street, Media, PA 19063

Raffaele Puppio describes itself as one of the largest and most established full-service law firms in Delaware County, Pennsylvania, with attorneys known among the bench and bar. The firm has decades of experience representing school districts, municipalities, businesses and individual clients, helping to solve legal problems while avoiding future ones.

Its lawyers have served as district attorneys, as Judge Pro Tem of the Court of Common Pleas of Delaware County, and in leadership roles for the Delaware County Bar Association. Some have served as school district and township solicitors; another has taught in the Delaware County public school system. The firm has been accepted to the Bar Register of Preeminent Lawyers, which includes only firms with the highest ratings in legal ability and ethical standards.
