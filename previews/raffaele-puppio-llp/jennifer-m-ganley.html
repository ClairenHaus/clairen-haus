About Jennifer

Jennifer M. Ganley joined Raffaele Puppio as an associate in 2022 and practices in the firm’s litigation department. Before joining the firm, she spent more than twenty years with an insurance defense firm, representing insured and self-insured corporations in Pennsylvania and New Jersey.

Jennifer earned her B.A., magna cum laude, from Saint Joseph’s University in 1997 and her J.D. from Villanova University School of Law in 2000. She is admitted to practice in Pennsylvania and New Jersey.

She serves on the Board of Trustees of the Elizabeth Ciunci Foundation and as Treasurer of the CYO Board of St. John Chrysostom in Wallingford, Pennsylvania, where she lives with her husband and three children.
