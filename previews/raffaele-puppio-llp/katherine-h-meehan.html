About Kate

Katherine H. Meehan was an associate with Raffaele Puppio from 2006 until 2017, when she became a partner. Her practice focuses primarily on education law. She works closely with education, government, municipal, commercial and private clients on employment, contract, Right to Know, Sunshine Act and policy matters, and she also has experience in civil litigation, including personal injury and discrimination matters.

Kate provides practical guidance that keeps in view the best interests of school boards, staff, students and the wider community. Before joining the firm she served as a law clerk to the late Honorable Joseph F. Battle, Jr. from 1997 to 2001.

She has presented and lectured on employment subjects including conducting investigations, document retention and criminal background checks. Kate has authored and co-authored many appellate briefs, has argued before the Pennsylvania Superior Court, and for many years co-authored materials on Pennsylvania’s Motor Vehicle Financial Responsibility Law for the Pennsylvania Trial Lawyers Association’s annual auto seminar.

She earned a B.A. in politics, with a minor in sociology, from Ithaca College and her J.D. from Villanova University School of Law, and is admitted in Pennsylvania state and federal courts.

Kate enjoys reading, cooking and gardening, and volunteers at a local soup kitchen and with her church on home repairs for neighbors in need.
