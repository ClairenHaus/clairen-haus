About Michael

A founding partner of Raffaele Puppio, Michael V. Puppio, Jr. concentrates in education law, municipal law, municipal finance and litigation. He serves as solicitor for school districts and municipalities throughout southeastern Pennsylvania; the firm’s site lists the Garnet Valley, Penn-Delco, Interboro and Chichester School Districts, Chester Community Charter School, Glenolden Borough, Ridley Park Borough and the Zoning Hearing Board of Collingdale.

Michael began his legal career with a clerkship for the late Judge Anthony R. Semeraro in the Court of Common Pleas of Delaware County, and then joined the law firm of Caine, DiPasqua, Sloane & Raffaele. He has also served as Solicitor for the Delaware County Board of Assessment and the Office of Employment and Training.

He formerly served as a Springfield Township Commissioner and as a Delaware County Councilman, with responsibility for all aspects of county government, including oversight of a $300 million budget. Born and raised in Springfield, Delaware County, he still lives there.
