Personal Injury

An injury can upend your health, your work and your finances. Raffaele Puppio represents people injured through someone else’s negligence.

How Raffaele Puppio can help

Personal injury work is led by J. Patrick Hickey, a dedicated trial lawyer whose practice includes personal injury, automobile liability, construction accidents and workers’ compensation, and who understands the complexities of catastrophic injury cases. Before joining the firm, he was an insurance defense trial lawyer in Philadelphia.

What we handle

  • Automobile accidentsClaims arising from car, truck and other motor vehicle crashes.
  • Medical malpracticeClaims where medical care fell below the accepted standard.
  • Product liabilityInjuries caused by defective or dangerous products.
  • Slip and fallInjuries on property that was not kept reasonably safe.
  • Construction accidentsInjuries on construction sites.
  • Workers’ compensationBenefits for workers injured on the job.

Every matter is different. The descriptions above are general information, not legal advice about your situation.
