How we help

A full-service firm, with a personal approach

Being a relatively small firm, we are able to offer our clients personalized, quality service. Don’t see what you need? Ask. This list is by no means all-inclusive.

Tax Preparation & Tax Planning

Returns and planning advice for individuals, partnerships, corporations and non-profits, filed electronically.

Business Management Services

Business start-up help, financial statement analysis and projections, and business sales and acquisitions.

Compiled Financial Statements

Financial statements prepared to professional standards, a useful tool for management decisions.

Payroll Support & Services

Complete payroll preparation and payroll tax reporting, with direct deposit and year-end W-2s.

NetClient CS® Client Portal

Your own secure, password-protected portal to your accounting and tax information, 24/7.

Retirement Plans & Section 105/Flex Plans

Implementation and periodic review of your retirement plan; Section 105 and flex plans through a third-party administrator.

Business Valuations

Useful, objective analysis of what a business is worth, for a purchase, a sale or a divorce.

Computer & Software Support

Help choosing hardware and designing your business’s computer systems, plus QuickBooks® support.
