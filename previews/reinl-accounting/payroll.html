Payroll Support & Services

Complete payroll preparation and payroll tax reporting, with direct deposit and year-end W-2s.

Our firm offers complete payroll preparation and payroll tax reporting services. Hectic schedule? Our services allow you to submit your payroll online, by phone, fax or e-mail at a day and time that is most convenient to you. We also offer direct deposit, EFTPS & MyTax Account filings and year-end W-2 processing. We will give you peace of mind with payroll compliance.

Payroll services

  • Payroll preparation
  • Payroll tax reporting
  • Submit payroll online, by phone, fax or e-mail
  • Direct deposit
  • EFTPS & MyTax Account filings
  • Year-end W-2 processing

Federal and Wisconsin

Wisconsin employers file federal payroll taxes through EFTPS and state withholding through the Department of Revenue’s My Tax Account. Each has its own deposit schedule and year-end forms.

General information only, not advice for your situation. Call us to talk it through.
