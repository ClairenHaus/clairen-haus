Our history

Serving Iowa and Illinois since 1965, Reiser, Jennings & Co., P.C. (RJPC), a Certified Public Accounting firm, assists clients in accounting, computer, tax, and management endeavors.

RJPC serves the client who is seeking a CPA firm that can play an important role in contributing to, not just reporting, profitability. Our clients are primarily individually owned, family owned and closely held small to medium-sized businesses.

Reiser, Jennings & Co., P.C. is a member of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants.

Our objective

RJPC’s objective is to provide our clients valuable accounting, computer, tax, and management assistance so as to successfully accomplish the clients’ objectives.

Our philosophy

We believe that an accountant should create something of value, and the value should be measured by the client in terms of tangible benefits and increased profitability.

We also believe an accountant should be concerned with the success of the client’s business. The accountant should be considerate and should communicate in an effective manner with the client.
