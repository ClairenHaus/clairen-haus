FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the firm, its offices and its services.

How long has Reiser, Jennings & Co. been in business?

The firm has served Iowa and Illinois since 1965.

Where are your offices?

There are three: 1900 E 54th St in Davenport, 519 9th St. in DeWitt, and 154 S. 4th Ave. in Eldridge, Iowa.

What services do you offer?

Accounting, computer, tax and management assistance, including personal, small business and corporate tax preparation, accounting and audit services, along with strategic business consulting.

Who are your typical clients?

Primarily individually owned, family owned and closely held small to medium-sized businesses.

Do you prepare personal tax returns?

Yes. Personal, small business and corporate tax preparation are all part of the firm’s services.

Do you work with clients in Illinois?

Yes. The firm has served clients in both Iowa and Illinois since 1965.

Are you a CPA firm?

Yes. Reiser, Jennings & Co., P.C. is a Certified Public Accounting firm and a member of the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants.

Can I pay my bill online?

Yes. Existing clients can use the firm’s secure online bill pay.

How do I reach you?

Call Davenport at (563) 322-6271, DeWitt at (563) 659-1577 or Eldridge at (563) 285-4121, or email rjpc1@rjpccpas.com.
