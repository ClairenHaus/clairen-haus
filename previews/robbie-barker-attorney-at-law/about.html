About

About the office

Experience born from both sides of the courtroom, and a practice built around people facing one of the hardest moments of their lives.

Why this office exists

When people hire an attorney, they are often in a very stressful situation. They need someone who cares about them. Robbie Barker makes sure clients receive not only high-quality legal services, but also the support and resources they deserve.

For many years, Robbie has been helping clients in Montgomery and Harris Counties. The practice is focused exclusively on criminal defense, and the office offers reliable advice and representation in all criminal matters, at competitive rates.

What the practice offers

  • An aggressive defense of your case
  • Advice and representation from an attorney focused on criminal law
  • Honest assessments of cases, whether good or bad
  • All work performed with the highest level of care and attention to detail

Integrity. Experience. Dedication.
The three words the office has always put its name to.
