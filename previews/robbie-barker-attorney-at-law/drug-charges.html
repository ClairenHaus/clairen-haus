Drug & Marijuana Charges

Possession, delivery and other controlled substance and marijuana cases. Robbie Barker handles these cases in Conroe, Montgomery County and Harris County.

How Texas grades drug charges

Texas groups controlled substances into “penalty groups.” How serious a charge is depends mainly on which group the substance falls into, how much of it there was, and whether the accusation is possession or delivery (which includes selling or intending to sell). The same substance can be a misdemeanor in a small amount and a serious felony in a larger one. Marijuana has its own set of rules, and possessing a dangerous drug without a prescription is a separate offense.

What a drug case often turns on

Many drug cases depend on how the evidence was found. Questions about the search of a car or home, whether there was valid consent or a warrant, whether the drugs can actually be tied to the person charged, and how the lab tested the substance can all shape the outcome.
