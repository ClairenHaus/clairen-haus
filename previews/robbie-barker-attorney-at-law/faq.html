FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the office, the free consultation and the cases Robbie Barker handles.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. The office offers a free initial consultation. Call (936) 900-9580 or email attorneyrobbie@yahoo.com to set one up.

What kinds of cases does Robbie Barker handle?

Criminal defense only. The office defends felonies and misdemeanors of all types, including DWI, drug and marijuana charges, assault and domestic violence, theft, burglary, weapons charges and probation violations, and also handles expunctions, record sealing and occupational driver’s licenses.

Does the office handle family law or civil cases?

No. The practice is focused exclusively on criminal defense.

Where is the office?

At 204 W. Davis in Conroe, Texas 77301, on the north side of the main Montgomery County courthouse, between N. Thompson and Main Street.

What are the office hours?

Monday through Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Appointments after hours are available by arrangement.

Which counties does the office serve?

Robbie Barker represents clients in Montgomery and Harris Counties.

Has Robbie Barker worked as a prosecutor?

Yes. Robbie is a former felony and misdemeanor prosecutor in Montgomery County and San Jacinto County, and a former special prosecutor, who handled hundreds of cases from Class C tickets to murder cases.

Where did Robbie Barker go to school?

Robbie earned a Bachelor of Science from Texas A&M University and a Doctor of Jurisprudence from Gonzaga University School of Law.

How can I reach the office?

Call (936) 900-9580, send a fax to (855) 865-4167, or email attorneyrobbie@yahoo.com.

Questions about your own case? Those are best answered in a consultation. Call (936) 900-9580.
