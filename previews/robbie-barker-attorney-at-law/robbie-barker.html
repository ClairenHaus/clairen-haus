Background

Robbie Barker practices criminal law in Conroe, Texas, defending clients in Montgomery and Harris Counties. Before opening a defense practice, Robbie served as a felony and misdemeanor prosecutor in Montgomery County and San Jacinto County and as a special prosecutor, handling hundreds of cases from Class C tickets to murder cases.

Today Robbie offers every client an honest assessment of the case, whether the news is good or bad, and an aggressive defense handled with the highest level of care and attention to detail.
