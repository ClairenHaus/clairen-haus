About us

A local office, honest work

S & A Taxes and Accounting Inc, formerly Callihan’s Tax Service, on W. Vienna St. in Clio.

Meet Stephanie Callihan

Stephanie is the tax preparer and bookkeeper. She has prior experience working under an enrolled agent. She received her tax license through H&R Block and has kept up on her classes through Michigan State, and she received her BBA in Accounting through Baker College.

What we do

We offer individual and small business tax preparation, bookkeeping, payroll, and quarterly and end-of-year tax preparation. Our aim hasn’t changed: preparing taxes and bookkeeping honestly, and getting the most for your money.
