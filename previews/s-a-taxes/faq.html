FAQ

Questions we hear

Prices, hours, the name change and more.

How much does it cost to have my taxes done?

Taxes are $105 to start for the main federal, state and city 1040. Senior taxes start at $75. Returns with more forms cost more, so call (810) 368-4687 for a quote. Prices are subject to change.

Is this the same office as Callihan’s Tax Service?

Yes. Callihan’s Tax Service is now S & A Taxes and Accounting Inc. It’s the same office at 185 W. Vienna St. in Clio, with Stephanie Callihan.

Who prepares my return?

Stephanie Callihan is the tax preparer and bookkeeper. She received her tax license through H&R Block, keeps up on her classes through Michigan State, has a BBA in Accounting from Baker College, and has prior experience working under an enrolled agent.

Do you do small business taxes?

Yes: small business tax preparation, quarterly taxes, end-of-year filings, bookkeeping and payroll.

What do bookkeeping and payroll cost?

Bookkeeping and payroll start at $85, plus $100 to start. Call for a quote.

I need more time. Can you file an extension?

Yes. Extensions are due by October 15, so make your appointment early enough that we can get it in by the deadline.

What are your hours?

Monday to Thursday 9 to 12 and 1 to 6, Friday and Saturday 9 to 12 and 1 to 9, closed Sunday. Hours can change around tax season and holidays, so call ahead.
