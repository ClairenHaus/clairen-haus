About the firm

A firm built on comfort, understanding and prompt service.

S. Page Allen & Associates, A Professional Corporation, practices real estate law, business entities, commercial law and estate planning from its office on Midlothian Turnpike in North Chesterfield.

01

To nurture our clients

The firm’s most important objective is to provide clients with a sense of comfort and understanding of how it can satisfy their legal needs within the framework of the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia, conducting business consistently, with honesty and integrity and without discrimination.

02

Prompt, capable service

To give the best possible service in prompt response to clients’ needs, the firm relies on its professional support staff, maintains a complete in-house law library with access to Westlaw, FindLaw and LexisNexis, and its professional staff consistently exceed their continuing legal education requirements.

03

Embrace our community

The firm seeks to be an integral part of the community it serves: informing the community of the attorney’s role in real estate transactions, providing legal advice for estate planning, assisting clients in business organization, and representing clients in disputed matters of the law.

Our purpose

Your counselor and representative.

It’s important to remember that your attorney is your counselor and representative, responsible for looking after your interests, and yours alone. While other people involved in your legal matters may be knowledgeable, no one can be expected to represent you as well as your own attorney.

Helping clients understand their rights and obligations, within the framework of the laws of Virginia, is one of the firm’s primary concerns.
