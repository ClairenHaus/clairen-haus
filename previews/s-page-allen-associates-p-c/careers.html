Work with S. Page Allen & Associates.

Team work is the cornerstone of our organization.

The firm is always looking for experienced applicants who are seeking to work in a congenial atmosphere where their ideas are considered and incorporated into policy-making decisions. Many staff members have been with the firm since it was organized, and some who ventured into other legal environments have returned after a short time.

Currently seeking

One full-time, experienced real estate paralegal, with a minimum of three years of experience in all phases of real estate transactions. The firm handles a wide variety of real estate properties, including commercial, residential and construction transactions, and real estate closings are one of its primary areas of focus.

What the firm offers

  • Earnings based on education and experience
  • SIMPLE IRA plan with company matching
  • Liberal annual leave and paid holidays
  • Overtime pay for overtime hours worked
  • Health care benefits
  • Free parking in a convenient Southside location
