Common questions
When should I involve a real estate attorney in a home purchase?

The firm advises retaining an attorney from the very beginning, before entering into a binding contract of purchase, to review the contract or draft one with the purchaser’s interests in mind. Even if an attorney is not involved before signing, it is advisable to have your attorney involved in your settlement.

Is a standard real estate form contract safe to sign as-is?

The firm explains that there is no such thing as a “typical” form contract. Each firm’s contract has its own provisions that affect the rights and obligations of buyers and sellers, so every provision should be read and understood.

What does the attorney check before closing?

Old records are reviewed to make sure there is no cloud on the seller’s title, such as forgeries or hidden liens, and that the sellers actually hold good title and have the right to transfer it.
