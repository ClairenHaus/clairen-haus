Business entities & commercial law

Help organizing a business, and with the commercial legal matters that follow.

Starting a business

S. Page Allen & Associates assists clients in business organization, and business entity formation is part of Ms. Allen’s practice. The form a business takes, whether a limited liability company, a corporation or a partnership, affects how it is owned, how it is managed, and how its owners are protected.

Questions a new business usually works through:

  • Choosing a form of business entity
  • Forming the entity under Virginia law
  • The organizing documents that set out who owns and runs the business

Commercial law

The firm’s work includes commercial law, and it represents clients in disputed matters of the law. If you have a contract, a transaction or a disagreement involving your business, call the office to talk it through.

It’s important to remember that your attorney is your counselor and representative, responsible for looking after your interests, and yours alone.
