Estate planning

Legal advice for planning your estate under Virginia law.

Planning ahead

Providing legal advice for estate planning is one of the ways S. Page Allen & Associates serves the community, and estate planning is part of Ms. Allen’s practice. In general terms, an estate plan sets out who receives your property, who handles your affairs, and who can act for you if you cannot.

An estate plan commonly includes:

  • A will
  • Powers of attorney
  • A plan for how your property, including your home, passes to your family

How you hold title matters

The way you take title to your home is part of the picture. For example, a husband and wife may take title as “tenants by the entirety with the common law right of survivorship,” which means that if one should die, the survivor takes full title by operation of law. Two unrelated people may take title as “tenants in common,” each owning a share that can be conveyed independently. Questions about the form of taking title can be complicated, and the language used must be precise.

Call the office to talk about your family, your property and what you would like your plan to do.
