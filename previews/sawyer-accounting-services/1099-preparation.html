1099 Preparation & Filings

1099 forms prepared and filed for the contractors and vendors you pay.

In general

Businesses usually need to send a 1099 to contractors and certain vendors they paid during the year, and file copies with the IRS. Deadlines come early in the year, so it helps to collect each vendor’s Form W-9 when you first pay them.

How we help

  • Preparing your 1099 forms
  • Filing them
  • Keeping it in step with your bookkeeping and payroll
