FAQ

Questions, answered.

Quick answers about appointments, new clients and deadlines. For anything else, call 417-745-0066.

When did Sawyer Accounting Services start?

In 2004, as a home-based business. It moved into an office in 2015 and is now on Jackson Street in Hermitage, Missouri.

Who prepares my return?

Jessica Sawyer is an Enrolled Agent. Our team also includes Julie Carnahan (tax specialist), Heather Horst (payroll specialist) and Carol Pearson (bookkeeping specialist).

Are you taking new clients?

Potential new clients fill out the New Client Interest Form (individual or business) to be placed on our current waiting list.

What if I bring my information after March 31?

Drop-off appointments and tax information received through the portal after March 31 may require an extension, which requires a $50 deposit for the extension fee when you submit documents.

How do I schedule an appointment from May to December?

Please call the office at 417-745-0066.

Do you work with businesses?

Yes: tax preparation, bookkeeping, payroll processing, business consulting, accounts receivable/payable, monthly bookkeeping, 1099 preparation and filing, QuickBooks Online subscription discounts and part-time CFO services.

Where do you serve?

Our office is in Hermitage, and we serve clients from all over Missouri.

Where’s my refund?

Use the IRS refund tracker, the IRS amended return tracker, or the Missouri return tracker, linked in our Client Center.
