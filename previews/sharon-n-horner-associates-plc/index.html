What changes

Specific fixes, drawn from the current site.

  • A site that fits a phone

    The current one-page site uses an XHTML/HTML4 doctype and <font> tags with no viewport tag, so it renders about 800px wide on a 390px phone. The concept is responsive.

  • Something for search and AI to cite

    Today the site is a banner, two practice lines and a MapQuest link, with a ©2010 footer and no schema. The concept adds LegalService JSON-LD with address, hours and phone, plus a short FAQ.

  • Tap to call, tap for directions

    The phone number is plain text and directions go to MapQuest. Here the phone is a tap-to-call link and the address opens in maps.

  • Room to grow

    Attorney bios and closing details can be added during the build. Nothing here is filled in beyond what the firm publishes.
