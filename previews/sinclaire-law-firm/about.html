About the firm

A Charleston practice built on courtroom experience

Sinclaire Law Firm, LLC is the law practice of John “Jack” Sinclaire, III, on River Landing Drive on Daniel Island.

From the Solicitor’s Office to private practice

Jack Sinclaire was raised in the Charleston area. He began in the State prosecutor’s office, where he litigated cases for 17 years, completed the National College of District Attorneys Career Prosecutor Course in 1986, and rose to Deputy Solicitor. In that role he prosecuted violent felony offenders and managed the Charleston County Solicitor’s Office, with 100 employees and 20 attorneys.

In January 2001 he went into private practice. Today the firm does two kinds of work.

Criminal defense

For clients charged in Federal, State, Magistrate or Municipal Courts: DUI and DUAC, criminal domestic violence, drug cases, probation violations, parole hearings and expungements. Criminal defense

Personal injury

For people hurt by the negligence of others: vehicle accidents, nursing home abuse, wrongful death, medical malpractice, work injuries, slip and fall, dog bites and victims of sexual abuse. Personal injury

Questions

What kinds of cases does Sinclaire Law Firm handle?

Two kinds: personal injury and criminal defense. On the injury side that includes auto, trucking and motorcycle accidents, neck and back injuries, nursing home abuse and neglect, wrongful death, medical malpractice, workers’ compensation, dog bites, slip and fall injuries and victims of sexual abuse. On the criminal side it includes defense in Federal, State, Magistrate and Municipal Courts, DUI and DUAC, criminal domestic violence, drug cases, probation violations, parole hearings and expungements.
