Expungements

An expungement clears an eligible charge from your public criminal record. Expungements are among the criminal matters the firm handles.

What an expungement does

A criminal charge can show up on background checks for jobs, housing and licenses long after a case is over. An expungement removes an eligible charge from the public record, so it no longer appears in those searches.

Who may be eligible

Eligibility in South Carolina depends on how the case ended and what it was. Charges that were dismissed or ended in a not-guilty verdict are often eligible. Some first-offense convictions, and cases completed through programs such as pretrial intervention, may also qualify after a waiting period.

How the process works

In South Carolina, expungement applications generally go through the solicitor’s office in the county where the charge was brought, and a judge signs the order. There are fees, and some records take longer than others to clear.
