FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers drawn only from what the firm itself publishes. General information, not legal advice.

What kinds of cases does Sinclaire Law Firm handle?

Two kinds: personal injury and criminal defense. On the injury side that includes auto, trucking and motorcycle accidents, neck and back injuries, nursing home abuse and neglect, wrongful death, medical malpractice, workers’ compensation, dog bites, slip and fall injuries and victims of sexual abuse. On the criminal side it includes defense in Federal, State, Magistrate and Municipal Courts, DUI and DUAC, criminal domestic violence, drug cases, probation violations, parole hearings and expungements.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. The firm offers a free consultation. Call (843) 853-9300 to set one up.

Who is Jack Sinclaire?

John “Jack” Sinclaire, III is a Charleston attorney who was raised in the Charleston area. He spent the first 17 years of his legal career litigating cases in the State prosecutor’s office, rising to Deputy Solicitor, and went into private practice in January 2001.

Was Jack Sinclaire a prosecutor?

Yes. After graduating from the National College of District Attorneys Career Prosecutor Course in 1986, he rose to the rank of Deputy Solicitor. He was responsible for prosecuting violent felony offenders and for managing the Charleston County Solicitor’s Office, which had 100 employees and 20 attorneys.

Which courts does the firm handle criminal cases in?

Jack represents clients charged in Federal, State, Magistrate and Municipal Courts.

Does the firm handle DUI and DUAC charges?

Yes. DUI and DUAC are among the criminal defense matters the firm lists, along with criminal domestic violence, drug cases, probation violations, parole hearings and expungements.

What kinds of injury cases does Jack take?

People who have been seriously injured in trucking, motorcycle and automobile accidents; those hurt or killed by nursing home abuse; innocent victims of sexual abuse; people hurt in work-related injuries or in a slip and fall; victims of dog bites; and any case where someone has been hurt by the negligence of others.

Where did Jack Sinclaire go to school?

He graduated from Porter-Gaud School in 1977, earned his Bachelor of Business Administration from the University of Georgia, and received his Juris Doctorate from the University of South Carolina School of Law in 1984.

When did Jack go into private practice?
