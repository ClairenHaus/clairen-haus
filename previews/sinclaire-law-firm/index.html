What changes

Specific fixes, drawn from the current site.

  • A site that works on a phone

    The current page has no mobile viewport and is built with HTML 4-era font tags and table colors, so it renders as a shrunken desktop page on a phone. The concept is mobile-first, with tap-to-call at the top.

  • Copy that keeps up with the career

    The homepage file was last changed in November 2013 and still says “his 30 years in the justice system.” The concept leads with dates that stay true: 17 years as a prosecutor, private practice since 2001.

  • Practice areas as real text

    Today the practice areas are a single image, so search engines and screen readers cannot read them. The concept lists them as text.

  • Schema that says “law firm”

    There is no structured data on the site. The concept adds LegalService, Person and FAQPage markup with the full address and phone.

About the practice

From the Solicitor’s Office to private practice.

Porter-Gaud School, 1977 · B.B.A., University of Georgia · J.D., University of South Carolina School of Law, 1984

Jack Sinclaire was raised in the Charleston area. After graduating from Porter-Gaud School in 1977, he earned his Bachelor of Business Administration at the University of Georgia and his Juris Doctorate at the University of South Carolina School of Law in 1984.

After graduating from the National College of District Attorneys Career Prosecutor Course in 1986, he rose to the rank of Deputy Solicitor, responsible for the prosecution of violent felony offenders and the management of the Charleston County Solicitor’s Office, with 100 employees and 20 attorneys.

He went into private practice in January 2001. He represents clients charged in Federal, State, Magistrate or Municipal Courts, and people injured at the hands of others, in any case where someone has been hurt by the negligence of others.
