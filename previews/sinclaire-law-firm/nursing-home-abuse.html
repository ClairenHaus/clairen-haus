Practice area

Nursing home abuse and neglect

The firm represents those hurt or killed by nursing home abuse, and the families who trusted a facility with their care.

When care becomes harm

Families place a parent or grandparent in a nursing home expecting safe care. When residents are injured or killed through abuse or neglect, the facility and the people responsible can be held accountable.

Warning signs

  • Bedsores (pressure ulcers) or wounds that do not heal
  • Unexplained falls, fractures or bruises
  • Sudden weight loss or signs of dehydration
  • Poor hygiene or soiled bedding
  • Medication errors, or a resident who seems over-sedated
  • Withdrawal, fear or changes in mood around certain staff

What families can do

Write down what you see and when, take photos where you can, keep copies of records and correspondence, and report immediate dangers to the facility and to the authorities. Then talk with a lawyer about your options.

If a resident has died

When abuse or neglect leads to a death, the family may have a wrongful death claim.
