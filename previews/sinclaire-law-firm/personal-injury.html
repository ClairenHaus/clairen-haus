Hurt because of someone else’s negligence

Jack Sinclaire represents people who have suffered injuries at the hands of others. In his words on the firm’s site, that includes “any case where someone has been hurt by the negligence of others.” The consultation is free.

Personal injury law lets an injured person seek compensation from the person or business whose carelessness caused the harm, usually through that party’s insurance. A claim may cover medical bills, lost income and pain and suffering, and it is often resolved by negotiation, though some cases are filed in court.

Auto, trucking and motorcycle accidents

Crashes are the most common source of serious injury claims. Trucking cases can involve a commercial carrier and its insurer as well as the driver, and motorcycle riders often suffer more severe injuries because they have so little protection. More on vehicle accidents.

Neck and back injuries

Neck and back injuries often follow a crash or a fall, and some take time to show their full effect. Medical records that document treatment from the start matter in any claim based on them.

Nursing home abuse and neglect

The firm represents families of those hurt or killed by nursing home abuse. More on nursing home cases.

Wrongful death

When negligence costs a life, South Carolina law allows a claim on behalf of the family. More on wrongful death.

Medical malpractice

Medical malpractice is injury caused when a doctor, nurse, hospital or other provider falls below the accepted standard of care. These cases usually require review by medical experts, and South Carolina sets particular steps that must happen before a malpractice lawsuit is filed.

Workers’ compensation and work-related injuries

Injuries at work are usually handled through workers’ compensation. More on workers’ compensation.

Slip and fall injuries

Property owners and businesses can be responsible when an unsafe condition they should have fixed or warned about causes a fall. Photos of the scene and the names of witnesses help.

Dog bites

Dog bites can cause serious injuries, scarring and infection. A claim is usually made against the dog owner, often through a homeowner’s or renter’s insurance policy.

Victims of sexual abuse

The firm’s site names innocent victims of sexual abuse among the people Jack represents. A civil case is separate from any criminal case, and it is handled with privacy and care.
