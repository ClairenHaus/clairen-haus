About the office

A one-attorney practice in downtown Virginia, a block from the Courthouse.

Personal, practical, local

Sipila Law Office, LLC is the law practice of attorney Angela E. Sipila, and the office offers a free consultation.

Angela’s site has long shared free court forms and public resources, and she works with them as needed to help clients get the job done for less.

“Drive-through law”

Angela can come out to your car

If getting out of the car is hard, Angela can step out and talk with you there. You can also meet at another location that is convenient for you, as long as the conversation can’t be overheard.

Service area

The Iron Range and Northeastern Minnesota

Angela serves clients from Orr to Duluth, down to Pine City, and over to Hill Lake Township, across these counties:

  • St. Louis County
  • Aitkin County
  • Cook County
  • Itasca County
  • Koochiching County
  • Lake County

Fees, honestly

Angela isn’t allowed to post fees publicly, but ask by email and she’ll tell you her hourly rate, her flat fees for a Will, Purchase Agreement, Easement or Deed, or send her standard fee estimate for a Probate or a Divorce.

Quick questions by email

If you live in Northeastern Minnesota, you can email a short question to ange@sipilaw.com. Don’t include anything confidential. Sending an email doesn’t make Angela your lawyer, and if your rights may be at stake, don’t wait: some claims expire if you wait too long.

Talk with Angela about your situation

Call the Virginia office or send an email to set up an appointment. The office offers a free consultation.
