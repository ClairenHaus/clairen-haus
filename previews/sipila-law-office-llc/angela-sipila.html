How Angela works

Call or email to set up an appointment. You can meet at her office or another place that is convenient for you, as long as the conversation can’t be overheard. Everything said between you is confidential.

Angela doesn’t take criminal or personal injury cases. If you live in Northeastern Minnesota, she can still take the time to answer a general question by email.

Practice areas

Probate & Estate Settlement, Wills & Estate Planning, Powers of Attorney & Health Care Directives, Ethical Wills, Deeds & Transfer on Death Deeds, Easements, Purchase Agreements & Leases, Divorce, Guardianship & Conservatorship.

A little more about Angela

Why she asks people who they are

Angela’s grandmother was Nelmi Koivu of Cook, who wrote a local newspaper column for almost 60 years. “Because of her,” Angela writes, “I stop to smell the lilacs, watch the robins and ask people who they are.”

She reads a book a month with her book group and has organized more yard sales, community parties and children’s activities than she can count.
