How divorce works in Minnesota

Minnesota calls divorce “dissolution of marriage.” It is a no-fault state: neither spouse has to prove wrongdoing, only that the marriage has broken down for good. Most of Minnesota’s family law is in Chapters 517, 518 and 519 of the Minnesota Statutes.

What a divorce decides

  • How property and debts are divided
  • Whether either spouse will pay spousal maintenance
  • Custody and parenting time for minor children
  • Child support

When children are involved

Custody is decided by the best interests of the child, as defined in Minnesota Statutes section 518.17. Grandparents’ rights are covered in section 257C.08.

Self-help forms, with a second look

The Minnesota courts publish free divorce forms. The hardest part is knowing which forms you need from a long list.
