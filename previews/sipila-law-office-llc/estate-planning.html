Wills & Estate Planning

A Will is the start. An estate plan makes sure everything you own follows it.

From Angela: “I do not sell any financial products nor direct my clients to any insurance agent. I can set up the framework for you to make decisions.”

What an estate plan is

An estate plan is a Will plus a check of all your “paperwork”: deeds, titles, bank accounts, life insurance and other financial assets. Many assets carry a sort of “mini-will” in their paperwork that says who gets them when the owner dies. A Will that conflicts with those mini-wills can cause confusion. An estate plan coordinates everything to avoid that confusion and keep costs down, including probate costs and estate taxes.

Keeping assets out of probate

For estate planning clients, Angela uses “Payable on Death,” “Transfer on Death” and similar designations so assets pass directly to the people you choose. Real estate can be handled with a Transfer on Death Deed.

Seeing the whole picture

Once you can see all your assets together, they are easier to manage for your own benefit. How much should you save for retirement? Do you need nursing home insurance, disability income insurance, or more or less life insurance? Angela sets up the framework so you can make those decisions.

The documents that usually go with it

  • A Will
  • A Power of Attorney and a Health Care Directive
  • Beneficiary and transfer-on-death designations
  • An Ethical Will, if you’d like one

Leaving something to charity

If you have no family and want to leave your estate to a favorite charity but can’t afford to pay much for a Will, Angela offers a discount. Just ask. Without a Will and without relatives, Minnesota itself receives the estate, and churches, schools and animal shelters would be far more honored to receive it.
