FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers from Angela about the office, fees and common legal questions.

What kinds of matters does Angela handle?

Probate and estate settlement, Wills and estate planning, Powers of Attorney and Health Care Directives, Ethical Wills, deeds including Transfer on Death Deeds, easements, purchase agreements and leases, divorce, and guardianship and conservatorship.

Does Angela take criminal or personal injury cases?

No. Angela does not take criminal or personal injury cases. If you live in Northeastern Minnesota, she can still answer a general question by email.

Where is the office?

The office is at 412 1st Street South, Suite 1, Virginia, MN 55792, a block from the Courthouse and across the avenue from the St. Louis County building. There is off-street parking behind the building, and the side door is the main door in use.

What area does Angela serve?

Angela serves the Iron Range and Northeastern Minnesota, including Aitkin, Cook, Itasca, Koochiching, Lake and St. Louis counties, from Orr to Duluth, down to Pine City and over to Hill Lake Township.

Can you answer a quick question by email?

Maybe. If you live in Northeastern Minnesota, you can send a short question to ange@sipilaw.com and Angela will try to reply within a day or two. Email is not secure enough for confidential information, so keep it general. Sending an email does not make Angela your lawyer.

How much does it cost?

Angela is not allowed to post her fees publicly, but if you ask by email she can tell you her hourly rate, her flat fees for a Will, Purchase Agreement, Easement or Deed, and send her standard fee estimate for a Probate or a Divorce. The office offers a free consultation.

What happens if a person dies without a Will?

Minnesota law decides. In general a surviving spouse is entitled to everything after debts are paid, though property not titled in the spouse’s name may need probate. With no spouse and no children, the assets pass to the parents. With children and no spouse, the children share equally. If no legal relative comes forward, the State of Minnesota receives the assets.

What is an Ethical Will?

An Ethical Will passes on your memories, wisdom and guidance to future generations, as a letter, a recording or part of your Will. Angela charges the same flat fee for an Ethical Will as for a Will.

What is a Transfer on Death Deed?

Since August 2008, Minnesota has allowed a Transfer on Death Deed. It passes the real estate it describes to the people you name only when you die, so the property avoids probate. You can revoke it for any reason and sell the property without anyone else’s signature.

What happened to the “Living Will”?

The term “Living Will” has been replaced by “Health Care Directive.” A Health Care Directive names one or more people you choose to make health care decisions for you when you can’t.

What is a Family Care Agreement?

A written contract for paying a family member who provides all or most of the care for another adult. It can be one page: names, the hourly wage, expected hours per day, the tasks, signed and dated.

Can I meet somewhere other than the office?

Yes. You can meet at the office or another place that is convenient for you, as long as the conversation can’t be overheard. Angela can also step out and talk with you in your car.
