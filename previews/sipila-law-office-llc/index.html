Your attorney

Meet Angela E. Sipila

Angela graduated from Cook High School and Mesabi Community College in Virginia, then the University of Minnesota’s Carlson School of Management, and earned her law degree at William Mitchell College of Law. She passed the Minnesota bar in 1994.

She has served as President of the Range Bar Association and is a member of the Finnish American Legal Association.
