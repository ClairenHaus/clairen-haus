What Angela doesn’t handle

Angela doesn’t take criminal cases or personal injury cases. If you live in Northeastern Minnesota she can still take the time to answer a general question by email, and the resources page lists free places to start.

Fees

Angela isn’t allowed to post her fees publicly, but she’ll tell you if you ask by email: her hourly rate, her flat fees for a Will, Purchase Agreement, Easement or Deed, and her standard fee estimate for a Probate or a Divorce.
