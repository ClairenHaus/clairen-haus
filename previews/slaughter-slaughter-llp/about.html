About the firm

A Newport Beach firm since 1986

Slaughter & Slaughter, LLP provides business, tax and estate planning legal services to individuals and businesses in coastal Orange County.

Our history

  • 1985
    Donald E. Slaughter, previously a tax partner with Ball, Hunt, Brown and Baerwitz in Long Beach, forms his own firm.
  • 1986
    W. James Slaughter, previously in-house counsel with General Electric Company, joins Don to form Slaughter & Slaughter.
  • Today
    The firm practices from 4 Upper Newport Plaza, Suite 100 in Newport Beach.

Who we represent

The firm represents primarily individuals and small and medium-sized businesses in Orange County, California. Many clients are located in Newport Beach, Costa Mesa, Huntington Beach and Irvine, and the firm also represents clients in Riverside County and Los Angeles County.

The firm serves as general counsel to a number of privately held companies. Typical business clients include corporations, entrepreneurs, family businesses and investors.

How we work

  • Practical and cost-effectiveThe firm provides high quality, cost-effective legal services, with a practical, solution-based approach.
  • Preventive counselThe firm works closely with clients to anticipate future legal issues and to provide early, preventive counseling.
  • Business, tax and estate work under one roofTaxation, business entities, business and tax litigation, probate and estate planning, handled by two partners who have worked together since 1986.
