The partners

Slaughter & Slaughter, LLP was formed in 1986 by Donald E. Slaughter and W. James Slaughter.

DS

Donald E. Slaughter

Partner · Certified Specialist in Taxation

Before forming his own firm in 1985, Don Slaughter was a tax partner with Ball, Hunt, Brown and Baerwitz in Long Beach. He advises individual and business clients in taxation, business transactions and estate planning, and is certified by the State Bar of California as a specialist in taxation.

JS

W. James Slaughter

Partner · Business Entities & Transactions

Before joining Don Slaughter to form the firm in 1986, Jim Slaughter was in-house legal counsel with General Electric Company. He advises individuals and closely held businesses on business entities and transactions, and also works in estate planning and estate administration.
