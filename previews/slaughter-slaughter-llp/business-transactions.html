Business Law

Business transactions

Contracts, mergers, acquisitions, liquidations and family business continuity for individual and corporate clients.

What the firm does

Slaughter & Slaughter represents individual and corporate clients in a variety of business transactions. The firm frequently assists clients with transitions such as mergers, acquisitions and liquidations, and with family business continuity planning, and it serves as general counsel to a number of privately held companies.

  • Contracts and business agreements
  • Mergers and acquisitions
  • Liquidations
  • Family business continuity planning
  • General counsel to privately held companies

The firm works closely with clients to anticipate future legal issues and to provide early, preventive counseling.

Transactions, in plain terms

Buying, selling or combining a business usually involves more than a purchase price. The parties decide whether assets or ownership interests change hands, what each side promises about the business, how liabilities are handled, and what happens after closing. The tax treatment of the deal can shape its structure from the start, which is one reason the firm pairs transaction work with tax advice.

Family business continuity

For a family-owned company, planning for the next generation is both a business question and an estate planning question: who will own the company, who will run it, and how the transition is funded. The firm advises on both sides.
