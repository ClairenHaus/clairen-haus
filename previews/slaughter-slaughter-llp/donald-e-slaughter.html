Background

Before forming his own firm in 1985, Don Slaughter was a tax partner with the law firm of Ball, Hunt, Brown and Baerwitz in Long Beach, California. In 1986 he and Jim Slaughter formed Slaughter & Slaughter.

Don advises individual and business clients in the areas of taxation, business transactions and estate planning. He emphasizes a practical, cost-effective, solution-based approach to his practice.

Don is certified by the State Bar of California as a specialist in taxation, and holds a Master of Laws (LL.M.) in Taxation from New York University.

Practice

His work spans the firm’s tax practice, from tax planning and general corporate taxation such as acquisitions, mergers and joint ventures, to contested proceedings before the IRS, the United States Tax Court and the California Franchise Tax Board, as well as estate planning and business transactions.
