What the firm does

Slaughter & Slaughter provides estate planning services to its clients, including preparation of wills, trusts and related documents. The firm serves clients with diverse backgrounds and desires, on issues ranging from simple wills to more complex matters.

  • Wills
  • Trusts
  • Generation-skipping trusts
  • Insurance trusts
  • Charitable remainder trusts
  • Private foundations

The three main goals of an estate plan

Estate planning is the process of planning for the disposition of your assets when you pass away. The firm describes three main goals it addresses:

  • Leaving your assets to your intended beneficiaries
  • Avoiding probate, the judicial process of distributing a decedent’s estate
  • Minimizing estate taxes where possible

“Many people put off estate planning. Yet our clients frequently tell us how pleased they are to have it done, and the peace of mind it brings. We try to make your estate planning process as simple and painless as possible.”

Tax-aware planning

Because Don Slaughter is a certified specialist in taxation, the firm’s estate planning work is done with the tax consequences in view, which matters most for larger estates, family businesses and charitable gifts.
