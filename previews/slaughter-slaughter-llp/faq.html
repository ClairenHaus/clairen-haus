Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about the firm, its attorneys and its practice areas.

What kinds of legal work does Slaughter & Slaughter handle?

The firm’s areas of emphasis are taxation, business entities (including corporations and limited liability companies), business transactions, business and tax litigation, probate and estate planning.

Who does the firm represent?

Primarily individuals and small and medium-sized businesses in Orange County, California. Many clients are in Newport Beach, Costa Mesa, Huntington Beach and Irvine, and the firm also represents clients in Riverside County and Los Angeles County.

Where is the office?

4 Upper Newport Plaza, Suite 100, Newport Beach, California 92660. The phone number is 949-721-9091.

Is Don Slaughter a certified tax specialist?

Yes. Donald E. Slaughter is certified by the State Bar of California as a specialist in taxation. He also holds a Master of Laws (LL.M.) in Taxation from New York University.

Can the firm help with a dispute with the IRS or the Franchise Tax Board?

Yes. The firm handles contested proceedings before the Internal Revenue Service, the United States Tax Court and the California Franchise Tax Board, including California and federal tax litigation.

Can you help me form a corporation or an LLC?

Yes. The firm is often involved in forming new business ventures, including corporations, limited liability companies and partnerships.

Does the firm act as outside general counsel for companies?

Yes. The firm serves as general counsel to a number of privately held companies.

What does estate planning at the firm include?

Wills, trusts and related estate planning services, from simple wills to more complex matters such as generation-skipping trusts, insurance trusts, charitable remainder trusts and private foundations.

What are the main goals of an estate plan?

The firm addresses three main goals: leaving your assets to your intended beneficiaries, avoiding probate (the judicial process of distributing a decedent’s estate), and minimizing estate taxes where possible.

Can the firm help after a death with a trust or probate?

Yes. The firm assists with trust funding and administration, probate and other court proceedings, and helps resolve disputes regarding wills, trusts and estates.

I received an official-looking notice telling my company to file a Statement of Information for a fee. Is it from the state?

Often it is not. The firm has seen many mailings from private companies with official-sounding names that offer to file a corporation’s or LLC’s Statement of Information for a large fee. The California Secretary of State has warned that these mailings do not come from the state, and it sends its own notice before the statement is due.

Does anyone at the firm speak Spanish?

Yes. W. James Slaughter speaks fluent Spanish.

How long has the firm been in practice?

Slaughter & Slaughter was formed in 1986. Donald E. Slaughter formed his own firm in 1985, after serving as a tax partner with Ball, Hunt, Brown and Baerwitz in Long Beach.

These answers are general information about the firm, not legal advice for your situation.
