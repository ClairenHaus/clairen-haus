What the firm does

The firm’s tax practice includes tax controversy work, including California and federal tax litigation. Slaughter & Slaughter handles contested proceedings before the Internal Revenue Service, the United States Tax Court and the California Franchise Tax Board.

  • Contested proceedings before the Internal Revenue Service
  • Cases in the United States Tax Court
  • Matters before the California Franchise Tax Board
  • California and federal tax litigation
  • Business and tax litigation

Tax disputes, in plain terms

A tax dispute often begins with a notice from the IRS or the Franchise Tax Board proposing changes to a return. Many disputes are resolved administratively; others move to an appeal or to court. Notices usually carry deadlines, and missing one can narrow the options, so it helps to have a tax lawyer review a notice soon after it arrives.

Don Slaughter is certified by the State Bar of California as a specialist in taxation and holds an LL.M. in Taxation from New York University.
