Tax planning

Corporate and individual tax advice from a State Bar of California certified specialist in taxation.

What the firm does

Donald E. Slaughter holds a Master of Laws in Taxation from New York University and is certified by the State Bar of California as a specialist in taxation. With that background the firm handles a diverse range of tax matters, from general corporate taxation work such as acquisitions, mergers and joint ventures, to advising clients on tax planning, avoidance of tax disputes, disclosure matters and related tax issues.

  • Tax aspects of acquisitions, mergers and joint ventures
  • General corporate taxation
  • Tax planning for individuals and businesses
  • Avoiding tax disputes and disclosure questions
  • Tax considerations in estate planning

Why planning comes first

Many tax questions are easier and less expensive to answer before a transaction closes or a return is filed than afterward. The firm’s approach is to work closely with clients to anticipate future legal issues and to provide early, preventive counseling.

Certified specialists in California are attorneys certified by the State Bar of California Board of Legal Specialization in a particular field of law.
