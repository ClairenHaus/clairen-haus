Background

Prior to joining Don Slaughter to form the firm in 1986, Jim Slaughter worked as in-house legal counsel with General Electric Company, working in labor, employment, transactional and trade regulation law.

Jim provides business entity and transaction advice to individuals and closely held businesses. He also works in estate planning and estate administration.

Jim speaks fluent Spanish, and some Portuguese and French. He studied at the Instituto de Estudios Europeos in Madrid before graduating magna cum laude from Santa Clara University.
