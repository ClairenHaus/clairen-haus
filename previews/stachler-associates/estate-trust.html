From our firm

Effective estate planning facilitates the orderly transfer of assets to your beneficiaries, provides security for your surviving spouse, and can reduce or eliminate the tax due on the transfer of your business and other assets. For business owners, providing for business continuity and succession of ownership is essential. We can guide you through the complex process of getting your financial affairs in order.

Estate and trust services

  • Estate planning

  • Gift tax returns
  • Business continuity for owners

Working alongside your attorney

Estate planning usually involves your attorney for the legal documents and your accountant for the tax side. We can work with the professionals you already have.

After a loss

When someone passes away, there may be a final individual return and, for some estates and trusts, separate fiduciary returns. We can help you understand what’s required.

General information only, not tax advice for your situation. Call us to talk through your own circumstances.
