FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the firm. For anything about your own taxes, call us at (765) 964-7514.

Where is your office?

Our main office is at 555 W Chestnut St, Union City, IN 47390.

How do I reach you?

Call (765) 964-7514, fax (765) 964-7515, or email melinda@stachlerandassociates.com.

Do you work with businesses as well as individuals?

Yes. We prepare income tax returns for all types of businesses and individuals, and we provide bookkeeping, payroll, financial statements and consulting for business clients.

What kinds of returns do you prepare?

Returns for individuals, corporations, partnerships, LLCs and LLPs, estates, trusts and gifts, and not-for-profit organizations, plus payroll, sales and use, and business property returns.

Can you help if I get a letter from the IRS or the state?

Yes. We provide IRS and state audit representation and represent clients before federal and state tax agencies.

Do you help with QuickBooks?

Yes. We help with QuickBooks® setup, support, training and reporting.

Can you help me start a business?

Yes. We offer business startup services, including help evaluating which type of entity (such as an S corporation, sole proprietorship or LLC) best fits your business, and accounting system setup for new businesses.

Wasn’t this firm called Peterson & Stachler?

Yes. Stachler & Associates, LLC was formerly Peterson & Stachler, PC.

Are you hiring?

We are pleased to accept resumes. Email melinda@stachlerandassociates.com.
