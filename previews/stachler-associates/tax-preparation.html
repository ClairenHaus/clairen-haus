What a return involves

A tax return pulls together a year of income, deductions and credits into the forms the IRS and your state require. For a business, it also ties back to the year’s books, so the cleaner the records, the smoother the return.

Getting ready

Gather the year’s income statements (W-2s, 1099s, K-1s), records of deductible expenses, last year’s return, and any letters you received from the IRS or the state. If you’re not sure whether something matters, bring it and ask.

General information only, not tax advice for your situation. Call us to talk through your own circumstances.
