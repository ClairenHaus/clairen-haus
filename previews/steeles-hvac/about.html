About Steele’s

A locally owned and operated, family HVAC company in Denton, serving Maryland’s mid and upper Eastern Shore for 40 years.

Who we are

Local, family owned, and based in Denton

Steele’s Refrigeration, Heating & Air Conditioning, Inc. is a locally owned and operated HVAC company serving Maryland’s mid and upper Eastern Shore. The company specializes in heating, air conditioning, HVAC service, ventilation, air purification and sheet metal fabrication, for both residential and light commercial customers.

Steele’s will service any brand of gas furnace, oil furnace, boiler, heat pump, air-conditioning and geothermal system, and is a Carrier Factory Authorized Dealer for new equipment.

Superior customer service and quality are the number one priority. Every employee goes through pre-employment screening and a background check, and installers and technicians get continual training and certification in the latest industry advances, so the technician at your door is qualified, professional and safe.
