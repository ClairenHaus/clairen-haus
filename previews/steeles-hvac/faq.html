FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about service, scheduling, estimates and the areas Steele’s covers.

What brands of equipment does Steele’s service?

Any brand. Steele’s services any brand of gas furnace, oil furnace, boiler, heat pump, air-conditioning and geothermal system.

What areas do you serve?

Maryland’s mid and upper Eastern Shore, including Denton, Easton, Cambridge, Centreville, Chestertown, Stevensville, Queenstown, Federalsburg, Ridgely and more. See the full service area list.

What are your office hours?

Monday through Friday, 7:30 am to 3:30 pm.

How do I schedule service?

Call (410) 643-0005 or (410) 479-5560 (toll-free 888-518-HVAC), email sales@steeleshvac.com, or use the online request form.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Estimates on air conditioning, heating and other HVAC needs are free.

Do you work on homes or businesses?

Both. Steele’s works in residential and light commercial applications, and also takes on new construction projects, small and large.

Do you offer maintenance plans?

Yes. The service department offers annual and semi-annual residential service agreements. See what a tune-up covers.

Are your technicians background-checked?

Yes. Steele’s carries out pre-employment screenings and background checks on all employees, and provides continual training and certification for its installers and technicians.

Is Steele’s licensed?

Yes. Maryland Master HVAC license #01-4795.

Do you install Carrier equipment?

Yes. Steele’s is a Carrier Factory Authorized Dealer and installs Carrier heating and cooling systems, including geothermal heat pumps.

Is financing available?

Financing is available on new Carrier systems, with approved credit. Ask about Carrier Cool Cash rebates, which run during select times of the year.

What payment methods do you accept?

Visa, MasterCard, Discover and American Express.

Do you offer duct cleaning and indoor air quality products?

Yes: duct cleaning, humidifiers, dehumidifiers, UV lights, air cleaners, air purification and ventilation.

Need service?

Call the Denton office, Monday–Friday 7:30 am–3:30 pm.
