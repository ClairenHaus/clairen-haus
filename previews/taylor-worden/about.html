About the firm

Quality legal advice at an affordable price

An Indianapolis firm focused on bankruptcy and general law, serving clients throughout Indiana.

Our approach

The Taylor Worden Law Firm provides quality legal advice for bankruptcy and general law needs at an affordable price. We are here to help with your legal needs, providing calculated, superior service for a fair price.

Legal issues can be very stressful and difficult on your life. We work diligently and professionally on every matter we can, to help get your life back to normal.

Built for people on limited funds

We understand that people filing for bankruptcy protection have limited funds. Our goal is quality legal services for a reasonable cost while maintaining a high standard of excellence.

A full-service firm

Bankruptcy is our focus, and our services also cover debt settlement, landlord-tenant matters, contract review and formation, and estate planning.
