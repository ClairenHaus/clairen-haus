Debt Settlement

Negotiate with your creditors to repay less than the total you owe, and possibly avoid filing for bankruptcy.

An alternative to bankruptcy

In addition to bankruptcy services, the Taylor Worden Law Firm offers debt settlement. People often turn to debt settlement when they cannot afford to repay their debts, despite immense effort.

Debt settlement allows you to work directly with your creditors to negotiate a repayment amount that is less than the total you currently owe. In some cases, creditors are willing to reduce balances significantly to guarantee repayment.

Is it right for you?

Debt settlement can be an effective tool for clients who are serious about resolving their debts and are hoping to avoid filing for bankruptcy. If it isn't the right fit, the firm also handles Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 bankruptcy.

Debt Settlement FAQ

What is debt settlement?

Debt settlement lets you work directly with your creditors to negotiate a repayment amount that is less than the total you currently owe. In some cases creditors are willing to reduce balances significantly to guarantee repayment.
