Practice Areas

Catastrophic Injury and Wrongful Death

High-exposure, high-profile cases involving catastrophic injury and wrongful death, where the damages sought run into the millions.

We have successfully defended clients against claims of liability for injuries involving automobiles, industrial equipment and consumer products. Our experienced, skilled and resourceful lawyers can handle any size personal injury case.

We have particular experience with high-exposure and high-profile cases involving catastrophic injury and wrongful death, where the damages sought are in the multimillion-dollar range.

Cases involving

  • Automobiles
  • Industrial equipment
  • Consumer products
  • Catastrophic injury and wrongful death claims

Trial experience on both sides

Deron Dacus has experience in catastrophic personal injury cases, including full defense verdicts, and Shannon Dacus’s practice includes personal injury claims. The firm brings the same trial-first approach to these cases as to its commercial and patent work.

What these cases generally involve

In general terms, catastrophic injury cases often turn on technical evidence such as accident reconstruction, product design and maintenance records, and on expert testimony about medical care and future losses. Preserving evidence early is often critical.
