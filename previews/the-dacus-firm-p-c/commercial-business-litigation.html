Practice Areas

Commercial and Business Litigation

From breach of contract cases to complex commercial disputes, for family-owned businesses through Fortune 100 corporations.

We have represented all types of companies, from family-owned businesses to Fortune 100 corporations, in nearly every industry, in lawsuits ranging from breach of contract cases to complex commercial disputes.

This breadth of experience allows us to develop the most efficient litigation strategy to meet your business objectives. With over 20 years of litigation know-how and an in-house CPA/attorney, we have the skills to build that strategy, and the experience to negotiate, mediate or take your case to trial with confidence.

Disputes we handle

  • Breach of contract and contractual disputes
  • Shareholder disputes
  • Legal and accounting malpractice
  • Construction disputes
  • Banking law
  • Oil and gas and real estate disputes
  • Intellectual property claims

A CPA in the courtroom

Deron Dacus is a licensed certified public accountant. Before beginning his legal career he was a senior-level accountant at Price Waterhouse, where he gained broad business experience representing clients. Business disputes often turn on financial records and damages models, and having an attorney who reads them as an accountant does is an advantage in discovery, with experts and in front of a jury.

Plaintiff or defendant

Representing both plaintiffs and defendants, the firm has obtained multi-million dollar verdicts for plaintiffs and successfully defended clients where hundreds of millions were at stake. The Dacus Firm held two of the top 10 jury verdicts in Texas in 2014.

Resolving a business dispute

In general, many commercial disputes resolve before trial through negotiation or mediation. The firm’s view is that a lawyer who is ready and able to try the case is in the strongest position to negotiate an early resolution when that serves the client.
