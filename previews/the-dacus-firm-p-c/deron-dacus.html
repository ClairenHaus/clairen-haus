Attorney

Deron R. Dacus

Trial Attorney

Direct (903) 705-7233ddacus@dacusfirm.comOffice (903) 705-1117

Mr. Dacus is a trial lawyer and a native East Texan. His legal practice is focused primarily on business, commercial and patent litigation, and he also has experience in catastrophic personal injury cases.

His commercial litigation practice covers a wide arena, including shareholder disputes, legal and accounting malpractice, contractual disputes, construction disputes, banking law, oil and gas, real estate, and intellectual property claims. Representing both plaintiffs and defendants, Mr. Dacus has obtained multi-million dollar verdicts for plaintiffs and successfully defended clients where hundreds of millions were at stake.

Mr. Dacus had plaintiff’s verdicts listed among Verdict Search’s Top 5 verdicts in Texas for 2014–2018. In the same period he had multiple full defense verdicts in patent, commercial and catastrophic personal injury cases, including a complete victory in the nationally publicized trade secret case of Huawei vs. CNEX Laboratories. Several Fortune 500 companies have named Mr. Dacus a “go to” lawyer.

Patent litigation in the Eastern District of Texas

Mr. Dacus has extensive experience in patent litigation in the Eastern District of Texas. He has represented clients in well over 1,000 patent cases in Tyler, Marshall, Texarkana and Lufkin, and has successfully tried dozens of patent cases to verdict. He has extensive knowledge of the local courts, rules and the district’s patent rules, and is a charter member of the T. John Ward Inn of Court.

A CPA’s background

Mr. Dacus is also a licensed certified public accountant. Before beginning his legal career he was a senior-level accountant at Price Waterhouse, where he gained broad business experience representing clients. After law school he practiced with a large, national law firm in Dallas before returning home to East Texas in 1998.
