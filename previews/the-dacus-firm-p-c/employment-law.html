Practice Areas

Employment Law

Representing employers in court and before agencies, and advising them before disputes start.

We represent employers before state and federal trial courts and administrative agencies in lawsuits, administrative investigations and proceedings involving employment-related claims.

We have successfully defended a variety of employment cases for employers, ranging from OSHA investigations to non-competition and confidentiality matters.

Matters we handle for employers

  • Employment lawsuits in state and federal court
  • Administrative investigations and proceedings
  • OSHA investigations
  • Non-competition and confidentiality matters

Advice before a dispute

The Dacus Firm also routinely advises employers on issues related to employment matters such as employment contracts, wage and hour law, and workplace discrimination.

Why early advice matters

In general, many employment disputes turn on documents created long before a claim is filed: the employment agreement, written policies, pay records and the paper trail around a decision. Reviewing those with counsel early often shapes how a dispute can be resolved.
