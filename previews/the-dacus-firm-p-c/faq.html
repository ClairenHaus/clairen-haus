FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers about the firm, its attorneys and its practice areas, drawn from the firm’s own information. For advice about your matter, call the office.

About the firm

Where is The Dacus Firm located?

The firm’s office is at 821 ESE Loop 323, Suite 430, Tyler, Texas 75701. The main office number is (903) 705-1117.

How long has the firm practiced in East Texas?

The Dacus Firm has had attorneys in East Texas since 1997.

Who are the firm’s attorneys?

The firm’s attorneys are Deron R. Dacus and Shannon M. Dacus, both trial attorneys and both graduates of Baylor University School of Law.

Does the firm represent plaintiffs or defendants?

Both. The firm represents plaintiffs asserting their rights and defendants defending against claims.

Does the firm only represent large companies?

No. The firm has represented more than 20 of the Fortune 100, as well as individuals and small businesses.

Will my case have to go to trial?

Not necessarily. The firm prides itself on seeking innovative, creative and early resolutions to disputes, and considers its success as trial lawyers one of its major strengths in negotiating resolutions early in a case.

Is the firm a certified woman-owned or minority business?

The firm has been certified as a Woman-Owned Business by the Women’s Enterprise National Council and as a Minority Business Enterprise by the National Minority Supplier Development Council, Inc.

Practice areas

What kinds of cases does the firm handle?

Patent litigation, commercial and business litigation, oil and gas, governmental defense, employment law, and catastrophic injury and wrongful death. See all practice areas.

Does the firm handle patent cases in the Eastern District of Texas?

Yes. The firm has represented clients in hundreds of patent cases in the Eastern District of Texas, for patent owners and for those accused of infringement, and has served as both local and lead counsel. Deron Dacus has represented clients in well over 1,000 patent cases in Tyler, Marshall, Texarkana and Lufkin.

Whom does the firm represent in oil and gas disputes?

Both individuals and oil and gas companies, including some of the country’s largest, in title and lease disputes, reservoir damage, surface damages, failure to prudently operate and operator removal, and personal injury cases.

Does the firm represent governmental entities?

Yes. The firm defends governmental entities and their officials and employees in litigation involving civil rights, constitutional law, employment, land use and personal injury claims, and advises them on employment law, risk management and compliance.

Does the firm represent employers or employees in employment matters?

The firm’s employment practice represents employers, before state and federal trial courts and administrative agencies, and advises employers on employment contracts, wage and hour law and workplace discrimination.

Working with the firm

Is an attorney at the firm board certified?

Yes. Deron Dacus is Board Certified in Civil Trial Law by the Texas Board of Legal Specialization. He is also a licensed certified public accountant.

Does anyone at the firm speak Spanish?

Yes. Shannon Dacus is fluent in Spanish.
