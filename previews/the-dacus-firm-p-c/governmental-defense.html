Practice Areas

Governmental Defense

Defending governmental entities, their officials and employees across East Texas, and helping them avoid litigation in the first place.

We have an experienced team of trial lawyers who defend governmental entities and their officials and employees in litigation involving civil rights, constitutional law, employment, land use and personal injury claims.

Our defense team practices all over East Texas, so we are able to provide localized service to our governmental clients in the communities they serve.

Claims we defend

  • Civil rights claims
  • Constitutional law claims
  • Employment matters
  • Land use disputes
  • Personal injury claims

Beyond the courtroom

Our representation is not limited to the courtroom. It includes working with our clients to avoid litigation. We advise and consult with our governmental clients in all areas, including employment law, general risk management, and regulatory and statutory compliance.

How these cases are different

In general terms, lawsuits against public entities and officials raise issues that private cases do not, such as governmental and official immunity, notice requirements and limits on damages under Texas and federal law. Those issues are often decided early, which makes prompt involvement of defense counsel important.
