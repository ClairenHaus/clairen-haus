What changes

Specific fixes, drawn from the current site.

  • A mobile layout

    The current site has no viewport tag, so on a phone it shows a shrunken desktop page. Google indexes the mobile version first. The concept is built mobile-first.

  • Structured data and a street address on the homepage

    The current site has no schema markup, no sitemap and no robots.txt, and the homepage does not show the street address. The concept adds LegalService and Person JSON-LD with full name, address and phone.

  • Alt text and more homepage substance

    11 of 12 homepage images lack alt text and the page carries about 256 words. The concept brings the firm’s practice descriptions and attorney credentials onto one page.

  • Questions, answered

    The current site has no FAQ content. The concept adds a short set answered only from the firm’s published facts.

Common questions

Answers from the firm’s own site.

Each answer below is drawn from facts the firm already publishes, marked up so search engines and AI assistants can cite it. General information only, not legal advice.

Where does The Dacus Firm practice?

The firm is based at 821 ESE Loop 323, Suite 430, Tyler, TX 75701 and has practiced in East Texas since 1997, including patent cases in the Eastern District of Texas courts in Tyler, Marshall, Texarkana and Lufkin.

Does the firm represent plaintiffs or defendants?

Both. The firm represents plaintiffs asserting their rights and defendants defending against claims, from individuals and small businesses to Fortune 100 corporations.

Is Deron Dacus board certified?

Yes. He is Board Certified in Civil Trial Law by the Texas Board of Legal Specialization, and is also a licensed certified public accountant.
