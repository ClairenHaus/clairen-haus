Practice Areas

Oil & Gas

Litigation for individuals and for oil and gas companies, including some of the country’s largest, across virtually every type of dispute the industry produces.

The Dacus Firm represents both individuals and oil and gas companies, including some of the country’s largest, in cases involving title and lease disputes, reservoir damage, surface damages, failure to prudently operate and operator removal, and personal injury.

We have litigated virtually every type of dispute that can arise in the oil and gas industry, both as the plaintiff bringing claims and as a defendant.

Cases we handle

  • Title and lease disputes
  • Reservoir damage
  • Surface damages
  • Failure to prudently operate and operator removal
  • Personal injury in the oil field

Industry knowledge and courtroom experience

It’s not hard to find an attorney in East Texas with knowledge of the industry, and it’s not difficult to find an attorney with courtroom experience. It is much harder to find a lawyer with both. Deron Dacus grew up in East Texas and as a part of the oil and gas industry. The combination of industry knowledge and courtroom experience is what the firm brings to positioning your case for success.

What these disputes often involve

In general terms, oil and gas cases often turn on the language of the lease or deed, the history of the chain of title, the operator’s duties under the lease and Texas law, and technical evidence about the reservoir or the surface. Gathering those documents early is usually the first step.
