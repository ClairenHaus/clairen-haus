About the Firm

The Granger approach

The goal of The Granger Firm is to provide hands-on, practical advice in a timely fashion to its real estate clients at a reasonable cost.

A full-service real estate firm

The Granger Firm is a full-service real estate law firm, handling all areas of commercial and residential litigation and transactions. It actively handles matters throughout the state and federal courts of Pennsylvania, and before regulatory bodies and municipalities.

The Firm’s clients include real estate and commercial brokerages, developers and builders, landlords and tenants, homeowners, businesses, title insurers, lenders, and individuals engaged in sophisticated transactions or litigation.

The Firm distinguishes itself from firms with multiple practice areas: because its attorneys concentrate only on real estate matters, the client is not paying for the education of its attorney on what are basic real estate fundamentals to the attorneys at The Granger Firm.

Educators in real estate law

Blair H. Granger and David J. Scaggs regularly lecture on real estate matters to other attorneys for the Pennsylvania Bar Institute, the educational arm of the Pennsylvania Bar Association, which provides continuing legal education to attorneys.

Mr. Granger is a course planner, is on the planning team for PBI’s annual Real Estate Institute held every December, and has co-presented its opening session reviewing the year’s cases and laws since 2002. Recent courses he has presented include “Advanced Issues in Title Insurance” (Conestoga Title College, January 2023) and “CLE Down the Shore” (Atlantic City, August 2019).

Mr. Scaggs is a course planner and presenter for PBI and the Pennsylvania Land Title Institute, including the alternating biennial Real Estate 101 and Title Insurance 101 courses, with recent presentations at Real Estate 101 (September 2022 and August 2026) and Title Insurance 101 (September 2021).

In the community

Involved where we live and work

The Firm is a contributor to the YMCA of the Greater Brandywine, which funds programs for those who would otherwise not be able to participate.

Blair H. Granger

Serves on the Board of Trustees for Main Line Health’s Hospice and Home Care Foundation, which provides home care, therapy and hospice for individuals in need. He also served on the Board of Advisors for the Upper Main Line YMCA from 2013 to 2014.

David J. Scaggs

Chairman of the Zoning Hearing Board in Westtown Township, which hears requests from residents and businesses for zoning interpretations or relief. He served on the Board of Advisors for the West Chester Area YMCA from 2013 to 2014 and volunteered for years as a youth sports coach.
