Blair H. Granger, Esquire

Mr. Granger is a widely recognized expert on real estate issues and a frequent lecturer for attorneys and other real estate professionals.

Practice

Mr. Granger’s lectures focus significantly on title disputes, community association disputes and construction litigation. A significant portion of his practice involves litigation on behalf of major title insurers for their insured owners and lenders, builders and community associations. He has litigated numerous complex title claims ranging from boundary disputes to lien priority and validity, and has successfully defended suits against title insurers, including suits involving bad faith and RICO claims.

Mr. Granger has tried numerous cases and has argued before all of the appellate courts of Pennsylvania. He successfully argued before the Pennsylvania Supreme Court, securing the dismissal of the appeal against a lending client after oral argument.

He has personally handled or supervised well over 1,000 real estate transactions conveying commercial, residential and development property, from the agreement of sale to settlement, and has directly handled or supervised hundreds of litigation matters through settlement, trial and appeal. He has acted as a mediator or arbitrator numerous times, has been accepted in both state and federal court as an expert on real estate legal issues, and was retained by the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania as an expert on mortgage fraud, testifying on the standard of care for a real estate closing.

Teaching and writing

Mr. Granger has taught courses for the Pennsylvania and Philadelphia Bar Associations since 1994, including “Handling the Failure to Disclose Case,” “Effective Representation of Buyers, Brokers and Sellers,” “Commercial Real Estate for the Non-Specialist,” “New Developments for the Real Estate Practitioner,” “Failed Real Estate Deals” and “Advanced Issues in Title Insurance.” He is on the planning team for the Pennsylvania Bar Institute’s annual Real Estate Institute and has co-presented its opening session, a year-end review of cases and laws, since 2002. He will present at the “Year in Review” session of the 2026 Real Estate Institute on December 3, 2026.

He has published a chapter for LexisNexis on “Litigating the Failed Real Estate Deal.”

Recognition and community

Mr. Granger was selected by Main Line Magazine to the “Best Lawyers on the Main Line,” real estate category, for 2010. He serves on the Board of Trustees for Main Line Health’s Hospice and Home Care Foundation, and served on the Board of Advisors for the Upper Main Line YMCA from 2013 to 2014.
