David J. Scaggs, Esquire

Mr. Scaggs litigates and closes real estate matters for title insurers, brokers, builders, businesses and consumers.

Call (610) 640-7500Email the Firm

Practice

Mr. Scaggs’ litigation practice includes representing title insurance companies and their insureds in matters including lien priority and ownership disputes. He represents real estate brokers and salespersons in claims of failure to disclose defects or breach of contract, and in disputes concerning commissions, competitors and broker/agent relationships. His representation of builder and developer clients includes consumer claims, zoning issues and adjoining landowner disputes.

He also represents consumers in failure-to-disclose litigation, construction defect litigation (including stucco homes), neighbor and boundary disputes, mechanic’s liens, agreement of sale disputes, failed real estate deals, contractor disputes, partition and zoning matters.

In his transactional practice, Mr. Scaggs has handled hundreds of commercial and residential real estate transactions, from pre-agreement investigation through the agreement of sale and closing to post-closing obligations. His practice also includes zoning relief for individuals and businesses, condominium and homeowner association documents, title issues, residential and commercial leases, easements, maintenance agreements and corporate formation.

Significant cases

  • City of Philadelphia v. Galdo, 194 A.3d 1043 (Pa. 2019)Political subdivisions are not immune from claims of adverse possession.
  • Lincoln Investors, L.P. v. King, et al., 152 A.3d 382 (Pa. Commw. 2015)A county-adopted watershed storm water plan is a prerequisite for liability against a property owner under Section 13 of the Pennsylvania Storm Water Management Act.
  • Shipley Fuels Marketing, LLC v. Medrow, et al., 37 A.3d 1215 (Pa. Super. 2012)A judgment lien does not attach to real property until it is indexed in the judgment index.

Teaching

Mr. Scaggs is a course planner and lecturer for the Pennsylvania Bar Institute’s biennial “Real Estate 101” and “Title 101” seminars, and has lectured for PBI on topics including “Handling the Failure to Disclose Case,” “Marking Up Title” and “Easements.” He presented at PBI’s “Real Estate 101” webcast seminar on August 6, 2026. He has lectured for the National Business Institute on “How to Obtain Good Title in Real Estate Transactions” and “Commercial Real Estate Closings From Start to Finish,” and has presented at the Pennsylvania Real Estate Institute on title insurance matters.

Recognition and community

In August 2014 Mr. Scaggs was named a “Top Lawyer” in Real Estate Law by Main Line Today magazine. In 2011 he was nominated for a three-year term as a Council Member of the Pennsylvania Bar Association’s Real Property, Probate and Trust Law Section. He is Chairman of the Westtown Township Zoning Hearing Board in Chester County.

He has volunteered as a coach for CYO, Little League and YMCA youth sports, served on the West Chester Area YMCA’s Board of Advisors and Youth Sports Advisory Committee in 2013–2014, received the 2013 Volunteer of the Year Award for Youth Development from the YMCA’s Osker Lasko Youth Program Center, and was a volunteer Cub Scout leader with Pack 69 in West Chester from 2012 to 2014.
