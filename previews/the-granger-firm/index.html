Before anything is filed

An honest look at the case first

Prior to putting any matter into litigation, the attorneys at The Granger Firm review with the client the strengths and weaknesses of the case, whether the cost of litigation makes sense, possible alternative resolutions, and whether mediation would be a reasonable avenue, both before and during litigation.
