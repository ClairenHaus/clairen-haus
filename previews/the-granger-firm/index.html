Questions clients ask

Answers drawn from the firm's own site.

Short, direct answers are what people and AI assistants look for first. Each one below comes from information the firm already publishes.

Does someone selling a house "as-is" in Pennsylvania still have to fill out a seller's disclosure form?

According to the firm's FAQ, in general yes: Pennsylvania's Seller Disclosure Law still requires disclosure of known material defects for residential sales of buildings with one to four units, unless an exemption applies. The law does not apply to commercial agreements of sale, though parties often add disclosure terms themselves.

Should buyers purchase title insurance?

The firm's FAQ says yes. Subject to its conditions and exclusions, title insurance covers defects in the chain of title for a one-time fee and, unless otherwise exhausted, lasts as long as the buyer owns the property.

Where is The Granger Firm located and which counties does it serve?

The office is at 1800 East Lancaster Avenue, Suite K, Paoli, PA 19301, on the Main Line. The firm serves clients throughout Pennsylvania, including Chester, Montgomery, Delaware, Philadelphia and Bucks Counties.

Does the firm represent real estate brokers?

Yes. Representing brokers is a listed service area, including errors and omissions defense, commission disputes and claims involving failure to disclose or breach of contract.
