Practice

Mr. Gade’s practice covers a broad range of commercial and residential real estate matters, including seller disclosure matters, construction defects, title disputes, tax assessment appeals, landlord/tenant disputes and mechanics’ liens for commercial and residential properties.

He works closely with the Firm’s shareholders and clients to help navigate the legal aspects of real estate matters, providing practical and detail-oriented guidance, and is dedicated to providing clients with effective legal representation.
