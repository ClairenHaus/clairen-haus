Practice area

Real estate transactions, prepared for what comes next

The Granger Firm serves residential, commercial and industrial real estate clients throughout Pennsylvania, from simple residential closings to sophisticated commercial transactions.

Preparation and diligence

A successful real estate transaction requires preparation and diligence. It’s about knowing what to expect and being ready for it: exploring the possibilities, presenting the options, and creating the best environment to achieve the client’s goals while minimizing risk.

The Firm has negotiated well over a thousand agreements of sale and attended the closings for them, handling issues from negotiating the agreement of sale through settlement and beyond. Its team handles complex commercial leasing, land use, development and zoning matters, planned community and condominium formation, and other transactions involving residential, commercial and industrial property.

A litigation firm behind every deal

In support of its transactional practice, the Firm’s clients have the security of knowing that it is also a full-service real estate litigation firm, ready to protect the client’s interests if a deal goes wrong.

The usual steps in a Pennsylvania transaction

Most purchases follow the same arc: the agreement of sale is negotiated and signed; the buyer completes inspections, a title search and financing within the deadlines the agreement sets; title insurance is arranged; and the deed is signed and recorded at settlement, when Pennsylvania’s realty transfer tax is paid. Each step has its own deadlines and choices, which is why the agreement of sale matters so much.
