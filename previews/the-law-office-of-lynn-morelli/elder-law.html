Practice area

Elder Law

Respectful, patient legal help for senior clients and the people who care for them.

What is elder law?

“Elder law” is not a distinct practice area. It is a term that covers a wide range of legal areas as they apply to senior clients. Lynn focuses on all of the legal needs of her senior clients, using a variety of legal tools to meet their specific goals.

Under this holistic approach, Lynn works with senior clients on general estate planning and counsels them about planning for incapacity with documents such as health care proxies and powers of attorney. She also helps senior clients and their caregivers plan for possible long-term care needs, including nursing home care and guardianships.

How Lynn helps

  • Locating the appropriate type of care
  • Coordinating private and public resources to pay for the cost of care
  • Working to protect a client’s right to quality care
  • Asset preservation and long-term care planning
  • Guardianships

An advocate who understands aging

Because she devotes a significant part of her practice to elder law, Lynn brings her senior clients more than legal knowledge. She understands the real physical and mental difficulties that can come with aging, and she advocates for seniors against ageism and stereotypes about “the elderly.”

Lynn is also connected to social workers, psychologists and other elder care professionals in the Berkshire community who may be able to help her senior clients. She treats every client with respect, patience and compassion, so that dealing with legal matters feels a little easier.
