Working with Lynn

You won’t feel rushed, or like a number.

Legal matters such as a Will or a Living Trust can be hard to face, and many people put off the planning that would give them peace of mind. Lynn approaches every matter with compassion, empathy and patience.

She listens carefully to her clients’ concerns, needs, hopes and dreams, and helps them meet their legal planning needs by actually hearing what they are trying to achieve. Then she brings the concepts down to earth, in terms you can easily understand.

  • Lynn works with you herself. When you hire Lynn, she is the one working with you, exclusively.
  • Big-city experience, hometown approach. Lynn practiced in Boston for almost a decade before returning home to Pittsfield, and offers reasonable rates.
  • A caring, nonjudgmental approach for individuals, families and businesses from all walks of life.
  • Connected locally. Lynn works alongside social workers, psychologists and other elder care professionals in the Berkshire community.
