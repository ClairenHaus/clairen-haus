Attorney profile

Lynn Morelli

Attorney at Law. Estate planning, elder law and probate administration in Pittsfield, Massachusetts.

Call (413) 822-9360Email Lynn

Lynn brings the expertise and experience of a big city law firm to her clients in the Berkshires and Pioneer Valley, with a hometown approach and reasonable rates.

Background

Lynn grew up in Pittsfield. She earned her law degree from Suffolk University Law School in 1997, after graduating cum laude from the University of Massachusetts at Amherst in 1991.

Before opening her own law firm in Boston in 1999, she was an attorney with the law firm of Hale and Dorr in Boston and in-house counsel for the Retirement Investment Services division of State Street Global Advisors. In 2005, after almost a decade of practice in Boston, Lynn moved her family and her law firm back to her hometown of Pittsfield.

Her approach

Lynn’s clients choose her because she is caring and attentive, yet thoroughly professional. She listens carefully, then helps clients grasp complex legal issues by explaining them in terms they can easily understand. Her approach fits the character of the Berkshire community, and when you hire Lynn, she is the one working with you.

In the community

Lynn is very involved in community activities, including giving free informational seminars on elder concerns, estate planning, and the process of administering an estate through the probate court.
