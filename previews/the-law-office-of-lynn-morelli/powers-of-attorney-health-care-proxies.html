Powers of Attorney & Health Care Proxies

Two short documents that let the people you trust step in if you can’t act for yourself.

Health care proxy

A health care proxy is a legal document that lets you name a person with authority to make health care decisions for you if you become unable to make them yourself. It is the only legally valid document for naming someone to step into your shoes on health care decisions.

Without one, your spouse or loved ones may have conflicting views or not know what you would want, and relatives, physicians or others may start costly, time-consuming court proceedings to resolve it.

Durable power of attorney

A durable power of attorney lets you name a person to have legal authority over your financial affairs if you become incapacitated.

Many people, single and married, have accounts in their name alone or accounts that require their own signature. If they become incapacitated, no one else can reach those assets. A durable power of attorney is a cost-effective way for a family member or friend to take charge of your finances.

Without one, your spouse or loved ones would have to go to court to become your guardian before they could pay your bills or manage your investments. A properly drafted power of attorney can avoid that court involvement.

Part of a complete plan

Lynn prepares these documents as part of estate planning and elder law planning, so the people you choose can act quickly if they ever need to.
