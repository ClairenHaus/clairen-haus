A general practice since 1978

Local, experienced and personal

The Law Offices of G. Randall Avery, Esq. is a general practice of law serving individuals and businesses. Clients work directly with Randall Avery, who has practiced since 1978 and has represented clients before all courts in Connecticut, including the Connecticut Supreme Court.

The practice was located in Stamford until 2009, when Randall Avery opened his present office in Darien to better serve clients who find it easier to meet in the new location. The office is in the First Floor Office Suites of Darien at 30 Old Kings Highway South, with free parking.

Rooted in the community

Randall Avery is a longtime resident of Rowayton, where he has served his local community and the City of Norwalk in many volunteer capacities: on the Sixth Taxing District Commission, the Board of Estimate and Taxation of the City of Norwalk, and the Norwalk Common Council, where he chaired the Public Works and Building Management committees. He has also chaired two separate Revaluation Taskforces, and has served on the boards of the Rowayton Civic Association, Preserve the Wetlands, and Shakespeare on the Sound.
