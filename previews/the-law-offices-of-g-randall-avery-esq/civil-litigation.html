Area of practice

Civil litigation in Darien and across Connecticut

When a dispute cannot be resolved by agreement, it may need to be decided in court. Litigation in all areas of law is the first service the firm lists.

What civil litigation covers

Civil litigation is the process of resolving a legal dispute between people, businesses or other organizations through the courts. It can involve contracts, property, business relationships, injuries, money owed, or the rights of buyers and sellers. A case may end in a negotiated settlement, a decision by a judge, a verdict at trial, or an appeal to a higher court.

How a case usually moves

  • Assessment. Understanding what happened, what documents exist, and what outcome you need.
  • Pleadings. The written complaint and the response that frame the dispute for the court.
  • Discovery. Each side exchanges information, documents and testimony.
  • Resolution or trial. Many cases settle; those that do not are tried before a judge or jury.
  • Appeal. A party may ask the Appellate Court, and sometimes the Supreme Court, to review the result.

Experience at every level

Randall Avery has practiced law since 1978. He has tried civil and criminal cases, and has represented clients before all courts in Connecticut, including the Connecticut Supreme Court. His appellate work includes DiNuzzo v. Dan Perkins Chevrolet Geo, Inc., before both the Appellate and Supreme Courts, and Reid v. Landsberger, before the Appellate Court.
