Area of practice

Criminal law

Being accused of a crime is frightening. Criminal law is one of the firm’s areas of practice, and Randall Avery has tried criminal cases in Connecticut courts.

What criminal defense involves

A person charged with a crime has important rights, including the right to be represented by a lawyer, the right to remain silent, and the right to require the State to prove its case. A defense attorney protects those rights at each stage of the case.

The usual stages of a case

  • Arrest and arraignment. The first court appearance, where charges are read and conditions of release are addressed.
  • Pretrial. Reviewing the evidence, filing motions, and discussing possible resolutions with the prosecutor.
  • Trial. If the case is not resolved, the State must prove the charges to a judge or jury.
  • Sentencing and appeal. Advocacy continues after a verdict, including any appeal.

Courtroom experience

Randall Avery has practiced since 1978 and has tried both civil and criminal cases. If you or a family member has been arrested or expects to be charged, call (203) 202-2070 as early as possible.
