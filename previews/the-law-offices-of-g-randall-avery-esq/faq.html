Frequently asked questions

Answers about the firm, Randall Avery and how to get in touch.

Where is the office?

The office is at 30 Old Kings Highway South, in the First Floor Office Suites of Darien, Darien, Connecticut 06820. There is free parking.

Do you offer a free consultation?

Yes. Call (203) 202-2070 or email graveryesq@gmail.com to arrange a free consultation.

What kinds of cases does the firm handle?

All forms of civil litigation, personal injury law, family law, criminal law, conveyancing (real estate transactions), estates, wills and trusts, and general counsel work for businesses, including corporate formation.

How long has Randall Avery practiced law?

Since 1978. He received his J.D. from the University of Connecticut School of Law that year.

Which courts has he appeared in?

He has represented clients before all courts in Connecticut, including the Connecticut Supreme Court, and has tried both civil and criminal cases.

Does the firm handle appeals?

Yes. His appellate work includes DiNuzzo v. Dan Perkins Chevrolet Geo, Inc., before the Connecticut Appellate Court and Supreme Court, and Reid v. Landsberger, before the Connecticut Appellate Court.

Can you help with a house closing?

Yes. Real estate transactions and conveyancing are among the services the firm most commonly provides.

Can you help me form a company?

Yes. Corporate formation and ongoing general counsel work for businesses are part of the practice.

Do you prepare wills and trusts?

Yes. The firm prepares wills and trusts and handles estates.

Why is the office in Darien?

The practice was located in Stamford until 2009, when Randall Avery opened his present Darien office to better serve clients who find it easier to meet there.

How do I reach the office?

Call (203) 202-2070, fax (203) 202-2209, or email graveryesq@gmail.com.
