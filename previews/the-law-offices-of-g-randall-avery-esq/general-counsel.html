Area of practice

General counsel & corporate formation

Businesses need a lawyer they can call. The firm forms new companies and serves as general counsel to businesses.

Forming a business

Choosing the right structure, whether a limited liability company, a corporation or a partnership, affects liability, taxes and how decisions are made. Corporate formation includes preparing and filing the formation documents and putting in place the agreements that govern how owners work together.

Ongoing general counsel

Many businesses do not need a full-time lawyer but benefit from having one they know and trust. As general counsel, the firm can help with:

  • Contracts with customers, suppliers and landlords.
  • Commercial leases and real estate.
  • Questions that come up in day-to-day operations.
  • Disputes, and litigation when it cannot be avoided.

One attorney who knows your business

Randall Avery has represented individuals and businesses since 1978. Because he also handles real estate, estates and litigation, a business owner can bring related questions to one office. Call (203) 202-2070 to start the conversation.
