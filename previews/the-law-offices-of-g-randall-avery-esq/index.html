What changes

What this redesign fixes.

Two honest observations from the current site, and what this concept does about them.

  • A 2012 footer and template creditThe current site's footer still reads “© 2012 by Lawyer & Lawyer,” a template credit from the site builder. The concept carries the firm's own name and a current date.
  • No headline, thin home pageThe home page has no H1 heading and roughly 250 words. The concept leads with a clear headline, the practice list and the free-consultation offer, all on one page.
  • Structured data for a law firmThe site's only schema is a generic WebSite record. The concept adds LegalService, Person and FAQ markup with the full name, address and phone so search engines and AI assistants can read who the firm is.
  • Alt text and a small typoThree of nine images lack alt text, and the Contact page prints the fax number as “1-2-3-202-2209.” Every image here is described and the fax reads (203) 202-2209.
