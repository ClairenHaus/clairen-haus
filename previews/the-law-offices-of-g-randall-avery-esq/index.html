The firm

An attorney committed to meeting your needs, sensitive to your concerns.

Randall Avery’s general practice of law was located in Stamford until 2009, when he opened his present office in Darien to better serve clients who find it easier to meet there.

He is a longtime resident of Rowayton, where he has served his local community and the City of Norwalk in many volunteer capacities, including the Norwalk Common Council and the Board of Estimate and Taxation.
