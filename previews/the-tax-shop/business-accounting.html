Recordkeeping: what to keep, and for how long

Tax records should be kept all year, not hastily assembled for your annual tax appointment. Without them you can forget valuable deductions, or have items disallowed if you are audited.

As a general rule, the IRS can audit a return for up to three years after it is filed, and up to six years if substantial income was left off. It is a good idea to keep most records for six years after filing, and to keep a copy of each year's return permanently. Records of property you own, and of improvements to it, are worth keeping for years after the property is sold. We'll help you set up a system so the right papers are there when you need them.
