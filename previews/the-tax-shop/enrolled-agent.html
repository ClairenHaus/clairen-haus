Why an Enrolled Agent

Don't trust your taxes to just anyone

The reasons to choose an Enrolled Agent, and what it means when Dennis Futch handles your return.

An Enrolled Agent is a tax professional licensed by the federal government. Dennis W. Futch is one, and it shapes how he can help you, from preparing your return to standing in for you if the IRS has questions.

The reasons

  1. Enrolled Agents are licensed by the federal government.
  2. Enrolled Agents are the only tax professionals who receive their right to practice from the United States government.
  3. Enrolled Agents are authorized to appear in place of the taxpayer at the Internal Revenue Service.
  4. Only Enrolled Agents are required to demonstrate their competence in taxation to the IRS before they represent a taxpayer.
  5. Enrolled Agents pass a difficult examination administered by the IRS, covering the taxation of individuals, corporations, partnerships, estates and trusts, procedures and ethics.
  6. Enrolled Agents must complete 72 hours of continuing education to keep up with tax-law changes.
  7. Enrolled Agents are bound by Circular 230, which gives them limited client privilege when representing a taxpayer in audit and collection matters.
  8. Enrolled Agents pass a rigorous background check conducted by the IRS.
  9. Unlike CPAs and attorneys, who may or may not choose to specialize in taxes, all Enrolled Agents specialize in taxation.

What it means for you

If you get a letter, an audit notice or a call about your taxes, the person who prepared your return is the same person who can represent you. More about IRS letters and representation.
