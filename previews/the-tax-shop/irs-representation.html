IRS Letters, Audits & Representation

If any government agency contacts you about your taxes, your first call should be to us.

If you are contacted by any government agency concerning your tax matters, your first call should be to us. We will take care of it so that you can get on with your business. We file your returns and serve as your advocate in all tax matters, including assistance and representation on tax audits.

Why an Enrolled Agent can help

Enrolled Agents are licensed by the federal government and are authorized to appear in place of the taxpayer at the Internal Revenue Service. They must prove their competence in taxation to the IRS before they can represent anyone.

What to do when a letter arrives

  • Don't ignore it. Most IRS letters have a response date.
  • Don't pay or reply until you understand what it is asking.
  • Call us and bring the letter. We'll explain it and handle the next step with you.
