Tax Planning

It is our job to see that you pay no more tax than the law requires. We put tax planning in plain English.

It is our job to see that you pay no more tax than the law requires. We monitor the changes in the tax law that affect you and recommend tax-saving strategies.

There is a certain mystique around tax planning. We are here to put it in plain English. Most plans use one or a combination of a few basic strategies:

  • Splitting income among family members or legal entities so more of it is taxed in lower brackets
  • Shifting income from one year to another so it falls where it is taxed at a lower rate
  • Shifting deductions to the year where the tax benefit is greater
  • Deferring tax through certain investment choices and retirement plan contributions
  • Structuring your affairs to get a deduction for some expenses tied to things you enjoy, a vacation home for example

Which of these fits you depends on your income, your family and your goals. The place to start is a conversation, and your first one with us is free.
