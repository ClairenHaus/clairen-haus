Tax Preparation & E-Filing

Federal and state returns of every size, prepared by an Enrolled Agent and filed electronically for free.

The Tax Shop and Mr. Futch specialize in income tax preparation. Every return we prepare is filed electronically for free, and if you need a Georgia return, it is filed electronically with your federal return at no extra charge. Our motto is simple: no return is too simple or complex.

What we prepare

  • Federal and state electronic filing, with returns prepared for any state
  • Income, gift and estate tax returns
  • Payroll, sales and other business tax returns
  • Returns for individuals, corporations, LLCs, partnerships, estates, trusts and fiduciaries
  • Computerized preparation, and we keep your records permanently for your convenience
  • Our fees can be taken from your refund, so returns with a refund need no cash up front

Why e-file

Electronic filing is faster and more accurate than paper. You get an acknowledgement that your return was received, which is proof you filed on time, and if anything is rejected we can correct it and resend it quickly.

ITIN applications (Form W-7)

The Tax Shop is an IRS-authorized ITIN Acceptance Agent, so we can help you apply for an Individual Taxpayer Identification Number with Form W-7.

Already filed?

Clients can check the status of an electronically filed return online, and documents can be shared through our secure client portal. Call us if you need help with either.
