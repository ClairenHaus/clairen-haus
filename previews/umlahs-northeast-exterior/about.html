Meet James & Alice Umlah

A family owned and operated business in Stewartstown, NH.

From the owner

My name is James Umlah, I am the owner of Umlah’s Northeast Exterior. My wife (Alice) and I moved to the North Country from the lakes region in 2008.

Being a family owned and operated business allows us to offer excellent quality at an affordable price. We will not encourage work that is unnecessary and we always work with our customers to find the best solution to a problem, all while being earth friendly and wallet friendly.

Throughout our years of business in the North Country we have serviced many local customers and non-local customers, saving them money and keeping them satisfied. We provide services throughout NH, VT, and ME.

I encourage you to look at our references and give them a call.
