FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about Umlah’s Northeast Exterior, our work and our service area.

Do you charge for quotes?

No. We give free detailed quotes, with no obligation.

What work do you do?

Our specialties include roofing, siding, windows, decks and framing, frame to finish. On Facebook we also list insulation and sill plates.

Where are you located?

We’re a family owned and operated business located in Stewartstown, NH. Our mailing address is 20 Circle Drive, Pittsburg, NH 03592.

What areas do you serve?

We service all of NH, Vermont, Maine, and Mass.

Who owns Umlah’s Northeast Exterior?

James Umlah is the owner. He and his wife, Alice, moved to the North Country from the Lakes Region in 2008.

Are you insured?

Yes. We are fully insured.

What guarantees do you offer?

A 3 year workmanship guarantee, guaranteed customer satisfaction, a guaranteed clean work site and the manufacturing warranty on products. These guarantees are for all projects and are included in the contract.

Will you try to sell me work I don’t need?

No. We will not encourage work that is unnecessary, and we always work with our customers to find the best solution to a problem, all while being earth friendly and wallet friendly.

What types of buildings do you work on?

Our quote request covers primary houses, vacation houses, mobile homes and commercial buildings.

What siding styles can I choose from?

We show CertainTeed vinyl siding in beaded, perfection, half rounds, insulated, Dutch lap, rough split, board & batten and clapboard profiles.

Can I talk to past customers?

Yes. Our references page lists local customers, a contractor and community organizations who are happy to tell you about their experience.

How do I reach you?

Call the home line at (603) 246-7160 or James’s cell at (603) 387-4706, email northeastexterior@gmail.com, or message us on Facebook.
