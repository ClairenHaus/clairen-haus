Roofing for North Country homes

Architectural shingles, metal roofing and hand-crafted copper work, from Stewartstown, NH.

Roofing we’ve done

Roofing is one of our specialties. The projects on our site include architectural shingle roofs, metal roofs and a copper roof we hand crafted ourselves.

A new roof is a great way to increase the value of your home.

Choosing a roof, in plain terms

Architectural shingles are thicker, layered asphalt shingles that give a roof more depth than flat three-tab shingles. Metal roofing comes in panels and sheds snow well. Copper is a specialty metal often used on bays, porches and accents. The right choice depends on your home, your budget and how long you plan to stay, which is why every job starts with a free detailed quote.
