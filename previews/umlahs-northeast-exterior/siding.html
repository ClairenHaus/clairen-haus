Vinyl siding, in plain terms

Vinyl siding is low-maintenance and comes in many profiles and colors. Profiles like Dutch lap and clapboard give a traditional look, board & batten gives a farmhouse look, and insulated siding adds a layer of foam behind the panel. We’ll help you pick the style that suits your home and your budget.

Old homes, done with care

On an old farm house restoration we used rough sawn pine boards to do some much needed repairs without taking away from the vintage feel of the historic home.
