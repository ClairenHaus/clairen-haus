About us

About Valley View Electric

Started in 1975 by the DeCramers. Owned by Mike & Jenny Siewert since January 2012.

Since 1975

Valley View Electric was started in 1975 by the DeCramers. For over 35 years they served the area with high quality electrical contracting service.

Mike & Jenny Siewert, owners

In January 2012, Mike & Jenny Siewert took over as new owners, and they strive to maintain the high quality of service that Valley View is known for.

You can reach Mike at mike@valleyviewelecmn.com and Jenny at jenny@valleyviewelecmn.com, or call the office at (507) 233-9457.

What we do

Valley View Electric, Inc. is a full service electrical contractor with an experienced staff specializing in industrial, commercial, agricultural and residential electrical needs. We are also a distributor and certified installer of AGI SureTrack CMC hazard monitoring and grain temperature systems.

  • Licensed, bonded and insured
  • Industrial, commercial, agricultural and residential
  • Electrical design and wiring, troubleshooting and repairs
  • Bucket truck with a 42′ working height

How we treat our customers

At Valley View Electric, Inc., we take care to provide our customers high quality services personalized for their unique needs. Our staff members are professional, courteous and efficient, and our attentive staff is available Monday through Friday to answer your questions and ensure you are 100% satisfied.
