A 42′ working height

We have a bucket truck capable of reaching a working height of 42′ for installation of new outdoor lights, electrical wiring or repairs of your existing outdoor lights, signs, electrical wiring, etc.

  • New outdoor lights
  • Repairs to existing outdoor lights
  • Sign lighting and sign repairs
  • Overhead electrical wiring and repairs

Lights and signs up high

Lights and signs mounted high up are hard to reach safely with a ladder. With our own bucket truck, we can install new outdoor lighting and repair what you already have, from outdoor lights to signs.
