FAQ

Questions & answers

Hours, services, and how to reach us.

Where is Valley View Electric located?

Our address is 18205 State Hwy. 15, Searles, MN 56073, in the New Ulm area. Call (507) 233-9457.

What are your office hours?

Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 4:30 PM. We are closed Saturday and Sunday.

Can I call outside office hours?

Yes. We are available to take your call 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. If no one answers your call, leave a message on our automated line and we will return your call as soon as possible.

What kinds of electrical work do you do?

We are a full service electrical contractor specializing in industrial, commercial, agricultural and residential electrical needs: electrical design and wiring, troubleshooting, bucket truck work, and hazard monitoring and grain temperature systems.

Are you licensed, bonded and insured?

Yes. Valley View Electric, Inc. is licensed, bonded and insured.

Can you help me plan a new electrical project?

Yes. Our experienced staff will work with you in planning and designing your electrical project. Your project is our top priority.

Something electrical isn’t working. What should I do?

Don’t guess what might be wrong. Call us and we will send one of our trained professionals to you to find the problem so it can be fixed safely.

How high can your bucket truck reach?

Our bucket truck reaches a working height of 42 feet, for installing new outdoor lights and electrical wiring and repairing existing outdoor lights, signs and wiring.

Do you install hazard monitoring or grain temperature systems?

Yes. We are a distributor and certified installer of AGI SureTrack CMC hazard monitoring and grain temperature systems.

How long has Valley View Electric been in business?

Valley View Electric was started in 1975 by the DeCramers. Mike & Jenny Siewert took over as owners in January 2012.

Who owns Valley View Electric?

Mike & Jenny Siewert, who took over as owners in January 2012.

How do I get a quote?

Call (507) 233-9457, email mike@valleyviewelecmn.com or jenny@valleyviewelecmn.com, or send a request through our contact page.
