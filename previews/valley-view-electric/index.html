About Valley View Electric

Started in 1975. Owned by Mike & Jenny Siewert since 2012.

Valley View Electric was started in 1975 by the DeCramers, who served the area with high quality electrical contracting service for over 35 years.

In January 2012, Mike & Jenny Siewert took over as owners, and they strive to maintain the high quality of service that Valley View is known for.
