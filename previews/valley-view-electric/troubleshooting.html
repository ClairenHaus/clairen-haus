Troubleshooting & repairs

Don’t guess. Call us.

When something isn’t working correctly, don’t guess what might be wrong. Call us and we will send one of our trained professionals to you to find the problem so it can be fixed safely.

Electrical problems aren’t always where they look. A breaker that keeps tripping, lights that flicker, an outlet or a piece of equipment that has stopped working: finding the real cause takes the right training and the right tools, and working on live electrical systems without them isn’t safe.
