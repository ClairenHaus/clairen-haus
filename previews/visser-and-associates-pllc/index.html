What changes

Two honest observations from the current visserlegal.com, and what this concept does about them.

Entity markup
The current schema describes the site only as a generic web page and never as a law firm or attorneys.

This concept carries LegalService markup with the full address and service area, and a Person entry for each of the three attorneys.

Answers

A short FAQ answered from the firm's own pages, marked up as FAQPage.

Structure

Visser and Associates had its origins in 1977 when Donald Visser returned from law school with the idea of providing the high-quality, cutting-edge services one might expect from a large firm, with the personal touch of a small one.

The attorneys have a long-standing commitment to the West Michigan area, its courts and its people and businesses. Often that means taking on clients who know they are in the right but have been discouraged by others about the difficulty of their case; other attorneys regularly refer the firm matters they consider too difficult to undertake.

The firm's legal team has handled multiple precedent-setting Michigan cases, extensive business law and operations work, and complex litigation and dispute resolution, delivered with the hands-on approach that clients, often the firm's West Michigan neighbours, have long associated with the Visser name.

Common questions

Questions people ask before they call.

Answered only from what the current site says. General information, not legal advice.

Where is Visser and Associates located?

2480 44th St SE, Suite 150, Kentwood, Michigan 49512, a few miles west of the Gerald R. Ford International Airport, with free parking.

What areas does the firm serve?

The firm concentrates its legal services in Western Michigan's Kent and Ottawa counties and handles many matters throughout the state.

Does the firm offer mediation?

Yes. The firm encourages alternative dispute resolution and offers confidential mediation services. Don Visser has been a certified mediator since 2003 and is a designated mediator for several Michigan circuit courts; the Kentwood offices have private conference rooms for mediation.

Does the firm handle appeals?

Yes. The site says the firm has been successful in appeals before the Michigan Court of Appeals and the Michigan Supreme Court, and Don Visser is a member of the Michigan Supreme Court's Advocates Guild.

Does the firm help with estate planning and probate?

Yes. The Individuals & Families practice covers simple and complex wills, trusts and other estate planning, and assists when probate becomes necessary.
