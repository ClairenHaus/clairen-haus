What changes

Specific fixes, drawn from the current site.

  • A site that works on a phone

    The current site is a 2000s table layout with no mobile viewport, font tags and jQuery 1.7.2, so phones get a shrunken desktop page. The concept is mobile-first.

  • Every attorney gets a working page

    M. Frederick Simpler, Jr.’s bio link on the Attorneys page returns a “404 – Page Not Found” error. The concept gives every lawyer on the roster a working profile.

  • One clear way to reach the firm

    The contact details are split: the Contact page lists (205) 254-3216, attorney emails use @wskllp.com while one uses @wskllc.com, and most addresses are hidden behind JavaScript. The concept puts one main number and tap-to-email links on every profile.

  • Schema that says “law firm”

    There is no structured data on the site. The concept adds LegalService, Person markup for each attorney and FAQPage markup, so search engines and AI assistants can cite the firm accurately.

About the practice

Insight, expertise, responsiveness.

Recognized by trial and litigation honoraries, Super Lawyers, Martindale-Hubbell and the Birmingham Business Journal, as the firm’s site states

Outstanding client service, in the firm’s words, requires insight, expertise, responsiveness, proactivity and integrity. The firm strives to understand each client’s business and goals, and assembles the right team to tackle each legal challenge.

The firm has represented a wide range of businesses, governments and individuals in all types of litigation, including personal injury, and its public sector attorneys are active statewide.

In the community, the firm sponsors children each year through the Gateways Christmas Angel Program, supports Miles College, the Birmingham Civil Rights Institute and the Salvation Army’s H2O Foundation, and hosts events such as Birmingham’s Art Walk at its offices.
