Common questions
Where can I close with Walther Law?

At the main office, 5755 North Point Parkway, Suite 202, Alpharetta, GA 30022, or the closing facilities at 1755 North Brown Road, Suite 200, Lawrenceville, GA 30043. The firm also closes in the area of Perimeter Mall, the Galleria, Peachtree Corners and Decatur, and says it can likely accommodate a mobile closing if you ask.

Where do I send a title order or a loan package?

Title orders and requests go to kimberly@pkwlaw.com or by fax to (770) 777-4780. Loan packages go to packages@pkwlaw.com.

Why do I need a Will in Georgia?

The firm explains that even with few assets a Will lets you choose a guardian for minor children, can leave your entire estate to your spouse, lets a trustee manage assets for children or people with special needs, and lets your executor manage property without court permission. It also notes that probate in Georgia is a simplified, relatively inexpensive process.

Does the firm represent debtors in bankruptcy?

No. Walther Law assists creditors with Proofs of Claim and proceedings for Relief from the Automatic Stay, and does no work for debtors.

Can Walther Law mediate a dispute?

Yes. Kevin Walther is a certified civil mediator and can serve in court-ordered proceedings or those the parties simply agree to.

About the practice

Practicing in Metro Atlanta since 1979.

  • P. Kevin Walther: Brown University, 1974; law degree cum laude, Indiana University at Bloomington, 1978
  • State Bar of Georgia since 1979; admitted to the Georgia Supreme Court, Georgia Court of Appeals, 11th Circuit U.S. Court of Appeals, U.S. District Courts for the Northern and Middle Districts of Georgia and the U.S. Tax Court
  • Real Property Law and Business Law Sections of the Georgia Bar; North Fulton County and Gwinnett County Bar Associations
  • Certified civil mediator

For the first dozen years of his practice, Kevin Walther handled a broad range of matters, including business civil litigation, business entities, securities, tax, bankruptcy, wills, trusts, estates and divorce. The firm says that breadth lets him spot issues early and solve them quickly at the closing table.

In 1991 Kevin and Bob Blackburn, with four staff, began the real estate law firm last known as Blackburn, Walther Sloan & Adair, which grew to four offices handling over 5,000 closings a year. He left in 2008 to establish the practice that became Walther Law, LLC.
