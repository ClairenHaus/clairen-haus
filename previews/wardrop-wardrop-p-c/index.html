About the practice

The information clients need before they call.

Thomas (Mac) Wardrop · University of Detroit Mercy School of Law, J.D. cum laude, 1985

Wardrop & Wardrop, P.C. is a family-owned law firm founded by Robb Wardrop. Mac Wardrop joined in 1992, and in 2005 Denise Twinney brought her bankruptcy expertise to the firm. Both Robb and Mac grew up in Mount Pleasant, Michigan, attended Central Michigan University and received their law degrees from the University of Detroit Mercy School of Law; Denise attended the University of Michigan and received her law degree from the Detroit College of Law.

The firm’s trial lawyers have represented clients in disputes ranging from simple contract actions to multi-party, high-dollar injury and business claims. On the bankruptcy side it handles Chapter 7 liquidations and Chapter 11, 12 and 13 reorganizations, and its creditors’ rights practice has represented individual creditors to Fortune 500 companies.

Robb, Mac and Denise have all obtained the highest rating for legal ability and ethical standards from the Martindale-Hubbell rating service, and the firm is rated as a “Best Law Firm” by U.S. News and World Report.

Common questions

Answers, drawn from the firm's own site.

  • What kinds of matters does Wardrop & Wardrop handle?

    The firm lists litigation, bankruptcy, business law, personal injury and creditors’ rights, serving both individuals and businesses.

  • Which bankruptcy chapters does the firm work with?

    Its site describes help with the two basic types of proceedings: Chapter 7, which involves liquidation and a trustee, and Chapters 11, 12 and 13, which involve rehabilitation so the debtor can use future earnings to pay creditors.

  • Does the firm offer mediation or arbitration?

    Mac Wardrop is a neutral arbitrator with the American Arbitration Association, a trained facilitative mediator, and sits on the case evaluation panels for Kent, Barry and Ottawa counties.
