Individual Tax Return Preparation

We prefer to take a proactive vs. reactive approach to tax services.

More than filling in the forms

By keeping current on new tax laws and legislation, we are in a position to identify key tax planning opportunities that minimize both your current and future tax liabilities. We provide our individual clients with the taxation expertise and knowledge they deserve throughout the year.

Individual tax services include

  • Tax planning and return preparation
  • Representation before taxing authorities
  • Divorce and support issues
  • Tax effects of buying or selling a business
