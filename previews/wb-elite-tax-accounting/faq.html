FAQ

Questions we hear most

Appointments, drop-off, hours, fees and Enrolled Agents.

Do you take walk-ins?

No. We work from appointments only and do not accept walk-ins wanting taxes done right then. Call to set up an appointment, or drop off your documents.

How do I get my documents to you?

Drop them off at the office or upload them through our secure portal. We also have a slot in our door for after-hours drop-off; make sure your papers are contained and your contact information is on whatever you leave.

Do I have to sit down with my preparer?

No. It is more efficient to drop off your documents. Your tax preparer will call you to discuss your return, then set up a way for you to review and sign it. If you would like to sit down with your preparer, just ask.

How much will my tax return cost?

We do not give estimates by phone or email. The cost depends on the complexity of your return and the forms and schedules that need to be completed. Bring in your forms and a tax preparer will review them and give you an estimate.

What are your hours?

Outside tax season: Monday to Thursday 10:00am to 4:00pm, and Friday to Sunday by appointment only. During tax season we work longer hours by appointment, and we can accommodate most schedules. Check our Facebook page for the most up-to-date hours.

Which holidays are you closed?

New Year’s Day, the day after Tax Day, Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day, Election Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas.

What is an Enrolled Agent?

A tax professional who has earned the privilege of representing taxpayers before the IRS, either by passing a three-part IRS exam or through experience as a former IRS employee. Three of our preparers are Enrolled Agents. More about EAs.
