How we work

Drop off. We call. You sign.

We work from appointments only. It is more efficient if you drop off your documents or upload them through our secure portal. There’s a slot in our door for after-hours drop-off.

The details

  1. 01
    Drop off or uploadBring your documents in, use the after-hours slot, or upload through the secure portal.
  2. 02
    Fill in our papersEmailed to you, or completed when you drop off.
  3. 03
    Your preparer callsTo talk through your return with you.
  4. 04
    Review and signWe set up a way for you to review and sign once it’s done.
