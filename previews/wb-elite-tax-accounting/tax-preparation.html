Our services

Tax Preparation

Our team knows the latest tax laws and makes sure your tax return is done properly. We find the errors that others make.

How it works

  • Drop off your documents at the office, or upload them through our secure portal.
  • Complete the papers we give you, emailed to you or filled in when you drop off.
  • Your tax preparer calls you to talk it through.
  • When your return is done, we set up a way for you to review and sign it.

What it costs

We don’t give estimates by phone or email. The cost depends on the complexity of your return and the forms and schedules it needs. Bring in your forms and a tax preparer will review them and give you an estimate. Value does not always mean the lowest price.

Prefer to sit down with your preparer?

Just ask. We don’t schedule sit-down appointments as a rule because drop-off is more efficient, but we will if you request it.
