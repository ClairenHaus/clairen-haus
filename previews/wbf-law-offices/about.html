About the firm

A Deerfield practice, opened in 2024.

WBF Law Offices is the estate planning and tax practice of William Bradley Fox.

The firm

William Bradley Fox opened WBF Law Offices (WBF Law Office, LLC) in Deerfield, Illinois in 2024. Before that, from 2002 to 2024, he was a tax partner at Lyon & Caron LLP, also in Deerfield.

The practice is focused: estate planning and estate administration, and the tax planning that goes with them, from estate tax analysis and gift tax planning to grantor-type gift trusts, irrevocable life insurance trusts, GRATs and deferred compensation, including Section 457 plans.
