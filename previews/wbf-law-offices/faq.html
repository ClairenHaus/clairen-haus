Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Straight answers about the firm, the attorney and how to get started.

What does WBF Law Offices handle?

Estate planning, estate administration, estate tax analysis, gift tax planning, “grantor” type gift trusts, irrevocable life insurance trusts, grantor retained annuity trusts (GRATs), and deferred compensation, including Section 457 plans. Each has its own page under Practice Areas.

Who is the attorney?

William Bradley Fox. He holds a J.D. from Duke University School of Law (1991) and an LL.M. in Taxation from New York University School of Law (1995), and he founded WBF Law Offices in 2024.

What is his background in tax law?

Beyond his LL.M. in Taxation from NYU, Mr. Fox was a tax partner at Lyon & Caron LLP in Deerfield from 2002 to 2024. He has written for the Illinois Bar Journal and Tax Management Inc. on tax topics.

When was the firm founded?

William Bradley Fox opened WBF Law Offices (WBF Law Office, LLC) in Deerfield, Illinois in 2024.

Where is the office?

790 Estate Drive, Suite 200, Deerfield, IL 60015. Get directions.

How do I make an appointment?

Email wfox@wbflawllc.com or call (224) 241-7063 to make an appointment.

How quickly will I hear back?

The office’s general response time is one business day.

What should I include when I first get in touch?

It helps to describe the issue you’re having first, and then say what you want to achieve. That lets the office understand your situation before discussing a solution.

Does the firm help executors and trustees, or only with planning?

Both. Estate administration is part of the practice, alongside estate planning.

Can the firm help with trusts such as GRATs and life insurance trusts?

Yes. Grantor retained annuity trusts, irrevocable life insurance trusts and “grantor” type gift trusts are all listed in the practice.

Does the firm work with deferred compensation and Section 457 plans?

Yes. Deferred compensation, including Section 457 plans, is one of the firm’s listed practice areas, and Mr. Fox contributed to a 1997 supplement on employee benefits law for the Illinois Institute for Continuing Legal Education.

Has Mr. Fox published on these subjects?

Yes: “Business Buyers’ and Sellers’ Guide to Reducing Taxes on Goodwill and Covenants Not to Compete” (Illinois Bar Journal, January 2005), “The State of State Trust Taxation: Pitfalls and Planning Possibilities” (Tax Management Memorandum, 1999), and the Employee Benefits Law 1997 Supplement (Illinois Institute for Continuing Legal Education).
