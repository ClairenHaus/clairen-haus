The attorney

One attorney, with a tax lawyer’s training.

Mr. Fox studied history at the University of Michigan, where he was elected to Phi Beta Kappa, earned his J.D. at Duke University School of Law in 1991 and his LL.M. in Taxation at New York University School of Law in 1995.

He spent 2002 to 2024 as a tax partner at Lyon & Caron LLP in Deerfield, and opened WBF Law Offices in the same town in 2024. He has written for the Illinois Bar Journal and Tax Management Inc., and contributed to an Illinois Institute for Continuing Legal Education volume on employee benefits law.
