What changes

Specific fixes this concept makes to the current site.

Each point below comes from a check of the firm’s live site, not from a template.

  • 01The current Squarespace one-pager describes itself to search engines only as a generic “WebSite,” with no LegalService, Attorney or LocalBusiness markup. This page adds LegalService, Person and FAQ structured data built from the firm’s own facts.

Common questions
What does WBF Law Offices do?

Estate planning and administration, with a tax focus: estate tax analysis, gift tax planning, grantor-type gift trusts, irrevocable life insurance trusts, grantor retained annuity trusts (GRATs) and deferred compensation or Section 457 plans.

Who is the attorney?

William Bradley Fox. He earned a B.A. in History from the University of Michigan (1988, Phi Beta Kappa), a J.D. from Duke University School of Law (1991) and an LL.M. in Taxation from New York University School of Law (1995), and was admitted to the Illinois bar in 1992.

How quickly does the office respond?

The current site says the general response time is one business day, and recommends describing the issue first and then the goal when you write.
