How it fits together

Three stages, one practice.

Most clients come in at one of three points. The practice is built so the same attorney can help at each of them.

  • PlanningEstate planning: wills, revocable trusts, powers of attorney and beneficiary designations that work together.
  • TaxEstate tax analysis and gift tax planning, using grantor-type gift trusts, life insurance trusts and GRATs where they fit, plus deferred compensation and Section 457 plans.
  • AfterwardEstate administration, for executors and trustees carrying out a plan.
