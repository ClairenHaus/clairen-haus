William Bradley Fox is an estate planning and tax attorney in Deerfield, Illinois. After 2002 to 2024 as a tax partner at Lyon & Caron LLP, he opened WBF Law Offices in 2024.

He earned his J.D. at Duke University School of Law in 1991 and an LL.M. in Taxation at New York University School of Law in 1995. As an undergraduate at the University of Michigan he studied history, was elected to Phi Beta Kappa and received the Angel Scholar Award and the William J. Branstrom Award.

His practice covers estate planning and estate administration, estate tax analysis, gift tax planning, “grantor” type gift trusts, irrevocable life insurance trusts, grantor retained annuity trusts (GRATs), and deferred compensation, including Section 457 plans.
