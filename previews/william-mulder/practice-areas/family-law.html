Family Law

Whether you are anticipating the joys of adoption or working through marital conflict, I have served families in the Saddle Brook area for over 20 years across every area of family law.

Family matters I handle

  • Divorce

    Through mediation or litigation, I work toward an equitable result: fair distribution of assets, the marital home, child support and custody.

  • Child custody and visitation

    Protecting your parental rights to raise your children in an appropriate and supportive home.

  • Child support

    Ensuring the amount you pay or receive is fair and supports a healthy, happy environment for your children.

  • Support modification and enforcement

    Support orders change only when the court modifies them. When circumstances change significantly, I guide you through the proper procedure.

  • Alimony

    Divorce should not become a financial crisis. I help establish an equitable amount of spousal support.

  • Adoption

    Domestic and international adoptions, with the legal work handled so you can concentrate on your new family.

  • Pre- and post-nuptial agreements

    Agreements that protect both spouses' interests and reduce stress if the marriage ends.

  • Mediation

    Mediation keeps the decisions in your hands and often produces faster, more predictable results than trial.

  • Juvenile proceedings

    Protecting parental rights in juvenile delinquency and dependency proceedings.

Common questions

Do I have to go to court to get divorced?
Not always. The firm handles divorce through mediation as well as litigation. Mediation keeps decisions in your hands and can be faster and more predictable than a trial.
Can child support be changed after the divorce?
Only by the court. If either parent has a significant change in circumstances, the firm can guide you through the procedure to modify the support order.
Does the firm handle adoptions?
Yes, both domestic and international adoptions.
