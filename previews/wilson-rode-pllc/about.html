Who we are

About Wilson Rode

A general practice law firm in Troy, Michigan, built on personal attention to each client’s objectives.

Wilson Rode, PLLC is a general practice law firm located in Troy, Michigan, that represents individuals and companies throughout the State of Michigan.

Our firm was formed in October 2007, but our attorneys previously practiced in other larger firms in the Detroit metropolitan area since 1980.

We are committed to providing the highest quality legal services to our clients, with the resources and knowledge needed to solve their legal issues and the expertise to handle everything from the most personal legal matters to more sophisticated business transactions and litigation matters.

Personal attention

At Wilson Rode, we recognize the importance of each client’s need for personal attention, and we strive to provide effective legal services with the utmost attention to our clients’ unique concerns. We never lose sight of the fact that we represent clients with their own objectives and ambitions.

We make the extra effort to understand our clients, their personal cares and concerns, and the personal and business issues that affect them on a daily basis. This focus enables us to deliver creative and innovative legal strategies that help our clients achieve their goals.

Cost-effective by design

We understand that legal fees can be a significant burden. We are committed to providing the highest quality legal services in an expeditious and cost-effective way. Our clients receive the benefit of our knowledge and experience without the costs typically associated with larger firms.

Our billing rates compare favorably with those of other firms of commensurate caliber and experience. We also perform work using alternative billing methods, including fixed-fee pricing, which help clients more accurately project their legal expenditures.
