Practice area

Business & Tax

A diverse corporate and business law practice for individuals, private and publicly held corporations, partnerships, LLCs, tax-exempt organizations, charities and foundations.

Business law covers the legal side of starting, running, financing, growing and eventually passing on or selling a company: how it is organized, the agreements it signs, the people who own it, and the taxes it owes.

Day-to-day business counsel

Our attorneys assist clients in all aspects of their day-to-day business that require legal attention.

  • Corporate governance and structure
  • Negotiating loan documents
  • Employment agreements
  • Shareholder agreements and shareholder disputes
  • Contracts with customers, suppliers and distributors
  • Confidentiality agreements
  • Communicating with federal and state regulatory authorities

Major business transactions

We also counsel clients who are conducting major business transactions.

  • Formation and capitalization of start-up companies
  • Venture capital activities
  • Mergers and acquisitions
  • Debt and equity financings for lenders and businesses
  • Restructurings, workouts and recapitalizations
  • Succession planning for closely held businesses

Tax matters

Our attorneys advise individuals and businesses on federal, state and local tax matters.

Legal advice with a business view

Today's business world is more complex than ever. Responding to a client's problem involves not only resolving the legal issue, but developing business strategies that meet the needs of an ever-changing business environment. Consistent with the firm's mission, these services are provided in an expeditious and cost-effective way.
