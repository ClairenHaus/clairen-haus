Estate Planning & Probate

Comprehensive estate planning and probate counsel, including litigation services, and advice on the design, drafting, administration and accounting of trusts and estates.

Estate planning is deciding, in advance, who will receive what you own and who will make decisions for you if you cannot. Probate is the court-supervised process of settling an estate after someone dies.

Planning for you and your family

Services range from the development of a basic estate plan to designing sophisticated arrangements for the protection of assets and the transfer of wealth. We advise clients on, and help them implement, strategies to minimize the income, estate, gift and generation-skipping transfer taxes associated with lifetime and testamentary transfers of assets.

  • Basic and advanced estate plans
  • Asset protection and wealth transfer
  • Medical and financial powers of attorney
  • Patient advocate designations
  • Charitable planning
  • Special needs and Medicaid planning

When a family needs help

Our attorneys provide personal and compassionate assistance to families dealing with the often devastating personal and legal effects of disability and death.

  • Guardianships
  • Conservatorships
  • Estate administration
  • Probate
  • Trust administration
  • Probate proceedings in court

Succession planning for business owners

Wilson Rode also provides integrated succession planning to corporate and closely held business clients. Where appropriate, we use a team approach, involving the client's accountant, financial advisor, insurance agent and other professional advisors in a concerted effort to achieve the client's planning objectives.
