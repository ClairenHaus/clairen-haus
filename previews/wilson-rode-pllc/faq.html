FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers drawn from the firm’s own information about its practice, office and fees.

Where is Wilson Rode located?

The office is at 2250 Butterfield Drive, Suite 220, Troy, MI 48084. The firm represents individuals and companies throughout the State of Michigan.

How do I contact the firm?

Call (248) 816-1000, email info@wilsonrode.com, or send a fax to (248) 816-1001. Each attorney also has a direct extension on the main line: L. James Wilson ext. 2, John J. Wilson ext. 4, and Patrick K. Rode ext. 5.

What kinds of legal matters does Wilson Rode handle?

Wilson Rode is a general practice law firm. Its four practice areas are business and tax, estate planning and probate, civil litigation, and real estate.

Who does the firm represent?

Individuals and companies: private and publicly held corporations, partnerships and limited liability companies, tax-exempt organizations, charities and foundations, as well as builders, developers, contractors, landlords, tenants, borrowers and lenders.

How long has the firm been practicing?

Wilson Rode was formed in October 2007. Its attorneys previously practiced in other larger firms in the Detroit metropolitan area, beginning in 1980.

How does the firm bill for its work?

The firm is committed to cost-effective service, and says its billing rates compare favorably with those of other firms of commensurate caliber and experience. It also works under alternative billing methods, including fixed-fee pricing, to help clients project their legal costs. In litigation, hourly, fixed-fee, contingent-fee or combined arrangements are discussed in advance to find what best suits the case and the client.

Does Wilson Rode handle estate planning and probate?

Yes. Services range from a basic estate plan to sophisticated asset-protection and wealth-transfer arrangements, along with medical and financial powers of attorney, patient advocate designations, charitable planning, special needs and Medicaid planning, guardianships, conservatorships, and estate, probate and trust administration.

Will the firm work with my accountant or financial advisor?

Where appropriate, yes. For estate and succession planning the firm uses a team approach that can involve your accountant, financial advisor, insurance agent and other professional advisors.

Will Wilson Rode take a case to trial?

The firm makes every appropriate effort to resolve disputes at the earliest possible stage, with the least expense and risk. If a case must be tried, it has the trial skills and experience to pursue it in state and federal courts throughout Michigan, and in arbitration and select federal courts throughout the country.

Can the firm help a contractor or subcontractor get paid?

Yes. Wilson Rode represents contractors and subcontractors seeking payment for their work through construction liens, bond claims and subsequent litigation.
