Common questions
Where is Wilson Rode located?

The office is at 2250 Butterfield Drive, Suite 220, Troy, Michigan 48084. The main line is (248) 816-1000, and the firm represents clients throughout Michigan.

How does the firm bill for its work?

The firm says it is committed to cost-effective service and also works on alternative billing methods, including fixed-fee pricing. For litigation it discusses hourly, fixed-fee, contingent-fee or combined arrangements with the client in advance.

Does Wilson Rode handle estate planning and probate?

Yes. The firm provides estate planning and probate counsel, from basic estate plans to sophisticated asset-protection and wealth-transfer arrangements, plus powers of attorney, patient advocate designations, guardianships, conservatorships and trust administration.

Will the firm try a case if it has to?

The firm says it makes every appropriate effort to resolve disputes early and at the least expense and risk, but if a case must be tried, it has the trial skills and experience to pursue it in state and federal courts throughout Michigan.

About the practice

The information clients need before they call.

  • L. James Wilson and Patrick K. Rode each hold an “AV” rating from Martindale-Hubbell, as stated on the firm’s site
  • All three attorneys earned their J.D. at the University of Michigan Law School
  • Admissions include the U.S. District Courts for the Eastern and Western Districts of Michigan, the Sixth and Federal Circuits, the U.S. Court of Federal Claims and the U.S. Tax Court

Wilson Rode was formed in October 2007 by attorneys who had practiced in larger Detroit-area firms since 1980. The firm’s stated aim is to handle everything from the most personal legal matters to sophisticated business transactions and litigation, with personal attention to each client’s objectives.

Litigation is led by Patrick Rode. Real estate work runs from land acquisition through construction financing, lease-up and permanent financing, and includes contractors and subcontractors seeking payment through construction liens and bond claims. Estate planning is coordinated with each client’s accountant, financial advisor and insurance agent where appropriate.
