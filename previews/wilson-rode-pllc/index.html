Our approach

Larger-firm experience, without larger-firm costs.

Wilson Rode’s attorneys practiced in larger firms in the Detroit metropolitan area beginning in 1980 before forming the firm in 2007. Clients receive the benefit of that knowledge and experience without the costs typically associated with larger firms.

The firm says its billing rates compare favorably with those of other firms of commensurate caliber and experience, and it also works under alternative billing methods, including fixed-fee pricing, so clients can more accurately project their legal costs.

Common questions

Before you call.

Where is Wilson Rode located?

The office is at 2250 Butterfield Drive, Suite 220, Troy, MI 48084. The firm represents individuals and companies throughout the State of Michigan.

How does the firm bill for its work?

The firm is committed to cost-effective service, and says its billing rates compare favorably with those of other firms of commensurate caliber and experience. It also works under alternative billing methods, including fixed-fee pricing, to help clients project their legal costs. In litigation, hourly, fixed-fee, contingent-fee or combined arrangements are discussed in advance to find what best suits the case and the client.

Will Wilson Rode take a case to trial?

The firm makes every appropriate effort to resolve disputes at the earliest possible stage, with the least expense and risk. If a case must be tried, it has the trial skills and experience to pursue it in state and federal courts throughout Michigan, and in arbitration and select federal courts throughout the country.
