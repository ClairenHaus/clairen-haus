Working together

One firm across the life of a client and a business.

Because the same attorneys handle business, estate, real estate and litigation work, matters that cross those lines (a closely held business’s succession plan, a commercial lease dispute, a probate matter in court) stay with one firm. For planning work, the firm can coordinate with your accountant, financial advisor, insurance agent and other advisors where appropriate.

Where we practice

Throughout Michigan.

The firm is based in Troy and represents clients throughout the State of Michigan. It is available to try cases in state and federal courts throughout Michigan, and in arbitration and select federal courts throughout the country.
