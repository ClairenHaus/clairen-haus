Practice area

Real Estate

A comprehensive real estate practice representing a wide array of clients in all categories of real estate transactions.

Real estate law covers buying, selling, financing, building on and leasing property, along with the disputes that can come with it.

Who we represent

Our attorneys counsel buyers, sellers, landlords, tenants, borrowers and lenders. We also represent builders and developers of all kinds, including builders of single-family homes and developers of shopping centers, office and other commercial buildings.

  • Buyers and sellers
  • Landlords and tenants
  • Borrowers and lenders
  • Home builders
  • Commercial developers
  • Contractors and subcontractors

Every phase of a project

We are involved in every phase of the process, from land acquisition through construction financing, lease-up and permanent financing. After a project is complete, we assist with leasing and managing the property: negotiating and drafting leases, and handling all aspects of landlord and tenant relations.

  • Land acquisition
  • Construction financing
  • Lease-up and permanent financing
  • Lease negotiation and drafting
  • Landlord-tenant relations
  • Refinancings and workouts

Getting paid for the work

Wilson Rode represents contractors and subcontractors seeking payment for their work through construction liens, bond claims and subsequent litigation. We also regularly help clients handle refinancings and negotiate workouts on behalf of borrowers and lenders.
