Estate and Trust Litigation

Mike began his career as a litigator, a partner in a litigation firm in the 1980s. That background lets him handle estate and trust disputes, and it shapes how he drafts plans designed to avoid them.

Disputes the firm handles

  • Estate, probate, will and trust contests
  • Elder abuse matters
  • Disputes involving no-contest clauses
  • Disqualified gifts or trustees
  • Negotiating and drafting settlement agreements
  • Disputes over appointing a personal representative or trustee
  • Proceedings to determine entitlement to estate or trust distributions
  • Disputed property claims
  • Breach of trust or fiduciary duty, and actions by or against a trustee or personal representative

Litigation experience that informs planning

Because he has seen how disputes start, Mike can counsel estate planning clients on structuring a plan to avoid conflict, which matters to the many clients who expect one. His litigation background also means many estate planning attorneys who do not litigate refer disputes to him.

Fiduciary duties

Many disputes turn on whether a trustee, executor, conservator, agent or guardian met their duties. The firm's fiduciary practice is confined to estate planning, trust and probate law, advising both fiduciaries and those who believe a fiduciary's conduct has harmed them.
