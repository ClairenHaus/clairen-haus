Frequently asked questions

Answers drawn from the firm's own practices and procedures.

Where is the firm's office?

The Law Offices of Michael J. Wittick are at 7700 Irvine Center Drive, Suite 800, Irvine, CA 92618. You can reach the office at 949-753-2829.

What kinds of matters does the firm handle?

The firm is devoted to estate planning, estate and trust administration, probate, estate and trust litigation, asset protection and business planning, including living trusts, tax planning, retirement and charitable planning, special needs trusts, Medi-Cal planning and planning for physicians and dentists.

Is Michael J. Wittick a certified specialist?

Yes. Mike is a Certified Specialist in Estate Planning, Trust & Probate Law, certified by the State Bar of California Board of Legal Specialization.

What happens at the first consultation?

Mike explains what distinguishes his practice, educates you about your planning alternatives based on your confidential information, and makes every effort to recommend solutions and quote a fee. For more complex estates he may recommend additional information or meetings before recommending solutions.

Does the firm charge for the first consultation?

The firm offers one free initial consultation of up to two hours. Reviews of existing estate plans are charged at Mike's hourly rate, payable upon review.

How are fees charged?

Mike prefers to quote fixed fees, because clients typically prefer them over hourly rates. If you retain the firm after the initial consultation, the retainer agreement sets the charges for the rest of the engagement.

What should I do before my first meeting?

Complete the firm's Confidential Client Worksheet at your own pace at home, and bring it with any other documents the firm requests in its confirmation letter.

Can my financial advisor, CPA or insurance professional attend?

Yes, if it would make you more comfortable and you are willing to waive attorney-client privilege for that meeting. The firm believes strongly in a team approach and will work with your advisors. Mr. Wittick receives no fees from any advisors.

Why does the firm usually recommend a living trust instead of a will?

A will guarantees probate, which is expensive, slow and public, and does no disability planning. A properly drafted and funded living trust avoids probate and conservatorship, plans for disability and keeps your affairs private.

Will my trust work if I never transfer my assets into it?

No. Unless you change title or beneficiary designations to your trust, your estate plan will not work, and the estate will usually go through probate anyway. Mike recommends that the firm transfer title to your real property to your trust.

Why should an estate plan be updated?

Your life changes, the law changes, and planning techniques improve. The firm's annual maintenance programs, the Minimum Required Plan and the Estate Protection Plan, are designed to keep your plan working.

What should I do if a relative passes away without a trust?

Ask the family to complete the estate section of the firm's Confidential Client Worksheet and call to arrange a consultation. Knowing how each asset is titled helps determine whether probate will be necessary.

Which communities does the firm serve?

The firm is located in Irvine and serves clients throughout Irvine, Lake Forest, Laguna Woods, Laguna Hills, Foothill Ranch, Tustin and Aliso Viejo, the surrounding areas of Orange County, and probate courts throughout Southern California.
