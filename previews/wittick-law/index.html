Common questions

Before you call

Straight answers from the firm's own practices. See all questions.

What happens at the first consultation?

Mike explains what distinguishes his practice, educates you about your planning alternatives based on your confidential information, and makes every effort to recommend solutions and quote a fee. For more complex estates he may recommend additional information or meetings before recommending solutions.

Does the firm charge for the first consultation?

The firm offers one free initial consultation of up to two hours. Reviews of existing estate plans are charged at Mike's hourly rate, payable upon review.

How are fees charged?

Mike prefers to quote fixed fees, because clients typically prefer them over hourly rates. If you retain the firm after the initial consultation, the retainer agreement sets the charges for the rest of the engagement.

Will my trust work if I never transfer my assets into it?

No. Unless you change title or beneficiary designations to your trust, your estate plan will not work, and the estate will usually go through probate anyway. Mike recommends that the firm transfer title to your real property to your trust.
