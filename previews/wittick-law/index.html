What changes

Specific fixes, drawn from the current site.

  • Mobile-first layout replaces a table-based template

    The current site is built on nine layout tables with jQuery 1.9.1 and an XHTML doctype. The concept rebuilds the same content as responsive, semantic HTML.

  • A meta description and a sitemap

    The homepage has no meta description, so search engines write their own snippet; no XML sitemap was found. Both are set here.

  • Structured data for AI answers

    The current site has no schema markup. The concept adds LegalService, Person and FAQPage JSON-LD built only from the firm’s published facts.

  • Alt text on every image

    25 of 40 images on the current site lack alt text. Every image here is described.

About the practice

A three-step strategy, not a boilerplate form.

Certified Specialist in Estate Planning, Trust & Probate Law · State Bar of California Board of Legal Specialization

The firm is exclusively devoted to estate planning, estate administration, probate, estate litigation, asset protection and business planning. It describes itself as a people-oriented practice for individuals, couples and business owners who want customized, goal-directed planning.

Its approach has three steps: working with a counseling-oriented attorney, establishing and maintaining an ongoing updating program, and post-disability or death administration. The firm works as part of a team with the client’s other advisors, and Mike teaches estate planning to individuals, business owners, insurance professionals, financial advisers and accountants.

Common questions

Answers from the firm’s own site.

Each answer below is drawn from facts the firm already publishes, marked up so search engines and AI assistants can cite it. General information only, not legal advice.

Is Michael J. Wittick a certified specialist?

Yes. He is a Certified Specialist in Estate Planning, Trust and Probate Law, certified by the State Bar of California Board of Legal Specialization. The firm also reports an AV Preeminent peer review rating from Martindale-Hubbell.

What areas does Wittick Law serve?

The firm is located in Irvine, CA and serves clients with estate and wealth preservation matters throughout Irvine, Lake Forest, Laguna Woods, Laguna Hills, Foothill Ranch, Tustin, Aliso Viejo and the surrounding areas.

How does the firm approach estate planning?

With a three-step strategy: counseling with the attorney, an ongoing updating and maintenance program, and administration after disability or death. The firm offers maintenance plans for keeping an estate plan current.

Does the firm handle probate and trust administration?

Yes. Probate law, trust administration and succession, and estate and trust litigation are among its listed practice areas.
