Mike spent part of his early career in personal injury law, including medical malpractice, which led to long relationships with physicians, dentists and their advisors. Today those professionals are a significant part of his estate planning clientele. His litigation background also informs his work in estate and trust disputes, and helps him design plans that avoid them.

Mike teaches estate planning at professional and charitable organizations and provides regular training at financial planning, insurance and accounting institutions. He has also taught estate planning at local colleges and on television.
