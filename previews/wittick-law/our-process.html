Our process

From the first call to the signing ceremony and beyond, every step is designed to create an estate plan that will work.

1. Schedule a confidential consultation

Call the firm to schedule a consultation for an individual, a married couple or business owners. Because every expert's opinion is only as good as the information it relies on, you will be asked to complete the Confidential Client Worksheet at home, at your own pace. A letter confirms your meeting and lists anything else to bring.

2. The initial consultation

Mike explains what distinguishes his practice, educates you about your alternatives based on your confidential information, and makes every effort to recommend solutions and quote a fee. The firm offers one free initial consultation of up to two hours. You are welcome to invite your financial, insurance or accounting advisors.

3. Design meeting

Usually the second meeting. Mike counsels you on every aspect and alternative of the plan until you are satisfied the design meets your objectives.

4. Signing ceremony

Usually the third meeting. Mike shows you where each agreed design element appears in your plan and explains the remaining steps to make it work.

5. Transfer of title and beneficiary designations

Unless your assets are transferred to your trust, your plan will not work. Mike recommends that the firm transfer your real property to your trust and guides you through the right process for every other asset.

6. Ongoing maintenance

An estate plan is a process, not a magic book. Changes in your life, in the law and in planning techniques all call for updates, and experience has shown the firm that clients rarely update unless they are enrolled in a formal program. Two annual maintenance programs are available:

Minimum Required Plan

Reasonable calls and emails at no charge, an annual review meeting on request, annual verification of asset titling, trustee training and a successor trustee manual, minor document updates such as trustee and agent changes, and a quarterly newsletter.

Estate Protection Plan

Everything in the Minimum Required Plan, plus review of advanced planning needs, an annual meeting with your advisors, 24/7 access to your health care documents, a trust administration workshop, and letters to your trustees, executors, agents and guardians explaining their roles.

Call the firm to discuss fees for these programs.
