Special Needs Trusts

The firm has a special love for families raising a child with special needs, and real admiration for the parents who care for them.

Planning for a longer future

Advances in medicine mean many children with disabilities will outlive their parents. Effective planning coordinates public program benefits with private family resources, because there is no guarantee that public benefits or agencies will provide adequate support over a lifetime.

How a special needs trust helps

  • Provides for your loved one's quality of life beyond what public programs cover
  • Is designed to preserve eligibility for need-based public benefits
  • Names trusted people to manage funds and advocate over the long term
  • Fits within your overall estate plan and living trust
