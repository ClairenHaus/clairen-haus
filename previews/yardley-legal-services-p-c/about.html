About the firm

A different kind of law firm, serving Pennsylvania and New Jersey for over 40 years.

Formerly Mead and Bane

Forty years of practice, now in Lower Makefield

Yardley Legal Services, P.C. has been providing experienced legal services in Pennsylvania and New Jersey for over 40 years. After 25 years in the Borough of Yardley, the firm, formerly known as Mead and Bane, is now located at 301 Oxford Valley Road, Suite 603B, in Makefield Executive Quarters, Lower Makefield Township, Yardley, PA 19067.

The firm is led by managing attorney David R. Bane.

Why “a different kind of law firm”

Yardley Legal Services takes the uncertainty out of the cost of legal services. Many clients avoid using lawyers because they fear receiving an unexpected large bill. After your free initial consultation in the office, you are advised of the services to be provided and the estimated fees and costs. You will not be charged for routine phone calls.

If you are disabled, an appointment can be scheduled at your residence. Credit cards are accepted. In the firm’s own words: we are here to serve you.
