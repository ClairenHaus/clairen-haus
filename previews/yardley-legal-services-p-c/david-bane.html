About David Bane

David Bane, the managing attorney of Yardley Legal Services, has been practicing law for over 40 years in Pennsylvania and New Jersey. The firm, formerly known as Mead and Bane, spent 25 years in the Borough of Yardley before moving to Makefield Executive Quarters in Lower Makefield Township.

His practice focuses on estate planning, real estate settlements and estate administration, and he writes the firm’s newsletter on death taxes.
