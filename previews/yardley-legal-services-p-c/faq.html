Why not just buy a will or trust online?

Estate planning documents should be tailored to each family. For example, a standard trust should not be used for a beneficiary who is disabled or receives public benefits; if documents are not prepared correctly, the beneficiary could lose those benefits or the inheritance may have to be paid to the government.

If I die without a will, does my spouse inherit everything?

Not automatically. Most married couples are not aware that without a will the surviving spouse does not inherit the whole estate, and the children receive a statutory share.

Does my will control my retirement accounts?

No. Retirement accounts and other payable-on-death accounts pass by their beneficiary designations, which must be coordinated with the will to get the distribution you want.

What does estate administration involve?

A meeting with the heirs to review the estate’s assets, having the executor or administrator appointed, notice to beneficiaries, the Inheritance Tax Return, an accounting to the heirs, and managing the sale and distribution of assets.

Does the firm handle real estate closings in New Jersey?

Yes. The firm provides complete real estate settlement services in Pennsylvania and New Jersey, and has handled thousands of real estate closings.
