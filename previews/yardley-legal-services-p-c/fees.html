No unexpected large bill

Many clients avoid using lawyers because they fear receiving an unexpected large bill. Yardley Legal Services takes the uncertainty out of the cost of legal services: after your free initial consultation, you are told what will be done and what it is estimated to cost.

For estate planning, the firm will recommend which documents you need and the legal fee after the free initial consultation.
