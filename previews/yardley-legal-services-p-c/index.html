Common questions

Answers drawn from the firm’s own site.

Question-and-answer content, marked up as FAQ schema, is the format search engines and AI assistants quote directly.

Is the initial consultation free?

Yes. The firm offers a free initial office consultation. Afterward you are advised of the services to be provided and the estimated fees and costs.

Will I be charged for phone calls?

The firm states that clients are not charged for routine phone calls.

Can the attorney come to my home?

If you are disabled, an appointment can be scheduled at your residence. Home visits are also listed among the firm’s estate planning services.

Do you handle matters in New Jersey as well as Pennsylvania?

Yes. The firm provides legal services, including real estate settlements, in both Pennsylvania and New Jersey, and David Bane is a member of both state bars.

What does estate administration involve?

As the firm describes it: an initial meeting with heirs to review the estate’s assets, arranging for the executor (with a will) or administrator (without one) to be appointed, notice to beneficiaries, preparation and filing of the Inheritance Tax Return, an accounting of assets and expenses to heirs, and managing the sale of real estate and other assets and distribution to heirs.
