Powers of Attorney & Living Wills

Choose who can act for you, financially and medically, if you cannot act for yourself.

Two documents, two roles

The firm prepares financial (durable) powers of attorney and healthcare powers of attorney, including living wills, as part of its estate planning work.

In general terms, a financial (durable) power of attorney names someone you trust to handle money and property for you, and is written so that it continues if you become unable to act yourself. A healthcare power of attorney names someone to make medical decisions for you, and a living will records your own wishes about treatment.

Prepared with the rest of your plan

These documents work alongside your will and beneficiary designations, which is why the firm tailors each plan to the family rather than relying on boilerplate forms. After the free initial consultation you are told which documents are recommended and the legal fee.

If you cannot come to the office

If you are disabled, an appointment can be scheduled at your residence.
