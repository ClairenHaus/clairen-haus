1. Introduction

Clairen Haus LLC ("Clairen Haus," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website located at clairenhaus.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information submitted through the Site.

By using this Site, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Site.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

We collect personal information when you voluntarily submit it through a form on the Site, such as our contact form or our Layla text message sign-up form. This may include:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Organization or business name
  • The nature of your inquiry or business situation
  • Your mobile phone number and your consent to receive text messages, if you sign up for Layla call summary texts (see Section 4A)

If your business uses Layla, our after-hours AI phone answering service, Layla collects the information callers give when they call your business (such as their name, callback number, service address and a description of the issue) so it can pass that information to you.

2.2 Analytics, Cookies and Tracking Pixels

The Site uses the following analytics and advertising tools, which collect information automatically when you visit:

  • Google Analytics (Google LLC), which uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as the pages you visit, how long you stay, how you arrived at the Site, your browser and device type, and your approximate location based on your IP address. We use it to understand how the Site is used and to improve it.
  • Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms, Inc.), which uses cookies and similar technologies to record visits to the Site (page views) and share them with Meta so we can measure and improve our advertising on Facebook and Instagram, including showing ads to people who have visited the Site.

Google and Meta process this information under their own privacy policies. You can limit this collection by blocking or deleting cookies in your browser settings, by using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on (tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout), and by adjusting your ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram account settings. Our Layla text message sign-up page does not load these analytics or advertising tools.

2.3 Payment Information

We do not collect payment information through this Site. All billing is handled offline or through separate invoicing systems.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information you provide solely for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiry or request
  • To determine whether our services are a good fit for your needs
  • To communicate with you about services, bootcamps, or cohort programs you have expressed interest in
  • To maintain records of business communications
  • To send Layla call summary text messages and related service messages to business owners who have opted in (see Section 4A)
  • To understand how the Site is used and to measure our advertising (see Section 2.2)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. How We Share Your Information

We may share your information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our business, subject to confidentiality obligations. These may include:

  • Customer relationship management (CRM) platforms (e.g., HubSpot)
  • Email communication tools (e.g., Gmail via Google Workspace)
  • Workflow automation tools (e.g., Zapier)
  • Analytics and advertising measurement tools (Google Analytics and Meta Pixel, described in Section 2.2)
  • Text message delivery providers, for Layla call summary texts

Each of these providers has their own privacy policy governing how they handle data. We only share the minimum information necessary to operate our services.

None of the sharing described in this section applies to mobile phone numbers or text messaging opt-in data and consent, which are handled only as described in Section 4A.

We do not share your information with any other third parties except:

  • When required by law, regulation, or valid legal process
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Clairen Haus, our clients, or the public

4A. Text Messaging (SMS)

If you are a Clairen Haus client and you sign up on our Site to receive Layla call summary text messages, we collect your mobile phone number and a record of your consent to receive those messages (including the date, time and the page where you agreed). We use your mobile number only to send you summaries of calls Layla answered for your business, to reply to messages you send us, and to send service notices about your Layla account. We do not send marketing text messages.

We do not sell, rent or share mobile phone numbers or text messaging opt-in data and consent with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be shared with subcontractors that support our services, such as customer service and message delivery providers, only so they can deliver the messages you signed up for. All other categories of sharing described in this Policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message; you will receive one confirmation message and no further texts unless you opt in again. Reply HELP for help, or contact us at hello@clairenhaus.com. See the Layla Call Summary Text Messages section of our Terms & Conditions for the full program terms.
