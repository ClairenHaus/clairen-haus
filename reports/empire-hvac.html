What is actually wrong
Critical

Your site takes 33 seconds to load on mobile

When a homeowner's AC goes out in July, they search from their phone. Google's own data shows 53% of mobile visitors leave a site that takes longer than 3 seconds. Your site takes 11 times that. By the time Empire's homepage finishes loading, that homeowner has already called the next result. You have 1,157 reviews at 4.9 stars. People want to hire you. Your website will not let them.

Critical

8.2 MB page weight from unoptimized assets

Your homepage weighs 8.2 megabytes. A well-built site serving the same content would weigh under 1 MB. The culprit is unoptimized images and uncompressed CSS/JavaScript. This is the root cause of the 33-second load time. Properly compressing and lazy-loading these assets could cut load time by 80% or more without changing how the site looks.

Critical

First paint takes 10.5 seconds

First Contentful Paint is the moment a visitor sees anything at all on screen. For Empire, that moment is 10.5 seconds. For the first ten seconds, the visitor is staring at a blank white screen on their phone. Most will assume the site is broken and hit the back button.

Important

584ms of main thread blocking

Even after the page starts loading, it remains unresponsive for nearly 600 milliseconds at a time because heavy JavaScript is blocking the browser's main thread. This means buttons, links, and the phone number are visible but do not respond to taps. A visitor tapping "Call Now" during this window gets no response.

Important

Font rendering delays

Custom fonts load late, causing text to be invisible or flash into a different style after the page appears. This creates a jarring visual experience and contributes to the perception that the site is slow or broken.

Important

Unused JavaScript and CSS

The site loads JavaScript and CSS files that are not used on the homepage. This adds unnecessary weight and slows down the initial load. Removing or deferring unused code would improve both load time and the performance score significantly.
