What is dragging your score down
Critical

Google scores your mobile performance at 53 out of 100

Google uses this score as a ranking signal. When a homeowner searches "roofing company near me" or "roof repair Marietta," Google evaluates which sites to place first. Your score of 53 puts you in the "Needs Work" range, just above the "Poor" threshold of 50. Competitors scoring 80+ are being favored in local results. You have 497 reviews and a FORTIFIED Roof certification. That credibility should be working for you in search, not being undermined by site performance.

Critical

40.6 MB page weight

Your homepage forces every visitor to download 40.6 megabytes of data. A well-optimized site serving the same content would weigh under 2 MB. Yours is 20 times that. The most likely cause is uncompressed video content (likely drone footage) loading automatically on every visit. This is the single largest factor in your low performance score. The footage can be compressed, thumbnailed, or loaded on demand without losing the visual impact.

Critical

Cumulative Layout Shift: 0.154

As your page loads, elements shift and jump around the screen. Google measures this as CLS and rates anything above 0.1 as "Poor." Yours is 0.154. This is both a ranking factor and a usability issue. On mobile, a visitor trying to tap your phone number could accidentally tap something else because the layout moved at the moment they reached for it. Google penalizes high CLS directly in search rankings.

Important

Links are not crawlable

Some links on your site cannot be followed by Google's crawler. This means pages that should be indexed and appearing in search results may be invisible to Google. For a company with 497 reviews and FORTIFIED Roof certification, every page should be discoverable.

Important

Best Practices score: 73 out of 100

This is the lowest Best Practices score across our analysis. Google flags issues with how the site handles browser APIs, security headers, and modern web standards. A low Best Practices score signals to Google that the site has not been maintained to current standards, which can compound the ranking penalty from the low performance score.

Important

Contrast ratio failures

Text on several sections does not meet minimum contrast requirements, contributing to the accessibility score of 88. Google evaluates accessibility as part of its overall site quality assessment. This is also a practical issue for homeowners viewing your site on their phone in direct sunlight while inspecting roof damage.
