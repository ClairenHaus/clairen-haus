What is actually wrong
Critical

Your site takes 15.5 seconds to show content on mobile

When a homeowner searches "roofing company near me" after a storm, they are on their phone. Google's data shows 53% of mobile visitors leave after 3 seconds. Your site takes five times that to show its first meaningful content. By the time your homepage appears, that homeowner has already tapped the back button and called your competitor. You are a GAF Master Elite contractor expanding into three states. Your site should reflect that.

Critical

8.1 MB page weight, almost entirely unoptimized images

Your homepage weighs 8.1 megabytes. The top-performing audit flag is "Improve image delivery." Your photos of completed roofing jobs are high quality, but they are being served at full resolution to every device, including phones on cellular data. Properly compressing, resizing, and lazy-loading these images would cut page weight by 70-80% without any visible quality loss.

Critical

758ms of main thread blocking

Your site's JavaScript blocks the browser for over three-quarters of a second at a time. During these moments, the page appears loaded but nothing responds to taps. A homeowner trying to tap your phone number or "Get a Free Quote" button gets no response and assumes the site is broken. This is the second-largest contributor to your poor performance score.

Critical

16.8 seconds to become interactive

Time to Interactive measures when a visitor can actually use your site: tap buttons, fill out forms, click phone numbers. At 16.8 seconds, your site is effectively frozen for the first 17 seconds of every mobile visit. The benchmark is under 3.8 seconds.

Important

Unused CSS and JavaScript

The site loads stylesheets and scripts that are not used on the homepage. This adds unnecessary weight and delays rendering. Removing or deferring this unused code is one of the fastest fixes available.

Important

No main landmark for accessibility

The site is missing a main content landmark, which affects screen reader users and contributes to the accessibility score of 85. This is a straightforward HTML fix that also signals to Google that the site follows modern web standards.
