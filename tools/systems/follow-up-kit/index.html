01
IMMEDIATELY
SMS
The Warm Opener
Hey [Name]! Just saw your message about [your offer]. I'd love to connect — what does [Day/Day] look like for a quick call?
Keep it short. One question. The goal is a reply, not a close.
02
2 HOURS LATER
EMAIL
The Value Introduction
Subject: Quick question, [Name] Hi [Name], Thanks for reaching out about [your offer]. I wanted to follow up and make sure you got my earlier message. While I have you — a lot of people come to me because [main pain point your offer solves]. If that's where you're at, I think we'd have a really useful conversation. Here's what working together typically looks like: [2-3 sentence overview of your process/outcome]. I have a few spots open this week. Here's my calendar: [booking link] Looking forward to connecting. [Your name]
This email sets context and delivers one clear next step. One CTA only — the booking link.
03
24 HOURS LATER
SMS
The Light Check-In
Hey [Name], just checking in — did my earlier message come through okay? Happy to answer any questions before we connect.
Assume technical issues, not disinterest. One question. No pressure.
04
3 DAYS LATER
EMAIL
The Social Proof Angle
Subject: What [a client / someone in your position] said after working together Hi [Name], I know you're busy — so I'll keep this short. [Client first name or "A recent client"] came to me with [their situation before working together]. After [your process / timeframe], [the result they got]. I think we could do something similar for [their business / their situation]. If you're open to a 20-minute call, here's my calendar: [booking link] Either way, I hope things are going well. [Your name]
Social proof resets the conversation without being pushy. Swap in a real result if you have one — even a small win works.
05
7 DAYS LATER
EMAIL
The Graceful Close
Subject: Closing the loop Hi [Name], I don't want to keep filling your inbox, so this is my last follow-up. If the timing isn't right — no problem at all. I'll be here when it is. If you do want to connect, here's my calendar: [booking link] Rooting for you either way. [Your name]
Closing the loop creates urgency without desperation. A lot of people book after this email because it removes the pressure.
01
SAME DAY
SMS
The Benefit of the Doubt
Hey [Name], I had us down for [time] today — I may have the wrong link or time zone. Totally fine if something came up. Want to find another time?
Assume the best. Never make them feel guilty. One question to re-open.
02
NEXT DAY
EMAIL
The Easy Rebook
Subject: Rescheduling — [Name] Hi [Name], Hoping everything's okay on your end. I wanted to make it easy to find another time if you're still interested. Here's my calendar: [booking link] Grab any slot that works. Looking forward to it. [Your name]
Short. Easy. One link. No guilt-tripping.
03
3 DAYS LATER
SMS
The Value Re-Touch
Hey [Name] — no pressure at all. I wanted to share something quick that might be helpful: [one useful tip or resource related to your offer]. Hope things are going well.
Give before you ask again. A useful tip resets the relationship.
04
7 DAYS LATER
EMAIL
The Soft Close
Subject: Still here if you need me Hi [Name], I'll keep this short. I'm going to stop following up after this — I don't want to be in your inbox if the timing isn't right. If you'd like to reconnect at some point, my calendar is always open: [booking link] Wishing you all the best. [Your name]
Leave the door open. Some of these people will book 2-3 months later when the timing shifts.
Start here. This sequence is your fastest path to results. Load it into your email or SMS tool, send to contacts who haven't heard from you in 60+ days, and watch what happens. Full walkthrough video in Component 5.
01
DAY 0
EMAIL
The Warm Re-Intro
Subject: Hey [Name] — checking in Hi [Name], It's been a while. No pitch, no agenda — I just wanted to check in and see how things are going in your business. What's the biggest thing on your plate right now? I'd love to hear what you've been up to. [Your name]
Open with a question, not a pitch. You're re-opening the relationship, not asking for a sale. Response rate on this message is usually 15-25% with a warm list.
02
DAY 1
EMAIL
The Value Drop
Subject: Something that might help Hi [Name], I've been thinking about [common challenge your audience faces] a lot lately. Here's something I've seen work really well: [one concrete tip, framework, or insight you can share for free]. No strings. Just something I thought you'd find useful. Talk soon, [Your name]
Give before you ask. This email builds goodwill and gets opens for the next message. Swap in your best free insight — the more specific, the better.
03
DAY 3
EMAIL
The Soft Pitch
Subject: For business owners dealing with [pain point] Hi [Name], I put together something for people who are struggling with [specific problem your offer solves]. Here's what it covers: - [Benefit 1] - [Benefit 2] - [Benefit 3] It's [your offer name] and it's [price/format]. Here's where to grab it: [link] [Your name]
One offer. One link. No multiple options. Make the decision easy.
04
DAY 5
EMAIL
The Objection Handler
Subject: "I don't have time to set it up" Hi [Name], I hear this a lot. The thing is — [your offer] is designed specifically for the person who doesn't have time. That's the whole point. [1-2 sentences on how fast/easy it is to implement]. The people getting the best results from it are the ones who felt like they didn't have time to deal with it. They set it up in [timeframe] and haven't had to think about [problem] since. Still available here: [link] [Your name]
Address the most common reason they haven't bought yet. Reframe the objection rather than dismissing it.
05
DAY 7
EMAIL
The Close
Subject: Last one Hi [Name], This is the last time I'll mention this — I know your inbox is full. If the timing's right, here's where to grab it: [link] If it's not, no worries at all. I'll still be here. Either way, I'm rooting for you. [Your name]
Last-chance energy without desperation. "I'm rooting for you" closes with warmth. A lot of people buy on this message because the pressure is completely off.

COMPONENT 03
Setup Day Checklist
Go live in one afternoon — in order
›
MORNING — CHOOSE YOUR TOOLS (1-2 hrs)
Pick your email tool. Free: Mailchimp or MailerLite. Mid: ActiveCampaign or ConvertKit. Full stack: GoHighLevel or HubSpot.
Pick your SMS tool. Free: Google Voice + Zapier. Low-cost: SimpleTexting or EZTexting trial. Full stack: GoHighLevel or Twilio.
Export your existing contact list from wherever it lives (Facebook, spreadsheet, old CRM). Save as CSV.
Import your contact list into your chosen email tool. Tag cold contacts as "Reactivation."
Load the Dead List Reactivation sequence into your email tool (5 messages, timing set to Day 0, 1, 3, 5, 7).
Test the sequence with your own email address. Confirm all 5 messages fire correctly.
AFTERNOON — SET UP INBOUND AUTOMATION (1-2 hrs)
Set up your lead capture trigger: Facebook Lead Ad form, website contact form, or DM keyword automation (Manychat free).
Connect trigger to your email tool via Zapier (free tier: 100 tasks/month) or native integration.
Load the New Inbound Lead sequence (5 messages) and set it to fire when a new contact is tagged "New Lead."
Connect your booking tool (Calendly free, TidyCal, or Cal.com) and add your booking link to messages 2 and 5.
Test the full inbound flow: submit a fake lead form entry, confirm the immediate response fires.
Send the Dead List Reactivation sequence to your "Reactivation" segment. You're live.
